MDF: v1.31

Fine bricks are similar in purpose to regular bricks, but are five times more valuable. Most regular bricks have an item value of 5☼, while fine bricks have a value of 25☼. Because of their higher value, they are a good choice for trade goods or improving fortress value.

A batch of four fine bricks requires two stone boulders, as opposed to the single-stone requirement of normal bricks.
