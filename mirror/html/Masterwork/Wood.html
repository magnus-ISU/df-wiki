MDF: v1.31

Wood in Masterwork Dwarf Fortress is greatly simplified from vanilla DF, a feature that helps prevent FPS death.

Types of wood

There are five types of wood in Masterwork DF:

  • Rough wood, produced from most above-ground trees
  • Fungiwood, produced by most underground trees
  • Netherwood, produced by deep-underground trees
  • Ironbark and Steeloak, only produced by rare above-ground trees

Processing

Wood and planted trees can be processed at the timberyard.
