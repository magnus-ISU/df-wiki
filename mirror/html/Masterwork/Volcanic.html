Volcanic is the third best metal in the game and represents the crowning achievement of dwarven metallurgy. An alloy of steel, slade, mithril and obsidian, it is produced in the Great Magma Forge. Warm to the touch, volcanic is excellent for armor and holds an edge twice as sharp as steel. Weapons made from volcanic do extra damage against Frost Giants.
