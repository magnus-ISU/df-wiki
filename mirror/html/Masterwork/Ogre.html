A giant humanoid monster found stomping about in the evil plains. Their low howls can be heard long before they are seen. Ogre meat is so tough that not even the best cook would dare to use it. They take a liking of kobolds and are often seen near kobold camps or caves. They guard their little friends well and in return are taken care of by the tribe, a symbiotic partnership.
