Slag is a material, usually found as bars or massive bars after smelting metals. It is intended as a waste product, causing clutter in the smelter or other workshops. It has no stockpile entries, so it cannot be transported by wheelbarrow or minecart.

Slag may be "disposed of" in a Slag pit, with reactions to produce nothing, or to produce concrete blocks.
