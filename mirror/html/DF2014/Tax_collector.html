This page refers to a Dwarf Fortress feature that no longer exists in v0.47.05. To see this page as it existed in previous versions, use the versioned links above.

The Tax collector does not exist in this version of Dwarf Fortress.

Before it was removed from the game, the tax collector was a noble who collected taxes from your dwarves on behalf of the baron, as part of the dwarven economy.

The tax collector was removed, along with the rest of the dwarven economy, in DF v0.31.01. Toady One has stated that the dwarven economy may return after sweeping changes are made, although such changes are "a long way off".
