A champion is an appointed noble position which becomes available after a fortress becomes a barony. They have no room requirements.

Other dwarves who talk to the Champion will get a special thought about "talking to a pillar of society", with the happiness bonus or penalty depending on the dwarf's viewpoint on tradition, and they will also satisfy their need to "uphold tradition".
