Liquid is a classification under the stocks menu, containing nonalcoholic fluids (drinks are separate) such as water, milk, lye, dwarven syrup, honey and royal jelly. Royal jelly which is still part of one of your hives is also shown here, while honey will only be shown after it has been pressed from a honeycomb. It also lists ichor, blood and venom.

Magma may also be listed as a liquid. This happens whenever the magma fluid melts a non magma-safe stone. As the raws for any stone list their liquid state as magma, when the magma fluid melts the rocks, they become listed as a magma glob on the stocks page.
