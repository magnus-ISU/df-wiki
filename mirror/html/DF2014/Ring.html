Summary Rings can be worn on the fingers and toes. Rings will be put first on the thumb, right hand; then on thumb, left hand; then on first finger, right hand; then on first finger, left hand; and so on. Once all the fingers have a ring, rings will be put on the toes, starting with the first toe, right foot; then first toe, left foot; etc.; looping until each digit has eleven rings for a total of 220 rings.

Crafting Rings In Dwarf Fortress require only one piece of material to create. (EX: 1 silver bar makes one silver ring, 1 claystone makes 1 claystone ring) If you make a metal ring you will also need fuel to create the ring.
