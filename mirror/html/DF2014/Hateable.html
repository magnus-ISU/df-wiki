Hateable vermin (creatures with the [VERMIN_HATEABLE] token) can appear as negative dwarven preferences. Dwarves who encounter detested vermin may experience a negative thought. Being hated by some dwarves does not prevent the vermin from appearing as a positive preference for other dwarves. Be careful when setting up your zoos - poor creature selections engravings can lead to civil unrest.
