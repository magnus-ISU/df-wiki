A gigantic arachnid with huge pincers and a poisonous barbed tail. It is found in the savage lands.

Giant desert scorpions were removed from the game in version 0.42.04, with plans for a replacement in a future update. Players who can't wait that long can simply re-add them themselves through modding by copying the raws provided below.

Admired for their tails.
