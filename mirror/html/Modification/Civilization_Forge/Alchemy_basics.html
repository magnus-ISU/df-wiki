Alchemy Guide

This is the Basic Alchemy Guide for the Civilization Forge Mod

Basic Alchemy items

Alchemical Jars: These jars are the basic unit to hold alchemical items. They are created at most workshops from most materials; glass, ceramic, or metal. Note that many reactions require 5 jars, so it is wise to keep many extras on hand.

Alchemical Transport Tubes: These are 'metal' bars that you can import that contain a jar's worth of an alchemical substance. Their contents can be transferred to a jar at the Alchemy Workbench. No longer used.



General Note: All reactions here are assumed to be at the Alchemist's workshop unless stated otherwise

Alchemical Components:

Oil 

   Found: Extracted from Rock Nut Paste in a Screw Press
    Uses: Used to create Night Crystal and Void Crystal
    Requires: 1 bunch of Oil Nuts and 1 alchemical jar OR 1 tunnel tube log and 3 alchemical jars
    Produces: 1 jar of Oil or 3 jars of Oil


Quicksilver 

   Found: Extracted from Cinnabar
    Uses: Used to create Blood Crystal and Void Crystal
    Requires: 1 boulder of Cinnabar and 1 alchemical jar
    Produces: 1 jar of quicksilver

Midnight Elixir 

   Found: Created from Cobaltite and Oil in a Frothing Pool
    Uses: Used to create Night Crystal and Void Crystal
    Requires: 1 boulder of Cobaltite and 5 alchemical jars filled with Oil
    Produces: 5 jars of Midnight Elixir

Black Antimony 

   Found: Extracted from Stibnite
    Uses: Used to create Void Crystal
    Requires: 1 boulder of Stibnite and 5 alchemical jars 
    Produces: 5 jars of Black Antimony

Saltpeter 

  Found: Either dug from the earth or created from Potash in the Saltpeter beds.
   Uses: Used to create Emen Aren
   Requires: 1 bar of potash
   Produces: 1 boulder of saltpeter

Green Vitriol 

  Found: Extracted from Melanterite
   Uses: Can be distilled to Oil of Vitriol
   Requires: 1 boulder of Melanterite and 5 alchemical jars 
   Produces: 5 jars of Green Vitriol

Oil of Vitriol 

  Found: Distilled from Green Vitriol or Alum
   Uses: Used to create Emen Aren
   Requires: 1 jar of Green Vitriol OR 1 Jar of Alum
   Produces: 1 jar of Oil of Vitriol

Blue Vitriol 

  Found: Extracted from Chalcanthite
   Uses: Used to create Emen Aren
   Requires: 1 boulder of Chalcanthite and 5 alchemical jars 
   Produces: 5 jars of Blue Vitriol

Alum 

   Found: Extracted from Alunite, Bauxite, or Alum Shale
    Uses: Used to make Oil of Vitriol and Emen Aren
    Requires: 1 boulder of an Alum Stone, 1 bar of Potash, and 5 alchemical jars 
    Produces: 5 jars of Alum

Emen Aren (Strong Water) 

   Found: Made from combining Saltpeter and Oil of Vitriol or combining Blue Vitriol, Saltpeter, and Alum
    Uses: Used to create Relon Aren and separate Mithril/Levisium from ore
    Requires: 1 boulder of Saltpeter and 5 alchemical jars filled with Oil of Vitriol OR 2 alchemical jars filled with Blue Vitriol, 1 boulder of Saltpeter, and 1 jar filled with Alum
    Produces: 5 jars of Emen Aren OR 3 jars of Emen Aren

Salt 

   Found: Mined from Rock Salt
    Uses: Used to create Nabid Ubal
    Requires: 1 boulder of Rock Salt and 5 alchemical jars 
    Produces: 5 jars of Salt

Nabid Ubal (Spirit of Salt) 

   Found: Made from Salt and Oil of Vitriol
    Uses: Used to create Relon Aren  
    Requires: 1 jar of Salt and 1 jar of Oil of Vitriol 
    Produces: 1 jar of Nabid Ubal and 1 empty alchemical jar

Relon Aren (Royal Water) 

   Found: Made from Nabid Ubal and Emen Aren
    Uses: Used to create the various Elemental Metals
    Requires: 3 jar of Nabid Ubal and 1 jar of Emen Aren
    Produces: 4 jars of Relon Aren

Ikal Nabid (Healing Salts) 

   Found: Made from Dolomite, talc, or olivine
    Uses: Used to create Tempered Crystal, Blood Crystal, Night Crystal, and Void Crystal
    Requires: 1 boulder of Dolomite, talc, or olivine and 5 alchemical jars 
    Produces: 5 jars of Ikal Nabid

Elixir of Frost 

   Found: Made from Frost Crystal and Relon Aren
    Uses: Used to create Glacium bars
    Requires: 1 cut Frost Crystal gem and 5 jars of Relon Aren
    Produces: 5 jars of Elixir of Frost

Elixir of Fire 

   Found: Made from Fireheart and Relon Aren
    Uses: Used to create Incendium bars
    Requires: 1 cut Fireheart gem and 5 jars of Relon Aren
    Produces: 5 jars of Elixir of Fire

Elixir of Fury 

   Found: Made from Storm Crystal and Relon Aren
    Uses: Used to create Tempestium bars
    Requires: 1 cut Storm Crystal gem and 5 jars of Relon Aren
    Produces: 5 jars of Elixir of Fury

Elixir of Stone 

   Found: Made from Ward Crystal and Relon Aren
    Uses: Used to create Ward Iron bars
    Requires: 1 cut Ward Crystal gem and 5 jars of Relon Aren
    Produces: 5 jars of Elixir of Stone

Pure Elixir 

   Found: Made from Frost Crystal, Fireheart, Storm Crystal, Ward Crystal and Relon Aren
    Uses: Used to create Elementium bars     
    Requires: 1 cut Ward Crystal, 1 Frost Crystal, 1 Fireheart, 1 Storm Crystal, and 5 jars of Relon Aren
    Produces: 5 jars of Pure Elixir

Infusion Chamber

The Infusion Chamber is used to create the various Elemental Metals

1 bar of iron + 1 Elixir of Frost = 1 Glacium Bar

1 bar of iron + 1 Elixir of Fire = 1 Incendium Bar

1 bar of iron + 1 Elixir of Fury = 1 Tempestium Bar

1 bar of iron + 1 Elixir of Stone = 1 Ward Iron Bar

1 bar of iron + 1 Pure Elixir = 1 Elementium Bar

Tempering Chamber

The Tempering Chamber is used to turn Raw Crystal Glass into the various Crystal Bars

1 Raw Crystal Glass + Ikal Nabid + Fuel = 1 Tempered Crystal Bar

1 Raw Crystal Glass + Ikal Nabid + Midnight Elixir + Fuel = 1 Night Crystal Bar

1 Raw Crystal Glass + Ikal Nabid + Quicksilver + Fuel = 1 Blood Crystal Bar

1 Raw Crystal Glass + Ikal Nabid + Quicksilver + Midnight Elixir + Black Antimony + Fuel = 1 Void Crystal Bar


Separation Chamber

Used to Separate Mithril from Truesilver and Levisium from Levitite

1 boulder Truesilver + Emen Aren = 1 Silver Bar + 1 Mithril Bar (20% chance)

1 boulder Levitite + Emen Aren = 1 Copper Bar + 1 Levisium Bar (33% chance)
