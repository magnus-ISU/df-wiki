Dracon Mod Background

The dracon mod is basically an add-on to the orc mod, but it is much, much stronger.

It is a future component in the Deserted squad mod. The Dracons are also like a wizard in that it uses magic. It can use staffs, as well as all the weapons Orcs can use.
