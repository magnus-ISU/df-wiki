Urist asks... How do I use this template?

Excellent question, Urist! To use this template, you need to add it to an article. Click the "edit" link of the appropriate article or section, and add the following text where you want to use this template: 

{{Urist asks|q=Question|a=Answer}}

* Replace "Question" with your question and "Answer" with your answer.

That text would produce something that looks like this:

Urist asks... Question

Answer

D for Dwarf

Urist asks... How do I mark a question as "D for Dwarf"?

Excellent question, Urist! To mark a question as "D for Dwarf" (often used for humorous answers), add "dfd=yes" before the question, like this: 

{{Urist asks|dfd=yes|q=Question|a=Answer}}

This produces:

Urist asks... Question D

Answer

Urist asks... How do I keep the question from being added to the category?

Excellent question, Urist! Replacing "dfd" with "dfdx" will prevent inclusion in the D for Dwarf category (useful for examples like these).
