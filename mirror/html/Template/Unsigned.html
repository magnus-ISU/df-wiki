Signing your comments

To make conversations easier to follow, please sign your comments on talk pages with 4 tildes: --~~~~, –~~~~, or ~~~~ are suggested (slight variations are also acceptable; only the 4 tildes are required to produce the signature). This works for any users (anonymous/unregistered users and registered/logged-in users). If you are logged in, you can change your signature in your preferences.

Template usage

Please subst this template when signing others' comments.

To do so, type 

{{subst:unsigned|username}}

This turns into:

– unsigned comment by username
