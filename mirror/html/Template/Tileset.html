It is best to specify filename=tileset.png, and leave title and image unspecified, in most cases.

Title herrbdog_16x16
Author Herrbdog
Dated 2007-11-15
Tile Size 16×16
Resolution 1280×400
Versions 14×14, 16×16
Comments This is Herrbdog's well-known tileset.
