Lists all the reasons dwarves like something. For example:

  • {{listprefstring|DF2014:Eagle/raw}} → Some dwarves like eagles for their high soaring.
  • {{listprefstring|DF2014:Eagle/raw}} → Some dwarves like rocs for their awe-inspiring size and dedication to their young.
  • {{listprefstring|DF2014:Nightwing/raw}} → Some dwarves like nightwings for their jackal heads, bat wings, long tails, manes, fangs, ability to suck blood, tightly-stretched skin, and needle-like horns.
  • {{listprefstring|{{DF2014:Nightwing/raw}}}} → Some dwarves like nightwings for their jackal heads, bat wings, long tails, manes, fangs, ability to suck blood, tightly-stretched skin, and needle-like horns.

Note that the reason for using DF2014 examples is merely that the raws for later version(s) have not been added at the time of writing

Argument can be any of the following:

  • Location of the raw file. e.g. DF2014:Nightwing/raw
  • Full text of the raw file.
  • None (Uses {{FULLPAGENAME}}/raw)
