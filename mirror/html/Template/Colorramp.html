Takes up to nine colors and creates a row of █ (block) characters in the specified order.

Examples: 

{{Colorramp|{{fgcolor|4:0}}|{{fgcolor|4:1}}|{{fgcolor|6:1}}|{{fgcolor|2:1}}|{{fgcolor|3:1}}|{{fgcolor|1:0}}|{{fgcolor|1:1}}|{{fgcolor|5:0}}}}
{{Colorramp|mediumvioletred|mediumvioletred|mediumpurple|midnightblue|midnightblue}}
{{Colorramp|#02f908|#009e75|#e45bd9|#3f55c6|#635589}}

Output:

████████

█████

█████
