This is like Template:Version except for whole articles or sections.
The use for which it was created was for articles copypasted from the old wiki.

Articles that include this template are automatically added to Category:Needs update.

More details can be included in a named parameter: 

{{old|Details here}}
This article or section may need to be updated due to recent changes.
Details here


A single version can be referenced with a v parameter: 

{{old|v=0.47.05}}
This article or section may need to be updated due to changes in 0.47.05.
