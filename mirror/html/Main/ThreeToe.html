ThreeToe is the nickname used by Zach Adams, co-designer of Dwarf Fortress and author of the short stories which highlight Dwarf Fortress's upcoming features. He can be reached at About Bay 12 Games or the Bay 12 Games Forum.

The other designer, and programmer, is Zach's younger brother, Tarn (Toady One).

ThreeToe married on July 15, 2017. All we know about his spouse Annie Forsman-Adams is that she has "royal blood". According to him, their wedding was planned to be "as dwarfy as possible." [1]

Since March 2019[2], ThreeToe has been keeping four smaller threetoes: Harry, Ramone, Sid and Bowie.
