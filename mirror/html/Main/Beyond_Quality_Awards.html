The Beyond Quality Awards are a set of awards that were given out by Bay 12 Games to various community members and games other than Dwarf Fortress, as part of the Dwarf Fortress 20th Anniversary Show held on August 5 2026 (Twitch VOD; YouTube VOD).

Category Description Winner
Origins Award The best game that influenced Dwarf Fortress. Angband
Storyteller Award For the most memorable Dwarf Fortress stories. Kruggsmash
Best Roguelike Award The best roguelike according to the Adams brothers. Caves of Qud
Best Descendant Award The best game inspired by Dwarf Fortress. RimWorld
Inside Scoop Award For contribution to the popularization of Dwarf Fortress. BlindiRL
Moment in Time Award For the best documentary work about Dwarf Fortress. Noclip
Outpost Liaison Award For contributions to community moderation outside of the Bay 12 Forums. clinodev
Wizardry Award For contributions to improving the Dwarf Fortress gaming experience. DFHack
Hidden Gem Award The best recent little-known game. Shadows of the Forbidden Gods
