A medium-sized spotted fish found in temperate rivers and lakes.

Rainbow trout are a type of aquatic vermin found in temperate freshwater rivers and lakes year-round. They are a ready source of food when cleaned at a fishery.

Some dwarves like rainbow trout for their coloration.
