Cabinets are a type of furniture used for storing clothes. They are a common requirement for nobles and they are also good for increasing the value of a room. A cabinet can hold close to 20 pieces of clothing. Cabinets do not block movement; dwarves and other creatures are able to walk through tiles with cabinets.

If a dwarf does not own a cabinet in their bedroom, they will leave their clothing on the floor.

Cabinets can be made from wood, stone, glass, and all ordinary types of metal except pig iron and bismuth.

Barberini Cabinet. Probably human made. Definitely not elf made.
