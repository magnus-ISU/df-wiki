Alexandra here with a post-Adventure Mode beta launch update. We hope you’re enjoying all the mischief and mayhem that the beta has to offer. Some of the stories you’ve been sharing have been hilarious and thought-provoking to say the least. This story about stealing a shield gone wrong made me laugh a lot.

— Kitfox and Bay 12 Games, April 18, 2024

  • Patch notes on Steam

The v51 Adventure mode beta ran from April 17, 2024, to January 23, 2025. During this period, Steam users could access the beta branch to play Premium Adventure mode. Many non-adventure-specific features were added to later v50 updates during the testing period, as development ran in parallel with the main branch.
