Moss is a special covering that appears on smooth stone outdoors. It is found primarily in shrines inhabited by bronze colossuses and titans. It is also sometimes found in the object testing arena[Verify]. It also is only visible when covering a wall (and only a wall) with premium graphics enabled.

Not to be confused with cave moss, a special type of grass that grows in subterranean soil.
