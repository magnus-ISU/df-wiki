Kitfox Games is a game studio focused on simulation games and roguelikes. Kitfox Games has developed several first-party titles, and serves as publisher for several indie games, including Dwarf Fortress.

Involvement with Dwarf Fortress

Bay 12 Games posted on April 2019 that they partnered with Kitfox, after a medical scare that made them worry about their long-term finances. Kitfox hired artists to work on the graphics set and soundtrack for the Premium release.[source]

In December 2022, Kitfox published Dwarf Fortress commercially to Itch.io and Steam.
