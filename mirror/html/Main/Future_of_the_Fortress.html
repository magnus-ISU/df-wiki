Future of the Fortress is the name of a series of threads on the Bay 12 Forums. Each month, Toady will round up any unanswered questions and answer them in a "Future of the Fortress reply". Other forum members answer questions if the answers can be found elsewhere or the question is a repeat.

History

Future of the Fortress threads started out as discussions of the development logs, called "Future of the Fortress" at the time. They eventually changed into a question and answer format.
