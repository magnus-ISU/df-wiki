"Spores" is an informal player term not found in the game, but often used in the community. It can refer to one of two things:

  • Spores from caverns (or old underground rivers) that allow underground shrubs and trees to grow in soil or muddy areas.
  • In versions before v0.40.19, the ability of DF dwarves and creatures to "breed by spores".
