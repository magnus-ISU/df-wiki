Toady One (or just Toady) is the nickname used by Tarn Adams, co-designer and programmer of Dwarf Fortress. He can be reached at About Bay 12 Games or the Bay 12 Games Forum.

Tarn Adams has a Ph.D in Mathematics from Stanford University. His dissertation was entitled Flat Chains in Banach Spaces.

In April 2009, Toady was adopted by Scamps.

The other designer is Zach Adams (ThreeToe), his older brother.
