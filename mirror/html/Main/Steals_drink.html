Creatures with the [CURIOUSBEAST_GUZZLER] creature token will happily devour your alcohol stocks, if given the chance - this can prove particularly fun when your newly-founded fort's entire alcohol supply is still sitting topside in the wagon.

This property can also be used to capture certain creatures (such as grizzly bears) by surrounding a small drink stockpile with cage traps.

Creatures who can steal drinks

The following creatures possess the [CURIOUSBEAST_GUZZLER] token.

  • Black bears
    • Black bear men
    • Giant black bears
  • Dark gnomes
  • Ettins
  • Giants
  • Giant moles
  • Giant rats
  • Grizzly bears
    • Grizzly bear men
    • Giant grizzly bears
  • Large rats
  • Mountain gnomes
  • Naked mole dogs
  • Polar bears
    • Polar bear men
    • Giant polar bears
  • Sloth bears
    • Sloth bear men
    • Giant sloth bears
