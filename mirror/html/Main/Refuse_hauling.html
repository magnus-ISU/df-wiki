Refuse Hauling is a labor which has no associated skill. A dwarf with refuse hauling enabled will haul items of refuse to refuse stockpiles. Refuse hauling is also used to haul items marked for dumping to a garbage dump zone.

All refuse hauling jobs are controlled by the "refuse collection" options in the standing orders menu.
