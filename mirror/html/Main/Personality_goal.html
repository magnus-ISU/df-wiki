Randomly generated personality of a miner.

Some creatures dream of accomplishing certain goals in their life, and these goals can, presumably, affect their behavior. If a creature has such goals, they will be listed in the "Values" sub-tab of the "Personality" tab of their character sheet. Dwarves will receive a happy thought upon completion of their goals, receiving the notation of "and this dream was realized" in the personality description following their original goals. Goals would apparently not currently be limited to one per creature, but for some sort of oversight: [1]. A creature needs the ability to learn in order to have a goal in life.

According to Toady, syndromes can cause creatures to re-evaluate their goals in worldgen; the boost to anxiety and distrust given to necromancers makes it more likely for them to develop the goal of ruling the world. [2]

Judging from the string dump, these personality facets may lead to choosing this goal:

  • High Pride (being too proud to give in to death)
  • High Vanity (being too vain to give in to death)
  • High Ambition (having ambitions for which death was only a small obstacle)
  • High Envy propensity (due to envy of those that live on)
  • High Anxiety propensity (due to panic about what happens after death)
  • Low Bravery (due to fear of death)
