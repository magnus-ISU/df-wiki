This page refers to a Dwarf Fortress feature that no longer exists in v53.16. To see this page as it existed in previous versions, use the versioned links above.

Back in version 0.28.181.40d and earlier, the Philosopher was a noble who arrived once your population reached 100; unlike most other nobles, it had no special room requirements and made no mandates.

In the current version, a Philosopher is merely a Scholar whose studies focus primarly on the appropriate topics.
