Creatures with the [NOSTUN] creature token cannot be stunned. These creatures can fall multiple z-levels, suffer great pain, or be injected with stunning venom without the momentary vulnerability of becoming stunned. Creatures with this token will attack relentless in combat until they are killed or knocked unconscious from pain. Luckily creatures with this token are rare.

Interestingly, this protection does not extend to large webs; webbed creatures will still pause and trigger any traps in their current tile.
