Creatures with the [NOPAIN] creature token feel no pain in (or out of) combat. These creatures will suffer grievous wounds and continue fighting without pause, making them significantly harder to kill - many foes are dispatched by a blow to the head after they fall unconscious from pain. Luckily creatures with this token are rare.
