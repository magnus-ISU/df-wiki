1st Granite 1056

So... Girdertrampled. I don't know what I was expecting exactly, but this certainly wasn't it. When I was told the story of Girdertrampled, I was told of a bustling, successful town. I found the town, but the people...

Oh, there they are. Dozens dead, and none of the survivors seem to care. When I talked to someone about the rest of the town they mentioned something about a nice waterfall. These dwarves are crazy...

The Baroness Consort Zefon, forbid me from exporting any and all toy hammers. I told her that if she wanted some so badly, she could just make some herself. She didn't take that to kindly.

There are 18 dwarves left. This is a decent number to get things going again, or rather it would be if I had some workers. Almost half of the population is either administration or children. Not good.

A wave of immigrants has arrived, along with a Baron and a Dungeon Master. I would almost feel excited, if I had any trapped animals that could be trained, I would be ecstatic. As it is, I don't even have the mechanisms to make all of the stone fall traps that I want, let alone the resources for any useful number of cage traps. I hope he'll pull his weight.

I've checked the logs, so far only three dwarves have arrived and they are all nobles. I'm sure that they will be useful...

Another guard killed. I have let the dwarves go outside. There is no use sealing us in here with that thing.
