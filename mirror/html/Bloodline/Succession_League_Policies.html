Serious Rules

Violating these repeatedly could lead to suspension, a theoretical possibility that should never happen.

  1. Don't vandalize wiki pages, even though they can be recovered. This includes non-League pages.
  • Discuss major changes and edits before implementing them!
  1. Don't intentionally ruin a succession game. Mistakes are part of DF, and losing is Fun, but intentionally ruining others' work is just wrong. Of course reverting to a previous save is always possible.
  2. Don't post links to sites that may contain Trojan Horses, adware, tracking bugs, viruses, explicit material, or any other questionable content without a clear warning proximate to the link.
  3. Don't post anyone else's email on a wiki page, or in a public forum, as this reveals it to webcrawlers and spam engines.

Preferred behavior

  1. Keep it clean, and in English. Some of us may have second languages, but we will only use them in private messaging.
  2. Avoid using Graphic tilesets when posting images to the Game page and/or its Discussion page. This is not a "RULE", but given the number of graphic sets out there it can get messy fast, and the only one none of us know is the default!

Suggestions

  1. Use private messages to send your email address.

If you have any questions about these policies, please visit the Discussion page.
