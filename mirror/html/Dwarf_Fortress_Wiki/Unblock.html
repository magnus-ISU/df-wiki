If you are mistakenly blocked as a spammer, please post on the wiki board of the Bay12 Forums and a wiki admin will reverse the block as soon as possible. Alternatively, you can send a PM on the forums to the admin who blocked you (note that "Briess" on this wiki is "Locriani" on the forums).

Do not try to create another account – it will almost certainly be autoblocked by the wiki software, and it can be hard to unblock your first account if your second one gets blocked too.
