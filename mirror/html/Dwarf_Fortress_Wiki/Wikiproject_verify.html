Welcome to wikiproject verify.


As DF updates, many of the things in the wiki go out of date, so we constantly need old information reviewed. This page is here to be a central hub for questionable information to be discussed, reviewed, and tested. The DF wiki needs your help to make sure everything is up to date.

If you have questions about something you want to add to (or correct in) an article, post it on the talk page.
