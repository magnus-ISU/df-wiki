Standard templates

All articles in version-specific namespaces (as well as Masterwork and Utility articles) should begin with {{quality|rating}}, followed by {{av}}.

Link templates

  • {{bug|issue ID}}, for linking to bugs on the bug tracker
    • {{bug|issue ID|link=1}}

See also: Category:Inline formatting templates

Citation templates

{{cite}} is the "main" citation template, although there are other, more specialized templates as well - see Category:Citation templates for a complete list.
