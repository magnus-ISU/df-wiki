This page tracks the distribution of the [Expanded Plants](Plants.md) across different biomes.

Once it's completed, I'll use this page to assign biome restrictions for the fictional Dwarf Fortress plants, and possibly massage the data on real-life plants. For example, I'm not sure any of the trees currently included in the game will grow in (non-mangrove) saltwater swamps; but "swamp" and "marsh" are differentiated by the fact that *swamps have trees growing in their water*. So I may need to place freshwater swamp trees in a saltwater swamp just to keep the place a swamp.
