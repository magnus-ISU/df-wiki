The duration values for the day are different from those in the vanilla game (where 1 day is 1200 ticks and 1 month is 28 days).

1 day = 1 day = 1120 ticks

1 week = 7.5 days = 8400 ticks

1 month = 30 days = 33600 ticks

1 season = 90 days = 100800 ticks
