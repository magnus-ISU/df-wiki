This page is for balancing the relative values of plant parts & plant products in the Expanded Plants mod.

**All information on this page should be considered tentative.**

Yes, I will be reducing the base values of thread, cloth, and a number of other things. We've got some crappy materials in the expanded plants mod; cattail clothing should *not* be as good as wool.
