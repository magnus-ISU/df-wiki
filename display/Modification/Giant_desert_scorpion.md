> *A gigantic arachnid with huge pincers and a poisonous barbed tail. It is found in the savage lands.*

**Giant desert scorpions** were removed from the game in [version 0.42.04](../DF2014/Release_information/0.42.04.md), with plans for a replacement in a future update. Players who can't wait that long can simply re-add them themselves through [modding](../Main/Modding.md) by copying the raws provided below.

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/8/87/Scorpion-panel2-col.png/250px-Scorpion-panel2-col.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Scorpion-panel2-col.png)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Scorpion-panel2-col.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Scorpion-panel2-col.png)

Admired for their *tails*.

To install the mod, add this creature definition to your raw/objects/creature_savage_tropical.txt file:
