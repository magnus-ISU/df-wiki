### Myriapoda (centipedes and millipedes)

Stone centipede [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithobius](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithobius) (Common representative species. Represents *Lithobius foricatus*. Found under rocks in damp areas.)

Tiger centipede [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scolopendra_polymorpha](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scolopendra_polymorpha) (Large striped species, found in desert areas.)

Soil centipede [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centipede#Geophilomorpha](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centipede#Geophilomorpha) (Long, threadlike centipede. Represents various geophilomorphs. Found in soil, but you probably guessed that.)

Cave centipede [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centipede#Scutigeromorpha](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centipede#Scutigeromorpha) (Generalised giant tropical scutigeramorph. Very long legs, evolved to locate prey in the dark, and terrify humanoids. Possibly painful (but not deadly) bite.)

House centipede [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scutigeridae](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scutigeridae) (Similar to above, but lives in your house. Name is arguably problematic. Suggest "fortress centipede".)

Greater centipede [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scolopendra_gigantea](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scolopendra_gigantea) (Renamed giant centipede. A tropical/cave-dwelling species (although there is presently no way to confine a creature to caves in tropical biomes). Preys on small mammals, such as bats, and has a bite that can cause medical complications in the bite area, although there are no recorded fatalities.)

Pill millipede [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pill_millipede](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pill_millipede) (Primitive millipede that can roll into a ball, similar to that of a woodlouse.)

Shocking pink dragon millipede [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desmoxytes_purpurosea](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desmoxytes_purpurosea) (Loudly colored tropical species. Produces cyanide that can poison those who handle it.)

Yellow-spotted millipede [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harpaphe_haydeniana](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harpaphe_haydeniana) (Similar to above, but tasteless in a different way.)

Chongololo [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archispirostreptus_gigas](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archispirostreptus_gigas) (a.k.a the giant African millipede. Tropical forests are its habitat.)

Black millipede [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tachypodoiulus_niger](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tachypodoiulus_niger)
