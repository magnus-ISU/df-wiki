### Dracon Mod Background

The dracon mod is basically an add-on to the orc mod, but it is much, much stronger.

It is a future component in the [Deserted squad mod](../Main/Deserted_squad_mod.md). The Dracons are also like a [wizard](../Main/Wizard.md) in that it uses magic. It can use staffs, as well as all the weapons Orcs can use.
