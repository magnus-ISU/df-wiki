DFConfig is a graphical utility for altering [d_init.txt](../Main/D_init.txt.md) settings. A standalone version can be downloaded from [here](http://dffd.bay12games.com/file.php?id=2563); the main [forum thread](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=59933) is here. The utility is integrated into the [Lazy Newb Pack](Lazy_Newb_Pack.md)—in fact, it's what the pack is built on—but users looking to "build their own" distributions can use the standalone version.
