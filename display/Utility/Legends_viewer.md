Legends Viewer is a [utility](../Main/Utility.md) for 0.47 and earlier versions of DF that loads up the legends you can export in a much more usable format than the [Legends mode](../Main/Legends_mode.md) of DF itself. Legends Viewer can open pages in new tabs, filter information on a wide range of criteria, and display information on the map as well. Binaries are only available for Windows, as well as the source (written in C#).

It takes four files as input: the legends XML file, the sites and pops text, world history text, and a map image. It can load these separately, but it is recommended to load them as a single compressed archive (folder) - the XML can be multiple GB uncompressed for long histories, and reduce to less than 5% of its size.

[LegendsViewer-Next](../Main/Utilities.md#legendsviewer-next) is a successor for v50 Steam/Premium.
