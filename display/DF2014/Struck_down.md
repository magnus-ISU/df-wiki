When the message "\[dwarf\] has been **struck down**" appears, some dwarf has received an immediately fatal injury in [combat](Combat.md), namely : the [brain](Brain.md) being damaged, the [head](Head.md) being chopped off or the lower/upper body being cut in two. Too much of this message and your fort may experience [large amounts of fun](Tantrum_spiral.md).

If a dwarf is not seen dying by another dwarf there will be no announcement of the dwarf dying.

More specific death messages for other scenarios include the following: [shot](Bow.md) [and](Crossbow.md) [killed](Magma_crab.md), [burned](Fire.md) [to](Magma.md) [death](Fire_imp.md), [drowned](Water.md), [encased in ice](Ice.md), [encased in obsidian](Obsidian.md), [melted](Dragon.md), [crushed by a drawbridge](Dwarven_atom_smasher~2.md), [fell into a deep chasm](Hell.md), crushed by a [cave-in](Cave-in.md), died after colliding with an obstacle, and, most commonly among [nobles](Noble.md), [killed by a](Unfortunate_accident.md) [spike](Trap.md).
