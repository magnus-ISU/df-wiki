**Brass** is an [alloy](Metal.md) of [zinc](Zinc.md) and [copper](Copper.md). Brass can be used to make [furniture](Furniture.md) and [other objects](Metal_crafter.md) at a [metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md).

Brass can be made using one of the following recipes:

- [Zinc](Zinc.md) bar + [Copper](Copper.md) bar = 2 brass bars
- [Sphalerite](Sphalerite.md) + [Native copper](Native_copper.md) or [Malachite](Malachite.md) or [Tetrahedrite](Tetrahedrite.md) = 8 brass bars

Zinc and copper both have a [material value](Material_value.md) of 2, but brass has a value of 7, making it a very profitable alloy to produce (specifically, a value increase of 250%). Note that brass is not [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md).
