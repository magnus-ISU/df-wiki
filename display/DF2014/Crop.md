Ripe for the harvest.

**Crops** are [plants](Plant.md) that can be [farmed](Farming.md), traded for, or acquired by [plant gathering](Plant_gathering.md). The farming of crops happens at [farm plots](Farm_plot.md). There are two types of crops: [aboveground](Aboveground.md) and subterranean (underground). The [seeds](Seed.md) of subterranean crops may be brought from the starting embark screen or, with some small luck, purchased from dwarven caravans. Above ground crops and seeds may be purchased from human or elven caravans or gathered by dwarves with the plant gathering labor enabled. Seeds may also be collected by processing the plants or eating them (but not by cooking!). There is a limit of 200 seeds per type (adjustable in [d_init.txt](D_init.txt.md)), after which no additional seeds will be produced.

A few of the plants listed below are not strictly crops, as they have no seeds and can't be planted (see note "4", below). "Wet" and "Dry" determine where plants are found in proximity to watercourses when gathering wild plants, and do not affect farm plots.

Most plants can be brewed into [alcohol](Alcohol.md), each plant type producing a different variation, and dwarves do prefer *some* variety in their drink. Some plants may be eaten raw, others must be cooked first, others must be processed (by [milling](Milling.md) or [plant processing](Thresher.md)) before they are edible, and still others are inedible, producing only non-food products (their seeds may be edible, though). Drinks may be cooked as ingredients in prepared meals, but at least one of the ingredients must be a non-liquid.

All drinks require a spare [barrel](Barrel.md) or [large pot](Large_pot.md) for storage, and some other products also require specific containers for storage. Plants can be stored in barrels or without a container; seeds are stored individually or in [bags](Bag.md) which can then be put into barrels. It may be advisable to have a small stockpile that accepts only seeds next to your farm(s), since dwarves will happily carry entire pots of seeds across the map to pick up a single seed, leading to cancellation spam and irregular planting.

### Standard plants

These are the plants defined in the file plant_standard.txt. Standard plants have been changed very little from the previous version; tiles and colors have been upgraded, and brewing, pressing and processing plants to bags have been re-implemented as reactions, which means these options will be unavailable in the workshop if materials are missing.

### Garden plants

All garden plants are above-ground, and can grow year-round. Garden plants are defined in a separate file from standard plants, named, somewhat predictably, plant_garden.txt.

Unlike standard plants, garden plants will also produce growths - leaves, buds, flowers, pods and fruits. As a general rule, leaves can only be cooked, buds can be both cooked and brewed.

### Best crop to grow

There is no "best" crop, but some are clearly superior to others, depending on your needs and available resources.

#### All-purpose

Many [dwarves](Dwarf.md) prefer the humble [plump helmet](Plump_helmet.md), which can be [grown](Farming.md) year-round, [eaten raw](Food.md), [cooked](Cook.md), or brewed into [wine](Alcohol.md), and many early fortresses rely on the plump helmet crop as a staple of their diet. All above-ground crops are all-seasons as well, the best of which are probably [sun berries](Sun_berry.md), [hemp](Hemp.md), and [whip vines](Whip_vine.md). See the above table summarizing the crop products, limitations, and values.

Because of [occasional above-ground inconveniences](Siege.md), having an underground [plump helmet](Plump_helmet.md) farm, or, at least, some [plump helmet](Plump_helmet.md) spawn, may be useful emergency preparation.

#### Booze

Four principal seeds available at the beginning of an expedition ([cave wheat](Cave_wheat.md), [pig tail](Pig_tail.md), [plump helmet](Plump_helmet.md), [sweet pod](Sweet_pod.md)) all produce underground crops that can be made into [booze](Booze.md) worth 10☼. Above ground crops will require you to [trade](Trade.md) or [gather](Gather_plants.md) for [seeds](Seed.md), but the best of them that can be [farmed](Farming.md) are [sun berries](Sun_berry.md), which produce [sunshine](Sunshine.md) worth 25☼.

Keep in mind that dwarves care more about the diversity of available booze than its [value](Value.md).

For quantity, [cave wheat](Cave_wheat.md) and [sweet pod](Sweet_pod.md) mature in 42 days, versus 25 for the other crops, so they may yield less alcohol per farm plot. [Plump helmets](Plump_helmet.md) mature in 25 days and grow year-round, which may be a good starting point for your [alcohol](Alcohol.md) [industry](Industry.md), but exclusive dependence on it will cause [stress](Stress.md).

Rope reeds and pig tails can be made into booze, but you might prefer to use them to make thread.

#### Dye

There are four dye crops. Three 20☼ [dye](Dye.md) crops: [blade weed](Blade_weed.md) and [sliver barb](Sliver_barb.md) (above ground) and [dimple cups](Dimple_cup.md) (underground). [Hide root](Hide_root.md) (above ground) is a 10☼ [dye](Dye.md). Dyeing should be considered a low priority because all it does is change the color of cloth or thread and slightly increase its value. You might not want a dedicated farm to grow dye crops; it can be enough to forage for them with [herbalism](Plant_gathering.md) or import them from caravans.

#### Food

In terms of sheer [dwarfbucks](Currency.md), [sweet pods](Sweet_pod.md) produce the most valuable foodstuff: [dwarven syrup](Dwarven_syrup.md) (100☼). In terms of quantity and value, other good crops are [quarry bushes](Quarry_bush.md) (which yield 5 quarry bush leaves when processed) and [whip vines](Whip_vine.md) (which can be [milled](Mill.md) into [whip vine flour](Flour.md)). Diversity in meals is also important to dwarves.

#### Thread

All plant thread is equally valuable (at 2☼ per unit), so the main decision is between a seasonal underground crop ([pig tails](Pig_tail.md)) or one of the year-round [above-ground crops](Textile_industry.md#crops) ([cotton](Cotton.md), [rope reeds](Rope_reed.md), [hemp](Hemp.md), [flax](Flax.md), [jute](Jute.md), [ramie](Ramie.md), [kenaf](Kenaf.md)). These plants are also equally useful for [making paper](Papermaking.md). [Papyrus](Papyrus.md) is slightly easier to process if you want to make a lot of paper, but papyrus can't be used for any other purpose and it is only available in some tropical wetlands.

### Bugs

- Plant handling/processing is incomplete for some crops.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[6940](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=6940)</sup> This results in plants that, when picked, are inedible because the wrong parts are picked, or where seeds are impossible to pull from the proper plant parts. Large numbers of crops are therefore incapable of being farmed.
