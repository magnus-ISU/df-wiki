**Weapon racks** (b-r) are a type of [furniture](Furniture.md) which can be used to create a [barracks](Barracks.md) or to appease a [noble](Noble.md)'s demand or raise the [value](Value.md) of a [room](Room.md).

In theory, when used in a barracks designated for Individual Equipment storage, dwarves will prefer to store their weapons on the racks instead of stockpiles. Doing so in concert with [armor stands](Armor_stand~2.md) would allow you to create centralized armories for your military. Unfortunately, this doesn't work.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[1445](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=1445)</sup>

A samurai-related weapon rack.

### Bugs

- Dwarves don't actually use weapon racks for storing anything. If you have no stockpiles anywhere that accept weapons, they'll just get left where they're dropped.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[1445](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=1445)</sup>
