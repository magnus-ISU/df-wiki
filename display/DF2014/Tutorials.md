Of course, instead of trial and error you could also just read the wiki...

*Dwarf Fortress* started as a pretty complex game back in the days when it was 2D and barely had a quarter of the features it has now. It's easy to get overwhelmed when you first fire it up. Don't worry though, following a tutorial is a very easy way to learn the basics of the game in an afternoon.

Don't worry if you don't understand why you do things on your first run, even after reading the tutorial's explanation. If one particular thing bugs you, try starting a new fort and skipping it, then seeing what goes wrong! Especially in the current version, trial and error is great way of having lots of [fun](Fun.md).

### Learning Fortress Mode

Most of the tutorials were written for the older (40d) version of *Dwarf Fortress*, but nonetheless, they are still helpful. If you get stuck within a specific aspect of the game, then [search](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Special:Search) the wiki and don't hesitate to ask questions in the [forum](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?board=7.0).

The following tutorials will walk you through world generation, embarking with your 7 dwarves, game basics, and building a full-fledged fortress.

#### Feeding Dwarves

There are several ways to [feed](Food.md) your dwarves. Your initial supplies will run out, but [fishing](Fishing.md) and [plant gathering](Plant_gathering.md) are easy ways to get additional food in a starting fortress. The default embark group starts with a [fisherdwarf](Fisherdwarf.md); you'll need a [fishery](Fishery.md) workshop to clean the fish your fisherdwarf catches, to make them edible.

It is very important to have enough [alcohol](Alcohol.md) for your dwarves, so building a [still](Still.md) is almost a requirement during the first year. [Caravans](Trading.md) bring some drinks, but not enough.

To ensure sustenance when the fortress grows, [farming](Farming.md) is the most reliable way. Above-ground farm plots are easy to set up (if you have soil on your map), but require [seeds](Seed.md) that can be obtained from gathered plants or by trading with humans or elves.

Buying food from [caravans](Trading.md) is a good way to prevent shortages.
