**"Extracts"** is a general term used to classify various types of substances derived from animals or plants. Non-food extracts do not have a purpose in the current build except as export goods.

### Producing extracts

A variety of buildings allow you to produce extracts:

#### Butcher's shop

> *Main article: [Butcher's shop](Butcher's_shop.md)*

Certain [vermin](Vermin.md) will yield extracts at the butcher's shop by using the extract from a dead animal task, requiring a living caged animal, a glass [vial](Vial.md) (metal flasks will not work), and a dwarf with the [small animal dissection](Animal_dissector.md) labor enabled.

- [Fire snakes](Fire_snake.md) create [liquid fire](Liquid_fire.md)
- [Phantom spiders](Phantom_spider.md) create [venom](Venom.md)
- [Cave spiders](Cave_spider.md) create venom
- [Bark scorpions](Bark_scorpion.md) create bark scorpion venom

#### Farmer's workshop

> *Main article: [Farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop.md)*

With the appropriate caged animal and a [milker](Milker.md), [barrels](Barrel.md), and [buckets](Bucket.md) you can produce milk at the farmer's workshop.

- Live (untamed) [purring maggots](Purring_maggot.md) create [Dwarven milk](Dwarven_milk.md), worth 2☼ per unit
- Milk can be cooked to make food, or turned into [cheese](Cheese.md).

[Golden salve](Golden_salve.md) can be created from [valley herbs](Valley_herb.md), requiring a [vial](Vial.md) and a dwarf with the [plant processing](Plant_processing.md) labor enabled:

- 1 valley herb makes 5 units of golden salve, worth 100☼ per unit

[Dwarven syrup](Dwarven_syrup.md) is created via "Process plants (barrel)"

- 1 [Sweet pod](Sweet_pod.md) makes 5 dwarven syrup, worth 20☼ per unit.
- Syrup can be cooked to make food.

[Milk](Milk.md) is created via "Milk Creature," requiring an empty bucket, an empty barrel (or available space in an existing milk barrel), a mature, non-pet, milkable animal, and a dwarf with the [milker](Milker.md) labor enabled. See the [milk](Milk.md) and [cheese](Cheese.md) pages for more details.

#### Still

> *Main article: [Still](Still.md)*

[Gnomeblight](Gnomeblight.md) can be created from [Kobold bulbs](Kobold_bulb.md) at a still via "Extract from plant", requiring a [vial](Vial.md) and a dwarf with the [Plant gathering](Plant_gathering.md) labor enabled:

- 1 Kobold bulb makes 5 units of Gnomeblight, worth 100☼ per unit

#### Fishery

> *Main article: [Fishery](Fishery.md)*

With a living caged fish and a [fish dissector](Fish_dissector.md), you can get extracts from our aquatic neighbors.

- 1 [Moghopper](Moghopper.md) makes five units of [Mog juice](Mog_juice.md), worth 50☼ per unit

#### Import only

Currently, there are some extracts that cannot be produced on site and need to be imported.

Various types of [venom](Venom.md) can also be purchased, but currently have no use:

- [Snakeman](Snakeman.md) Venom
- [Giant desert scorpion](Giant_desert_scorpion.md) Venom
- [Giant cave spider](Giant_cave_spider.md) Venom

Occasionally caravans will bring a barrel filled with some creature's [blood](Blood.md).

Though this would be a great service to the world, (plus all that tasty [elf](Elf.md) juice to sell for aforementioned [Armok](Armok.md)[-pleasing](Emotion.md) blood [barrels](Barrel.md)) unfortunately no such machine has been devised.
