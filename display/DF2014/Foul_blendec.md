> *A man-shaped creature with the legs of a goat and the empty-eyed skull of a goat.*

**Foul blendecs** are [fanciful](Fanciful.md), intelligent [creatures](Creature.md) found in certain [evil](Evil.md)-aligned [forests](Forest.md). Standing as the size of a [dwarf](Dwarf.md), these skull-headed creatures spawn rarely, in groups of 3 to 5 individuals who will menace everything smaller than themselves. They will attack dwarves who provoke them (or at least are supposed to, but currently don't <sup>[Bug](#bugs):[10031](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=10031)</sup>) and may cause great injury unless countered by a [military](Military.md) squad. They are a male-only race - no female foul blendecs exist - and are also immortal, only dying to violence and disease.

Foul blendecs are sapient and can open unlocked [doors](Door.md), though they're unlikely to enter a fortress unless chasing dwarves inside. As described, they're completely eye-less, but can still see through `[EXTRAVISION]` (meaning it's impossible to blind them). They possess a [pet](Pet.md) value of 250, but lack the necessary tokens to be [trainable](Animal_trainer.md) (and even if [modded](Modding.md) to be trainable, the fact they are intelligent creatures will lead to [strange behavior](Animal_trainer.md#taming-intelligent-creatures)). Due to the aforementioned intelligence, dwarves will not [butcher](Butcher.md), eat or use products made from foul blendecs, limiting their use to occupying space in your [refuse](Refuse.md) [stockpile](Stockpile.md), [live training](Live_training.md) or putting them somewhere more to your liking if you capture one in a [cage](Cage.md) [trap](Trap.md).

Their [good](Good.md) counterparts are the [satyrs](Satyr.md).

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) foul blendecs for their *goat heads*, their *goat legs* and their *rotten eye sockets*.
