**Nickel** is an uncommon, low-value, [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md) [metal](Metal.md) used for the creation of [nickel silver](Nickel_silver.md) (note that nickel silver is not magma-safe). However, the [zinc](Zinc.md) required for nickel silver is often better saved for producing [brass](Brass.md), which is more than twice as valuable as nickel silver. It can also be used to create most metalcrafting and construction objects. It can only be smelted from [garnierite](Garnierite.md). Nickel is the cheapest of the four magma-safe metals and thus is the easiest minecart metal to buy if you're using it for magma.
