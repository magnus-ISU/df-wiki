The **soundtrack** for *Dwarf Fortress* consists of two compositions.

The in-game **music** is played on a 6-string guitar by [Toady One](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=DF2014:Toady_One&action=edit&redlink=1) himself. [Partial tabs to the game song](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=651.0) have been provided by Toady One on the *Dwarf Fortress* forum. This inspired LoopyDude to provide [a complete fan made tab](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=54611.0). A [piano arrangement](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=82490.0) by Occoris/Sirocco is also available which layers additional parts together with Toady One's original.

Music can be turned off in the [init.txt](Init.txt.md) file, and the volume can be adjusted in the Esc menu.

The Dwarf Fortress File Depot contains several [alternative sound packs](http://dffd.wimbli.com/search.php?string=music&id=). Existing music is in .ogg format; replacing these files with your own music in .ogg format would allow your choice of music.

Gameplay sounds and more music are available by using [SoundSense](../Utility/SoundSense.md), which parses the gamelog.
