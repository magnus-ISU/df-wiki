<br> **Bismuth** is a metal smelted from [bismuthinite](Bismuthinite.md). Bismuth bars can normally only be used to make the [alloy](Alloy.md) bismuth bronze (although [moody](Strange_mood.md) dwarves can apparently construct almost anything out of bismuth).

<br>

- 1x Bismuth bar + 1x [Tin](Tin.md) bar + 2x [Copper](Copper.md) bar = 4x [Bismuth bronze](Bismuth_bronze.md) bar

You can stud other objects with bismuth, although the option isn't available at the forge, and instead needs to be ordered by the [manager](Manager.md).
