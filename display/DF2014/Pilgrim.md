<br> **Pilgrim** is a profession for some inhabitants of generated [sites](Site.md), like [towns](Town.md). Some pilgrims show up as [visitors](Visitor.md) in fortress mode, and [agents](Agent.md) sometimes try to pose as pilgrims. Pilgrims are [shown in white](Unit_type_token.md#pilgrim).

### World gen

In the world, a [historical figure](Historical_figure.md) that has become a pilgrim will go on a pilgrimage. Pilgrimages consist of said pilgrim visiting sites with [temples](Temple.md) dedicated to their [god](Religion.md), and praying inside the temple. Pilgrims will sometimes become [prophets](Prophet.md) as a result of these pilgrimages.

### Fortress mode

As [visitors](Visitor.md), pilgrims will be attracted by temples open to visitors.

Pilgrims will not [migrate](Immigration.md) to your fortress in this version.
