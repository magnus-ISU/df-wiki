**Siege workshops** produce [ballista](Ballista.md) and [catapult](Catapult.md) parts with [siege engineer](Siege_engineer.md) [labor](Labor.md). They are also responsible for the assembly of siege ammo (namely, [ballista arrows](Ballista_arrow.md)), whether from wooden logs alone or including a [metal](Metal.md) tip.

[Siege engineers](Siege_engineer.md) may assemble ballista and catapult parts into stationary [siege engines](Siege_engine.md), to be loaded and fired by [siege operators](Siege_operator.md).
