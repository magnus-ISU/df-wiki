Trade **companies** are mostly world-gen only entities focused on lucrative trade. Their [counting houses](Counting_house.md) can be found in [towns](Town.md). In worldgen, traveling merchants and companies piggy-back on the more abstract industry/trade simulation that links most sites. At their peak, the companies will form multiple trade outposts in other civs along the trade routes to facilitate more cross-civ link formation.

Trade companies are distinct from [mercenary](Mercenary.md) orders, although these are sometimes also called companies.
