<br> A **press cake** is a low-value [food](Food.md) item [pressed](Screw_press.md) from an [oil](Oil.md)-producing [seed](Seed.md) or [olive](Fruit.md) at a rate of one press cake per unit of oil. It is a by-product of the pressing process, consisting of the leftover solids from the base material.

Press cakes will be used in the [kitchen](Kitchen.md) when 'prepare meal' tasks are queued, if they are enabled in the [Kitchen tab](Status.md#kitchen-status-screen) in the Status screen.

Generally not a human food...

### Press cake-producing crops

The following crops produce materials that can be pressed into [oil](Oil.md) and press cakes:

| Plant Name | Underground? | Press Cake Name | Press Cake Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Quarry bush](Quarry_bush.md) | Yes | rock nut press cake | 1☼ |
| [Cotton](Cotton.md) | No | cottonseed press cake | 1☼ |
| [Flax](Flax.md) | No | linseed press cake | 1☼ |
| [Hemp](Hemp.md) | No | hempseed press cake | 1☼ |
| [Kenaf](Kenaf.md) | No | kenaf seed press cake | 1☼ |
| [Olive](Olive.md) (fruit) | No | olive pomace | 1☼ |

To press oil from the various seed crops - all except olives - the seed must first be [milled](Miller.md) into paste at a millstone or quern. Only this intermediate product can be pressed. Olives can be pressed directly.
