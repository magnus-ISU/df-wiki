A **zoo** is a [room](Room.md) defined around a built [cage](Cage.md) or [restraint](Restraint.md) that is not designated for "[justice](Justice.md)" (i.e. [jail](Jail.md)). Zoos can be assigned to specific dwarves, or left unassigned for communal enjoyment. An unassigned zoo seems to function like a [meeting hall](Meeting_hall.md) where idle dwarves will congregate. While dwarves may receive positive [emotions](Emotion.md) from admiring a zoo's [restraints](Restraint.md), [cages](Cage.md), and any [preferred](Preference.md) [creatures](Creature.md), they do not appear to consider them "tastefully arranged" (unlike [furniture](Furniture.md) in [sculpture gardens](Sculpture_garden.md)).

Caged [grazing](Grazer.md) animals will eventually get hungry and die if not fed, making them impractical for use in a zoo. [Hateable](Hateable.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) can inspire negative thoughts in visiting dwarves, and should similarly be avoided. Although filling your zoo with your fortress's prisoners seems very dwarfy, a mix of regular animals is more likely to match your dwarves' [preferences](Preference.md). A zoo can even function without animals, though the likelihood of positive emotions will be reduced.

Note that the game neither uses the term *zoo* nor calls a room created from a cage a meeting hall.
