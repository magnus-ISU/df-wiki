**Ash glaze** is a type of [glaze](Glaze.md) that can be applied to jugs, statues, large pots, and crafts made of stone or [ceramics](Ceramic.md). It is applied to these items by a [glazer](Glazer.md) at a [kiln](Kiln.md) or [magma kiln](Magma_kiln.md) by using a bar of [ash](Ash.md). [Earthenware](Earthenware.md) [jugs](Jug.md) and [pots](Pot.md) can be glazed to allow them to hold liquids.

Unlike [tin glaze](Tin_glaze.md), the association between ash glaze and its source material is hardcoded into the game itself.
