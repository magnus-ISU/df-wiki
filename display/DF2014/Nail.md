**Nails**, **claws** and **talons** are a product of some [animals](Animal.md) when [butchered](Butcher.md). They are stored in a [stockpile](Stockpile.md) that accepts [body parts](Body_parts.md), and [have no known use](Refuse.md).

Civilized creatures may occasionally wear [jewelry](Jewelry.md) made out of nails, despite it being uncraftable in [Fortress mode](Fortress_mode.md).

<br> Should not be confused with hardware nails, which currently do not exist in the game. All wooden [buildings](Building.md) can be constructed with [wood](Wood.md) alone. These are fingernails or toenails. Thus, your [dwarves](Military.md) will only use their [hammers](War_hammer.md) for pounding the latter type of nail. Hopefully not their own thumbs (ouch!).
