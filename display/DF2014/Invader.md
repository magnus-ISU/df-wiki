**Invader** is a catch-all term for creatures that come to your fortress with the sole purpose of killing and/or enslaving your [dwarves](Dwarf.md). Outside of hostile wildlife, they serve as the main source of [fun](Fun.md) of a playthrough as more and more critters choose to try and destroy you. Whenever an invader is discovered, the game will pause, center on them, and a red message will alert the player of what exactly they are.

Whether a fortress is targeted by invaders is mainly related to its total population, its accumulated [wealth](Wealth.md) and its distance from the invader's base of operations, though some types of invasions may skip certain prerequisites. Most invaders also require a land path to the fortress in order to invade it, meaning some invasions can be avoided by settling on an [island](Island.md).

The presence of invaders can be customized in [d_init.txt](D_init.txt.md); changing \[INVADERS:YES\] to \[INVADERS:NO\] will disable invaders until re-enabled.

### Types of invaders

- [Ambushes](Ambush.md) and [sieges](Siege.md), launched by [civilizations](Civilization.md) at [war](War.md) with your dwarves
- [Megabeasts](Megabeast.md) and [semi-megabeasts](Semi-megabeast.md)
- [Thieves](Thief.md), such as [goblin](Goblin.md) snatchers and [kobold](Kobold.md) thieves
- [Werebeasts](Werebeast.md)

But what with goblin [merchants](Merchant.md) popping up every year (admittedly only at a few new-age dwarven forts that proclaim "free love" and "tolerance" and "baloney"); and both [humans](Human.md), [elves](Elf.md) and even other [dwarves](Dwarf.md) mustering their forces for war, such racist remarks are no longer tolerated in today's modern dwarven society,

### Trivia

- Invading creatures can do so regardless of their [age](Age.md), which can lead to rare and unusual events such as being attacked by a werebeast [child](Children.md) or a [roc](Roc.md) hatchling.
