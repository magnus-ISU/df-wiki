**Tools** are objects used for mundane purposes by civilians and [night creatures](Night_creature.md). Some tools can also be used as [weapons](Weapon.md), though it is usually much better to use, say, a [sword](Sword.md) over a carving fork. Other tools can be used by your dwarves in [fortress mode](Fortress_mode.md).

You can select these tools as starting equipment, if your adventurer hails from a human civilisation.
