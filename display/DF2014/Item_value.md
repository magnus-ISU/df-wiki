**Item value** is determined by a number of factors: the form of the item, the material it is made out of, the [quality](Quality.md) of its creation, and any [decorations](Decoration.md) on it.

The **final value** of an item is the **base value** of the form of the item (e.g. [block](Block.md) *or* [statue](Statue.md)) multiplied by the **material multiplier** (e.g. [granite](Granite.md) *or* [gold](Gold.md)), multiplied by the **quality modifier** (e.g. fine *or* masterful) if any, then summed with the final value of all **[decorations](Decoration.md)**.

## Base values of items

The **base value** of an item is determined by the form of the item, not what it is made out of. For example, a [wood](Wood.md) [block](Block.md) uses the **base value** for a block; a native gold block uses the *same* **base value**, since it too is in the form of a "block". In both cases, the **final value** is then determined by adjusting the base value with the appropriate modifiers.

> *Notes*:
>
> - \1) Coins minted at a forge are always of base quality
> - \2) Goods made out of [cloth](Cloth.md) get the following built in as decorations: the cloth (with quality mod) and the [thread](Thread.md) (without quality). [Dyes](Dye.md) are added separately (with quality), as a powder, not a decoration.
> - \3) "[Decorations](Decoration.md)" include "It is decorated with \<material\>", "It is encircled with bands of \<material\>", "It is adorned with hanging rings of \<material\>", "This object menaces with spikes of \<material\>", "On this object is an image of \<description\> in \<material\>", "It is studded with \<metal\>". Decorations of [bolts](Bolt.md) have a 1/3 base value instead.
> - \4) Prepared meals always have a base value of 10, with a quality modifier. Each ingredient is calculated separately, with its own base value and "minced" quality modifier. The total value of a meal stack is the sum of the above values multiplied by the total quantity, regardless of the proportion of ingredients. A more thorough explanation and example exists at the [Cook](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/v0.34:Cook#Prepared_Meal_Value) page.
> - \5) These items have their value based on their properties - see categories below:
