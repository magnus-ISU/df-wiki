If you see this message, or a dwarf with the "Hunt for small creature" job, the dwarf can't find any [food](Food.md).

A dwarf may not be able to find any food because:

1. There is no more food for any dwarf. (Did you bring enough on embark? Did you trade for enough from the [traders](Trader.md)? Did you cook all your edible [seeds](Seed.md)?)
2. This dwarf is hungry, but is blocked from accessing the food stores. (Is a door locked? Is there flooding? Did an underground tree grow across a path? Did you mine out stairs or ramp you shouldn't have?)

See [starvation](Starvation.md) for more details.
