**Iron** is an important resource for a fortress, as an often-plentiful [metal](Metal.md) that can be used to make just about anything necessary, from weapons to [furniture](Furniture.md) to [trade goods](Trade_good.md). Iron is also a necessary ingredient in the production of [steel](Steel.md), the best non-spoiler metal in the game for [weapons](Weapon.md) and [armor](Armor.md). If you are unable to produce enough steel in your fort, then the next best metal for military purposes is iron.

The three ores of iron, which are [Hematite](Hematite.md), [Magnetite](Magnetite.md), and [Limonite](Limonite.md), can only be found in [sedimentary layers](Sedimentary_layer.md), with the exception of [hematite](Hematite.md), which can occasionally be found in [igneous extrusive layers](Igneous_extrusive_layer.md).

Note that iron does not rust (though it may in future versions), so there's no harm in leaving iron objects out in the rain or submerging them in [water](Water.md).

### Uses

- Melee weapons. Mostly useful for edged weapons. Good for bolts as well. You can also make crossbows out of iron but there are better metals.
- Armor. It's pretty good, though not the best.
- Construction, if you are so inclined.
- Good quality [furniture](Furniture.md). It is quite heavy, however.
- Magma-safe mechanisms, though there are plenty of magma-safe [stones](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=DF2014:Stones&action=edit&redlink=1) that would work just as well, if you can get your hands on them.
- [Anvils](Anvil.md), which can only be made from iron or [steel](Steel.md).

### Production

Iron comes from three ores: [hematite](Hematite.md) (most common), [magnetite](Magnetite.md) (appears in large clusters), and [limonite](Limonite.md) (least dense).

Any of these iron ores can be smelted at a [smelter](Smelter.md) to produce four iron bars. If you have a standard smelter, a bar of [fuel](Fuel.md) will be necessary; magma smelters require no fuel to operate.

Iron will also be available through [goblinite](Goblinite.md), as well as the dwarven and human caravans.

### Alloys

Iron may be combined with other materials at a [smelter](Smelter.md), mostly for the production of [steel](Steel.md)

- Iron + [Flux](Flux.md) + [Fuel](Fuel.md) = [Pig iron](Pig_iron.md)
- Iron + [Pig iron](Pig_iron.md) + [Flux](Flux.md) + [Fuel](Fuel.md) = 2 × [Steel](Steel.md)

Note that [fuel](Fuel.md) is used as a reactant: even [magma smelters](Magma_smelter.md) need fuel for these reactions. A standard smelter will require two bars of fuel, one as a reactant and one to operate the furnace. This conforms with the process of carbonization of iron and [pig iron](Pig_iron.md) in ferrous metallurgy in real life.
