**Messengers** are an assigned [occupation](Occupation.md) at your site. They can be assigned in the Locations and Occupations menu (l), and will act as liaisons with your [holdings](Holding.md) and conquered sites. When a messenger has been assigned, they can be sent on missions from the Civilizations screen (c) to request (r) dwarves from your [holdings](Holding.md) and conquered sites to become [citizens](Citizenship.md) of your fortress. Only dwarves that are considered [historical figures](Historical_figure.md) may be summoned to join your fortress in this way.
