> *For the month, see [Calendar](Calendar.md).*

**Felsite** is the name of the third month of the dwarven [calendar](Calendar.md), covering late Spring. This is the month that the [elven](Elf.md) [caravan](Caravan.md) is most likely to arrive in (although it can also arrive in [Granite](Granite.md) or [Slate](Slate.md)), as well as, sometimes, the month in which most [migrants](Immigration.md) arrive.

Felsite, although it is a fine-grained volcanic rock in real life, does not exist as a [stone](Stone.md) type in *Dwarf Fortress* since the 2010 version; it has subsequently been replaced with [Dacite](Dacite.md).
