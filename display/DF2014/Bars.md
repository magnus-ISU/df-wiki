**Vertical bars** and **floor bars** consist of a lattice of strong bars that prevent the passage of everything but fired (not falling) [ammunition](Ammunition.md), [liquids](Liquid.md), items being pushed by liquids, and [low levels of light](Tile_attributes.md). Bars can be used vertically as transparent walls or on a flat surface as floor grates. Note that [grates](Grate.md) are functionally almost identical to bars.

Bars are most commonly used to seal open [aqueducts](Aqueduct.md) intended for the passage of [water](Water.md), or [magma](Magma.md) if they are built from [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md) materials, whether to keep dwarves from falling in or to keep [fire imps](Fire_imp.md) from invading through them. In this role they are similar to [fortifications](Fortification.md), which can also be used for the same purpose, except that bars can be destroyed by [building destroyers](Building_destroyer.md) (fortifications cannot). It is recommended to cover any exposed waterways with bars (floor bars do not block [fishing](Fishing.md) or taking water directly from a [water source](Activity_zone.md#water-source)) to prevent [melancholy](Insanity.md) dwarves from contaminating your water supply, although this might not help that much: [wells](Well.md), unfortunately, cannot be grated.

Bars can also be fired through like fortifications by [marksdwarves](Marksman.md) and [enemies](Siege.md) alike, and provide no protection for either (unlike [fortifications](Fortification.md)). Be warned that [building destroyers](Building_destroyer.md) can and will destroy any vertical bars that block their path into your fortress. Vertical bars only have a small advantage over fortifications in that they can be linked to a [lever](Lever.md) or [pressure plate](Pressure_plate.md), allowing them to retract when the [mechanism](Mechanism.md) is activated.

Floor bars, like floor grates, will catch ammunition that is already falling. Ammunition that has missed its target will not start falling, unless it strikes a wall while over empty space. In other words, bars or grates (or simply floor) can be used to catch ammunition one z-level beneath from a hole, but if a grate or bar is placed at the surface of the hole, ammunition will simply be destroyed rather than falling.

### Placement

Bars can be built via the [b](Building.md) menu by a dwarf with the [furniture hauling](Hauling.md#furniture-hauling) labor, and take one [bar](Bar.md) of any type to build (including not only [metal](Metal.md) bars, but things like [charcoal](Charcoal.md) and [soap](Soap.md)). They can be constructed as either "Vertical Bars" (B) or horizontal "Floor Bars" (Alt + b). Floor bars are intended to be built over Open Space, though it is possible to build them directly on top of an existing floor (mainly useful for increasing [room value](Room.md)).
