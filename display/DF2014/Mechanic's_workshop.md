The **mechanic's workshop** is a [workshop](Workshop.md) which produces [mechanisms](Mechanism.md) out of stone. It is also used to construct [traction benches](Health_care.md#traction-benches).

### Menu

**Mechanic's Workshop**

- t : Make [Rock](Rock.md) [Mechanisms](Mechanism.md)
- R : Make [Traction Bench](Health_care.md#traction-benches)

### Common Uses

- Make [Mechanisms](Mechanism.md) to control [floodgates](Floodgate.md), [traps](Trap.md), etc.
- Make [Traction benches](Health_care.md#traction-benches) to equip [hospital](Activity_zone.md#hospital) [zones](Zone.md).

### Notes

[Traction benches](Health_care.md#traction-benches) require:

- a [Rope](Rope.md) (produced in [Clothier's shop](Clothier's_shop.md)), or a [Chain](Chain.md) (produced in [Metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md))
- a [Table](Table.md)
- a [Mechanism](Mechanism.md)
