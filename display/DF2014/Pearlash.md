**Pearlash** is a [wood-based](Wood_industry.md) product which is used primarily in the manufacture of [clear](Clear_glass.md) and [crystal glass](Crystal_glass.md).

### Producing pearlash

Pearlash can be created at a [kiln](Kiln.md) or [magma kiln](Magma_kiln.md) by a dwarf with the [furnace operator](Furnace_operator.md) labor enabled. Doing so requires one unit of [potash](Potash.md), as well as whatever [fuel](Fuel.md) is necessary to run the kiln. Pearlash is actually one of the most labor intensive products in the game to produce, requiring you to burn one unit of [wood](Wood.md) to [ash](Ash.md) at a [wood furnace](Wood_furnace~2.md), then convert the ash to potash at an [ashery](Ashery.md) just to acquire the input material used in its creation.

Pearlash is produced in [bars](Bar.md) with no quality level. These bars can be stored in bar [stockpiles](Stockpile.md), and are viewable in the stocks screen under "bars."

### Uses

Pearlash's primary use is to allow the production of [clear glass](Clear_glass.md) (also requires [sand](Sand.md)) and [crystal glass](Crystal_glass.md) (also requires [rock crystal](Rock_crystal.md)) at a [glass furnace](Glass_furnace.md) or [magma glass furnace](Magma_glass_furnace.md).

Alternatively, pearlash is a [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md) (though eccentric) building material, and can be used in any [constructions](Construction.md).

### Real world

[Pearlash](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pearlash) (or salt of tartar) is a more refined form of potash. Historically, this fine white powder was produced by burning away potash's impurities in a kiln. It was used in glassmaking, china, and soap production, and has many widespread uses today.
