**Tropical** [climates](Climate.md) are those that are perennially warm. [Bodies of water](Murky_pool.md) on the surface may evaporate during dry [seasons](Calendar.md) (usually summer). But they can be refilled by [rain](Weather.md). Rivers are unaffected. Most tropical climates offer vast varieties of [animal](../Category/DF2014%253AAnimals.md)- and [plantlife](../Category/DF2014%253APlants.md) (for example [moist broadleaf forests](Forest.md#tropical-moist-broadleaf-forests)).
