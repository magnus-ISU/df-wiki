Dwarf cancels fill pond: Inappropriate building

This error occurs if a [dwarf](Dwarf.md) tries to fill a [pond](Pond.md) and the pond zone isn't over [open space](Open_space.md). To fix it, [channel](Channel.md) underneath the pond zone, or delete the pond zone and make another over open space.
