> *A tiny winged humanoid with shifting colors which performs intricate aerial dances in large groups.*

**Pixies** are a very common type of [fanciful](Fanciful.md) above-ground [vermin](Vermin.md) that live in [good](Good.md) areas. They drift in the air in faintly glowing, colorful groups, dancing intricately. (Repetitive music and recreational pixie dust use are likely involved.) They, alongside [fairies](Fairy.md), are the only intelligent vermin in the game, though they can't be interacted with on the basis of being vermin. When encountered in [adventure mode](Adventure_mode.md), they will glow in the dark. While they possess a [pet](Pet.md) value of 10, they can't be captured with [animal traps](Animal_trap.md).

Despite their description, pixies do not actually have wings (though they can still fly without them). A comment in their raws suggests that [Toady](../Main/Toady_One.md) intended to eventually give them lacy wings.

Some [dwarves](Dwarves.md) [like](Preferences.md) pixies for their *shifting colors* and their *intricate aerial dances*.
