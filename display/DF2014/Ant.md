> *This tiny insect can be found in huge colonies in the dirt. They overwhelm their enemies with swarms. Some have poison bites.*

**Ants** are a type of unremarkable [above ground](Above_ground.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) found in large numbers in any area that isn't freezing. The color of their wild colonies reflects the material underneath it.

Some [dwarves](Dwarves.md) [like](Preferences.md) ants for their *propensity to dig*.

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/f/fa/Vermin-Ant.jpg)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Vermin-Ant.jpg)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Vermin-Ant.jpg](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Vermin-Ant.jpg)

Admired for its *propensity to dig*.
