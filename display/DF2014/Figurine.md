A **figurine** `Å` is a [trade good](Trade_good.md) made at a [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop.md) or at a [metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md) under any particular category. Once queued, a figurine may be customised by pressing d on the task in the workshop's job list. Here, a specific player-defined image or civilisation-related artwork may be commissioned.<sup>[v0.42.01](../Category/Version_0.42.01.md)</sup>

Dwarf civilians may claim ownership of figurines, and will carry them around much like how they wear [jewelry](Jewelry.md). While they use one hand to hold them, it doesn't appear to interfere with any [jobs](Job.md) requiring hands. A dwarf holding a figurine may use it for self-defense in [combat](Combat.md), making use of the [Misc. Object User](Combat_skill.md) [skill](Skill.md).

On rare occasions, a gem figurine, instead of a large gem, can be made at a [jeweler's workshop](Jeweler's_workshop.md). Similar to [engravings](Engraving.md), they can show historic events, depict creatures, venerate religious figures, or list something about a person the [craftsdwarf](Craftsdwarf.md) knows.
