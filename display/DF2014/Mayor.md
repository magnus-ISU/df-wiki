A dwarven mayor conducting a "meeting" with some goblin "liaisons".

The **mayor** is a [noble](Noble.md) whose job it is to speak with unhappy citizens, and (until a [baron](Baron.md) is appointed) entertain foreign [diplomats](Diplomat.md). They are automatically elected from the most social [dwarf](Dwarf.md), most likely the [expedition leader](Expedition_leader.md), once the population reaches 50. A new mayor may be elected from time to time as your dwarves' social skills change.

### Mayoral nobility

A mayor or expedition leader is required to appoint other nobles. Should your leader suffer an [unfortunate accident](Unfortunate_accident.md), you will have to wait for a new leader to be elected before appointing nobles.

Mayors are classy individuals, who require certain living standards in their rooms and furniture. They may [demand](Demand.md) certain types of furniture in their rooms and will get unhappy if you don't comply. They can [mandate](Mandate.md) the production of certain goods, and receive a happy thought if you comply. If the mandated items are not produced in time, the mayor will receive an unhappy thought and will order the [punishment](Justice.md) of whoever they consider responsible. Likewise, they can prohibit the export of certain goods instead of mandating their construction, resulting in dwarves being punished for trade violations.

Mayors are not replaced by [barons](Baron.md), [dukes](Duke.md), [counts](Count.md) or [monarchs](Monarch.md); if one of these nobles arrives, or if a citizen of your fortress is promoted to one of these positions, the mayor will still issue mandates and require quarters.

### Mayoral elections

Mayors are elected for one year. Elections take place at exactly 4:00 PM on the 17th day of summer (specifically, 2000 season-ticks after the beginning of the season). If you don't have a mayor, then the election will happen at the beginning of *every season*, rather than just summer.

When an election happens, it's usually the dwarf with the highest social skills who gets elected. Since [children](Children.md) have nothing to do but socialize, they gain lots of social skills which carry over when a child becomes an adult at age 12. Thus, it's not unusual for a 12-year-old to become mayor. Their chief competitors are [vampires](Vampire.md) (who also tend to have high social skills), tamed [gremlins](Gremlin.md), and other non-working citizens of your fortress, since they cannot be put to work either. The mayor doesn't actually need to be dwarven, any [citizen](Citizenship.md) can be elected - however, dwarves currently on a [mission](Mission.md) or otherwise absent from the fortress cannot.

### Miscellaneous

Curiously, a mayor is able to appoint replacements for elected positions, including their own.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[2512](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=2512)</sup> If your mayor likes something unpleasant like [puzzleboxes](Finished_goods.md), you can simply have them appoint their own replacement. This also creates a historical event, which makes for good [statues](Statue.md) of dwarves rejecting the old mayor with which to brighten up the new mayor's office. Note that the ex-mayor may periodically win election back into the mayorship.
