A **Mason's workshop** is where [masons](Mason.md) create [stone](Stone.md) objects, such as [blocks](Block.md), [statues](Statue.md), and [furniture](Furniture.md). [Querns](Quern.md), [slabs](Slab.md) and [millstones](Millstone.md) can only be made at this workshop.

Depending on the generated world, musical [instruments](Instrument.md) may or may not be made here.

The workshop can be made out of any building material and requires the Masonry [labor](Labor.md).

### Common Uses

- Construct [rock](Rock.md) [blocks](Block.md), which can be stored in [bins](Bin.md). This frees up storage space.

### Notes

- As it is cheap to build a Mason's workshop, it can be useful to temporarily make one in any area where you have lots of stone you need to get rid of (e.g. future stockpile space) and then dismantle it when you are through.
- [Clay](Clay.md) boulders can not be used in place of stone.
- Altars require stone crafting instead of the standard mason labor.
