The **Dyer's shop** is the [workshop](Workshop.md) used to [dye](Dye.md) [thread](Thread.md) and [cloth](Cloth.md). It requires a [barrel](Barrel.md) and a [bucket](Bucket.md) to build, and must be built by a [dwarf](Dwarf.md) with the [Dyeing](Dyer.md) [labor](Labor.md) enabled. Dyeing increases the [value](Value.md) of the dyed thread or cloth, which can be useful for [trading](Trading.md). Dyes can't be applied to finished goods made from cloth, such as clothing, bags, or crafts.

There are currently four colors of dye: Midnight Blue, Emerald, Red, and Black. Midnight blue is extracted from [dimple cups](Dimple_cup.md), emerald is extracted from [blade weed](Blade_weed.md), red is extracted from [hide root](Hide_root.md), and black from [sliver barb](Sliver_barb.md). Sliver barbs are only found on evil areas, so [elves](Elf.md), [dwarves](Dwarf.md), and [humans](Human.md) won't bring sliver barbs to trade, so you must embark in an evil area and pull it from the ground yourself.
