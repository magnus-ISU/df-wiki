**Soap** is a particularly useful type of [bar](Bar.md) used for [personal cleaning](Clean_self.md), which increases happiness ("recently took a soapy bath") and lowers the chances of an [infection](Health_care.md) in case they are [wounded](Wound.md), and for cleaning [wounds](Wound.md) in [hospitals](Hospital.md), preventing infections from developing. It is thus a vital commodity in dwarven [health care](Health_care.md), and one not traded in [caravans](Caravan.md): you're going to have to make some soap yourself. Since soap is considered to be a [bar](Bar.md), it can be used to make [constructions](Constructions.md) and [workshops](Workshop.md).

Unlike most other items, bars of soap have a "durability" level which allows jobs to partially consume them - each bar starts with 150 units, and cleaning jobs consume varying quantities of soap before ultimately using it up.

### Manufacture

Soap is made of two components, [lye](Lye.md) and [fat](Fat.md) (either [tallow](Tallow.md) or [oil](Oil.md)), and requires a dedicated workshop, the [soap maker's workshop](Soap_maker's_workshop.md). It has a somewhat complicated production process; lye must be produced at an [ashery](Ashery.md) from [ash](Ash.md), which in turn must be created at a [wood furnace](Wood_furnace~2.md) from [wood](Wood.md) logs that must first be [cut down](Woodcutting.md). [Tallow](Tallow.md) is rendered from the [fat](Fat.md) from butchered [animals](Creature.md) at a [kitchen](Kitchen.md), requiring either [livestock](Meat_industry.md) or [hunting](Hunting.md) activities, while oil must be [pressed](Pressing.md) from [seeds](Seed.md) or [rock nuts](Rock_nut.md) at a [screw press](Screw_press.md), which first requires [gathering](Plant_gathering.md) or [growing](Farming.md) [crops](Crop.md) and then processing them at the [farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop.md). One unit of tallow or oil plus one of lye creates a single bar of finished soap.

### Hygiene

Dwarves do not require soap to clean [contaminants](Contaminant.md) such as mud and blood from themselves - if necessary, they will use [murky pools](Murky_pool.md), artificial pools of water, brooks, or a [well](Well.md). However, using soap will often generate the happy [thought](Thought.md) "recently took a soapy bath". It is possible to construct bath-houses (rooms containing pools of water, a soap stockpile, and perhaps a few nice statues) so dwarves living deep underground need not venture to dangerous cave pools or surface brooks to clean off a little mud or some bloodstains. For cleaning wounds and preventing infection after [surgery](Surgery.md), however, [hospitals](Hospital.md) should be kept stocked with a small amount of soap. Soap will get used up as dwarves wash themselves, and dirtier dwarves consume soap more quickly.

Dwarves have an internal "dirtiness" level, which slowly builds up over time, gets lowered when they have a bath, and lowered further when they have a soapy bath. This "dirtiness" value is connected to the chance of getting an infection if the dwarf is injured, making soap useful as a preventative as well as treatment.

Soap is stored in bar/block stockpiles with the "soap" option enabled.

A convenient way to keep an emergency stockpile of soap is to use it as a building material for workshops or constructions such as walls. When/if you need more soap, you can deconstruct and get the soap bars back. Since soap in a hospital is reserved for hospital use, this is especially useful in case you start to produce soap before setting up a hospital.
