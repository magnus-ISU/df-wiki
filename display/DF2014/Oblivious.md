**Obliviousness** is a temporary emotional breakdown triggered by [stress](Stress.md), experienced mainly by dwarves with high [propensity to anxiety](Personality_trait.md). Oblivious dwarves will cancel any job they may be doing at the moment and stumble around until they regain their composure.

Unless stress levels are reduced, Dwarves with a history of depression will (unsurprisingly) tend to become [depressed](Depression.md), while those with anger management issues will throw [tantrums](Tantrum.md). If unchecked, this can repeat until the dwarf eventually [goes stark raving mad](Insane.md).
