<br> An animal's **multiply_value** affects the [value](Item_value.md) of animal products such as meat, raw hides, silk thread, and anything made from these raw materials, including leather or silk clothing. While it's possible for multiply_value to apply to only specific parts of a creature, all non-modded creatures use SELECT_MATERIAL:ALL, so all parts of the animal are multiplied.
