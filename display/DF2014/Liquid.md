**Liquid** is a classification under the [stocks](Stocks.md) menu, containing nonalcoholic fluids ([drinks](Drink.md) are separate) such as [water](Water.md), [milk](Milk.md), [lye](Lye.md), [dwarven syrup](Dwarven_syrup.md), [honey](Honey.md) and [royal jelly](Royal_jelly.md). Royal jelly which is still part of one of your [hives](Hive.md) is also shown here, while honey will only be shown after it has been [pressed](Screw_press.md) from a [honeycomb](Honeycomb.md). It also lists [ichor](Ichor.md), [blood](Blood.md) and [venom](Venom.md).

[Magma](Magma.md) may also be listed as a liquid. This happens whenever the magma fluid melts a non [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md) stone. As the raws for any stone list their liquid state as magma, when the magma fluid melts the rocks, they become listed as a magma [glob](Glob.md) on the [stocks](Stocks.md) page.
