**Critical thinker** is a [scholar](Scholar.md) skill. It is used by scholars when thinking about history [topics](Topics.md). It seems to be an important skill for [scholars](Scholar.md) in general, especially when writing [books](Book.md)/[scrolls](Scroll.md). As dwarves level in this skill very slowly, it can be worth assigning dwarves that already have a few levels in it as your [scholars](Scholar.md).
