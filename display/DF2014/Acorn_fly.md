> *An insect many times the size of its peers. It is known for its deafening buzz.*

**Acorn flies** are a type of [vermin](Vermin.md) which spawn around [murky pools](Murky_pool.md) in [savage](Surroundings.md) areas. It may be observed in swarms around [rotting](Miasma.md) corpses. Unlike regular [flies](Fly.md), [dwarves](Dwarves.md) do not ever [detest](Hateable.md) acorn flies, though they can be [mildly annoyed](Thought.md) by them. Can be caught with "Capture Live Land Animal" job, but can not be trapped in [built](Building.md) [animal traps](Animal_trap.md). All acorn flies possess Legendary [skill](Skill.md) in [climbing](Climber.md).

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) acorn flies for their *deafening buzz*.
