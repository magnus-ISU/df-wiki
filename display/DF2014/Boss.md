**Bosses** are the leaders of [criminals](Criminal.md), leading criminal organizations or gangs of bandits. They may also be called a ringleader, master, warlord, chieftain, etc.

Criminal bosses can often be located in a [dungeon](Dungeon.md), while bandit leaders can be found in [camps](Camp.md). When you encounter them, they will be flashing like other leaders in the game. They are effectively treated as lords of their respective place of residence, meaning talking to a boss (if they haven't been provoked yet) will allow you to join them as a lieutenant, lay claim to their territory, etc. Bosses are often part of the [intrigue](Intrigue.md) system in some capacity.

Criminal leaders and their subordinates can occasionally be weaponmasters, and are often the only ones that are generated during [world generation](World_generation.md). Certain criminal bosses in capital cities will also come equipped with a random assortment of masterwork-quality items.
