**Variscites** are low-value green [gems](Gem.md) found in [bauxite](Bauxite.md) clusters.

Since they occur in bauxite, along with [rubies](Ruby.md) and [sapphires](Sapphire.md), they can occasionally overlap a cluster of these gems, which can prevent [star rubies](Star_ruby.md) and [star sapphires](Star_sapphire.md) from appearing.
