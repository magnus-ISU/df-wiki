> *A tiny parrot found in flocks of many hundreds. Exceptional birds can be taught to speak.*

**Parakeets** are a species of [above ground](Above_ground.md) [vermin](Vermin.md), found in a number of [tropical](Tropical.md) [biomes](Biome.md). They may be captured in [animal traps](Animal_trap.md) and turned into [pets](Pet.md). All parakeets possess Legendary [skill](Skill.md) in [climbing](Climber.md).

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) parakeets for their *coloration* and their *ability to speak*.
