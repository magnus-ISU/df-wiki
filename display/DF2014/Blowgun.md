*A Yagua (Yahua) tribeman demonstrating the use of a **blowgun***

A **blowgun** is a ranged [weapon](Weapon.md) that fires projectiles called [blowdarts](Blowdart.md). Blowguns are relatively rare, being used primarily by certain bands of [animal people](Animal_people.md). Currently it is not possible for your dwarves to add a poison or toxin to a weapon without significant workarounds (and a bit of luck), making blowguns even more underwhelming.

As ranged weapons, blowguns use and train the [blowgunner](Blowgunner.md) skill; as melee weapons, blowguns use and train the [swordsdwarf](Swordsdwarf.md) skill, instead. Dwarves cannot normally [forge](Forge.md) blowguns nor darts, limiting supply to whatever low-[quality](Quality.md) specimens can be wrested from indigenous populations. During a [strange mood](Strange_mood.md), however, an aspiring yet misguided [bowyer](Bowyer.md) may create an [artifact](Artifact.md) blowgun at a [bowyer's workshop](Bowyer's_workshop.md). Even if you do manage to get your hands on a blowgun, the severe lack of ammunition will make it nearly impossible to field an effective blowgun-dwarf. All dwarves can equip blowguns.

Units from other civilizations spawned as blowgunners are hardcoded to special behaviour. Unlike other ranged weapon users, they lack quivers, but their ammunition will be covered in any animal extract that is available to the civilization and causes an injected or contact syndrome.
