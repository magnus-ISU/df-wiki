**Malachite** is one of several [ores](Ore.md) of [copper](Copper.md). It is found as [veins](Vein.md), and is rare compared to other copper ores. Malachite is among the few types of stone that are not [fire-safe](Fire-safe.md).

Other copper ores include [native copper](Native_copper.md) and [tetrahedrite](Tetrahedrite.md).

"Malachite" is the name of the 5th month of the dwarven [calendar](Calendar.md), covering mid-Summer.

### In Real Life

Malachite is a hydrated copper carbonate (Cu<sub>2</sub>(CO<sub>3</sub>)(OH)<sub>2</sub>) that forms as a result of the weathering of preexisting copper deposits. Malachite is known to form alongside both [Chrysocolla](Chrysocolla.md), and Azurite (which is not present in *Dwarf Fortress*) which it commonly pseudomorphs from.
