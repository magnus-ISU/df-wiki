*A **spear***

A **spear** is a piercing [weapon](Weapon.md) that is essentially a sharply pointed blade mounted on the end of a long handle. Spears are half the size of [pikes](Pike_(weapon).md), but only suffer a 17% decrease in penetration.

Spears use and train the [speardwarf](Speardwarf.md) skill. Dwarves can [forge](Forge.md) spears out of any weapon-grade [metal](Metal.md) but those with [superior edge properties](Material_science.md) are more effective. For the purposes of building a realistic [danger room](Danger_room.md), less lethal wooden spears may be used if purchased from elves first. All dwarves can equip spears, but [some must use two hands](Weapon.md#size).

Spears may also be installed in [upright spear traps](Trap.md#upright-spear2fspike), allowing them to be mechanically operated; this is particularly useful for constructing [danger rooms](Danger_room.md).

### Forging and melting

- Metal spears cost **one** [metal](Metal.md) bar to [forge](Forge.md), or **three** [adamantine](Adamantine.md) wafers.
- When a non-adamantine metal spear is [melted down](Melt.md), it will return **0.9** metal bars, for an **efficiency of 90%**.
- When an adamantine spear is melted down, it will produce **0.9** wafers, for an **efficiency of 30%**
