**Personality goals** (or just **goals**) things that creatures dream of accomplishing. They can be seen in a dwarf's [thoughts and preferences](Thoughts_and_preferences~2.md) description page, which can be accessed by viewing that dwarf, then p, z, Enter, or from the unit menu with view, Enter.

Dwarves will receive a happy thought upon completion of their goals, receiving the notation of "and this dream was realized" in the personality description following their original goal. Goals would apparently *not* currently be limited to one per creature, but for some sort of oversight: [\[1\]](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=169696.msg8083871#msg8083871).
