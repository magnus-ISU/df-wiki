*A [bronze](../Main/Bronze.md) **whip***

A **whip** is a [blunt](Attack_types.md) [weapon](Weapon.md) that consists of a flexible length of [material](Material.md) that can be swung with great speed. Whips have the lowest contact area and highest velocity multiplier in the game, making them amazingly effective, even against [armored](Armor.md) opponents; this behavior has been reported as a [bug](#bugs). Whips are similar to [scourges](Scourge.md), though the scourge is an edge weapon with a larger contact area.

Whips use and train the [lasher](Lasher.md) skill. As foreign weapons, whips cannot be [forged](Forge.md) by dwarves. However, adventurers can be made to start with masterwork whips, and low-[quality](Quality.md) specimens can be traded from human [caravans](Caravan.md), or scavenged from invaders. [Even the smallest dwarves](Weapon.md#size) can equip a whip one-handed.

<br>

### Bugs

- Whips currently cut through steel armor like butter. While this is very beneficial (given that it is not being used against you), it is still a bug, and is bound to be fixed sometime. <sup>[Bug](#bugs):[2712](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=2712)</sup>
