[Materials](Material.md) have a sharpness property, defined as `[MAX_EDGE:<number>]`. In combat, this presumably defines how well the material works when used for [slashing](Slashing.md) attacks.

Most materials draw this from their [Material template](Material_templates.md), but placing a `[MAX_EDGE]` tag in the material's entry will override this and allow it to be sharper than other objects of its kind. For example, Obsidian has `[MAX_EDGE:20000]`, while the Stone Material Template has `[MAX_EDGE:1000]`. Incidentally, it seems that this is what allows Obsidian to be made into stone swords; a value of at least 10000 has been proven to also work.

Actual weapon sharpness depends on its [quality](Quality.md): masterwork and [artifact](Artifact.md) blades have the above-mentioned maximum material edge, while regular-quality items get only half as much.
