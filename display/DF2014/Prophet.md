<br> **Prophet** is a profession for some inhabitants of generated [sites](Site.md), like [towns](Town.md). Some prophets show up as [visitors](Visitor.md) in fortress mode, and [agents](Agent.md) sometimes try to pose as prophets. In world generation, prophets will attempt to establish and expand [religions](Religion.md). Prophets are [shown in white](Unit_type_token.md#prophet).

<br>

### Fortress mode

Prophets will not [migrate](Immigration.md) to your fortress in this version.

### Adventure mode

In adventure mode, the player can create a false identity for themselves as a prophet. This allows them a special dialogue option called "Prophesize" which can be quite entertaining. Examples:
