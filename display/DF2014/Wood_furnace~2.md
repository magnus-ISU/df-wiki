A **wood furnace** is a specialized form of [furnace](Furnace.md) designed to burn fresh [wood](Wood.md). Unlike the other non-[magma](Magma.md) furnaces, a wood furnace does not require fuel to operate.

### Uses

Any dwarf with the [wood burner](Wood_burner.md) [labor](Labor.md) enabled can burn a single log in order to produce either [ash](Ash.md) or [charcoal](Charcoal.md). While it's hardly efficient, charcoal serves as the most ubiquitous form of [fuel](Fuel.md) available to run a [metal industry](Metal_industry.md) on. Ash sees use in multiple industries, such as [glazing](Glazing.md) [earthenware](Earthenware.md) items, advanced [glassmaking](Glassmaking.md), [crop fertilization](Farm_plot.md), and [soap](Soap.md) production. As with all buildings that require an [architect](Architect.md), passing dwarves may admire wood furnaces and receive a good [thought](Thought.md).

### Construction

Wood furnaces can be built from any [fire-safe](Fire-safe.md) material. Once you designate a spot for your wood furnace to be built, a [building designer](Building_designer.md) will bring the material to the site and design the building before the actual [mason](Mason.md) (for stone or glass furnaces) or [metalworker](Metalsmith.md#metalsmith-profession) (for metal furnaces) comes along and builds it. Strangely enough, [charcoal](Charcoal.md) and [coke](Coke.md) are valid mason's materials for building a wood furnace, since their ignition point is above the cutoff for fire safety.

The overall value of the wood furnace building helps determine the strength of the good thought dwarves receive when admiring it. This value is determined by both the value of the material used in its construction, as well as the quality of both the architect's and builder's efforts.

### Minimalist start

For players trying to maximize the value of their embark points, you may wish to avoid bringing finished [battle axes](Battle_axe.md) or [picks](Pick.md), choosing instead to bring metal ore and fuel with you, so you can forge them yourself. Including a wood furnace can be extremely useful here, allowing you to turn logs into fuel much more cheaply than bringing coke, while bars of ash are inexplicably magma-safe building "materials."
