> *A tiny sea mollusk. It can change color and skin patterns for communication.*

**Cuttlefish** are a type of aquatic [vermin](Vermin.md) found in any [ocean](Ocean.md) year-round, and are a ready source of [food](Food.md) when [cleaned](Fish_cleaning.md) at a [fishery](Fishery.md). Their [blood](Blood.md) is blue and they possess the ability to squirt ink, though this can't be seen, due to them being vermin. All cuttlefish possess Legendary [skill](Skill.md) in [climbing](Climber.md).

Some [dwarves](Dwarves.md) [like](Preferences.md) cuttlefish for their *ability to change color* and their *distinctive pupils*.
