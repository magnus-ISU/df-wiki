**Smoothing** rough [stone](Stone.md) is a simple way to increase the value of a [room](Room.md). Walls and floors become Smooth *Material* Wall/Floor when smoothed. It is impossible to [climb](Climb.md) a smooth wall.

### How to Smooth

A dwarf must have the [Stone Detailing](Engraver.md) labor active. Designate the area to be smoothed (d-s). Note that this can only be done with natural stone — soil cannot be smoothed, and [constructions](Construction.md) are already considered to be smooth for most purposes.

Smoothed "natural" [ice](Ice.md) has different names than regular smoothed items. The walls are "straight" and floors are "level".

### Follow Up

After a natural stone surface is smoothed, it may be further improved by [engraving](Engraving.md). This increases the room's value, but, unlike smoothing, engraving has quality levels. A wall can only be engraved once (for floors, there is a [workaround](Engraving.md#removal)). Using only skilled [engravers](Engraver.md) will result in higher room and fortress values. One way to achieve this is to have a limited number of dwarves with the [Stone Detailing](Engraver.md) labor active smooth large areas of the fortress until the dwarves are experienced, then, have those same dwarves engrave. The result will be [high-quality](Legendary.md) engravings.

Smoothed walls are also a prerequisite for carving [fortifications](Fortification.md). Engraved walls can also be carved into fortifications, but the engravings will be destroyed in the process.
