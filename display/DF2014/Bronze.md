**Bronze** is an [alloy](Alloy.md) of [tin](Tin.md) and [copper](Copper.md), which can be used to create all metallic items in the game.

### Smelting

Bronze can be made at the [Smelter](Smelter.md) or [Magma smelter](Magma_smelter.md) using one of the following recipes:

- from [ore](Ore.md): 1 [Cassiterite](Cassiterite.md) + 1 {[Native copper](Native_copper.md), [Malachite](Malachite.md) or [Tetrahedrite](Tetrahedrite.md)} = 8 Bronze bars
- from [bars](Bar.md): 1 [Tin](Tin.md) bar + 1 [Copper](Copper.md) bar = 2 Bronze bars

### Use

Bronze can be forged into [weapons](Weapon.md) and [armor](Armor.md), and has [material qualities](Weapon.md#material) very close to [iron](Iron.md). Bronze can also be used to make [furniture](Furniture.md) and other objects at a [metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md). It has a [value](Value.md) of 5 and uses the same ingredients as [fine pewter](Fine_pewter.md) (which also has a value of 5). If you find yourself short of [tin](Tin.md), use this metal to make your objects of art instead of using [fine pewter](Fine_pewter.md). If you are using ores to smelt bronze, you can produce 8 bars of weapon-grade metal with one unit of [fuel](Fuel.md), making it an extremely useful material.

Alternatively, copper and tin can also be alloyed with [bismuth](Bismuth.md), in order to make the alloy [bismuth bronze](Bismuth_bronze.md). While this doesn't improve the properties of the materials, it does result in a modest increase in [value](Value.md) and cuts the cost in tin in half.

The one major drawback of bronze compared to iron is that it cannot be used to make [anvils](Anvil.md).

### Material properties

As mentioned before, bronze has material properties that are very similar to those of [iron](Iron.md), and is a significant improvement over [copper](Copper.md), except for blunt weapons where its superior edge properties are virtually irrelevant. If your fortress is in an area without significant quantities of iron ore, then bronze is a very good substitute. However, the material properties of bronze are inferior to those of [steel](Steel.md) in virtually every way.

The properties of [bismuth bronze](Bismuth_bronze.md) are identical in every way to standard bronze, the only differences are that items made from bizmuth bronze are 20% more [valuable](Value.md) (×6 vs. ×5), and the [color](Color.md) of bismuth bronze items in-game is yellow rather than brown.
