**Gold** is a high-value, relatively common [metal](Metal.md). It is the third highest value metal, tied with [steel](Steel.md). The only [ore](Ore.md) of gold is [native gold](Native_gold.md), which yields **gold nuggets** when mined. Each gold nugget can be turned into four gold [bars](Bar.md) at a [smelter](Smelter.md) or [magma smelter](Magma_smelter.md) by a dwarf with the [furnace operating](Furnace_operating.md) labor enabled. Gold used to be one of the three metals (with [copper](Copper.md) and [silver](Silver.md)) used to mint [coins](Currency.md) for the dwarven [economy](Economy.md) and has the highest value of the three. Gold is [fire-safe](Fire-safe.md) but *not* [magma safe](Magma_safe.md).

Gold may be combined with other metals at a smelter or magma smelter to form several [alloys](Alloy.md):

- [Black bronze](Black_bronze.md) = Gold + [Silver](Silver.md) + 2× [Copper](Copper.md)
- [Electrum](Electrum.md) = Gold + [Silver](Silver.md)
- [Rose gold](Rose_gold.md) = 3× Gold + [Copper](Copper.md)

None of the gold alloys have a greater material value than gold itself, but may prove useful if gold is particularly scarce on a map. Further, if gold nuggets (material value 30) are smelted directly with a low-value [silver](Silver.md) ore such as [galena](Galena.md) (material value 5) or [tetrahedrite](Tetrahedrite.md) (material value 3), it will produce eight bars of [electrum](Electrum.md) (material value 20) for a net gain in total value.

Refining gold nuggets produces 4 bars, which can result in significantly increased value compared to the original nuggets; however in some cases it may be advantageous to make furniture directly from gold ore, since it requires no [fuel](Fuel.md) and uses a labor that is easily trained. Native gold furniture may also be useful to satisfy a [noble](Noble.md)'s [mandate](Mandate.md) or [demand](Demand.md). To use native gold for furniture and the like, you should place a stone [stockpile](Stockpile.md) allowing only native gold near your [Mason's workshop](Mason's_workshop.md) (native gold furniture, blocks, etc.), [Craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop.md) (native gold crafts), or [Mechanic's workshop](Mechanic's_workshop.md) (native gold mechanisms) and enable "gold nuggets" (**not** "gold") using the z : `Stone` menu. To avoid wasting gold nuggets, assign your stockpile to 'give' only to one specific workshop, and disable non-economic use upon completion.

Note that a dwarf who has a preference for gold metal will not be particularly impressed by items crafted from native gold nuggets, and vice-versa.
