The **fishery** [workshop](Workshop.md) handles the preparation of raw [fish](Fish.md). This process yields prepared fish, [turtles](Turtle.md), and [lobsters](Cave_lobster.md), and will additionally give [shells](Shell.md) in the case of turtles, oysters, and other shelled animals.

### Menu

**Fishery**

- p :Process a Raw Fish
- e :Extract from a Raw Fish
- f :Capture a Live Fish

### Notes

- The preparation task is automated by default, a procedure that can be changed with o[rders](Standing_orders.md), Workshop Orders, Auto fishery.
- Using the Capture Live Fish task when water sources are frozen results in a fisherdwarf (or any [Noble](Noble.md) who 'volunteers' to help the community by helping out the poor, poor, peasants) spending the season outside and holding the trap.
- Without an available [animal trap](Animal_trap.md), the Capture Live Fish task will stop automated fish preparations.
