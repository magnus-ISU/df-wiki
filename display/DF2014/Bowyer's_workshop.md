The **bowyer's workshop** is a [Tier 1](Workshop.md#tier-1-workshops) [workshop](Workshop.md) used to make [wooden](Wood.md) or [bone](Bone.md) [crossbows](Crossbow.md). It requires any [stone](Stone.md), [wood](Wood.md) or [block](Block.md) to build, and must be built by a [dwarf](Dwarf.md) with the [crossbow making](Bowyer.md) [labor](Labor.md) enabled.

Take note that the entire left column of the workshop is composed of [impassable tiles](Impassable_tile.md), making it easy to accidentally trap dwarves inside the workshop after it is built.

### Menu

**Bowyer's Workshop**

- Make [bone](Bone.md) [crossbow](Crossbow.md)
- Make [wooden](Wood.md) [crossbow](Crossbow.md)

### Common Uses

- Outfit an [archer](Archer.md), [military](Military.md) [squad](Squad.md), or [hunter](Hunter.md).

### Notes

- Use of a [crossbow](Crossbow.md) also requires [bolts](Bolt.md), which are made in a [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop.md) or [forge](Forge.md), and a [quiver](Quiver.md), made in a [leather works](Leather_works.md).
