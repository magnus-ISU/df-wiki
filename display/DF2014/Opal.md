**Opal** is a broad category of ornamental (low-value) and semi-precious (mid-value) [gems](Gem.md#varieties). The exact gem will be one of a couple dozen specific types of opal, such as bandfire opal, pipe opal or pineapple opal. All opals in *Dwarf Fortress* are listed below.

"Opal" is also the name of the eleventh month of the dwarven [calendar](Calendar.md), covering mid-winter.
