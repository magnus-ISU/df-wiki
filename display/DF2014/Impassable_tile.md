**Impassable tiles** are tiles in a [building](Building.md) that creatures cannot move into – they function similarly to a constructed [wall](Wall.md). Some [workshops](Workshop.md), like the [bowyer's workshop](Bowyer's_workshop.md) and [jeweler's workshop](Jeweler's_workshop.md), have what is in effect a three square wall down one side. Non-workshop buildings (such as [furnaces](Furnace.md) and [screw pumps](Screw_pump.md)) may also include impassable tiles.

Impassable tiles appear as a dark green color during initial placement (passable tiles are shown in light green). One common use for impassable tiles is to block access to other [z-levels](Z-level.md). A [magma smelter](Magma_smelter.md), for instance, has a single impassable tile in the top center--placing this tile over your [magma](Magma.md) access channel prevents unlucky dwarves from falling in, and [unwelcome beasts](Fire_imp.md) from crawling out. It is worth noting that [building destroyers](Building_destroyer.md) can only destroy buildings that they can [path to the z-level of](Building_destroyer.md#destroying-from-underneath). For [machinery](Machinery.md), the impassable tiles can be used to connect power through z-levels (see [pump stack](Pump_stack~2.md) for an example).

Be careful when building workshops in tight quarters; more than one dwarf has perished from thirst after constructing a [building](Jeweler's_workshop.md) that blocks the only path back to the fortress proper.
