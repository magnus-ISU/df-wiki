The **building list screen** is accessed from the main view by pressing Shift - R. A list of the [buildings](Building.md) (along with their owners) is displayed, along with any differences in actual value and perceived value by the owner (due to material preferences). If a [room](Room.md) in the list appears in red, then it is a room which overlaps with some other defined room-space, drastically reducing the quality value of these rooms.

On some buildings, specifically those that need the [architecture](Architecture.md) [skill](Skill.md) to be built ([trade depots](Trade_depot.md), [bridges](Bridge.md), [archery targets](Archery_target.md), [furnaces](Furnace.md), [pumps](Pump.md), [roads](Road.md), etc.), the [quality](Quality.md) levels of both the design and the construction (as well as the names of the dwarves involved) can be viewed by pressing Enter. The building's material is determined by the *oldest* selected *base* item (that is, the chosen bar/log/stone that has existed in your fort the longest).<sup>[\[1\]](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=134816.msg4867108#msg4867108)</sup>
