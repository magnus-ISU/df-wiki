**Calcite** is a [flux](Flux.md) stone; however, it is not particularly special as it is found solely within [limestone](Limestone.md) and [marble](Marble.md), both of which are *also* flux. Its only distinguishing characteristic is that it is a [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md) rock.

### In real life

Calcite is a crystalline form of calcium carbonate (CaCO<sub>3</sub>). Calcite is extremely common, forming multiple types of rocks, such as [limestone](Limestone.md), [marble](Marble.md), and [chalk](Chalk.md), it is also known to form as a concrete in some [sandstones](Sandstone.md) and [siltstones](Siltstone.md). Calcite, along with other crystalline forms of calcium carbonate such as Aragonite (which is not present in *Dwarf Fortress*) is readily edible (although not easily chewed, and not very palatable), and can be used as a supplementary source of calcium in an otherwise calcium-poor diet.
