**Lead** is a heavy, low-value [metal](Metal.md) available only from [galena](Galena.md) [ore](Ore.md). Unlike in real life, lead is **not** a biological toxin. Lead is not magma-safe or even fire-safe - it will melt in a grass fire.

### Uses

Lead can be forged into [furniture](Furniture.md) and other objects, like most metals, but its low value and high weight may make it a poor choice for personal objects or anything portable, respectively. However, lead items that are not [weapons](Weapon.md) or [armor](Armor.md) are just as strong as those of any other metal - a lead [chain](Chain.md) or [bridge](Bridge.md) is exactly as strong as a [steel](Steel.md) bridge, even against a [dragon](Dragon.md) or a [building destroyer](Building_destroyer.md).

Rare [moods](Mood.md) might result in a lead weapon or lead armor - and material strength is considered in [combat](Combat.md), as well as weapon weight. Lead armor would not be a good result (it's so fragile it can literally be torn apart by a normal [human's](Human.md) bare hands), but a lead mace could be very nice.

Lead can also be used to make massive [minecarts](Minecart.md) to send crashing into [goblin](Goblin.md) [invaders](Invader.md). What fun!

If you have a surplus of it, lead can be semi-useful for decorating objects and training [metal crafters](Metal_crafter.md); lead crafts may be traded for a value of 100☼ or more, depending upon [quality](Quality.md).

Lead can be [alloyed](Alloy.md) with [copper](Copper.md) and [tin](Tin.md) to produce [lay pewter](Lay_pewter.md).

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Lead_ingots.jpg](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Lead_ingots.jpg)

Lead ingots.

In [adventure mode](Adventure_mode.md), lead items (or items made of other heavy material) tend to stay close to their original position upon [reclaim](Reclaim.md). So if you want to find the item easily, make it out of lead, or place it in a lead [bin](Bin.md).

<br> Since lead poisoning does not (yet) exist in *Dwarf Fortress*, trading lead [barrels](Barrel.md) and [crafts](Craft.md) only weighs down the [caravan](Caravan.md). This, unfortunately, eliminates the possibility of lead poisoning the elves or humans through trade of lead-based crafts and lead-laced alcohol. However, if your dwarves happen to be irradiated as a result of an [unfortunate accident](Unfortunate_accident.md), lead coffins are a safe and practical way to ensure the remains are properly contained.
