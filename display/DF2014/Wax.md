**Wax** is a material [pressed](Screw_press.md) from a [honeycomb](Honeycomb.md). Wax can be used to make [crafts](Craft.md) by a [wax worker](Wax_worker.md).

Honeycombs are produced by [honey bees](Honey_bee.md) at a [hive](Hive.md) and harvested as part of the [beekeeping industry](Beekeeping_industry.md). A dwarf with the [pressing](Presser.md) labor active, a [screw press](Screw_press.md), and empty [jugs](Jug.md) are required to press a honeycomb. A jug of [honey](Honey.md) is produced as a byproduct. Wax will be stored in a [fat](Fat.md) stockpile.

Wax has a low base value of 1, the same as [wood](Wood.md) or non-flux [stone](Stone.md).
