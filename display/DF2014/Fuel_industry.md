The **fuel industry** deals with the harvesting and use of wood or minerals for the production of fuel in the [Metal industry](Metal_industry.md), [Glass industry](Glass_industry.md), and [Ceramic industry](Ceramic_industry.md). It is considered a secondary industry because it refines raw harvested materials, and prepares them for further use in secondary industries.

### Products

> *Main article: [Fuel](Fuel.md)*

- Coke
- Charcoal
