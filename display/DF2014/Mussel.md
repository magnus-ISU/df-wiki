> *A small creature found in both salt and fresh water that lives in an asymmetrical shell rooted to the bottom.*

**Mussels** are a source of [food](Food.md) for fortresses located near any body of water, except [murky pools](Murky_pool.md). They are also one of the few types of creatures, along with [oysters](Oyster.md), [cave lobsters](Cave_lobster.md), [nautiluses](Nautilus.md), and [turtles](Turtle.md), that are sources of [shells](Shell.md). They can't be [butchered](Butcher.md), but can be [cleaned](Fish_cleaning.md) at a [fishery](Fishery.md), which generates a shell and a prepared mussel, or cooked whole (as a "raw mussel") or live (as a "live mussel"), which loses the shell.

Mussels are [hateable](Hateable.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) and will give unhappy [thoughts](Thought.md) to dwarves who absolutely detest mussels when they encounter them.

Some [dwarves](Dwarves.md) [like](Preferences.md) mussels for their *beauty*.
