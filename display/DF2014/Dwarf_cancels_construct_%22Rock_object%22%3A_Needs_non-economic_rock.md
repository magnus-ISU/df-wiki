Urist McDwarf cancels Construct rock Door: Needs non-economic hard rock.

This error message occurs when a dwarf attempts to make a rock object but there are no loose [stones](Stone.md) available. Note that "availability" can be influenced by both [burrows](Burrow.md) and linked [stockpiles](Stockpile.md) -- there may be loose stones right next to the workshop, but if they've been excluded by a burrow or stockpile link, you'll still receive this message. If your stone supply really is exhausted, you can [mine](Mining.md) out [rock](Rock.md) to create more stones. You can also get this error when a dwarf walls themselves into a corner and is unable to leave to get more rocks. Also, be careful where you build your [Mason's workshop](Mason's_workshop.md), as some parts of it obstruct movement.

Sometimes the only rocks available are [economic stones](Economic_stone.md). By default, these types of rock are forbidden for general use. You can toggle economic stones for non-economic use under "Stone" in the [stocks](Stocks.md) menu -- stones in red are reserved for economic use; stones in green are available for any non-economic use.
