> *A colorful lizard that spends its life hunting insects with its long tongue. It moves slowly through the trees, spying the surroundings with its independently roving eyes.*

**Chameleons** are a species of [above ground](Above_ground.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) found in a variety of [biomes](Biome.md), from [deserts](Desert.md) to [tropical](Tropical.md) [forests](Forest.md).

They are notably the greatest [egg](Egg.md)-layers of all vermin, laying 40 to 50 eggs at a time. However, due to being vermin, these eggs can't be gathered through [egg production](Egg_production.md). Fortunately, they can potentially be requested when [trading](Trading.md) with other races such as [humans](Human.md) if they happen to settle in a region with chameleons in it, and their [giant chameleon](Giant_chameleon.md) counterparts lay just as many eggs which can be freely used by your [dwarves](Dwarves.md).

Curiously, chameleons in *Dwarf Fortress* have a body striped with all the colors of the rainbow, as their ability to change color has not been implemented yet.

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) chameleons for their *ability to change color* and their *eyes*.
