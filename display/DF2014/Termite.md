> *A tiny insect capable of destroying large wooden structures when in large numbers.*

**Termites** are a species of [above ground](Above_ground.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) who may be found in any non-freezing [biome](Biome.md). They live in mounds which can be spotted in the surface, and they can't be captured in [animal traps](Animal_trap.md). Despite what their description indicates, they pose no danger to logs or wooden structures.

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) termites for their *industrious nature* and their *ability to devour wood*.
