**Marble** is the only [metamorphic](Metamorphic.md) [flux](Flux.md) [stone](Stone.md), and as such is very valuable when in a primarily metamorphic biome. It also frequently contains a wide variety of [gems](Gem.md).

Older saves from previous versions in the DF2014 release cycle are still <a href="Save_compatibility.md">compatible</a> with this version.  </td></tr></tbody></table>

Being a [calcium carbonate](Calcium_carbonate.md) stone, marble can be made into [quicklime](Quicklime.md) at a [kiln](Kiln.md), to make [parchment](Parchment.md) [sheets](Sheet.md).
