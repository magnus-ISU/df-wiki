**Sphalerite** is the only [ore](Ore.md) of [zinc](Zinc.md), and can also be used as an ingredient in the [smelting](Smelter.md) of [brass](Brass.md). In the game, sphalerite is found as veins in [metamorphic layers](Metamorphic_layer.md). Interestingly, dwarves can smelt sphalerite into zinc while the stone itself does not melt, but *sublimates* (going from solid to gas without melting).

### Smelting

Sphalerite is needed to make two types of [metal](Metal.md) [bars](Bar.md) at a smelter:

- [Zinc](Zinc.md) bars
- [Brass](Brass.md) bars - with [native copper](Native_copper.md), [malachite](Malachite.md), or [tetrahedrite](Tetrahedrite.md)

### Other uses

Sphalerite is twice as [valuable](Value.md) as common stone and is [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md), but zinc itself is not. As such, it's worth considering to enable sphalerite to be used for [masonry](Masonry.md) (in the [status](Status.md) screen, accessed by z), especially if your fortress lacks other magma-safe materials.
