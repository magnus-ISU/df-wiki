**Silty clay** is one of the many types of [soil](Soil.md) that can be found in [soil layers](Soil_layer.md). [Aquifers](Aquifer.md) are never present in silty clay.

Silty clay can be used as a source of [clay](Clay.md) for production of [earthenware](Earthenware.md).
