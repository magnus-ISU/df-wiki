A **sculpture garden** is a [room](Room.md) defined around a [statue](Statue.md) or unengraved [slab](Slab.md). A **memorial hall** is a room defined around a [memorial](Memorial.md) slab, functionally identical to a sculpture garden. [Museums](Museum.md), defined from a [pedestal](Pedestal.md) or [display case](Display_case.md), also behave the same. Sculpture gardens can be assigned to specific dwarves, [locations](Locations.md) (such as a [tavern](Tavern.md), [temple](Temple.md) or [library](Library.md)) or left unassigned, for communal enjoyment. A communal (non-assigned) garden is similar to a [meeting hall](Meeting_hall.md) in that it provides a place for idle dwarves and animals to congregate and socialize - however, there are some important differences.

### Advantages over dining rooms

The biggest advantage a sculpture garden has over a [dining room](Dining_room.md) is simplicity. Since dwarves are not going to eat in it, there is no need to build tables or chairs to accommodate them. As such, there is plenty of room to put in all sorts of [valuable](Item_quality.md) [furniture](Furniture.md), which can give your dwarves positive [emotions](Emotion.md) – furthermore, all furniture in a sculpture garden will be "tastefully arranged", potentially increasing the dwarves' emotional responses. As well, since only a statue or slab is required, it is easy to create a sculpture garden outside. This is very useful to prevent [cave adaptation](Cave_adaptation.md) and can double as an [early warning system](Security_design.md) against [thieves and snatchers](Thief.md).

### Disadvantages

Unassigned sculpture gardens don't possess the same interactivity and bonuses of [locations](Location.md). Before [version 0.42.01](Release_information/0.42.01.md), dwarves would also waste much of their time throwing [parties](Party.md) in sculpture gardens, though these particular activities no longer exist. Having a sculpture garden separate from other locations ultimately comes down to player preference.

### Private gardens

If a sculpture garden is private (assigned to a particular dwarf) then that dwarf will hang out there, in preference to communal meeting places. This is useful if you want a particular dwarf to hang out at a particular place when idle, also increasing the emotional response the owner gets from admiring their "**own** tastefully arranged" furniture.
