### Hair on living creatures

**Hair** is part of the cosmetic layers that any creature can have. It can also have different colors, turning grey or white as creatures age.

### Hair as an item

**Hair** is a byproduct of [butchering](Butcher's_shop.md) some animals. It can be spun into [thread](Thread.md) at a [farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop.md). Hair thread cannot be used to weave [cloth](Cloth.md), but it can be [dyed](Dye.md) and used to [suture](Suturer.md) wounds in a [hospital](Hospital.md) or bind [books](Book.md) in a [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop.md).
