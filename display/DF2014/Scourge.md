> *For a comparison of different weapons, see [Weapon](Weapon.md).*

<br> A **scourge** is an [edge](Attack_types.md) [weapon](Weapon.md) that consists of a spike or bladed weight on the end of a flexible length of [material](Material.md) that can be swung at high speed. Scourges have low contact area and high velocity, making them effective even against [armored](Armor.md) opponents, [shredding](Wear.md) armor, flesh and bone. Scourges are similar to [whips](Whip.md), though the whip is a blunt weapon with an even smaller contact area.

Scourges use and train the [lasher](Lasher.md) skill. As foreign weapons, dwarves cannot [forge](Forge.md) scourges, limiting supply to whatever low-[quality](Quality.md) specimens can be scavenged from [goblin](Goblin.md) invaders. [Even the smallest dwarves](Weapon.md#size) can equip a scourge one-handed.

Though typical goblin scourge has 1 hitting part instead of human's dozen, it's still better than a sword.
