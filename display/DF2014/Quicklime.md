**Quicklime** is a [stone](Stone.md)-derived product used in the production of [parchment](Parchment.md).

### Producing quicklime

Quicklime can be produced at a [kiln](Kiln.md) or [magma kiln](Magma_kiln.md) by cooking any of the [calcium carbonate](Calcium_carbonate.md) stones ([calcite](Calcite.md), [chalk](Chalk.md), [limestone](Limestone.md), or [marble](Marble.md)). A dwarf must have the [furnace operator](Furnace_operator.md) [labor](Labor.md) active to do this. If you're using a standard kiln the process will consume one unit of [fuel](Fuel.md), and in either case you'll need an empty [bag](Bag.md) to store the finished product.

### Uses

On its own, a bag of quicklime has no practical use. However, it can be further processed at an [ashery](Ashery.md) into [milk of lime](Milk_of_lime.md), which is a crucial ingredient in the production of [parchment](Parchment.md). Much like [certain](Lead.md) [other](Cinnabar.md) [entertaining](Pitchblende.md) real-world materials, in-game quicklime does not pose any health hazard to your dwarves and can't be weaponized for its [Fun](Fun.md) reaction with water.

### Storage

Quicklime is stored under "Boxes and Bags" in [furniture](Furniture.md) [stockpiles](Stockpile.md), which makes it difficult to separate from empty bags. One workaround is to set up a kiln dedicated to producing quicklime and a furniture stockpile set to take only from that kiln. Quicklime produced at this kiln will then be accepted only by the aforementioned stockpile, which can in turn serve as a feeder stockpile for your milk of lime ashery. This approach will not remove existing quicklime bags from circulation among your other "Boxes and Bags" stockpiles, and purchased quicklime will not be accepted there.

### Real world

Quicklime (also known as *burnt lime* or *calcium oxide*) is a caustic, alkaline crystal that forms a fine white powder. While its use in *Dwarf Fortress* is very limited, in reality it is a common industrial product, with usage dating back to ancient times in roles as varied as production of plaster for homes to use as a primitive chemical weapon.
