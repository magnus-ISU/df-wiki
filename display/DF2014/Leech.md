> *A tiny, aquatic, worm-like creature that feeds on blood.*

**Leeches** are [hateable](Hateable.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) who can be found in [pools](Pool.md) and [lakes](Lake.md). They will give [unhappy thoughts](Thought.md#unhappy-thoughts) to [dwarves](Dwarf.md) who absolutely detest leeches when they encounter them. [Giant leeches](Giant_leech.md), on the other hand, are likely to give dwarves unhappy *deaths*. They can't be captured in [animal traps](Animal_trap.md).

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) leeches for their *feeding habits*.
