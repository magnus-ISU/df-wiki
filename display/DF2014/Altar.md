**Altars** are a [furniture](Furniture.md) item used in [temples](Temple.md). In fortress mode, altars can be made from most materials, like wood at a [Carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop.md), stone at a [Mason's workshop](Mason's_workshop.md) (although using the [Stonecrafter](Stonecrafter.md) labor), glass at a [Glass furnace](Glass_furnace.md)/[Magma glass furnace](Magma_glass_furnace.md) and metal at a [Metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md)/[Magma forge](Magma_forge.md). They can be built with b-Alta labeled as **Offering Places**, although they currently serve no purpose other than increasing a [temple](Temple.md)'s value and decoration, as temples don't require them to function.

In [adventure mode](Adventure_mode.md), you can often find [dice](Dice.md) that can be rolled on altars.
