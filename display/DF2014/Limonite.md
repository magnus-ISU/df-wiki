**Limonite** is an [ore](Ore.md) of [iron](Iron.md), a mid-value metal useful for [weapons](Weapon.md) and [armor](Armor.md). It can be smelted at a [smelter](Smelter.md) by a dwarf with the [furnace operating](Furnace_operating.md) [labor](Labor.md) activated to produce [iron](Iron.md) [bars](Bar.md).

It is, for all [metalsmithing](Metalsmithing.md) purposes, identical to [magnetite](Magnetite.md) and [hematite](Hematite.md). It has a noticeably lower solid [density](Density.md) (read "weighs less") than the other iron ores and can thus be hauled to its destination more quickly than the other two ores.
