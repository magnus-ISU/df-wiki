The **general** is the highest-ranking member of the mostly-unrealized, civilization-wide dwarven army, responsible for determining military strategy as well as the appointment of [lieutenants](Lieutenant.md) and [captains](Captain.md). The general commands the lieutenants, who in turn command the captains. The general arrives alongside the [monarch](Monarch.md).

In the current implementation, there does not seem to be anything special about the general. There are no squads under the general's control.

[Human](Human.md) and [goblin](Goblin.md) civilizations occasionally have generals as one of their variable positions, but they aren't guaranteed.

[Elves](Elf.md) additionally have the princess role, with the same functions and likewise appointing [ranger captains](Ranger_captain.md). Despite the name, the position is not inherited, instead appointed by the [druid](Druid.md). However, the princess is defined as the successor to the [queen](Monarch.md).
