**Shearer** is the [skill](Skill.md) associated with the shearing [labor](Labor.md). Shearers shear the [wool](Wool.md) from creatures in a [farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop.md) using the spin thread S command. The result of the action is the production of [yarn](Yarn.md).

There are only three animals that can be sheared by dwarves: [sheep](Sheep.md), [llamas](Llama.md), and [alpacas](Alpaca.md). [Trolls](Troll.md) can also be sheared, but only by their [goblin](Goblin.md) masters. Therefore, the only way to acquire troll fur is to steal it from a goblin site during a raid. [Shearable](Shearable.md) animals have `[YARN]` token under `[SELECT_MATERIAL:HAIR]` section of their RAWs. Wool can be spun into [yarn](Yarn.md), which in turn can be used to make [cloth](Cloth.md) at a [loom](Loom.md).

Shearable animals are ready to be sheared 300 days after shearing, as defined in raws.

Shearer is the only skill that doesn't appear to train any [attributes](Attribute.md). This may be an oversight.
