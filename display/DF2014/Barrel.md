**Barrels** are storage units that, like [bins](Bin.md) and [bags](Bag.md), conserve [stockpile](Stockpile.md) space by containing several items of the same type. They can be made out of [wood](Wood.md) at a [carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop.md) (requires one [log](Log.md) for each barrel) or forged from [metal](Metal.md) at a [forge](Forge.md) (requires 3 [metal](Metal.md) [bars](Bar.md)). Non-magma [forges](Forge.md) also require one [bar](Bar.md) of [fuel](Fuel.md).

For information on managing barrels in stockpiles, see [using bins and barrels](Using_bins_and_barrels.md). When a barrel is emptied, it may be used again or stored in a furniture stockpile.

For most uses, barrels can be replaced by a [large pot](Large_pot.md), which has the same storage capacity (and requires no [wood](Wood.md) or [metal](Metal.md)) and is only one quarter as heavy *as a barrel made of the same material*. Typical stone pots will be 33% heavier than wooden barrels. Fire clay "stoneware" pots weigh the same as a wooden barrel, while earthenware pots are lighter but must be glazed.

Barrels are used in building [dyer's shops](Dyer's_shop.md) and [asheries](Ashery.md). Using a valuable masterwork or [artifact](Artifact.md) barrel to build one of these buildings can significantly increase fortress [wealth](Wealth.md).

Barrels can hold up to 60 prepared meals, plants, or cheeses, 30 pieces of meat or fish, any number of units of brewed [alcohol](Alcohol.md) (but only a single stack), 100 units of [lye](Lye.md) or [milk](Milk.md), or 6000 eggs regardless of size. However, alcohol brought at [embark](Embark.md) will be in barrels that only contain up to 5 units each; this due to alcohol barrels allowing only 1 stack and embark items coming in stacks of 5 units or less.

The name of a barrel can vary by circumstance: the name of an empty barrel is the material it is made of, e.g. "tower-cap barrel", otherwise a barrel will be named for what it contains with its material appended, e.g. "dwarven wine barrel (tower-cap)". Additionally, if a barrel is assigned for use in a stockpile, the number of the stockpile will be appended, e.g. "dwarven wine barrel (tower-cap) \<#1\>".
