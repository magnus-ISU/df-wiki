**Rose gold** is an [alloy](Alloy.md) of [gold](Gold.md) and [copper](Copper.md), made at a [smelter](Smelter.md). It is made by combining three gold [bars](Bar.md) with one copper bar, which results in four rose gold bars. The value of this is a little subjective, as the total material worth of the produced metal is the same as that of the ingredients (3\*30 and 1\*2 -\> 4\*23). However, on screen it is a lovely purple color.

Disregarding [fuel](Fuel.md), you lose the possible utility of the copper bar to get purple-coloured furniture and crafts (for buildings and constructions, [bismuth](Bismuth.md) will produce the same color). If you have no magma access then the transition will cost you fuel as well, so it's really a question of how important it is that you get purple [metal](Metal.md).

One quirk of rose gold is that it is the same density as gold, despite using a much lighter copper bar in its creation. If you're in need of a lot of especially heavy items for your [stupid dwarf trick](Stupid_dwarf_trick.md), this bears consideration.
