This message occurs when a tame [troll](Troll.md) is interrupted at work, due to being taken for [shearing](Shearing.md) in the [farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop.md). This can occur only in [modded](Modding.md) games, because in vanilla the [dwarven](Dwarf.md) [civilization](Civilization.md) cannot tame trolls in the first place. After being sheared, the troll will continue his work.
