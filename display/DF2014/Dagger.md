*A **large dagger** (specifically a "rondel dagger")*

A **large dagger** is an [edged](Attack_types.md) [weapon](Weapon.md) that is essentially larger than a knife but smaller than a [short sword](Short_sword.md). Compared to the standard short sword, large daggers have no "flat" attack and a significantly less lethal "slash" attack, but gain a much more vicious "stab" attack with 1/10 the contact area of a sword point. This makes large daggers particularly effective against [armored](Armor.md) opponents: an iron dagger has a fair chance to pierce even [steel](Steel.md) armor, and the resulting internal injuries can be debilitating and deadly.

Large daggers use and train the [knife user](Knife_user.md) skill, and are common weapons for [kobold](Kobold.md) and [goblin](Goblin.md) thieves. As foreign weapons, dwarves cannot [forge](Forge.md) large daggers, limiting supply to whatever low-[quality](Quality.md) specimens can be scavenged from ambushers. [Even the smallest dwarves](Weapon.md#size) are able to equip large daggers one-handed.
