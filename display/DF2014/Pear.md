**Pear** is one of the many genera of trees found above ground. Like the vast majority of above ground trees, pear [wood](Wood.md) is brown and produces brown products.

Pear trees can be harvested by dwarves, with the obvious result being pears. Pears can be eaten raw, cooked or brewed into [perry](Alcohol.md).

Some [dwarves](Dwarf.md) [like](Preferences.md) pear trees for their *fruit*.
