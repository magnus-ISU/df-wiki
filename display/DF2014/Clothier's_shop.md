The **Clothier's shop** is a [workshop](Workshop.md) used by a [Clothier](Clothier.md) to make [clothes](Clothes.md), [ropes](Rope.md) and [bags](Bag.md) out of [cloth](Cloth.md), [yarn](Yarn.md) and [silk](Silk.md). It can also be used to [sew images](Decoration.md) onto [finished goods](Finished_goods.md). Cloth and silk must be either bought from [caravans](Trading.md) or [woven](Weaver.md) at a [loom](Loom.md).

The clothier's shop can be built with any building material, and must be built by a [dwarf](Dwarf.md) with the Clothesmaking [labor](Labor.md) enabled.

To create differently sized clothing<sup>[v0.42.01](../Category/Version_0.42.01.md)</sup>, request it to be made from the workshop as usual. Afterwards, go back to the main workshop menu and look at the details of the issued job. filter for the race you want to make the item for and press enter twice.

Note that although cloth/silk backpacks and quivers exist, they cannot be made by the player.
