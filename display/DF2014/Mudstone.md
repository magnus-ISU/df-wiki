**Mudstone** is an unremarkable [sedimentary](Sedimentary.md) [stone](Stone.md).

Mudstone cavern floors do not count as [mud](Mud.md), and are thus unsuitable for [farming](Farming.md).

### In Real Life

Mudstone is a fine grained sedimentary rock which originates from [clay](Clay.md) and [mud](Mud.md). With increased pressure over time, the clay minerals can form into parallel layers. This finely bedded material that splits readily into thin slices is then called [shale](Shale.md).
