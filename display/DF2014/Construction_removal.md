Dwarves with the **construction removal** [labor](Labor.md) enabled (default) will remove any constructions designated for removal. To designate a construction for removal, open the d [designations menu](Designations_menu~2.md) and select n Remove Construction.

A dwarf will (temporarily) cancel the removal of a walkable tile if another creature is standing on the tile. He won't cancel, though, if \*he\* is the one standing on the tile (as a result of a "creature swap", for instance), making construction removal at least moderately dangerous work.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[8253](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=8253)</sup>
