In *Dwarf Fortress*, a **creature** is defined as any animate, normally-mobile (and for the sake of this article, non-[vermin](Vermin.md)) being that can interact with the world and any element inside it. The creatures in the game range from being entirely realistic to completely mythical. Although most creatures are animals, [dwarves](Dwarf.md), [giant cave spiders](Giant_cave_spider.md), and even [megabeasts](Megabeast.md) are all also considered creatures. Various creatures can and will interact with a fortress or adventurer in many different ways.

Some creatures have [skills](Skill.md) that match what type of creature they are (e.g. [monkeys](Monkey.md) having legendary climbing skill). Though most creatures can be found in any mode, some are exclusive to [adventure mode](Adventure_mode.md) or [fortress mode](Fortress_mode.md).

Also note that creatures with the `[ARENA_RESTRICTED]` or `[DOES_NOT_EXIST]` tokens cannot be spawned in the [object testing arena](Object_testing_arena.md), similarly to [vermin](Vermin.md) (e.g. [flies](Fly.md), [worms](Worm.md)).

### Spawning

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/4/44/Creatures_many.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Creatures_many.png)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Creatures_many.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Creatures_many.png)

Many creatures packed into one area.

The creatures that will spawn on any given fortress map depend on the biome(s) that the fortress is in. Additionally, there are several [creature tokens](Creature_token.md) in the [raws](Raws.md) that deal with creature spawning:

- `[FREQUENCY:X]`: This tag dictates *how often* a creature will spawn. It ranges from 0-100, and is a comparative number, where the higher this number is, the more likely the creature is to spawn.
- `[CLUSTER_NUMBER:X]`: This determines *how many* creatures will appear at one time on a map.
- `[POPULATION_NUMBER:X]`: This determines the *total number* of this type of creature that can ever visit your fortress. The exact number varies depending on the map. For example, [deer](Deer.md) have a `[POPULATION_NUMBER:15:30]`, meaning that if you kill between 15-30 deer, no more deer will ever visit your fortress.

### Reading the Table

| Tile | Name | Playable | Hostile | Food Source | Adult Body Size | Pet Value (×) | Biome | Features |

The above columns indicate, in order:

- **Tile:** The tile assigned to the creature, how you will see it without a graphic set.
- **Name:** The name of the creature as it displays in-game.
- **Playable:** If "No", the creature is not playable in any modes. "Fort" indicates that the creature is playable in fortress mode (`[SITE_CONTROLLABLE]`). "Adv" indicates that the creature is playable in adventure mode. All creatures except humans must have a population in an `[ALL_MAIN_POPS_CONTROLLABLE]` civilization in order to be playable in adventure mode; goblins (and other creatures) cannot be played from a goblin civ. Humans can be played whether or not a population exists due to `[OUTSIDER_CONTROLLABLE]`, but an `[ALL_MAIN_POPS_CONTROLLABLE]` civ still needs to have existed at some point. Creatures with `[LOCAL_POPS_CONTROLLABLE]` are also playable in adventure mode.
- **Hostile:** If "Yes", then the creature will attack on sight, if "No" then the creature is either neutral, or friendly. [Undead](Undead.md) creatures are always hostile to living things.
- **Food Source:** If "Yes", then the creature can be butchered into an [edible substance](Meat.md) that your dwarves will feed on.
- **Adult Body Size:** The average [size](Size.md) of the creature when an adult. This can be anywhere from 500 for a [rabbit](Rabbit.md) to 25,000,000 for a [dragon](Dragon.md). The creature's volume in cm<sup>3</sup>, which, for creatures made of flesh, more-or-less equals the creature's weight in grams.[\[1\]](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=30026.msg831163#msg831163) These sizes do not correspond to the sizes which trigger [pressure plates](Pressure_plate.md). Size is modified with height and broadness (i.e. incredibly skinny and short is below the average weight, while a fat and tall one is above it).
- **Pet Value:** This is the value the creature can be bought and sold for as a [pet](Pet.md) during [trading](Trading.md).
- **Biome:** Where the creature can be found.
- **Features:** Any special features the creature possesses, including things such as [causing a syndrome](Cave_blob.md), [breathing fire](Dragon.md), or [spinning webs](Giant_cave_spider.md).

Note: If you wish to view alternate ways of sorting creatures, such as sorting creatures by biomes and location, or sorting domestic creatures by features, there is a new page found here: [Alternate creature sorting](Alternate_creature_sorting.md)
