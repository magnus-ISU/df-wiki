An alluvial layer of land in [Canyon State Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Rock_Canyon_State_Park_(California)).

Several minerals, ores, and gems are specified to occur at least in part within **alluvial layers**. Alluvial stone is loose and unconsolidated stone that has been eroded away, smoothed down by water flow, and then redeposited in a non-marine setting. In Dwarf Fortress, all [sedimentary](Sedimentary.md) layers are capable of containing alluvial deposits.

Whether or not a particular [stone](Stone.md), [gem](Gem.md), or [ore](Ore.md) appears in the alluvial layer is controlled by the `[ENVIRONMENT:ALLUVIAL:...]` tag in its [raw files](Raw_file.md). Alluvial environments can contain [clusters](Cluster.md) of:

> - [native gold](Native_gold.md)
> - [native platinum](Native_platinum.md)
> - [cassiterite](Cassiterite.md)
> - jade [gems](Gem.md) ([blue](Blue_jade.md), [lavender](Lavender_jade.md), [pink](Pink_jade.md), [white](White_jade.md), and [green](Green_jade.md))
