**Depression** is a temporary emotional breakdown experienced mainly by dwarves with high [propensity to depression](Personality_trait.md). Dwarves who are prone to anxiety will instead become [oblivious](Oblivious.md), while those with anger management issues will throw [tantrums](Tantrum.md).

Depressed dwarves will cancel any job they may be doing at the moment and mope around until they regain their composure. Unless [stress](Stress.md) levels are reduced, bouts of depression will repeat until the dwarf is eventually [stricken by melancholy](Insanity.md).
