A **champion** is an appointed [noble](Noble.md) position which becomes available after a fortress becomes a [barony](Baron.md). They have no [room](Room.md) requirements.

Other dwarves who talk to the Champion will get a special [thought](Thought.md) about "talking to a pillar of society", with the happiness bonus or penalty depending on the dwarf's [viewpoint on tradition](Personality_value.md#tradition), and they will also satisfy their [need](Need.md) to "uphold tradition".
