> *A tiny creature made up of a series of crackling knobs set at strange angles.*

**Knuckle worms** are a species of [above ground](Above_ground.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) found in any [evil](Evil.md) areas that are not [freezing](Biome.md), and may cause nearby [food](Food.md) to [rot](Rot.md). They are tied with the [fox squirrel](Fox_squirrel.md) as the [most valuable](List_of_creatures_by_pet_value.md) of all vermin. They can be captured by [trappers](Trapper.md) and turned into [pets](Pet.md).

Some [dwarves](Dwarves.md) [like](Preferences.md) knuckle worms for their *knobs and angles* and their *crackles and pops*. <br><br>
