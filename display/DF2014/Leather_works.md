**Leather works** is a [workshop](Workshop.md) which produces [leather](Leather.md) items out of tanned [hides](Skin.md) made at the [tanner's shop](Tanner's_shop.md). These items include [armor](Armor.md), [backpacks](Backpack.md), [waterskins](Flask.md), [quivers](Quiver.md) for [military](Military.md) dwarves, and [clothes](Clothes.md) and [bags](Bag.md) for civilian use. [Leather armor](Armor.md#types-of-armor) is typically less protective than metal armor, but is [lighter and thus better](Armor.md#encumbrance) for initial [armor wearing](Armor_user.md) training or for [squads](Squad.md) that require [speed](Gait.md) over defensive ability.

[Decorating](Decoration.md) [finished goods](Finished_goods.md) with leather is also done at this workshop.

[Crafts](Crafts.md) made from leather are made at the [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop.md).

To create differently sized clothing or armor<sup>[v0.42.01](../Category/Version_0.42.01.md)</sup> request it to be made from the workshop as usual. Afterwards, go back to the main workshop menu and look at the details of the issued job. filter for the race you want to make the item for and press enter twice.
