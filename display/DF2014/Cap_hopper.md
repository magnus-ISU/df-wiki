> *A tiny amphibian with a long prehensile tail. It lives in underground swamps and tower-cap forests on which it feeds.*

**Cap hoppers** are a species of subterranean, amphibious [vermin](Vermin.md) [creatures](Creature.md) that live in the first and second layers of the [caverns](Cavern.md). They can be captured by [trappers](Trapper.md) and turned into [pets](Pet.md), and are otherwise unremarkable. All cap hoppers possess Legendary [skill](Skill.md) in [climbing](Climber.md).

Some [dwarves](Dwarf.md) [like](Preferences.md) cap hoppers for their *swinging antics*.
