**Jugs** are a type of [container](Container.md), made from [ceramic](Ceramic.md) at a [kiln](Kiln.md), [metal](Metal.md) at a [forge](Forge.md), [glass](Glass.md) at a [glass furnace](Glass_furnace.md), stone by a [stone crafter](Stone_crafter.md) at a [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop.md), or [wood](Wood.md) at a [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop.md). They are used in [beekeeping](Beekeeping_industry.md) to store [royal jelly](Royal_jelly.md) or at a [screw press](Screw_press.md) to store [honey](Honey.md) or [oil](Oil.md). Jugs made from [earthenware](Earthenware.md) need to be [glazed](Glaze.md) before they can be used to hold [liquids](Liquid.md) like honey. Jugs are stored in the [Tools](Tool.md) section of the Finished Goods stockpile, and managed from the **tools** section of the Stocks screen. Even when filled with food, they will be stored in the Finished Goods stockpile. Jugs are stacked in [bins](Bin.md).

Jugs can be used in [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md) to store water like a [waterskin](Waterskin.md), storing 16 units of water.

### Forging and Melting

- Metal jugs cost **one** [metal](Metal.md) bar to forge, or **one** [adamantine](Adamantine.md) wafer.

- When a jug is melted down, it will return **0.3** metal bars/adamantine wafers for an efficiency of **30%**
