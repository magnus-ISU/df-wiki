A **screw press** is a special workshop used to press liquids out of various substances. Currently, this consists of pressing [seed](Seed.md) pastes or fruit ([olives](Olive.md)) to yield [oil](Oil.md) (which can then be made into [soap](Soap.md) or cooked) and [press cake](Press_cake.md) (which can be cooked), and pressing honeycombs to yield [honey](Honey.md) (which can then be brewed into mead) and wax cake (which can be made into wax crafts). Each pressing job requires an empty [jug](Jug.md) to hold the oil or honey.

[Slurry](Slurry.md) may be pressed into sheets of [paper](Paper.md) at the screw press. Unlike other jobs at the screw press, this requires [paper making](Papermaker.md) and not [pressing](Pressing.md). Sheets of paper also have quality levels, while other products at the screw press do not.

### Bugs

- Screw presses sometimes do not allow the "press [honeycomb](Honeycomb.md)" job to be added directly; adding the job via the [manager](Manager.md) interface should get the work started.
