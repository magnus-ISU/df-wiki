Back in version 0.28.181.40d and earlier, the [Philosopher](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/40d:Philosopher) was a noble who arrived once your population reached 100; unlike most other nobles, it had no special room requirements and made no [mandates](Mandate.md).

In the current version, a Philosopher is merely a [Scholar](Scholar.md) whose studies focus primarly on the appropriate [topics](Topic.md#philosophy).
