A **Magma smelter** is a special variation of the basic [smelter](Smelter.md) that uses [magma](Magma.md) as a source of heat, instead of [fuel](Fuel.md). Like all magma-powered buildings, it is not visible under the Build menu until your dwarves discover a source of magma.

To function, magma smelters must be built with at least one of the eight edge tiles occupying an open space with at least 4/7 magma on the next floor down. This magma can be natural, or channeled from a larger source, or otherwise [transported from another level](Magma.md#bringing-magma-up) to a trench designed to hold the magma.

If the only opening to the magma is under the [impassable tile](Impassable_tile.md) of the smelter, then magma creatures won't be able to enter the workshop area through the opening; otherwise they can.

Magma smelters do not consume any magma from their magma source - you can use a single tile of 4/7 (or deeper) magma below the smelter as an infinite heat source.

Note that both [pig iron](Pig_iron.md) and [steel](Steel.md) production still require "[fuel](Fuel.md)" as a part of the recipe, as a source of carbon for the reaction.
