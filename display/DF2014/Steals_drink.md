Creatures with the `[CURIOUSBEAST_GUZZLER]` [creature token](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/v0.34:Creature_token#C) will happily devour your [alcohol](Alcohol.md) stocks, if given the chance - this can prove particularly [fun](Fun.md) when your newly-founded fort's entire alcohol supply is still sitting topside in the [wagon](Wagon_(embark).md).

This property can also be used to capture certain creatures (such as [grizzly bears](Grizzly_bear.md)) by surrounding a small drink stockpile with [cage](Cage.md) traps.

### Creatures who can steal drinks

The following creatures possess the `[CURIOUSBEAST_GUZZLER]` token.
