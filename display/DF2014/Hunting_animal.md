*For information on the selection and training of hunting animals, see [animal trainer](Animal_trainer.md).*

Animals with the `[TRAINABLE]` or `[TRAINABLE_HUNTING]` [creature token](Creature_token.md) can be trained for hunting by an [animal trainer](Animal_trainer.md). Trained **hunting animals** gain speed, agility, and possibly ambusher and observer bonuses. Once trained, they can be assigned to follow specific [hunters](Hunter.md) and accompany them when hunting.
