The `[OBJECT:PLANT]` [token](Token.md) defines the properties of [plants](Plant.md); this being a full list of all known plant tokens.

### Growth tokens

These tokens are used for all plants and specify growths growing on a plant.

Edible or otherwise usable growths should have \[STOCKPILE_PLANT_GROWTH\] in their [material definitions](Material_definition_token.md) for proper stockpiling. This also lets them be collected from plant gathering and farming jobs.

<li>BRANCHES_AND_TWIGS / LIGHT_BRANCHES_AND_TWIGS</li>
<li>BRANCHES / LIGHT_BRANCHES</li>

<tr>
<td> <a href="#growth-trunk-height-perc">GROWTH_TRUNK_HEIGHT_PERC</a>
</td>
<td>
<ul><li>integer:integer</li></ul>
</td>
<td>Controls the height on the trunk at which the growth begins to appear. The first integer is the percent of the trunk height where the growth begins appearing: 0 will cause growths to appear along the entire trunk above the first tile; 100 will cause growths to appear only at the topmost trunk tile.
<p>The second integer currently <i>must</i> be -1, but might be intended to control whether it counts height starting from the bottom or top.
</p>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td> <a href="#growth-density">GROWTH_DENSITY</a>
</td>
<td>
<ul><li>integer</li></ul>
</td>
<td>Currently has no effect.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td> <a href="#growth-timing">GROWTH_TIMING</a>
</td>
<td>
<ul><li><a href="Time.md">time</a> start:end (0-403200)</li></ul>
</td>
<td>Specifies at which part of the year the growth appears.  Default is all year round.
<p>A single growth can only have one GROWTH_TIMING tag. If multiple are declared, the last one will be used. To make a growth appear multiple times during the year, you need to create a different growth for every GROWTH_TIMING interval. By using the same material for all of the duplicate growths, all of them will be stockpiled together and be eligible for the same reactions. Edible/brewable growths will have separate entries in the kitchen menu, though.
</p><p>There is no known way to declare a growth timing that lasts from winter into spring. Including numbers below 0 or above 403200 in the range will make the growth available at all times, as though you hadn't defined a growth timing at all. So will including a range for which the start time is later than the end time.
</p><p>This has no effect on farmed growths; all eligible growths that have [STOCKPILE_PLANT_GROWTH] in their materials will be harvested, regardless of if they are currently within their growth timing or not.
</p>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td> <a href="#growth-print">GROWTH_PRINT</a>
</td>
<td>
<ul><li>overworld tile</li>
<li>item tile</li>
<li>color</li>
<li>time (0-403200) start:end, ALL, or NONE</li>
<li>priority</li></ul>
</td>
<td>Specifies the appearance of the growth.  Can be specified more than once, for example for autumn leaves. Transitions between different timing periods will happen gradually over the course of 2000 ticks. Priority seems to control what to display when there would be multiple symbols on a tile.<sup>[<a href="https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/DF2014_Talk:Plant_token">Verify</a>]</sup>
<p>The GROWTH_PRINT tile will only be displayed when the growth in question is actually present, even if its timing parameter is ALL.
</p>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td> <a href="#growth-has-seed">GROWTH_HAS_SEED</a>

<td>

<td>The growth drops a seed if eaten raw.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td> <a href="#growth-drops-off">GROWTH_DROPS_OFF</a>
</td>
<td>
</td>
<td>Growths drop from the plant, producing a cloud of items which fall on the ground, which <a href="Herbalist.md">herbalists</a> can collect.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td> <a href="#growth-drops-off-no-cloud">GROWTH_DROPS_OFF_NO_CLOUD</a>
</td>
<td>
