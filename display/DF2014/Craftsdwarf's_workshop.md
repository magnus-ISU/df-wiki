The **Craftsdwarf's workshop** is used to make [crafts](Craft.md) of many different kinds and perform a few specialized jobs. It can be built from any construction material by a dwarf with the [Bone Carving](Bone_carver.md), [Stonecrafting](Stonecrafting.md), or [Woodcrafting](Woodcrafting.md) [labors](Labor.md) enabled.

The craftsdwarf's workshop is the cornerstone of [trade](Trading.md) in *Dwarf Fortress*. All crafts can be traded for [food](Food.md), [alcohol](Alcohol.md), [weapons](Weapon.md), and other goods. Many crafts can also be acquired by dwarves as personal items, displayed on [pedestals](Pedestal.md), or used in activities like drinking, music, and play. Various goods, [furniture](Furniture.md) and [bolts](Bolt.md) can also be [decorated](Decoration.md) with [bone](Bone.md), [pearl](Pearl.md), [horn](Horn.md), [ivory](Ivory.md), or [shells](Shell.md) to increase their [value](Value.md).

Other crafts are more directly useful to your fortress: [tools](Tool.md) such as [large pots](Large_pot.md), [jugs](Jug.md), and [nest boxes](Nest_box.md) can be created at this workshop. Large pots, particularly, can serve as a plentiful replacement for [wooden](Wood.md) [barrels](Barrel.md) in most circumstances.

The craftsdwarf's workshop can also be used to create [armor](Armor.md) from bone or shell, make [bolts](Bolt.md) from [wood](Wood.md) or bone, assemble rock [short swords](Short_sword.md) from [obsidian](Obsidian.md) and [wood](Wood.md), engrave [memorial](Memorial.md) [slabs](Slab.md), and extract strands of [adamantine](Adamantine.md) for refinement.

### Ammo

Trees and the bones of butchered or slaughtered animals provide a plentiful and renewable source of material for ammunition. When making [wood](Wood.md) bolts, a single log produces a stack of 25 bolts. When making [bone](Bone.md) bolts, an entire stack of bones is brought to the workshop, and then *one* bone from that stack becomes a stack of 5 bolts.

### Armor

Bone or shell armor, while vastly inferior to [metal](Metal.md) [armor](Armor.md), is still better than nothing. Only 4 armor parts may be made from bone: leggings, greaves, gauntlets and helms. Only 3 parts may be made from shell: leggings, gauntlets and helms.

Making bone leggings or greaves requires 3 stack of bones, but it only consumes 1 bone from each stack. (You cannot use a stack of 3 bones instead. There must be 3 separate stacks.) Making bone gauntlets consumes 1 bone from 1 stack, and produces both a right and a left gauntlet. Making a bone helm consumes 1 bone from 1 stack.

### Notes

- The name of this workshop is actually derived from the `[PROFESSION_NAME:CRAFTSMAN]` value specified for your fortress's primary race - for example, if you were to use mods to play as [humans](Human.md), this would be called a "Craftsman's Workshop" instead.
- Most crafting jobs require the respective labors, however; [shell](Shell.md), [ivory](Ivory.md), tooth, [horn](Horn.md), and [pearl](Pearl.md) are all worked by Bone Carvers.
- [Metal](Metal.md) crafts are made at a [metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md). You can, however, make crafts out of metal **ores** by enabling the appropriate nugget use in the z [Stone](Stone.md) menu. This will result in fewer crafts, but [quality](Quality.md) modifiers may offset the difference in value.
- Other [cloth](Cloth.md) and [leather](Leather.md) items are made at a [clothier's shop](Clothier's_shop.md) or [leather works](Leather_works.md), respectively.
- [Moody](Strange_mood.md) dwarves without a moodable skill will claim a craftsdwarf's workshop and produce a legendary stone, wood, or bone craft.
- Multi-part [Instruments](Instrument.md) can be assembled here (assumed that single-part bone/thread/stone instruments can as well)<sup>[v0.42.01](../Category/Version_0.42.01.md)</sup>
- Instrument pieces can be made here from bone, thread, and stone.<sup>[v0.42.01](../Category/Version_0.42.01.md)</sup>
