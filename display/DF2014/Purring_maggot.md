> *A tiny larva which is prized for its milk.*

**Purring maggots** are a type of [cavern](Cavern.md)-living [vermin](Vermin.md) notable for being the only underground source of [milk](Milk.md), known as [dwarven milk](Dwarven_milk.md). They can be obtained by [trapping](Trapper.md) them with [animal traps](Animal_trap.md). They are [hateable](Hateable.md) vermin and will inspire an unhappy [thought](Thought.md) in [dwarves](Dwarves.md) that detest them.

To obtain these maggots you will need to discover the 2nd or 3rd cavern layer, then construct (b-m) [animal traps](Animal_trap.md) on cave moss there. Use q to set meat/fish for bait. A higher quality trap will increase the chances of catching a purring maggot instead of losing the bait.

Some dwarves [like](Preference.md) purring maggots for their *comforting whirs*.

### Bugs

- Tamed purring maggots can't be milked. <sup>[Bug](#bugs):[3670](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=3670)</sup>
- After being milked, purring maggots are left inside the workshop as an item unless they are assigned to a [cage](Cage.md). <sup>[Bug](#bugs):[3668](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=3668)</sup>
