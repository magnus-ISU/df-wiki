A **metamorphic layer** is a [stone layer](Stone_layer.md) comprised of metamorphic rock. Metamorphic layers contain several unique and valuable types of [gems](Gems.md); however, they are generally poor in metal ores, lacking any ores of [iron](Iron.md). Furthermore the only metamorphic [flux](Flux.md) layer stone is [marble](Marble.md).

Metamorphic rocks arise from the transformation of existing rock types, in a process called metamorphism, which means "change in form". The original rock is subjected to heat and pressure, causing profound physical or chemical change. The protolith may be a sedimentary, igneous, or existing metamorphic rock.

### Metamorphic layers

The following types of metamorphic stone form layers:

- [Quartzite](Quartzite.md)
- [Slate](Slate.md)
- [Phyllite](Phyllite.md)
- [Schist](Schist.md)
- [Gneiss](Gneiss.md)
- [Marble](Marble.md)

### Stone found in metamorphic layers

The following types of stone are found in formations within metamorphic layers:

- [Galena](Galena.md) (veins)
- [Sphalerite](Sphalerite.md) (veins)
- [Cobaltite](Cobaltite.md) (veins)
- [Rutile](Rutile.md) (small clusters)
- [Hornblende](Hornblende.md) (small clusters)
- [Orthoclase](Orthoclase.md) (large clusters)
- [Mica](Mica.md) (large clusters)
