**Black bronze** is a semiprecious alloy of [copper](Copper.md), [gold](Gold.md) and [silver](Silver.md). Unlike [regular](Bronze.md) or [bismuth bronze](Bismuth_bronze.md), black bronze cannot be used to create [weapons](Weapon.md) or [armor](Armor.md), restricting its uses to [blacksmithing](Blacksmith.md) and [metalcrafting](Metal_crafter.md) purposes. Black bronze cannot be [smelted](Smelter.md) directly from [ore](Ore.md), but only from [bars](Bar.md) of pure [metals](Metal.md). Unlike some [alloys](Alloy.md), the [value](Item_value.md) of a bar of black bronze is equal exactly to the average value of its components – creating this alloy does not increase the total value of the material (2 [copper](Copper.md) bars at 2☼ each + 1 [silver](Silver.md) bar at 10☼ + 1 [gold](Gold.md) bar at 30☼ = 44☼/4 bars = 11☼/bar of black bronze).
