> *If cow cheese is made from cow's milk, what is dwarven cheese made of?*-- Anon

**Dwarven milk** is an [extract](Extract.md) obtained from having a [milker](Milker.md) milk a [purring maggot](Purring_maggot.md), and has a value of 2☼. Dwarven milk can be cooked or made into dwarven [cheese](Cheese.md) (worth 20☼ per unit) by a [cheese maker](Cheese_maker.md) at a [Farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop.md). Using dwarven cheese as an ingredient thus creates incredibly valuable prepared meals. Further, some dwarves prefer dwarven milk or cheese and will get a [happy thought](Thought.md#happy-thoughts) from it.

Purring maggots are native to the second and third layers of the [caverns](Caverns.md) and, thus, are not available from the [embark screen](Embark.md). Currently, only wild purring maggots can be milked; if tamed they become useless for milk production.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[3670](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=3670)</sup>. [Modding](Modding.md) them to inhabit the first cavern layer will make dwarven milk available on embark and in dwarven [caravans](Caravan.md).
