*A [wood](../Main/Wood.md) **training sword***

**Training weapons** are [edgeless](Edge.md) [wooden](Wood.md) [weapons](Weapon.md) created at a [carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop.md). There are three kinds available; axes, spears, and swords.

[Dwarves](Dwarves.md) won't harm each other (or themselves) during [combat drills](Combat_drills.md), or [sparring](Training.md#sparring-and-demonstration) while using [metal weapons](Metal.md), so training weapons are only useful when you have no metal weapon of the kind you'd like your soldiers to train with, and in a few specific circumstances.

Training weapons are virtually useless in [combat](Combat.md), so make sure that your soldiers have exchanged them for real weapons before sending them into battle, even if it means using a weapon they are totally unfamiliar with. Their utter uselessness can prove useful for [live training](Live_training.md), however.

Note that soldiers equipped with training weapons for extended periods of time can grow [attached](Attachment.md) to their worthless wooden weapons. Congratulations - you now have a legendary stick.

Training weapons also used to have a few specific applications: [poor-quality](Quality.md) training spears were a prime component in [danger room](Danger_room.md) design. Training weapons were also ideal non-lethal equipment for your [fortress guard](Fortress_guard.md), when they still used their weapons to [seriously wound or kill](Fun.md) [criminals](Justice.md#crimes), including [mandate violators.](Mandate.md)

Training axes cannot be used for [cutting down trees](Wood_cutter.md). A bug that let dwarves do this (thereby freeing up [embark points](Embark.md#prepare-carefully), as weapon-grade metal axes are expensive) was [fixed in 0.43.03](http://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/dev_2016.html#2016-05-22).
