Forged by the gods themselves.

**Divine equipment** is a catch-all term for special, procedurally-generated [weapons](Weapon.md), [armor](Armor.md), and [clothing](Clothing.md) that can be found in [vaults](Vault.md). Divine equipment is used by [angels](Angel.md), who ruthlessly protect the vault's priceless treasures. Divine equipment is usually made from [divine metals](Divine_metal.md) and [divine fabrics](Divine_fabric.md).

### Traits

Currently, divine equipment is produced by copying the default properties of the "base" item, and adding an adjective ("bulging", "bulky", "segmented", etc.) or renaming the item altogether ("blade", "curved sword"). Dwarves in [strange moods](Strange_mood.md) which select from all items with a certain tag may produce one of these procedurally generated items. Since they retain the properties of their base items, divine equipment should be as usable as standard items of the base type.
