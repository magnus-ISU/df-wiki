<br> A **monastery** is a type of [religious](Religion.md) site, taking up a 3×3 square of tiles on an embark map. They are built by monastic orders, which are headed by an abbot.

They always have one each of the following structures: a dormitory which will be lined with beds, a canteen which will contain two rows of tables and chairs of wildly-varying materials, a warehouse consisting of a few empty rooms, and a [temple](Temple.md), a religious site in the shape of a multi-floor rectangular building with engraved walls and statues scattered about. The engravings and statues are predominantly religious in nature, featuring the [object of worship](Deity.md) and the ascension of custom-named [priests](Priest.md). On the bottommost floor, [pedestals](Pedestal.md) are kept for storing [artifacts](Artifact.md).

In addition to the main four structures, there will be a number of [shrines](Temple.md#shrines), statues standing outside, surrounded with varying patterns in the earth. There is a maximum of five shrines to a monastery, though it is currently unknown if the minimum number is 1 or 0. These shrines will sometimes have small altars beside them, which will sometimes contain divination [dice](Dice.md).

Monasteries may be the destinations of [pilgrims](Pilgrim.md), who travel there to see the site, or stop there on the way to [a more prominent holy place](Temple.md). They are also populated by [prophets](Prophet.md).
