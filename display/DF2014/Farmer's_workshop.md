The **Farmer's workshop** is the [workshop](Workshop.md) used for any [jobs](Job.md) involving processing [plants](Plant.md) or [animal](Animal.md) products. It is built from any construction material, and must be built by a [dwarf](Dwarf.md) with the [Plant processing](Thresher.md), [Milking](Milker.md), [Shearer](Shearer.md), [Spinner](Spinner.md), or [Cheese making](Cheese_maker.md) [labors](Labor.md) enabled. [Gelding](Gelding.md) also occurs at a farmer's workshop. This is not a selectable menu option: instead, any animals that are designated to be gelded will automatically be assigned a gelding job at a farmer's workshop.

### Menu

**Farmer's Workshop**

- (p) Process Plants - make [thread](Thread.md) from plants ([pig tails](Pig_tail.md), [rope reed](Rope_reed.md), [flax](Flax.md) plants, [jute](Jute.md) plants, [hemp](Hemp.md) plants, [cotton](Cotton.md) plants, [ramie](Ramie.md) plants, and [kenaf](Kenaf.md) plants)
- (l) Process Plants (Barrel) - with an empty [barrel](Barrel.md), turn [sweet pods](Sweet_pod.md) into [dwarven syrup](Dwarven_syrup.md) & [seeds](Seed.md)
- (v) Process Plants (Vial) - with an empty [vial](Vial.md), turn [valley herbs](Valley_herb.md) into [golden salve](Golden_salve.md)
- (c) Make Cheese - make [cheese](Cheese.md) from any [milk](Milk.md)
- (m) Milk Creature - with an empty [bucket](Bucket.md), [milk](Milker.md) a milkable creature
- (s) Shear Creature - shear [wool](Wool.md) from [alpacas](Alpaca.md), [llamas](Llama.md) and [sheep](Sheep.md) (or tame [troll](Troll.md))
- (S) Spin Thread - make [thread](Thread.md) from wool (see previous), or from hair from other creatures
- (b) Process plant to bag - with an empty [bag](Bag.md), process [quarry bushes](Quarry_bush.md) into [leaves](Leaves.md) and rock nuts
- *(n/a)* Make sheet from plant - make papyrus [sheets](Sheet.md) from the [papyrus sedge](Papyrus_sedge.md) plant

> > If a text is red then you lack the raw material to perform that task.

### Common Uses

- Turn plants into [thread](Thread.md), for [weaving](Weaving.md) at a [loom](Loom.md).
- Turn certain plants into [food](Food.md) and [extracts](Extracts.md)
- Harvest [milk](Milk.md) and turn it into [cheese](Cheese.md).
