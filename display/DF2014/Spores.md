"**Spores**" is an informal player term not found in the game, but often used on the forums. It can refer to one of two things:

> - Spores from [underground rivers](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/40d:Underground_river) (or [caverns](Caverns.md)) that allow underground shrubs and trees to grow in muddy areas.
> - In versions before v0.40.19, the ability of DF dwarves and creatures to "[breed](Breeding.md#breeding) by spores".
