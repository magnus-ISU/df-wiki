**Catacombs** are subterranean [human](Human.md)-made [structures](Structure.md) constructed to inter deceased members of their civilization. They are located in [towns](Town.md), accessible by traversing below [temples](Temple.md). Catacombs occasionally connect to [tombs](Tomb.md), which specially hold the remains of dead rulers.

Rock passageways and chambers are littered with [skeletons](Corpse.md). Some chambers contain multiple [coffins](Coffin.md) which house the corpses of historical figures, accompanied with grave [slabs](Slab.md) marked with information of the deceased. Loot, primarily [clothing](Clothing.md), are scattered throughout the floor. [Statues](Statue.md) can also be found. Knocking them down will not anger any gods; that must be done at the temple.

Various hostile and nefarious critters such as [kobolds](Kobold.md) can be encountered in catacombs. [Criminal organizations](Town.md#criminal-organizations) may move their headquarters to catacombs.
