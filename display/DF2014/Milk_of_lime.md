**Milk of lime** is a [stone](Stone.md)-derived liquid product used in the production of [parchment](Parchment.md).

### Producing milk of lime

Milk of lime is produced at an [ashery](Ashery.md). Any dwarf with the [lye maker](Lye_maker.md) [labor](Labor.md) enabled can convert one bag of [quicklime](Quicklime.md) into one unit of milk of lime. You must have an empty [bucket](Bucket.md) available to do so.

### Uses

Milk of lime's sole use is in the production of parchment. A dwarf with the [tanner](Tanner.md) labor enabled can bring a [skin](Skin.md) and a bucket of milk of lime to a [tanner's shop](Tanner's_shop.md) to produce a sheet of parchment.

### Real world

Milk of lime (or *limewater*) is a milky solution of calcium hydroxide in water. It has had a wide variety of uses since ancient times, including fresco painting, food preparation, and personal hygiene.
