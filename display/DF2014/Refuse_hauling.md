**Refuse Hauling** is a [labor](Labor.md) which has no associated [skill](Skill.md). A dwarf with refuse hauling enabled will haul items of [refuse](Refuse.md) to refuse [stockpiles](Stockpile.md). Refuse hauling is also used to haul items marked for [dumping](Dump.md) to a [garbage dump](Garbage_dump.md) zone.

All refuse hauling jobs are controlled by the "refuse collection" options in the [standing orders](Standing_orders.md) menu.
