**Miscellaneous object user** is the use of non-weapon objects in direct [combat](Combat.md), such as [clothes](Clothing.md), [shields](Armor.md), fallen allies, limbs, etc. This skill is sometimes found in [fortress](Dwarf_fortress_mode~2.md) dwarves after a tantrum, since they might've used a random object such as a sock to attack. Despite the strangeness, the mighty sock is able to kill. In [adventure mode](Adventure_mode.md), some players enjoy swinging heavy chests full of loot for massive blunt damage, or smacking an [elf](Elf.md) with their own severed ear for massive emotional damage.
