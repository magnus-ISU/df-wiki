> *A feathered person with the head and wings of a great horned owl.*

**Great horned owl men** are [animal people](Animal_people.md) variants of the common [great horned owl](Great_horned_owl.md), who inhabit most [savage](Savage.md) regions. They spawn in groups of anywhere between 5-10 individuals and are generally content to keep to themselves. In terms of size, they are a little over half the weight of the average [dwarf](Dwarf.md). All great horned owl men are born with Legendary [skill](Skill.md) in [climbing](Climber.md).

Like other savage animal people, great horned owl men can join [civilizations](Civilization.md), become [historical figures](Historical_figure.md), appear as [visitors](Visitor.md) and be playable in [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md).

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) great horned owl men for their *ear tufts* and their *piercing yellow eyes*.
