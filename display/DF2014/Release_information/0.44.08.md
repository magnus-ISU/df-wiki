> This should stop Linux from freezing. It also includes some other bug fixes that could be quickly incorporated.
> —[Toady One](../../Main/Toady_One.md), March 29, 2018

### Major bug fixes

- Stopped linux from freezing
- Fixed crash from demanding tribute from parentless site entities
- Stopped [artifacts](../Legendary_artifact.md) from moving out of backpack and similar when at old forts or lairs
- Properly stopped [imprisoned](../Jail.md) [dwarves](../Dwarf.md) from claiming positions in fort

### Other bug fixes/tweaks

- Made dwarves visit [museums](../Museum.md) properly
- Stopped every tribute rejection from being attached to your fort's history
- Cleaned tribute/spoil reports between forts properly
- Added [military tactics](../Military_tactics.md) and leaderships as relevant [skills](../Skill.md) for squad-leading [nobles](../Noble.md)/appointments
- Made uniform settings copy properly when pasting schedule orders
