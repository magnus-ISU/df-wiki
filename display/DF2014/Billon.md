**Billon** is an alloy of equal parts [silver](Silver.md) and [copper](Copper.md). Historically, billon was used to mint [coins](Coins.md), but it remains to be seen if billon coins will be useful when the [dwarven economy](Dwarven_economy.md) is re-developed.

Two billon bars can be made at a [smelter](Smelter.md) from 1 silver [bar](Bar.md) and 1 copper bar, which does not create an increase in value. However, eight billon bars can be created by smelting 1 silver-bearing [ore](Ore.md) and 1 copper-bearing ore. Since [tetrahedrite](Tetrahedrite.md) counts as both types of ore, two tetrahedrite nuggets can yield eight bars of billon for a roughly 70% increase in value over smelting the tetrahedrite nuggets separately. This recipe will only be used when no other silver-bearing ores are available to the [smelter](Smelter.md), otherwise [silver nuggets](Silver.md) or [galena](Galena.md) ore will be used as the silver-bearing ore, though tetrahedrite can still serve as the copper-bearing ore.

### Notes

Unlike both of its constituent metals, billon cannot be used to forge weapons or ammunition. Billon can be used to make moderately valuable furniture and other objects at a [Metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md). Be aware, however, that it is not [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md).
