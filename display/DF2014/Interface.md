The game interface is made up of several different parts. In the screenshot you will notice:

- **A**: The left panel is the main game area.
- **B**: The right panel displays the game [Menu](Menu.md) and indicating the relevant hot keys.
- **C**: The latters indicated new [Reports](Reports.md). Press r to view these reports. A red 'C' means combat, a green 'H' means hunting, and a blue 'S' means sparring.
- **D**: The number of idle dwarves and current [date](Calendar.md).
- **E**: The [z-level](Z-level.md) you are currently viewing. Above z-level counter column, at top right, there is a moon phase indicator.

You can press tab to bring another panel that shows a mini-map of your embark site.
