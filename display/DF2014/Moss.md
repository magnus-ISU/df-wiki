**Moss** is a special covering that appears on smooth stone outdoors. It is found primarily in [shrines](Shrine_(megabeast).md) inhabited by [bronze colossuses](Bronze_colossus.md) and [titans](Titan.md). It is also sometimes found in the [object testing arena](Object_testing_arena.md).<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/DF2014_Talk:Moss)\]</sup>

Not to be confused with [cave moss](Cave_moss.md), a special type of [grass](Grass.md) that grows in [subterranean](Subterranean.md) [soil](Soil.md).
