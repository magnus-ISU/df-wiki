An "Open space" is a [map tile](Map_tile.md) state that indicates there is nothing there - no floor, no walls, no creatures, absolutely nothing.

Open space map tiles come in two versions:

1. Normal open space: ·
2. Open space with wind: ▒

This distinction is normally not important. Just for a [windmill](Windmill.md) to operate, the tile above the central tile must have wind.

In both [Dwarf fortress mode](Dwarf_fortress_mode~2.md) and [Adventure mode](Adventure_mode.md), you can look at the world around you. A description of the tile being examined will be displayed on the right hand side of the screen.
