The **captain of the guard** is an appointed [noble](Noble.md) position in charge of internal security of the [Fortress](Fortress.md), that supersede the [sheriff](Sheriff.md). They have simple [room](Room.md) requirements.

Once a fortress has elected a mayor and reached 50 population, in [nobles screen](Nobles_screen.md) the position of sheriff will disappear and the position of captain of the guard becomes available, and in the [military screen](Military_interface.md) a "captain of the guard" [squad](Squad.md) (with no members yet assigned) will appear at the head of the squadron list. A captain of the guard can be assigned through both screens.

The captain of the guard leads the [fortress guard](Fortress_guard.md), which, alongside its regular military duties, aids in the enforcement of [justice](Justice.md) by investigating crimes, [capturing](Jail.md) criminals, and delivering beatings. As such the captain of the guard functions as both a sheriff and a [militia captain](Militia_captain.md). Once your fortress has reached a middling population, be prepared to dig out and furnish rooms for your middling captain to satisfy their additional room requirements. See [fortress guard](Fortress_guard.md) for why you might want your guardsman to be the *opposite* of a man of justice.
