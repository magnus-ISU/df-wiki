Bamboo is the only type of grass that [pandas](Panda.md) can feed on ([elves](Elf.md) will not mention this to you).

There are three types of bamboo in *Dwarf Fortress*:

- [Arrow bamboo](Arrow_bamboo.md)
- [Golden bamboo](Golden_bamboo.md)
- [Hedge bamboo](Hedge_bamboo.md)
