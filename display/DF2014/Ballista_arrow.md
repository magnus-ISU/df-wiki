**Ballista arrows** are, essentially, giant [arrows](Arrow.md), created at a [siege workshop](Siege_workshop.md) and used as ammunition by a [ballista](Ballista.md). There are two types:

- Simple wooden ballista arrows, which are made from one unit of [wood](Wood.md) at the workshop,
- And much stronger metal ones, which first requires the creation of a ballista arrowhead at a [metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md) from three units of [metal](Metal.md) bars and then final assembly with [wood](Wood.md) at the siege workshop.

The type of [wood](Wood.md) that is used for the arrow impacts its strength, where heavier types of wood such as [glumprong](Glumprong.md) or [blood thorn](Blood_thorn.md) are better. Both ballista arrowheads and the completed ballista arrows are stored in Furniture/Siege Ammo stockpiles.

For such a big, cumbersome, inaccurate weapon, wooden ballista arrows are surprisingly ineffective, dealing only comparatively minor amounts of blunt damage. Metal ballista arrows, on the other hand, are extremely lethal, and can kill two fully-grown [elephants](Elephant.md) standing in a line if the arrow hits squarely; any [goblins](Goblin.md) you hit will be smeared all over the space on which they stood.

Creating arrowheads is tedious and resource-consuming, and both [catapults](Catapult.md) and [ballistas](Ballista.md) train the [siege operator](Siege_operator.md) skill, at the same rate, when used. Since [catapults](Catapult.md) use [stones](Stone.md) you can literally pick up off of the ground, they're a much better practice platform for your operator, whereas a ballista armed with *metal* arrows is a much stronger actual weapons platform. As such, there's never much reason to produce wooden ballista arrows.

Ballista arrows normally have a range of 200 tiles, but impacting a creature will subtract an amount from that range based on the [weight](Weight.md) of the arrow: arrows weighing 50Γ or less (i.e. [adamantine](Adamantine.md) and every type of [wood](Wood.md)) subtract 50 tiles per hit, arrows weighing 150Γ or more (i.e. every other vanilla weapons-grade [metal](Metal.md)) subtract just 5 tiles per hit, and everything in between would scale linearly.
