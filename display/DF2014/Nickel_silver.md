**Nickel silver** is a [metal](Metal.md) produced from a mixture of [nickel](Nickel.md), [zinc](Zinc.md) and [copper](Copper.md). Despite its name, it does not contain [silver](Silver.md). Because the components of nickel silver can be forged into (much more valuable) [brass](Brass.md) instead, there is no specific reason to create nickel silver unless one of your dwarves [likes it](Preferences.md).

Smelting nickel silver produces 4 bars, meaning there is a net gain in value from 8 to 12.

Nickel silver is **not** magma-safe.
