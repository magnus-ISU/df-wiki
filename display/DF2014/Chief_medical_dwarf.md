The **chief medical dwarf** is an appointed [noble](Noble.md) position in charge of the [healthcare](Healthcare.md) of the [fortress](Fortress.md). They keep track health status, injuries, scheduled treatments and medical history of all inhabitants, and (hopefully) have the medical know-how to do something about it.

Appointing a chief medical (in the nobles screen) enables the fortress-wide [z-health screen](Health_screen.md) medical summary. The only relevant skill for the position is [diagnostician](Diagnostician.md), though it is not actually necessary for a dwarf to have any medical skills to be appointed to this position, or even for them to have the diagnosis [labor](Labor.md) enabled, though one may be inclined to turn their chief medical dwarf into a doctor for practicality's sake.

Notes:

- For more tips on healthcare, see: [healthcare](Healthcare.md)
- The [Noble](Noble.md) position has no requirements or quirks.
- Contrary to what the name suggests, citizens of any race can be appointed to the position of chief medical *dwarf*.
