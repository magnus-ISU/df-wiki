**Metalsmith** is a category [profession](Profession.md) that encompasses any or all skills relating to the [smelting](Smelting.md) of [metals](Metal.md) and [alloys](Alloy.md) and the crafting of various metal products, including metal [armor](Armor.md) and [weapons](Weapon.md), metal [furniture](Furniture.md), [chains](Chain.md), metal [toys](Toy.md), [buckets](Bucket.md) and [barrels](Barrel.md), metal [bars](Bar.md) or [blocks](Block.md), and so on. Although each of these may require a separate [skill](Skill.md) to produce, they are all grouped together under the [profession](Profession.md) of "Metalsmith".

<br> An adult [dwarf](Dwarf.md) whose best [skill](Skill.md) is in this category, and who has accumulated ranks in other metalsmithing skills\*, will be labeled with the "Metalsmith" profession and known as a metalsmith (whether or not they have the actual "Metalsmith" skill). If a dwarf has [experience](Experience.md) in only one metalsmithing skill and that is her/his highest skill, s/he will be known by that skill. If a dwarf has several metalsmith skills, once they achieve "grand master" skill (Rank 14) in one of these skills, but not in any of the others, s/he will then be known by that specific skill.

> *(\* exactly how much is not clear)*

<br> As the job requires "any metalsmithing skill" *(even if "Rank 0")*, metalsmiths are usually responsible for constructing any [architectural](Architecture.md) buildings that have been [designed](Architect.md) using metal bars or metal blocks. These buildings can include [trade depots](Trade_depot.md), [bridges](Bridge.md), [furnaces](Furnace.md), and [wells](Well.md). *(see ["known bug"](#bugs) below)*.

### Bugs

- Buildings which are built using material-dependent labors (e.g. a trade depot) may require "any metalsmithing skill," but will *only* check and raise the [metalsmith](Blacksmithing.md) skill, not any other qualifying "metalsmith" skill (e.g. weaponsmithing). The [quality](Quality.md) of any such buildings is based off this skill alone. <sup>[Bug](#bugs):[4899](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=4899)</sup>
