**Liquid fire** is an extract derived from [fire snakes](Fire_snake.md) at a [butcher's shop](Butcher's_shop.md) by a [dwarf](Dwarf.md) with the [animal dissector](Animal_dissector.md) [labor](Labor.md) enabled. It has no known use except as a [trade](Trade.md) good. 1 Fire snake will yield 5 units of liquid, worth 100☼ each, for a total of 500☼ per [vial](Vial.md). Extraction kills the fire snake.
