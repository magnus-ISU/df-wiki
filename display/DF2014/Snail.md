> *A tiny land mollusk with a large shell.*

**Snails** are a species of [hateable](Hateable.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) who may be found in any non-freezing [biome](Biome.md). They will cause unhappy [thoughts](Thought.md) on [dwarves](Dwarf.md) who absolutely detest them should they encounter one. They possess a [pet](Pet.md) value of 10, but may not be caught in [animal traps](Animal_trap.md).

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) snails for their *shells*.
