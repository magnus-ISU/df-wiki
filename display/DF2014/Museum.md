<br> A **museum** is a [room](Room.md) defined around a piece of display furniture, like a [pedestal](Pedestal.md) or [display case](Display_case.md). Museums can be assigned to specific dwarves, [locations](Locations.md) (such as a [tavern](Tavern.md), [temple](Temple.md) or [library](Library.md)) or left unassigned for communal enjoyment. An unassigned museum seems to function like a [meeting hall](Meeting_hall.md), where idle dwarves will congregate. Museums seem to also attract [visitors](Visitors.md).

Museums sized as rooms count as [sculpture gardens](Sculpture_garden.md).
