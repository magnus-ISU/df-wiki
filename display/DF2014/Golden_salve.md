**Golden salve** is an [extract](Extract.md) made from [valley herbs](Valley_herb.md), prepared by a [thresher](Thresher.md) at a [farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop.md) using the "Process plants (to [vial](Vial.md))" job and a glass [vial](Vial.md). Currently, golden salve has no use, save as a [trade good](Trade_good.md). One valley herb will yield five units of golden salve, worth 100☼ each, for a total of 500☼ per vial; processing valley herbs purchased from caravans (and thus coming in stacks of five) will fill each vial with 25 units of salve, worth a total of 2500☼.

Because golden salve is stored in [vials](Vial.md), you will need to search for vials when moving goods to your trade depot, and then look inside the vial to confirm it has golden salve.
