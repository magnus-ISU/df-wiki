> *(For workshop profiles, see [Manager](Manager.md#setting-workshop-profiles).)*

The **profile** is an important part of the [interface](Interface.md), giving you all the information you can ever need on a particular dwarf. The profile is accessible through u-v or v-z, and consists of several screens: the main screen, listing their holdings and owned objects, their thoughts and preferences, a detailed breakdown of their characteristics, their relationships, their health, their kills, and an option to customize the dwarf by giving them a nickname.

### Breakdown

#### Holdings and owned objects

This is the relatively-simple main screen, listing the amount of items the dwarf owns - usually 10-14 to start with, being the number of articles of clothing on their person - as well as their holdings in terms of [bedrooms](Bedroom.md), [offices](Office.md), [dining rooms](Dining_room.md), [cabinets](Cabinet.md), [coffers](Coffer.md), etc. (and, if they are nobles, their requirements in this regard). This is the easiest way to check on the quality of a dwarf's housing.

#### Thoughts and preferences

> *Main article: [Thoughts and preferences](Thoughts_and_preferences~2.md)*

The most detailed screen is the thoughts and preferences screen, accessible with Enter, which gives you a detailed breakdown of your dwarves' happiness and [thoughts](Thought.md), familial status, [civilization](Civilization.md) membership, age and date of birth, physical descriptors (and [wounds](Wound.md)), physical [attributes](Attribute.md), [preferences](Preference.md), mental attributes, and [personality traits](Personality_trait.md), in that order. All dwarves have two pieces of their profiles in common: "He/she needs [alcohol](Alcohol.md) to get through the working day" (indicating that all dwarves are alcoholics), amongst their personality traits, and "A short sturdy creature fond of drink and industry" at the end of the screen (this is actually the dwarf's tagline, and will appear at the beginning for all other [creatures](Creature.md)). Non-citizens have profiles too, although these are far less detailed, only giving physical characteristics, wounds, and their tagline.

#### Relationships

> *Main article: [Relationships](Relationships.md)*

This section, accessible with r, lists the dwarf's [relationships](Relationships.md) with other dwarves. The more relationships, generally, the happier they are, but also the more impacted they will be by potential deaths.

#### Kill list

> *Main article: [Kill list](Kill_list.md)*

A dwarf's k[ill list](Kill_list.md) is a record of what creatures they have killed ([slaughtering](Meat_industry.md) aside). Only dwarves that have actually killed things in hostile combat get kill lists, hence the option. Some dwarves arrive at your fortress with a few kills already under their belt. Although vampires draining others of blood count as kills, in practice their true kill lists are hidden and usually empty as part of their disguise.

#### Health

The last item is the dwarf's health status, a list of their prior ailments. It is functionally identical to the health [status](Status.md) screen, except individualized. It is only available once you have a [chief medical dwarf](Chief_medical_dwarf.md) with some [diagnosis](Diagnosis.md) skill, and will only list ailments they have suffered in their time in your fortress.

#### Customize

The customize option, accessible with y, allows you to give a dwarf a unique nickname and a custom profession. It has mostly two uses: giving your dwarves cool surnames and helping with identifying individual dwarves.
