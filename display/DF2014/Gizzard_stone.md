Native gold gizzard stone.

A **gizzard stone** (or [gastrolith](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastrolith)) is a rock ingested by an animal and held within the gastrointestinal tract for the purpose of grinding food or providing ballast. Gizzard stones are supplied by [butchering](Butcher.md) specific [animals](Animal.md) such as [ostriches](Ostrich.md) and [cave crocodiles](Cave_crocodile.md); the [material](Material.md) of the stone is randomly selected, and may include materials not otherwise available on the map. Gizzard stones are treated as cut stone [gems](Gem.md), suitable for [encrusting](Encrust.md) goods and furniture. Gizzard stones can be farmed as a renewable supply of [decorations](Decoration.md) to increase the [wealth](Wealth.md) of your fortress. Elk birds are among the more common creatures that drop gizzard stones. Oddly enough, numerous animals, such as the [giant kakapo](Giant_kakapo.md) and [emu](Emu.md), yield a [gizzard](Gizzard.md) but not a gizzard stone.

Creatures that drop gizzard stones on butchering:

- [Alligator](Alligator.md)
- [Beak dog](Beak_dog.md)
- [Cave crocodile](Cave_crocodile.md)
- [Elk bird](Elk_bird.md)
- [Jabberer](Jabberer.md)
- [Ostrich](Ostrich.md)
- [Saltwater crocodile](Saltwater_crocodile.md)
- [Roc](Roc.md)
- Giant variants of the above (when applicable)
