Some items have characters surrounding the name, which is a way to determine certain properties at a glance.

### Quality

**Sword** Ordinary

**-Sword-** [Well-crafted](Item_quality.md) *(dashes/hypens)*

**+Sword+** [Finely-crafted](Item_quality.md) *(plus signs/addition signs)*

**\*Sword\*** [Superior quality](Item_quality.md) *(asterisks/stars)*

**≡Sword≡** [Exceptional](Item_quality.md) *(equivalents/triple equal signs)*

☼**Sword**☼ [A masterpiece](Item_quality.md) *(stars/open stars/circle stars)*

See [Item quality](Item_quality.md) for a full explanation of how better qualities improve [value](Value.md) and [combat modifiers](Weapon.md).

### Improvement / Decoration

**«Sword»** Improved or [decorated](Decoration.md) *(much-less-than and much-greater-than signs/double less-than or greater than signs)*

Decoration can have a quality modifier themselves, leading to combinations such as:

**\*«Sword»\*** Superior improvement/decoration

**≡«+Sword+»≡** Exceptional improvement/decoration on a finely-crafted object

**\*«≡Sword≡»\*** Exceptional sword with superior decoration

These quality modifiers can be hidden in [init.txt](Init.txt.md) with \[SHOW_IMP_QUALITY:NO\].

### Wear

**xSwordx** *Showing some [wear](Wear.md)* - worth <sup>3</sup>⁄<sub>4</sub> *(surrounded by x's)*

**XSwordX** *Heavily worn* or *threadbare* - worth <sup>1</sup>⁄<sub>2</sub> *(surrounded by capital X's)*

**XXSwordXX** *In tatters* or *mangled* - worth <sup>1</sup>⁄<sub>4</sub> *(surrounded by double capital XX's)*

### Provenance / Ownership

**(Sword)** Not produced on-site (imported by traders or looted from raiders), does not count towards [Created Wealth](Created_wealth.md). *(parentheses)*

**{Sword}** Unclaimed/[Forbidden](Forbid.md) *(braces)*

**$Sword$** Owned by someone else. Only appears in adventure mode. *(dollar signs)*

### Other

**‼Sword‼** On [fire](Fire.md) *(double exclamation points)*

**◄Sword►** [Magical](Magic.md) (not actually implemented) *(forward and backward triangles)*

### Trivia

The longest possible item designation is **‼XX({≡«+Sword+»≡})XX‼**, which designates (reading from the front) that it's on fire ( **‼** ), in tatters ( **XX** ), imported ( ( ), forbidden ( { ), exceptionally ( ≡ ) decorated ( « ), and of fine quality ( + ).
