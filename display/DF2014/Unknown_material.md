> *Not to be confused with [unknown substance](Unknown_substance.md).*

**Unknown material** is a [placeholder material](Hardcoded_material.md) that is not legitimately obtainable in-game. It appears as a light gray solid with no name, which is only referred to as "unknown material" in [building](Building.md) descriptions. It has an internal [material ID](Material_token.md) of -1 or `[NONE]`, the same as used in tasks that require items of any or unspecified material.

Objects without a material can appear as the result of [bugs](Bug.md). [Doors](Door.md) and [hatch covers](Hatch_cover~2.md) in [dark pits](Dark_pits.md)<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[6776](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=6776)</sup> and [dead civilizations](Civilization.md#dead-and-struggling-civilizations)<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[3478](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=3478)</sup> are some examples.

### Trivia

- Since the material ID of -1 is used as a placeholder value for any material, items made of the unknown material cannot be specifically requested as an ingredient even in [modded](Modding.md) [reactions](Reaction.md).
- [Boxes](Box.md) made of unknown material are referred to as a "boxes and bag". This specific string is used in the [preference](Preference.md) "Urist McDwarf likes boxes and bags".
