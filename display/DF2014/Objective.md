**The basic point of Fortress mode is:**

- Build/[dig](Dig.md) a place for your dwarves to work and live
- Ensure survivability by providing [food](Food.md) and [water](Water.md)/[drink](Alcohol.md) for every dwarf
- Provide basic comfort by building [beds](Bed.md)/[quarters](Bedroom.md), a [dining room](Dining_room.md)
- Defend your fortress ([traps](Trap.md)/[military](Military.md))
- From the way Toady implements some features, have a lot of (twisted) [fun](Fun.md)
- Accumulate [wealth](Wealth.md)
- Build and sustain a bustling full 220-dwarf fortress (this is already a bit "optional")

**Most people prefer to do some/most of these steps:**

- Expand the fortress to have specific areas for specific purposes ([Bedrooms](Bedroom.md), [workshops](Workshop.md), [farms](Farm.md) etc.)
- Create various items for exporting ([Trade](Trade.md))
- Create a working [military](Military.md)
- Dig out the riches of the earth (mainly [platinum](Platinum.md), [gold](Gold.md), [gems](Gem.md), [adamantine](Adamantine.md) etc.)
- Create luxury for your dwarves and a generally grand and nicely smoothed fortress
- Make your fort self-sustaining and self-supplied
- Experiment with and experience the full depth of all game features
- Complete [megalomaniac construction projects](Megaprojects.md)
- Experiment with and/or discover [stupid dwarf tricks](Stupid_dwarf_trick.md)
- Kill elves
- Create the world's largest brewery
- Do it all over again with one skill-less dwarf in a [terrifying](Terrifying.md) biome
- Profit!

Ultimately, it's really up to your own imagination what you wish to do. After all, this game is an [open-ended game](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_gameplay#Open_worlds_and_Sandbox_modes). The possibilities are endless.

If you feel the need to play a more goal-based game, check out the list of [game goals](Challenge.md) for suggestions.
