> *In Dwarf Fortress, bituminous coal is not the same as "[coal](Coal.md)", an ingredient in many reactions – see that article for more information.*

**Bituminous coal** is found in [veins](Vein.md) in [sedimentary layers](Sedimentary_layer.md) and is one of the two mineral sources of [fuel](Fuel.md). When processed at a [smelter](Smelter.md) or [magma smelter](Magma_smelter.md), one unit of bituminous coal produces 9 units of [coke](Fuel.md#coke2c-from-bituminous-coal-or-lignite). If done at a regular [smelter](Smelter.md), this processing requires one pre-existing unit of fuel (either charcoal or coke), leaving a *net* production of 8 fuel.

Bituminous coal is flammable—if exposed to [fire](Fire.md) or [magma](Magma.md), an item made of bituminous coal will burn for the better part of a year before [wearing](Wear.md) away. Exposure to [water](Water.md) (including rain) will extinguish it, unless it happens to be stored in a [bin](Bin.md).

However, since its ignition point is above the cutoff for fire-safety<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[9795](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=9795)</sup>, it is considered a [fire-safe](Fire-safe.md) material and as such can be used for the construction of non-[magma](Magma.md) [furnaces](Furnace.md) and [forges](Forge.md).

Bituminous coal is **not** the same as ["refined coal" or "coal"](Fuel.md), though it is immediately related.

Because bituminous coal only costs 3 embark points, and produces a large amount of fuel when processed, it may be wise to bring some bituminous coal with you on embark as an early source of fuel.

It otherwise behaves as an ordinary stone. If you don't need to produce coke, you can use it to produce goods with a mason or stonecrafter. By default, bituminous coal is considered an Economic Stone and it will be reserved for coke production unless changed in the Stone submenu.
