Certain jobs generate **noise** which may disturb [dwarves](Dwarves.md) sleeping nearby. Depending on a job's noise level and proximity, your dwarves could sleep "uneasily due to noise", "very uneasily due to noise", or be woken up by noise. These events will produce unhappy [thoughts](Thought.md) in varying degrees.

Neither [walls](Wall.md) nor [doors](Door.md) will insulate dwarves from noise - distance and how loud a job is are the only factors determining whether a dwarf's sleep will be disturbed. Noise is produced when a noisy job is completed, but thoughts about noise do not appear until the sleeping dwarf has woken up.

Also, dwarves walking through a sleeping dwarf's room (even over their heads) will not cause noise, which is important for [bedroom design](Bedroom_design.md).

**Note**: Contrary to common belief based on [earlier versions](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Noise), [workshops](Workshop.md) do *not* currently produce any noise.

### Noisiness of various jobs

Only 5 jobs are known to cause noise. These include the following:

- [Digging](Digging.md) - 8 tiles
- [Wood cutting](Wood_cutting.md) - 8 tiles
- [Engraving](Engraving.md) - 4 tiles
- [Fortification](Fortification.md) carving - 4 tiles
- Removing [constructions](Construction.md) - 8 tiles

##### Visualization

Noise radiates across [z-levels](Z-level.md) in the shape of a cube, in all 6 directions (north/south, east/west, *and* up/down), so noise that travels 4 tiles will generate noise in a 9x9 pattern on 9 successive floors. The noise radiates from the center of the noise-producing things.

### Avoiding unhappy thoughts caused by noise

- Don't dig near bedrooms when there are dwarves sleeping in them.
- Smooth your bedrooms before placing the actual furniture.

### Designing in anticipation of noise

- Putting all your bedrooms 8 levels or lower below ground eliminates noise from wood cutting (*and all other relevant noise related concerns, that being beside the point*).
- Remember that [jails](Jail.md), [hospitals](Hospital.md), and sleeping [barracks](Barracks.md) should also ideally be kept away from noise, in addition to standard and noble bedrooms.

Facilities that *can* be safely built around or above/below bedrooms and other sleeping facilities, to separate the area set aside for the "quiet zone", include anything that does *not* make noise.

### In adventurer mode

In [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md), various noises that can be heard in unseen areas will be represented by exclamation marks of different colors. Double exclamation marks denote louder noises.

- ! ! ‼ ‼: Talking.
- ! ! ‼ ‼: Movement, such as walking or running.
- ! ! ‼ ‼: [Combat](Combat.md).
- ! ! ‼ ‼: Mechanical noises, such as from a [lever](Lever.md)-operated [door](Door.md).

Most wildlife doesn't make any noise in adventurer mode, with a few exceptions such as [capybaras](Capybara.md) and [kakapos](Kakapo.md).
