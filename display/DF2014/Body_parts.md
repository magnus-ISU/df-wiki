Almost all [creatures](Creature.md) in *Dwarf Fortress* possess a body composed of **body parts**, [ghosts](Ghost.md) being a notable exception. These bodies can be composed of [bones](Bone.md), limbs, [shells](Shell.md), [hooves](Hoof.md), [hair](Hair.md), [teeth](Teeth.md), and other pieces. [Corpses](Corpse.md), [tame](Tame.md), [trained](Trained.md), or [trapped](Trap.md) animals may be butchered at a [butcher's shop](Butcher's_shop.md), yielding these parts as individual items.

A creature may lose a body part in battle, it will then fly some distance away, and can then be [butchered](Butcher.md), yielding the materials inside that body part. These are then collected as part of the [butcher](Butcher.md) animal task, even though the animal is dead.
