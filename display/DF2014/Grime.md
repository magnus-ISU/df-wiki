**Grime** is a brown-colored [contaminant](Contaminant.md) that makes [water](Water.md) from [murky pools](Murky_pool.md) disgusting to drink. When in item form, the water will be described as "laced with grime". [Ice](Ice.md) mined from frozen murky pools will be described as "dirty ice", and will also be coated in grime.

Randomly generated creatures such as [forgotten beasts](Forgotten_beast.md) may occasionally be composed of grime.
