Illustration of Ruins

**Ruins** are [sites](Site.md) which will appear on the map when a player-made [fortress](Fortress_mode.md) is [abandoned](Abandon.md) to ruin rather than retired, or when sites belonging to any civilized race are abandoned or destroyed due to invading [armies](Army.md) or [megabeasts](Megabeast.md). These abandoned fortresses and other [sites](Site.md) will appear as μ on the world and [fast travel](Adventurer_mode.md#fast-travel) maps in both fortress and [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md). Any sites that once belonged to the player race (or the player him/herself) (normally dwarves) may be reclaimed using [reclaim fortress mode](Reclaim_fortress_mode.md).

Barring abandonment, ruins typically appear when a [civilization](Civilization.md) which is at [war](War.md) with another civilization doesn't `[LIKES_SITE:x]` the type of site that they have just taken over, leaving only a tiny few straggler-forced converts that can be slain by beasts. Megabeasts such as [forgotten beasts](Forgotten_beast.md) and [titans](Titan.md) also appear to frequently kill and/or drive out the residents of, and take up residence in, dwarven fortresses. This has the potential of making those reclaims exceptionally [fun](Fun.md) depending on the monster. Abandoned settlements and such may or may not be reclaimed by their parent civilization during either [world generation](World_generation.md) or gameplay.

See the [map legend](Map_legend~2.md) for more information on map symbols.
