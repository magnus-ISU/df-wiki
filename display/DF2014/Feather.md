**Feathers** are a raw material produced by [butchering](Butcher.md) feathered creatures. While in the real world, feathers have traditionally been used for decoration, insulating fill, writing instruments, and fletching for [arrows](Arrow.md) and [bolts](Bolt.md), there are currently no uses for feathers in *Dwarf Fortress*, making them a prime candidate for disposal via a [refuse](Refuse.md) pile, incinerator, [atom smasher](Atom_smasher.md), or the like.

Fortunately (or unfortunately, for those who prefer [modding](Modding.md) uses for feathers), a large number of birds aren't butcherable to begin with.

[Feather trees](Feather_tree.md) have feathers instead of [leaves](Tree.md#growths).
