**Hematite** is an [ore](Ore.md) of [iron](Iron.md), a mid-value [metal](Metal.md) useful for [weapons](Weapon.md), [armor](Armor.md), and [steel](Steel.md) production. Hematite is found as [veins](Vein.md) in [sedimentary](Sedimentary.md) and [igneous extrusive](Igneous_extrusive.md) [layers](Stone_layers.md), and is notable as the only source of iron available outside of sedimentary layers.

Hematite can be [smelted](Smelter.md) by a dwarf with the [furnace operating](Furnace_operating.md) [labor](Labor.md) to produce four iron [bars](Bar.md) per ore. Raw hematite can be also be [Cut](Gem_cutter.md) by a [jeweler](Jeweler.md) to produce cabochons (stone [gems](Gem.md)), however [studding](Stud.md) with iron produces four times as many [decorations](Decoration.md), with higher material [value](Value.md) as well.

'Hematite' is also the name of the fourth month of the dwarven [calendar](Calendar.md), and takes place during early Summer.
