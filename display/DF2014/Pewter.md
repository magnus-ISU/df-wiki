**Pewter** is an alloy, primarily containing [copper](Copper.md) and [tin](Tin.md). In *Dwarf Fortress*, there are three forms of pewter:

- [Fine pewter](Fine_pewter.md), made from one part copper and three parts tin, with material value 5
- [Trifle pewter](Trifle_pewter.md), made from one part copper and two parts tin, with material value 4
- [Lay pewter](Lay_pewter.md), made from one part copper, two parts tin, and one part [lead](Lead.md), with material value 3
