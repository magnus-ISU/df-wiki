The [corpses](Corpse.md) of [vermin](Vermin.md) are called **remains**. Unhappy dwarves in a macabre [strange mood](Strange_mood.md) will sometimes request vermin remains to construct their [artifacts](Artifact.md). Remains cannot be [butchered](Butcher.md), and are not otherwise useful.

Creatures with the [`HUNTS_VERMIN`](Creature_token.md#h) tag, notably [cats](Cat.md), will kill vermin, leaving the remains scattered around the fort. (Cats who have adopted a dwarf seem to drag the remains of vermin they kill to their ~~owner~~ slave). While this prevents unhappy [thoughts](Thoughts.md) from [hateable vermin](Vermin.md#hateable-vermin), it can also cause areas of your fort to become littered with remains if not properly [taken care of](Stockpile.md#refuse).

[Honey bees](Honey_bee.md), for example, will leave behind remains after stinging.

With time, remains rot away. Unlike corpses, remains do not cause miasma or leave [bones](Bone.md) upon full decomposition; they simply disappear. Remains are stored in a [refuse stockpile](Stockpile.md#refuse), subject to [standing orders](Standing_orders.md) refuse settings.

[Night trolls](Night_troll.md) have vermin remains on tables or in cauldrons, because they have the habit (`[HABIT:COOK_VERMIN]`).
