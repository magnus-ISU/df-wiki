A **maul** is a [blunt](Attack_types.md) [weapon](Weapon.md) that is essentially a very large [war hammer](War_hammer.md) (similar to a modern-day sledgehammer). Mauls are more than three times larger than standard war hammers, with a similar "bash" attack. Mauls also have a 10x larger contact area and greatly increased penetration, but cannot be swung with nearly as much velocity. Their great size grants them a large amount of momentum however, and they easily bend and mangle joints when striking limbs and other extremities.

Mauls use and train the [hammerdwarf](Hammerdwarf.md) skill. Being foreign weapons, dwarves cannot [forge](Forge.md) mauls, limiting supply to whatever specimens can be traded from human [caravans](Caravan.md) (which can never be [steel](Steel.md) ones, but can be of masterwork [quality](Quality.md) if you're lucky) or scavenged from invaders; although, it is possible that a dwarf in a [strange mood](Strange_mood.md) craft a maul as a [legendary artifact](Legendary_artifact.md).

[Due to the size of mauls](Weapon.md#size), most dwarves are unable to equip them. With height and broadness modifiers, some dwarves *should* be able to use a maul, and a select few might manage to do so one-handed. Unfortunately, due to a [bug](#bugs), mauls cannot currently be equipped by any dwarves, but they work really well inside a [weapon trap](Weapon_trap.md). Alternatively, you can grant residency to human [visitors](Visitors.md) and have them wield them for you.

### Bugs

In fortress mode, one-handed vs. two-handed checks are performed correctly, but can wield vs. can't wield ignores height and broadness modifiers, so dwarves cannot equip mauls.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[0005812](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=0005812)</sup> See [this forum post](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=119068.msg3790913#msg3790913) for details.
