**Driftwood** is a feature of [beach](Ocean.md) maps. It replenishes over time, and can randomly appear on any non-water tile in an [ocean](Ocean.md) biome. It cannot be harvested for [wood](Wood.md), nor can it be [smoothed over](Smoothing.md). Note that you cannot see the rock type of a driftwood tile while the driftwood is present.

Driftwood can be removed by building a [road](Road.md) or a Construction (such as a [wall](Wall.md)) on the tile, which will transform the tile into an ordinary tile of the base [rock](Stone.md) type. Removing the construction will leave an ordinary rock or soil tile. [Furniture](Furniture.md), however, can be constructed on top of driftwood without affecting it. Additionally, driftwood is immune to [dwarven atom smashers](Dwarven_atom_smasher~2.md). [Cave-ins](Cave-in.md) will only destroy driftwood when a natural [wall](Wall.md) lands on it.

Driftwood can appear when mining out a section of a frozen [ocean](Ocean.md) directly above a rock or soil layer (i.e. the ocean floor), but not in a [glacier](Glacier.md).

Driftwood is not destroyed by contact with [magma](Magma.md), but it is destroyed by casting in [obsidian](Obsidian.md).
