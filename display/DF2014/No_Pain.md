[Creatures](Creature.md) with the **`[NOPAIN]`** [creature token](Creature_token.md#n) feel no [pain](Symptom.md#pain) in (or out of) [combat](Combat.md). These creatures will suffer grievous [wounds](Wound.md) and continue fighting without pause, making them significantly harder to kill - many foes are dispatched by a blow to the head after they fall [unconscious](Unconscious.md) from pain. Luckily, creatures with this token are rare.
