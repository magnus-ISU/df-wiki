A **nest** is a structure found in the [lairs](Lair.md) of [rocs](Roc.md) (and any other egg-laying megabeasts with `[LAIR:WILDERNESS_LOCATION]`). If it contains any [eggs](Egg.md), they will be drawn in the center tile.
