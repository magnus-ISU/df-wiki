> *A tiny winged creature that hunts at night and rests in caves during the day.*

**Bats** are a species of [vermin](Vermin.md) which can be encountered both in [caverns](Caverns.md) and non-freezing above-ground [biomes](Biome.md). They can be captured by [trappers](Trapper.md) and turned into low-value [pets](Pet.md), though they are one of the species of hateable vermin; some [dwarves](Dwarves.md) absolutely detest bats, and will receive unhappy [thoughts](Thoughts.md) when encountering them. All bats are born with Legendary skill in [climbing](Climber.md).

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) bats for their *haunting cries*.
