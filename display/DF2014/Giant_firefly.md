> *A large monster in the shape of a firefly.*

**Giant fireflies** are the giant version of [fireflies](Firefly.md) that appear in [savage](Savage.md) biomes. They get to about the size of a [grizzly bear](Grizzly_bear.md) and stay that size their whole lives. They are generally benign but will cancel work orders and occasionally attack your dwarves if provoked. All giant fireflies are born with legendary [skill](Skill.md) in [climbing](Climbing.md) and as full sized adults.

Giant fireflies can be caught in [cage traps](Cage_trap.md) and [trained](Animal_trainer.md) into exotic [pets](Pet.md) with the standard pet value of 500. However, they don't live that long and are difficult to breed, so they aren't a great choice of pet. They do [butcher](Butcher.md) into a decent amount of [meat](Meat.md) and [fat](Fat.md) each, however. Since they are exotic [mounts](Mount.md) they can spawn as mounts during [elf](Elf.md) [sieges](Siege.md).

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) giant fireflies for their *enchanting glow*.
