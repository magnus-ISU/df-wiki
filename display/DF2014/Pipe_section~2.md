**Pipes**, or **pipe sections**, are used as components in the construction of [screw pumps](Screw_pump.md). You can't build pipes by themselves in the current version, nor connect them to form a conduit - to move [water](Water.md) or [magma](Magma.md) from one place to another, see [aqueduct](Aqueduct.md).

Pipe sections can be manufactured from [wood](Wood.md) at a [carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop.md), from [metal](Metal.md) at a [metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md), or from [glass](Glass.md) at a [glass furnace](Glass_furnace.md). A glass pipe section is called a **tube**. They are [stockpiled](Stockpile.md) as [furniture](Furniture.md).

It takes 1 [wood](Wood.md) log or 3 [metal](Metal.md) bars or 1 [sand](Sand.md) to create a pipe section.
