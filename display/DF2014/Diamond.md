**Diamonds** are particularly precious, carbon-based [gems](Gems.md) that are generally found in [gabbro](Gabbro.md) [layers](../Main/Stone_layers~2.md). The rarer diamond types are the most valuable gems in *Dwarf Fortress*, along with the [star sapphire](Star_sapphire.md) and the [star ruby](Star_ruby.md).

### In real life

Historically, diamonds were ranked among the five cardinal gems. The others included [amethyst](Amethyst.md) (no longer as valued due to New World deposits), [emerald](Emerald.md), [ruby](Ruby.md), and [sapphire](Sapphire.md). Its value has increased beyond that of the other precious gems since the 19th Century, most famously as a result of advertising campaigns.
