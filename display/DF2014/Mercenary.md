A **Mercenary** is a [historical figure](Historical_figure.md) who has decided to sell their services as a soldier. Mercenaries are found in fort mode as [visitors](Visitor.md), hired by civilizations in legends mode. You cannot actively hire mercenaries in fort mode; instead, they come on their own and sometimes they will petition for residency.

### World generation

[Historical figures](Historical_figure.md) will sometimes decide to become mercenaries, operating out of a town. Once they have enough money, they may improve their equipment. Mercenaries can be hired as scouts, for defense or for attack, as they sometimes may be hired for assassination and theft.

#### Mercenary Orders

Sometimes, mercenaries will start mercenary orders, a type of formal group that dedicates themselves to certain weapons, which the members train in, and receive titles based on their skills and accomplishments.

Mercenary orders are hired by civilizations for attacking or defence of a site. Once they have enough money, they will either upgrade the equipment of their members, or build a [fort](Fort.md). Mercenary orders either dedicated to a specific [deity](Deity.md) or, perhaps, linked to its [religion](Religion.md), may also build a [temple](Temple.md) in said fort. Mercenary companies usually only have a leader position, but sometimes will assign a position for [record keeping](Bookkeeper.md) as well.

### Adventurer Mode

You can visit the forts of mercenary orders in adventure mode, and adventurers can join them the same way bandits can be joined. (this requires a certain [reputation](Reputation.md), like all joining requests) After joining, you will then be able to receive [quests](Quest.md) (it is unknown if you can receive titles this way).

### Fortress Mode

A mercenary is a type of [visitor](Visitor.md). If you offer rented rooms through your [tavern](Tavern.md), they may try to petition for residence in your fort for the purpose of soldiering.

Sometimes enemy civilizations will employ mercenaries to bolster their forces, throwing a dangerous element into what would normally be a much less dangerous force.

### Behaviour

If you accept their residency request, they will essentially become members of your fort, appearing on your fort's civilian list as though they were new migrants. However, they will refuse to accept any assigned labors. Their only purpose is to be enlisted into your military by adding them to a squad, and they will then adhere to any orders the squad receives.

### Considerations

Because visitors cannot be made [nobles](Noble.md), you cannot have a mercenary be the leader of a squad. They will not arrive with their own armor, so you'll need to provide such equipment from your own supplies (they *will* bring their own regular clothing, at least). To forge equipment sized for non-dwarves, go to the workshop producing armor (this works for clothing, leather armor, and metal armor), move the cursor to the job producing armor, press details and select the race involved. You can find the desired race more easily by using the filter. This will change the produced armor's size to the selected creature's size.

Mercenaries of a larger race than dwarves, like the typical human, can more effectively use larger weapons such as [pikes](Pike.md) or [mauls](Maul.md). Also note that since only dwarves suffer from [cave adaptation](Cave_adaptation.md), non-dwarven mercenaries are better suited to fighting outside. Be aware, however, that non-dwarves also possess much smaller livers, and are more susceptible to [drunkenness](Drunk.md).
