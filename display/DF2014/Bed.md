A **bed** is a piece of [furniture](Furniture.md) manufactured in a [carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop.md) and then constructed on an indoor floor. A constructed bed is the base object used to designate [bedrooms](Bedroom.md). Once a bedroom is set up, it may be left unassigned (to be claimed by any roomless [dwarf](Dwarf.md)), assigned to a dwarf, or designated as a [barracks](Barracks.md) or a [dormitory](Dormitory.md). Dwarves gain unhappy [thoughts](Thoughts.md) from [sleeping](Sleep.md) without a bed and happy thoughts from having their own, high-quality bedrooms, so building beds is often a necessity for a happy fortress.

Unlike many other pieces of furniture, standard beds can only be created from [wood](Wood.md) (beds made of other materials such as [metal](Metal.md), [stone](Stone.md), [gems](Gem.md), or [bone](Bone.md) only exist in the form of [legendary artifacts](Legendary_artifact.md) created during [strange moods](Strange_mood.md)). If your fortress has no surface [trees](Tree.md), you will have to bring wood with you, or rely on [subterranean](Subterranean.md) trees or [trade](Trading.md) to get wood for beds. Manufacturing a bed requires one piece of [wood](Wood.md). To construct a bed query a [carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop.md), assign new task, build bed.

In the first season after [embark](Embark.md), your first dwarves will want to sleep sometime around 6 weeks after embark (around the 28th of the 2nd month by default), although the exact timing will vary between each dwarf. A beginning fortress might only need 4-5 beds for their initial 7 dwarves, although as time passes and their sleeping rhythms get more out of sync, that number drops. Although a fortress of 100 dwarves might only need a dozen or so beds to accommodate all sleepers at any one time, by the time a fortress reaches that size, some private [bedrooms](Bedroom.md) will, most likely, be a good idea, and bedrooms can *only* be defined from a bed.

It's not a big deal for a dwarf to "sleep in the dirt" that first time, but doing so will create a negative [thought](Thought.md), and it will take a while for that thought to go away. So, just in case more [bad thoughts](Tantrum.md) arise later for some unlucky emo dwarf, it's always best to avoid them whenever you can. You almost certainly have [better things to do](Quickstart_guide.md) than to dig out the perfect [housing tract](Bedroom_design.md) those first few weeks, so a few beds stuffed into some random corner should be more than good enough for your first seven.

### (De-)Construction

Because beds may only be built [indoors](Indoors.md), a bed on the surface will need to have a [floor](Floor.md) or [wall](Wall.md) built above it before it can be constructed.

Beds can be deconstructed even while a dwarf is sleeping in them, causing the dwarf to wake up. See [noise](Noise.md), however.

### Bed Assignments

To create a bedroom from the bed, first you must build the bed as furniture. Then query the bed, make it a room, and use +/- to adjust the size of the resulting [room](Room.md). If, after defining a bedroom, you leave the bedroom unassigned, the next dwarf without a bedroom to sleep in the bed will claim it.

To assign the bed to a specific dwarf, create the bedroom as above, then hit assign, and choose a dwarf from the list. **Green** names indicate dwarves that have not been assigned beds, while **brown** names already have an assigned bed.

You can (re-)assign or free (unassign) a bed, even if a dwarf is sleeping in it at the time. The change in assignment will take effect when they wake up. Each bed can only be assigned to one dwarf at a time, however, it is possible for one dwarf to be assigned to multiple beds. If, after you assign a dwarf their bed, you see two dwarves listed, don't panic - it's okay, they're [married](Marriage.md), and will share that bed. Married dwarves can sleep in the same bed at the same time, along with all 34 war dogs assigned to them.
