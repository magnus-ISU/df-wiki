**Peat** is one of the many types of [soil](Soil.md) that can be found in [soil layers](Soil_layer.md). Peat layers occasionally contain an [aquifer](Aquifer.md).

Peat

### In real life

[Peat](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peat) can be used as a home-heating fuel (after drying), and some places have power stations either directly fueled by peat, or by distilled [alcohol](Alcohol.md) made from peat. On geological timescales, buried peat eventually turns into [lignite](Lignite.md). It's also used to give Scotch whisky its distinctive taste. None of these uses are implemented in *Dwarf Fortress*.
