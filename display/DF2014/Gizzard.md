A **gizzard** is an organ found in many birds, such as the [albatross](Albatross.md). When butchered, it becomes a "prepared \<[creature](Creature.md)\> gizzard", which is an edible organ that can be eaten exactly like [meat](Meat.md) or other bodily organs. They have similar size to many other bodily organs, such as the [brain](Brain.md) or the [liver](Liver.md).

Some creatures with gizzards also provide [gizzard stones](Gizzard_stone.md) when butchered.
