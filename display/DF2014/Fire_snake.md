> *A serpent made of pure fire which can inject liquid fire as venom.*

**Fire snakes** are [fire-proof](Fire-safe.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) made entirely out of [fire](Fire.md). They spawn only around [magma](Magma.md) sites, which are defined as the original [biomes](Biome.md), and don't include any [channels](Channel.md) that carry magma away from magma pipes or magma pools. They may start fires, so locating [fuel](Fuel.md) or [graphite](Graphite.md) [stockpiles](Stockpile.md) near magma (or passages leading to magma) is risky. They will also inspire an unhappy [thought](Thought.md) if a [dwarf](Dwarf.md) that [hates](Hateable.md) them encounters one.

With a body temperature of 14000 °U, a fire snake is hotter than magma and hotter than the melting point of nearly all [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md) materials. It can melt or burn its way out of nearly any [animal trap](Animal_trap.md) (even [nether-cap](Nether-cap.md), though the trap remains unharmed), with the exception of [adamantine](Adamantine.md). Fortunately, like most other vermin, a [cat](Cat.md) will handily kill them, especially if pastured in the area of the infestation.

Although fire snakes spawn near magma, a dwarf on trapping duty can unwittingly transport a fire snake to an animal stockpile elsewhere in your fortress. Once set down by the [trapper](Trapper.md), a fire snake's high temperature can destroy the animal trap that holds it. Once free, a fire snake can cause all sorts of [fun](Fun.md) with your loose objects, including the destruction of [masterworks](Item_quality.md), leading to unhappy thoughts in their creators, and [cages](Cage.md), releasing any creatures held therein.

As vermin, fire snakes don't "spit" any sort of fire - the only thing they do is destroy wooden items that they 'step' on.

[Liquid fire](Liquid_fire.md) is a valuable [extract](Extracts.md) derived from captured fire snakes.

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) fire snakes for their *mystery*.
