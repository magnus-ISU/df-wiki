**Aquatic** [creatures](Creature.md) have the `[AQUATIC]` [creature token](Creature_token.md), and so are restricted to life underwater. An aquatic creature that is located in water less than 4/7 [deep](Water_depth.md) or removed from water entirely will air-drown; aquatic animals caught in such situations even briefly will be momentarily [winded](Status_icon.md). Usually such situations are the result of dwarven interaction, except in the case of [beached](Beaching.md) [whales](Whale.md). Note that aquatic creatures in [cages](Cage.md) (or [aquariums](Aquarium.md) for flavor) do not currently drown, unless they were drowning when they were placed or caught in the cage.
