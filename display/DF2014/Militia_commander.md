The **militia commander** is an appointed [noble](Noble.md) who serves as the senior leader in the [military](Military.md) command hierarchy of your [fortress](Fortress.md). They can be assigned at any point (from either the [nobles screen](Nobles_screen.md) or the [military screen](Military_interface.md)), and have no room [requirements](Noble.md#needs). The militia commander is only surpassed by the [general](General.md) in terms of military rank among dwarves who may inhabit a playable fortress.

The militia commander administrate your armies, allowing to expand your militia with new [squads](Squads.md) and assign [militia captains](Militia_captain.md) to serve as squad leaders, but is primarily a figurehead and grants no bonuses unless actively participating in military stuff. They also lead a squad of their own managing their troops assignments, participates in their training, and venture with them on [missions](Missions.md) like any other squad leader.

The militia commander position favors individuals with [military tactics](Military_tactics.md) [skill](Skill.md), which is used when squads are sent into missions.<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=DF2014_Talk:Militia_commander&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup> [Ambusher](Ambusher.md) skill is also useful for raids, where dwarves will attempt to avoid detection.<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=DF2014_Talk:Militia_commander&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>
