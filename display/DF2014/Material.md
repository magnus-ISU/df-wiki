> *For information on the physical properties of materials, see [Material science](Material_science.md).*

<br> **Materials** are the wide array of base resources from which objects can be created. Different materials have different base properties (including [value multipliers](Value.md#material-multipliers) which can significantly affect the [value](Value.md) of the item - a [gold](Gold.md) [throne](Throne.md) is worth more than an identical-quality [lead](Lead.md) one, for example).

Not every end-product can be made of all materials, and similar materials may have very different properties - for instance, [obsidian](Obsidian.md) is the only [stone](Stone.md) that can be used to make stone-based [weapons](Weapon.md), only [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md) stones will not melt when immersed in [magma](Magma.md), and [fire imp](Fire_imp.md) [leather](Leather.md) will not burn. While nearly all metals can be made into furniture and crafts, only a select few can normally be made into [weapons](Weapon.md) and [armor](Armor.md). These metals are generally called weapon-grade metals. This alone doesn't necessarily give these materials a higher base value, but it may make them worth more to you and your fortress in a practical sense.

Some dwarves have [preferences](Preference.md) for one material over another - they receive a happy [thought](Thought.md) when working with the material, tend to produce higher-[quality](Quality.md) results, and perceive items (or rooms) made of that material as higher-[quality](Quality.md), which can also give them more happy [thoughts](Thought.md):

Unib Rulushlikot likes [rhyolite,](Rhyolite.md) [silver,](Silver.md) [amber opal,](Amber_opal.md) [persimmon](Persimmon.md) [wood](Wood.md) and [giant emu](Giant_emu.md) [leather.](Leather.md)

General groups of materials include, but are not limited to:

> - [Stone](Stone.md)
> - [Wood](Wood.md)
> - [Metal](Metal.md)
> - [Glass](Glass.md)
> - [Leather](Leather.md)
> - [Cloth](Cloth.md)
> - [Bone](Bone.md)
> - [Shell](Shell.md)
> - [Gem](Gem.md)
> - [Wax](Wax.md)

Certain materials tend to be geared toward making certain items - while stone, wood, metal, and glass can be made into similar furniture, there are some items which are normally restricted to certain materials. For example, [beds](Bed.md) can only be made out of wood, and [bins](Bin.md) and [buckets](Bucket.md) cannot be made out of stone. Cloth and leather can both be made into clothing, but leather can also be used to make [armor](Armor.md). Gems are largely for [encrusting](Decoration.md) finished items made from other materials. Of over two dozen different [metals](Metal.md) and [alloys](Alloy.md), only a handful are usable to make [weapons](Weapon.md).

In all cases, each group of materials is divided into many possible sub-types of material; there are, for instance, 11 pure [metals](Metal.md), another 14 [alloys](Alloy.md), scores of different types of [stone](Stone.md) and well over 100 different types of [gems](Gem.md). It is best to look up each individual category, or particular material, to become familiar with its value and what it can and cannot be used for.
