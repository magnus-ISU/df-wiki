**Beaching** is a characteristically odd behavior carried out by [creatures](Creature.md) with a `[BEACH_FREQUENCY]` creature [token](Token.md). These [aquatic](Aquatic.md) animals will occasionally strand themselves on land, where they are immobile and unable to breathe. This is intended to model the poorly-explained real-life behavior of similar animals; see [cetacean stranding](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/cetacean_stranding) on Wikipedia for more information.
