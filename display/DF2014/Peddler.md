<br> **Peddler** is a profession for some inhabitants of generated [sites](Site.md), like [towns](Town.md). Some peddlers show up as [visitors](Visitor.md) in fortress mode, and [agents](Agent.md) sometimes try to pose as peddlers. Peddlers are [shown in grey](Unit_type_token.md#peddler).

<br>

### Fortress mode

Peddlers will not [migrate](Immigration.md) to your fortress in this version.
