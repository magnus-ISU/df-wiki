**Unconscious** is the state wherein a [dwarf](Dwarf.md) is unable to move or do anything of their own accord. It's noted under the basic wounds screen (view creature -\> wounds).

Dwarves can become unconscious for a variety of reasons, the most common, of course, is that they're [sleeping](Sleep.md). In combat, an injured creature can fall unconscious as well, mostly due to extreme pain from injuries. The amount of pain required to knock out a creature is based on its toughness attribute. [Syndromes](Syndrome.md) can also cause periodic unconsciousness as one of their symptoms. Unconscious is different from [Rest](Rest.md): dwarves that are unconscious will not heal from their injuries. Very often, attacks that hit bones will cause unconsciousness or worse if they chip a bone, due to the high amount of pain that [bone fractures](Wound.md) cause compared to other tissue.

Anything that falls unconscious can be caught in a [trap](Trap.md), including creatures normally [immune](Trapavoid.md) to them. This includes dwarves, who might decide to take their last nap ever on a tile with [10 Giant Axe Blades](Trap_component.md).
