The see-through kind.

**Glass** is produced at the [glass furnace](Glass_furnace.md) using [fuel](Fuel.md) with either [sand](Sand.md) (green glass), sand and [pearlash](Pearlash.md) (clear glass), or [rock crystal](Rock_crystal.md) and pearlash (crystal glass). A [magma glass furnace](Magma_glass_furnace.md) can also be used, in which case no fuel is required. Raw glass (of all three types) can also be acquired from some [trade](Trading.md) caravans and be purchased in the [embark](Embark.md) screen. Note that raw glass can ***not*** be made into glass items (excluding [strange moods](Strange_mood.md)), but can only be [cut](Gem_cutting.md) into [gem](Gem.md)-like items which can be used for [decorating](Decoration.md) items. You have to buy or collect [sand](Sand.md) to get the raw materials to make glass.

Glass value corresponds to the difficulty of its production, with green glass having the lowest material value (2), clear glass, intermediate (5), and crystal glass, the highest (10).

### Uses

Glass can be used for most things that can be produced at a [mason's workshop](Mason's_workshop.md), with the exception of slabs, querns and millstones. Glass can also be used to make [tubes](Tube.md). Note that the names for some glass products are different from the same products made from different materials. For example, a glass tube has the same uses as a wooden pipe. It can also be used to produce goods normally made at a [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop.md) by a [stone crafter](Stone_crafter.md), with the exception of obsidian short swords and generic crafts. In addition, it may be made as raw glass, which can then be cut as a gem at a [jeweler's workshop](Jeweler's_workshop.md) by a [gem cutter](Gem_cutter.md), in a similar fashion to cutting generic stones.

Raw glass, especially raw green glass, is excellent practice for aspiring jewelers (and its production provides experience for [glassmakers](Glassmaker.md)). The value of glass is on par with low-to-mid value gemstones, meaning that a source of sand on your map used in tandem with a [magma glass furnace](Magma_glass_furnace.md) makes decoration with cut raw green glass an essentially free, and very lucrative, way to increase the value of your goods, while training legendary [glassmakers](Glassmaker.md) and [jewelers](Jeweler.md) – particularly when those goods are themselves made of glass.

Glass can also be made into [blocks](Block.md) which can be used to build [workshops](Workshop.md) and [constructions](Construction.md), even glass [trap components](Trap_component.md). Unique goods made from glass include glass [windows](Window.md) and [vials](Flask.md), as well as [renamed versions](Furniture.md#furniture-types-with-multiple-names) of common furniture.

For more information on the use of glass, see the [Glass industry](Glass_industry.md) page.

Raw and cut glass storage options can be found under the Gem [stockpile](Stockpile.md) sub-menu. Glass blocks are stored in Bar/Block stockpiles.

### Glass and magma

Glass behaves somewhat oddly with [magma](Magma.md), despite being strictly magma-safe (with a melting point of 13600 °U) - **built** glass furniture will survive indefinitely when covered with magma, but **unbuilt** glass items tend to disappear instantly when submerged in magma.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[10314](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=10314)</sup>
