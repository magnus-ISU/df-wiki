<td>Movement
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td> 
</td>
<td>
<ul><li>82467913↑↓←→ - Move</li>
<li>Alt+(movement key) - Move carefully or enter dangerous terrain</li>
<li>Shift+(movement key) - Move in direction and upwards</li>
<li>Ctrl+(movement key) - Move in direction and downwards</li>
<li>&lt; or Shift-5 (num lock off) - Ascend</li>
<li>&gt; or Ctrl-5 (num lock off) - Descend</li>
<li>. - Wait for 10 instants</li>
<li>, - Wait for 1 instant</li>
<li>j - Jump. Sprint in movement options to run and jump.</li>
<li>h - Start climbing by grabbing hold of a wall or tree; mount and lead pets<small><sup><a href="../Category/Version_0.47.01.md">v0.47.01</a></sup></small></li>
<li>s - Stand or lie down*</li>
<li>S - Movement Speed and Sneak settings</li>
<li>T - Fast Travel</li>
<li>m - Open movement preferences interface</li></ul>
</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>*Use s to lay down, and then crawl past NPCs blocking your way.
