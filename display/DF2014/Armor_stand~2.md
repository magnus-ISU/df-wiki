**Armor stands** are a type of [furniture](Furniture.md). Much like the [weapon rack](Weapon_rack~2.md), the armor stand can be used to create a [barracks](Barracks.md). After being built (b-a), you may define the barracks dimensions (q-r, then +-). It can also be placed in a [noble's](Noble.md) [room](Room.md) to [appease](Requirement.md) them.

When used in a barracks designated for individual and squad equipment, dwarves will prefer to store their armor on stands instead of stockpiles. Doing so in concert with [weapon racks](Weapon_rack~2.md) will allow you to create centralized armories for your military.

Armor stands do not block dwarf movement.
