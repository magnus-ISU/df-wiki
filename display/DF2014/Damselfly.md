> *A tiny, colorful, long-bodied insect.*

**Damselflies** are a species of unremarkable [above ground](Above_ground.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) who may be found in [pools](Pool.md). They will not appear in areas during the [winter](Calendar.md), and can not be caught in [built](Building.md) [animal traps](Animal_trap.md), but can be caught with a "Capture a Live Land Animal" job if the dwarf happens to find one. All damselflies possess Legendary [skill](Skill.md) in [climbing](Climber.md).

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) damselflies for their *coloration*.
