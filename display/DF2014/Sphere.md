A **sphere** is an aspect where a being has influence. [Deities](Deity.md), [forces](Force.md), [angels](Angel.md), [demons](Demon.md), [megabeasts](Megabeast.md), [semi-megabeasts](Semi-megabeast.md), [forgotten beasts](Forgotten_beast.md) and [titans](Titan.md) may be associated with one or more spheres, and [civilizations](Civilization.md) may prefer certain spheres when selecting creatures to worship. There are currently a total of 130 spheres.

### Understanding spheres

A being such as a [deity](Deity.md), [demon](Demon.md) or [megabeast](Megabeast.md) may come to represent or epitomize certain things like emotions, qualities, terrain types, and items, which are called "spheres". As an example from real-world mythology, the Greek goddess [Athena](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athena) is goddess in the "spheres" of wisdom, warfare, handicrafts and reason.

Gods and adored megabeasts can grant secrets related to their spheres. In unmodded *Dwarf Fortress*, this means that death gods can teach the secret of life and death to their worshipers, turning them into [necromancers](Necromancer.md).

Dwarves and other creatures will come to worship certain "powerful beings" and, as such, will follow the "religion", and have a relationship with its "deity".

### Creatures with pre-determined spheres

While many procedurally generated creatures in *Dwarf Fortress* are given random spheres upon creation, there are others who have static spheres, which remain constant across all save files:

- [Bogeyman](Bogeyman.md): Misery, night, nightmares
- [Bronze colossus](Bronze_colossus.md): Metals, strength, war
- [Cyclops](Cyclops.md): Light, longevity, minerals, strength, thunder
- [Dragon](Dragon.md): Fire, wealth
- [Ettin](Ettin.md): Speech, strength
- [Experiment](Experiment.md): Deformity, night
- [Giant](Giant.md): Food, strength
- [Hydra](Hydra.md): Muck, rebirth, strength
- [Minotaur](Minotaur.md): Caverns, chaos, darkness, deformity, strength
- [Nightmare](Nightmare.md): Misery, night, nightmares
- [Roc](Roc.md): Hunting, sky, wind
- [Werebeast](Werebeast.md): Animals, chaos, moon, night

### Available spheres

According to Toady: "*Spheres have a parent/child list, a friend list, and a preclude list. A deity has either one or two base spheres, and then they pick friends of those spheres, while never choosing a precluded or parent/child sphere of any of the spheres they already have. I tried to be pretty lax with the preclude list so that interesting relationships could pop up.*"
