> *A tiny yellow bug. It has pincers and a stinging tail which can prove fatal.*

**Bark scorpions** are a species of [hateable](Hateable.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) found in [deserts](Desert.md) and a number of [tropical](Tropical.md) [biomes](Biome.md). They may sting [dwarves](Dwarf.md), leading to pain but no long-term consequences. All members of the species possess Legendary [skill](Skill.md) in [climbing](Climber.md).

Bark scorpions can be captured by [trappers](Trapper.md) and turned into [pets](Pet.md), but curiously have no pet value (making them be worth 0 dwarfbucks). An [animal dissector](Animal_dissector.md) can also make a [vial](Vial.md) of bark scorpion [venom](Extract.md) with a captured live scorpion.

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) bark scorpions for their *pincers* and their *stinging tail*.
