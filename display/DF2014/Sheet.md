A single parchment sheet.

**Sheets** are used in the production of [scrolls](Scroll.md) and [quires](Quire.md). They can be made from either paper or parchment, and are stored under Sheet (top stockpile category).

For more information, see: [Paper industry](Paper_industry.md).

### Paper

Paper is made from cloth plants mashed into [slurry](Slurry.md) (at a [quern](Quern.md)/[millstone](Millstone.md)) and then pressed on a [screw press](Screw_press.md). Both of these actions use the [papermaking](Papermaking.md) labor.

### Papyrus

Papyrus sheets are made directly from the [papyrus sedge](Papyrus_sedge.md) plant (which grows in any [tropical](Tropical.md) [wetland](Wetland.md)) at a [farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop.md). Note that the simple term "papyrus" can refer to *either* the plant *or* the paper.

### Parchment

Parchment is made from [hide](Hide.md) and [milk of lime](Milk_of_lime.md) at a [tanner's shop](Tanner's_shop.md). Parchment made from the [hide](Hide.md) of a [cow](Cow.md) is called vellum.
