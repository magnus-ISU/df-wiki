**Wool** is produced after [shearing](Shearing.md) (or [butchering](Butcher.md)) one of a [handful of animals](../Category/DF2014%253AShearable.md), currently [llamas](Llama.md), [alpacas](Alpaca.md), [trolls](Troll.md), and [sheep](Sheep.md). Animals can be sheared again to produce more wool after 300 days have passed. Wool is stored in a refuse stockpile under Hair/Wool and appears under [Body parts](Body_parts.md) in the [Stocks](Stocks.md) menu. Wool can be [spun](Spinner.md) into [yarn](Yarn.md) [threads](Thread.md) which can then be woven into [cloth](Cloth.md) at a [loom](Loom.md) and be used to create a variety of textile products such as [bags](Bag.md), [clothing](Clothing.md), [ropes](Rope.md) or [crafts](Crafts.md). *(For a full discussion of the process and possibilities, see [textile industry](Textile_industry.md).)*
