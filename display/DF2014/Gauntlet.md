**Gauntlets** are [armor](Armor.md) worn on any [body part](Body_part.md) that can [grasp](Creature_token.md#grasp), which for [dwarves](Dwarf.md) are the hands. They are similar to mittens and gloves, but act as an armor layer and provide much more protection. Like other armor, gauntlets can be made of [metal](Metal.md), [shell](Shell.md), or [bone](Bone.md). [Elves](Elf.md) sometimes bring [wooden](Wood.md) gauntlets in their [caravans](Trading.md).

Each crafting job takes one bar, and produces a pair of gauntlets -- one right-handed and one left-handed. The items from a single job may have different [quality](Quality.md) levels. A [strange mood](Strange_mood.md) will only produce one half of a pair of gauntlets.
