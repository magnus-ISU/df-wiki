### Standard templates

All articles in version-specific [namespaces](Namespace.md) (as well as Masterwork and Utility articles) should begin with {{[quality](../Template/Quality.md)|*rating*}}, followed by {{[av](../Template/Av.md)}}.

### Link templates

- `{{bug|issue ID}}`, for linking to bugs on the [bug tracker](http://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/mantisbt/my_view_page.php)
  - `{{bug|issue ID|link=1}}`

See also: [Category:Inline formatting templates](../Category/Inline_formatting_templates.md)

### Citation templates

{{[cite](../Template/Cite.md)}} is the "main" citation template, although there are other, more specialized templates as well - see [Category:Citation templates](../Category/Citation_templates.md) for a complete list.
