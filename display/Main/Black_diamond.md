**Black diamonds** are particularly rare [gems](Gem.md), similar to [yellow](Yellow_diamond.md), [red](Red_diamond.md), [blue](Blue_diamond.md), [green](Green_diamond.md) and [clear](Clear_diamond.md) [diamonds](Diamond.md) - they occur only as individual gems within existing [clusters](Vein.md) of [faint yellow diamonds](Faint_yellow_diamond.md), which are themselves found only within [kimberlite](Kimberlite.md), itself found only within [gabbro](Gabbro.md) [layers](Stone_layers~2.md). Correspondingly, they are among the most [valuable](Item_value.md) gems that can be found in *Dwarf Fortress*.

Like all precious and rare gems, they may also make up part of the walls of [unusual volcanic walls](Unusual_volcanic_wall.md).
