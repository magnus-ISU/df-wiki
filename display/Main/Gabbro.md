**Gabbro** is an [igneous intrusive](Igneous_intrusive_layer.md), [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md) [stone](Stone.md), which can form whole [layers](Stone_layers~2.md).

It is the only stone that contains [kimberlite](Kimberlite.md) (which, in turn, is the only stone that contains [diamonds](Diamond.md)) and [garnierite](Garnierite.md) (the only ore of [nickel](Nickel.md)). There are several [gems](Gem.md) unique to gabbro environs, as well.

Gabbro is also left behind by a defeated [gabbro man](Gabbro_man.md).

### In real life

Gabbro is the rock that forms layers on oceanic plates. Some variants are known by the trade name "black granite," mainly for countertops and gravestones. It is otherwise unremarkable to the layman.
