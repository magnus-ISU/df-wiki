**Pelagic clay** is one of the several types of [soil](Soil.md) that can be found on the floors of deep [oceans](Ocean.md). Not surprisingly, such layers occasionally contain an [aquifer](Aquifer.md). Despite the name, pelagic clay cannot be fired in a [kiln](Kiln.md).
