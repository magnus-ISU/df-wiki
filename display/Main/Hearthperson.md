# Hearthperson

A **hearthperson** is a [soldier](Soldier.md) that has pledged allegiance to a [local ruler](Quest.md#commander). In [Adventure mode](Adventure_mode~2.md), you can be a hearthperson, or invite people to be your hearthpersons. Various entity types have their own terms for this position or equivalents to it: **hearthperson** for [human](Human.md) civilizations, **fortress guard** for [dwarven](Dwarven.md) civilizations, **soldier** for the governments of occupied sites, and **lieutenant** for [criminal organisations](Boss.md).

### Becoming a hearthperson

In adventure mode, you may either start the game as a newly-minted hearthperson, or become one by simply talking to a local [noble](Noble.md) and asking to become one. If you have sufficient [fame](Fame.md), they will accept the request. Which noble you need to talk to varies for the different civilizations; lord/lady for humans, [captain of the guard](Captain_of_the_guard.md) for dwarves, etc. Most smaller sites, like hillocks, will not have such leaders.

Being a hearthperson has a number of effects, the most notable one being that you may request any number of [quests](Quest.md) from your leader, allowing you to easily build up [reputation](Reputation.md) in that leader's [site](Site.md). In addition to the normal reputation you'd gain as a [bandit](Bandit.md) or beast slayer, you will also build up the reputation of a loyal soldier to your superior. Being a hearthperson also means your loyalty lies with that of your leader, i.e. any [insurrections](Insurrections.md) against them are also against *you*, since you are effectively one of the leader's guards.

### Acquiring hearthpersons

You may have hearthpersons yourself by becoming a leader by [claiming](Claim.md) a [site](Site.md) with no conflicting dispute<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Hearthperson&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>. Killing everyone linked to a civilized site before claiming it might work, but isn't usually doable except for the smaller ones. Once you are awarded the title of "lord", "lady", or similar<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Hearthperson&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>, you may then start hiring guards by inviting them to become hearthpersons. Note that your hearthpersons are not your companions unless they already were.
