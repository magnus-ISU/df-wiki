**Flux** [stones](Stone.md) are classified as materials that can be used in the creation of [pig iron](Pig_iron~2.md) and [steel](Steel.md) at a [smelter](Smelter.md). All flux has a material [value](Value.md) of 2, which is twice that of common stone (material value 1), making it preferable for [workshops](Workshop.md), [construction](Construction.md), and almost all stone products. *(Only [obsidian](Obsidian.md) and some higher-value [ores](Ore.md) have a higher material value for stonework.)*

Note that [blocks](Block.md) made out of flux materials *cannot* be used in the production of pig iron or steel.

All flux stones form [layers](Layer.md) (which are marked on the embark screen as "Flux Layer(s)") except for [calcite](Calcite.md), which exists as small clusters within other flux layers. Whether or not a site has flux can be determined from the pre-embark screen. [Limestone](Limestone.md), [dolomite](Dolomite.md) and [chalk](Chalk.md) form [sedimentary layers](Sedimentary_layer.md), which can have large deposits of [iron](Iron.md)-bearing ore. [Marble](Marble.md) is a [metamorphic layer](Metamorphic_layer.md), so it can be found within almost any [biome](Biome.md); notably, marble can be found in the same biome as [igneous extrusive layers](Igneous_extrusive_layer.md) (i.e. near [volcanoes](Volcano.md)), unlike sedimentary layers.

Note that [borax](Borax.md), a real life flux, can not be used as a flux stone in the game.

#### Trading for flux

If your map has no flux on it (which is a common situation), then you can request it from the [dwarven](Dwarf.md) traders (assuming the traders have access to flux, which is not guaranteed\*). Make sure you order them from the 'Stone' section, since you can't use flux*blocks* to create steel. [Bituminous coal](Bituminous_coal.md) and [lignite](Lignite.md) might be worth ordering while you're there.

> \* If you are able to bring flux stone at [embark](Embark.md), you will be able to request it from the [caravan](Caravan.md) in meetings with the [liaison](Liaison.md) (as with pretty much everything else), but they aren't likely to bring much, generally 3 or 4 stones of each type. Your [steel](Steel.md) industry may be better served by requesting steel bars directly or by buying up everything they have that's made of steel, especially toys, instruments, and crafts, and [melting](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/v0.34:Melt_item) it all down.

### Calcium carbonate stones

All of the flux stones besides [dolomite](Dolomite.md) are also [calcium carbonate](Calcium_carbonate.md) sources, used to make [quicklime](Quicklime.md) and [milk of lime](Milk_of_lime.md) for production of [parchment](Parchment.md). The parchment production process therefore depletes non-dolomite flux stones, and you may wish to avoid parchment making if flux is limited.
