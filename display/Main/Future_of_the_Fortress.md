# Future of the Fortress

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Future_of_the_Fortress) &middot; revision 319005 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-12 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Main/Future_of_the_Fortress.wiki)*

---
**Future of the Fortress** is the name of a series of threads on the [Bay 12 Forums](Bay_12_Forums.md). Each month, [Toady](Toady.md) will round up any unanswered questions and answer them in a "Future of the Fortress reply". Other forum members answer questions if the answers can be found elsewhere or the question is a repeat.

### History

Future of the Fortress threads started out as discussions of the [development logs](Dwarf_Fortress_Development.md), called "Future of the Fortress" at the time. They eventually changed into a question and answer format.
