> *A small, pale arthropod found in underground streams and ponds.*

**Cave lobsters** are a type of aquatic [vermin](Vermin.md) found only in [underground bodies of water](Caverns.md). Cave lobsters cannot be caught by [fishing](Fishing.md), but instead must be caught in baited [animal traps](Animal_trap~2.md). Once caught, [processing](Fish_cleaning.md) it will yield an [edible cave lobster](Food.md) and a [shell](Shell.md) that can be used to craft or decorate items.

Some [dwarves](Dwarves.md) [like](Preferences.md) cave lobsters for their *beauty*.
