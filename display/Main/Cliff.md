**Cliffs** are a [terrain](Map_tile.md) feature, in which there is a sheer drop along the side of a [mountain](Mountain.md). In *Dwarf Fortress*, this is shown when the edge of one [z-level](Z-level.md) is not connected to a lower level by a [ramp](Ramp.md). This means that dwarves are unable to traverse between the z-levels without [digging](Mining.md), [constructing](Constructions.md) [staircases](Stairs.md), [climbing](Climbing.md), or [falling](Gravity.md).

Cliffs can vary widely in height, but the default world generation settings include an option to *periodically erode sharp cliffs*, which will limit the amount and height of the cliffs on the map, making almost all of the z-level changes connected by natural ramps.

Cliffs are a welcome spot for dwarven [suicides](Suicide.md), as well as combat accidents. Depending on your \*ahem\* *goals*, you might want to keep an eye on them, or even wall them off.
