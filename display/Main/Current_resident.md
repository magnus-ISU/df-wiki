When an [animal](Animal.md), like a [dragon](Dragon.md) or [alligator](Alligator.md), kills a humanoid during worldgen, it is named (same as during [fortress mode](Fortress_mode~2.md)). As a result, it will move into a [cave](Cave.md) and can be found by a player who embarks on said cave, or travels there in [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md). Instead of "**Wild Animal**" they will be designated "**current resident**". The same happens during adventure mode when you or your ~~meatshields~~ fellow adventurers are killed by a creature. They are not affected by [traps](Trap.md) and they are stronger than regular animals of their kind.<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Current_resident&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>

Other creatures like [trolls](Troll.md) or [giant rats](Giant_rat.md) that may actually dwell in the cave too will be listed as a wild animal, unless they are named.
