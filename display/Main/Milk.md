**Milk** is a product [extracted](Extract.md) from milkable [creatures](Creature.md). Milk can be made into [cheese](Cheese.md) or used in [cooking](Cook.md) [prepared meals](Prepared_meal.md). It cannot be consumed "as is". Milk is found under "Liquid" in the Stocks screen k.

Female versions of certain animals are milkable. In order to perform milking, you must have a dwarf with the [milking](Milking.md) labor active, a [farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop~2.md), an empty [bucket](Bucket.md) and a mature, non-[pet](Pet.md), milkable animal.

### Milkable creatures

The following creatures can be milked. However, animal women (despite producing milk) cannot be milked due to ethics, still milk from such animal people can be brought by merchants.

Queue a "Milk Creature" job at a [farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop~2.md). The milker will not necessarily pick the closest animal available. Caging, pasturing or restraining the milkable creatures near the workshop might be a good idea, but is not mandatory. The animal must also have milk available. Once milked, the creature will be free to roam. If the creature was previously caged, pastured or restrained, some dwarf will be assigned the job of taking it back. You can avoid these pasture animal jobs if the [farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop~2.md) is in the animal's pasture.

Milk should eventually be transferred from its bucket into a [barrel](Barrel.md) or [large pot](Large_pot.md) for long-term storage. If you plan to make [cheese](Cheese.md), it's a good idea to let the milk accumulate so that you get stacks of cheese (up to size 5) instead of single (size 1) cheeses.

An exploit to maximise embark point returns is to take 1 of each kind of milk - milk costs only 1 point at embark and comes with a separate barrel for each type, effectively giving you free barrels, and (after some work) stacks of 1 cheese from each unit of milk for your [prepared meals](Prepared_meal.md), worth 10 times what the milk is worth.

Milk from milkable animals domesticated by a [civilization](Civilization.md) can be ordered in [trade agreements](Trade_agreement.md) with that civilization. Each barrel of milk brought by a [caravan](Caravan.md) contains 10 units of milk.

The frequency with which animals are able to be milked is defined in the raw files with the tag `[MILKABLE:LOCAL_CREATURE_MAT:MILK:20000]`. All milkable creatures currently have this tag set to 20000 [ticks](Time.md), which, at 1200 ticks per day, means they all can be milked every 17 in-game days.

#### Work orders

A basic way to automate the "Milk creature" job is to set up a work order that "Restarts if completed, **checked monthly**." Then change the number of times the task is performed, equal to the number of milkable creatures. This will have to be manually counted.

It is currently not possible to assign a condition such as "Milkable creatures at least 1," because work orders does not support "creatures" as a valid item type.

#### Dwarven ice cream

Milk has a [freezing point](Temperature.md) of 10000 °U, the same as [water](Water.md). If milk is left in conditions below this temperature, even on a tile marked [inside](Tile_attributes.md), it will freeze, and become "frozen *{animal}'*s milk". Frozen milk is treated as a cookable solid <sup>[Bug](#bugs):[2787](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=2787)</sup>, and may show up as an ingredient in your roasts--at 1/10th of the value of cheese, if the roasts do not just melt entirely. There is no entry for frozen milk in stockpile menus, so dwarves will not haul a frozen milk barrel to any type of stockpile. <sup>[Bug](#bugs):[3398](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=3398)</sup> Careless attempts to dump the barrel to an indoor [garbage dump](Garbage_dump.md) may result in dwarves hauling the frozen milk and the barrel separately, resulting in a puddle of milk when it thaws. Dwarves will not make cheese out of frozen milk. For these reasons, milking industries should be set up underground, as Armok intended.

If your [trade depot](Trade_depot~2.md) is outdoors in freezing weather, any barrels of milk (or [lye](Lye.md)) brought by a caravan will be frozen and appear as empty barrels in the [Trading](Trading.md) screen. They can be distinguished from empty barrels by having a price 10☼ higher than the price of the barrel, or by viewing their contents.

To move frozen milk to an indoor garbage dump without spilling it, do not use the [Designations Menu](Designations_Menu.md). Instead, just mark the barrel for dumping.

### Bugs

- After finishing a milking job, the milker may generate a cancellation message if trying to take another job immediately: "Urist McMultitasking cancels job, handling dangerous animal". They will subsequently just leave the milked animal to wander away on its own and retask anyway.
- It is unclear why pets cannot be milked; this seems to be a bug because milking is a requirement for some animals and presumably the Animal Care dwarf would be aware of this and oversee their regular milking.
- Frozen milk melts while being cooked, leaving unusable milk items unside the kitchen. <sup>[Bug](#bugs):[2787](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=2787)</sup>
- Tamed purring maggots can't be milked. <sup>[Bug](#bugs):[3670](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=3670)</sup>
- After being milked (or attempting to milk them?), purring maggots are left inside the workshop as an item unless you assign them to a [cage](Cage.md). <sup>[Bug](#bugs):[3668](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=3668)</sup>
- Cooks will only cook milk and other fluids as a last resort, instead preferring to cook solid foods with solid foods. <sup>[Bug](#bugs):[2393](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=2393)</sup> For more information, see the [kitchen](Kitchen.md#bugs) page.

Dwarven milk does not in fact come from dwarves. This is generally a relief to human caravans, until they find out where it really [comes from](Purring_maggot.md).

Because milk is not considered a drink, dwarves will not drink it, even if they are dying of [thirst](Thirst.md). Indeed, dwarves consider any drink that isn't alcoholic to be no better than water, and refuse to drink milk because it's of better use as cheese. Another theory is that dwarves refuse to imbibe milk because it would make them puny milk-drinkers. What is for certain, however, is that unlike in real life, dwarves have yet to discover how to make alcoholic fermented milk beverages, such as kumis or kefir.
