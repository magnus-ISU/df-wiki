A **bed** is a piece of [furniture](Furniture.md) manufactured in a [carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop~2.md). Once built a bed can be placed with [![Ui b.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b1/Ui_b.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_b.png)b-[![Ui bf.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b0/Ui_bf.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bf.png)f-[![Ui bfb.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/5/5d/Ui_bfb.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bfb.png)b. A bed not contained within a [zone](Zone.md) will be used by any roomless [dwarf](Dwarf.md). Dwarves gain unhappy [thoughts](Thoughts.md) from [sleeping](Sleep.md) without a bed and happy thoughts from having their own, high-quality bedrooms, so building beds is often a necessity for a happy fortress.

[![Top to bottom: wood, stone, and metal bedframes. Once made at a carpenter's workshop, it will be complete with a pillow and a colored blanket!](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/7/70/Beds_v50.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Beds_v50.png)

Unlike many other pieces of furniture, standard beds can only be created from [wood](Wood.md) (beds made of other materials such as [metal](Metal.md), [stone](Stone.md), [gems](Gem.md), or [bone](Bone.md) only exist in the form of [legendary artifacts](Legendary_artifact.md) created during [strange moods](Strange_mood.md)). If your fortress has no surface [trees](Tree.md), you will have to bring wood with you, or rely on [subterranean](Subterranean.md) trees or [trade](Trading.md) to get wood for beds. Manufacturing a bed requires one piece of [wood](Wood.md). To construct a bed, click on a [carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop~2.md), click on "Add new task", then click on "Make bed".

In the Premium version of *Dwarf Fortress*, only the color of the bed's frame reflects the material used - the appearance of the sheets and pillows are selected randomly at item creation.

#### Your first beds

In the first season after [embark](Embark.md), your first dwarves will want to sleep sometime around 6 weeks after embark (around the 28th of the 2nd month by default), although the exact timing will vary between each dwarf. A beginning fortress might only need 4-5 beds for their initial 7 dwarves, although as time passes and their sleeping rhythms get more out of sync, that number drops. Although a fortress of 100 dwarves might only need a dozen or so beds to accommodate all sleepers at any one time, by the time a fortress reaches that size, some private [bedrooms](Bedroom.md) will, most likely, be a good idea.

It's not a big deal for a dwarf to "sleep in the dirt" that first time, but doing so will create a negative [thought](Thought.md), and it will take a while for that thought to go away. So, just in case more [bad thoughts](Tantrum.md) arise later for some unlucky emo dwarf, it's always best to avoid them whenever you can. You almost certainly have [better things to do](Quickstart_guide.md) than to dig out the perfect [housing tract](Bedroom_design~2.md) those first few weeks, so a few beds stuffed into some random corner should be more than good enough for your first seven.

#### (De-)Construction

Because beds may only be built [indoors](Indoors.md), a bed on the surface will need to have a [floor](Floor.md) or [wall](Wall.md) built above it before it can be constructed.

Beds can be deconstructed even while a dwarf is sleeping in them, causing the dwarf to wake up. See [noise](Noise.md), however.

#### Bed Assignments

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/d/d2/Bed_preview.png/300px-Bed_preview.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Bed_preview.png)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Bed_preview.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Bed_preview.png)

Not good enough for a noble.

Beds within a [bedroom](Bedroom.md) zone can be assigned to a specific dwarf by selecting the bedroom with the ([![Ui z.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/1/13/Ui_z.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_z.png)z) zone menu open, then clicking the dwarf head with a plus sign. You can choose a dwarf from the list. Dwarves who already have assigned beds will have "Has *Quality* Quarters" written above their name.

You can (re-)assign or free (unassign) a bed, even if a dwarf is sleeping in it at the time. The change in assignment will take effect when they wake up. Each bed can only be assigned to one dwarf at a time, however. If, after you assign a dwarf their bed, you see two dwarves listed, don't panic - it's okay, they're [married](Marriage.md), and will share that bed. Married dwarves can sleep in the same bed at the same time (along with all 34 war dogs assigned to them).
