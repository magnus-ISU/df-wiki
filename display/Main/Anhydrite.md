**Anhydrite** is a [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md) [stone](Stone.md) of base [value](Value.md) that is only found within various types of [gypsum](Gypsum.md). In real life, anhydrite is essentially dehydrated gypsum, and on contact with water will immediately hydrate and soften; however in *Dwarf Fortress*, anhydrite [floodgates](Floodgate.md) are just as effective as floodgates made from [granite](Granite.md). Oddly, unlike the gypsum in which it occurs, anhydrite **cannot** be used to make [gypsum plaster](Gypsum_plaster.md).
