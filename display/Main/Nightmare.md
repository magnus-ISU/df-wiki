**Nightmares** `Ñ` are procedurally-generated, [fanciful](Fanciful.md) [night creatures](Night_creature.md) summoned by [necromancers](Necromancer.md) and [demons](Demon.md)<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Nightmare&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup> to fight in battle, larger than [your](Dwarf.md) [standard](Human.md) [humanoids](Elf.md), but smaller than [titans](Titan.md) and [forgotten beasts](Forgotten_beast.md). They are short-lived, and neutral to creatures with `[NOFEAR]`. Nightmares summoned in fortress mode will be friendly. When summoned, nightmares will possess Talented [skill](Skill.md) in [observing](Observer.md), [fighting](Fighter.md), [wrestling](Wrestling.md), [biting](Biter.md), [striking](Striker.md), [kicking](Kicker.md) and [dodging](Dodging.md). They do not need to [sleep](Sleep.md), [eat](Eat.md) or drink, feel no [pain](Pain.md) (though still have nerves that can be [damaged](Wound.md), therefore affecting motor function), cannot be stunned, feel no fear, completely avoid [traps](Traps.md), don't need to breathe, or feel dizziness, nausea, have [extravision](Creature_token~2.md#extravision), and never get tired from over-exertion.

Graphically, the sprites that nightmares use will be of randomized sprites and colors that resemble the generated appearance they've been given. This same setup is used for other procedurally-generated creatures, such as [bogeymen](Bogeyman.md) and [experiments](Experiment.md).

Since they are procedurally-generated, their in-game appearance (which can be seen by spawning them in the [object testing arena](Object_testing_arena.md)) will vary by world, but will always end with: "Now you will know why you fear the night." This same quote is associated with [bogeymen](Bogeymen.md) and some other night creatures. By default, there are multiple nightmare types per world, and the number can be set in [advanced world generation](Advanced_world_generation.md#number-of-nightmare-types). Like many procedurally-generated creatures in the game, not only can their appearance be randomized, but the color of their blood. They are one of two creatures that can have pink blood, with the other being bogeymen.

As with all procedurally generated creatures how much of a threat they are varies greatly, however their large size and innate combat skills along with the possibilities of procedural generation means an adventurer can end up fighting something above a [semi-megabeast](Semi-megabeast.md) in threat. A [necromancer](Necromancer.md)'s ability to summon them is arguably their currently most dangerous ability, though the nightmare may also end up fighting their summoner's undead minions.

All nightmares are linked with the [spheres](Sphere.md) of misery, night, and - unsurprisingly - nightmares.

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) nightmares for their *unfathomably horrifying nature.*
