### Automated Desalination Plant

Removes salt from water, producing fresh water by pumping [salt water](../DF2014/Water.md#salt-water) through a [screw pump](Screw_pump.md) to a container made of constructed walls and floors with a [windmill](Windmill.md) powering the pump and ramps for easy access to water.

### Building Destroyer... Destroyer

Trap designed to affect [building destroyer](Building_destroyer.md) creatures. A [support](Support.md) holding up a constructed floor causes a [cave-in](Cave-in.md), which will harm the creature. You will need pressure plates or some other trigger, as well as a building as bait, as [building destroyers](Building_destroyer.md) will NOT target or destroy [supports](Support.md).
