*Not to be confused with the propel interaction available to [intelligent undead](Intelligent_undead.md).*

**Pushing** is an attack used by combatants too injured to use normal attacks, or by a [creature](Creature.md) who possesses no pre-defined [natural weapons](Natural_weapon.md).

Examples are [spine-damaged](Wound.md) dwarves who cannot otherwise attack, and [giant grasshoppers](Giant_grasshopper.md) who have no attacks. Pushing is always treated as a blunt attack, and, depending on the [size](Size.md) of the creature, can be either harmless or very deadly. A push from a large creature can break [bones](Bone.md) or [skulls](Skull.md) easily because of all the weight behind the push. [Giant sponges](Giant_sponge.md) in particular are well-known 'push-killers', because of their great size.

Note that, despite the name, a push doesn't actually move the target creature unless they dodge out of the way, or are sent flying from it - it's treated like any other melee attack. Push attacks cannot be aimed.

### Creatures who use push attacks

These creatures are incapable of attacking normally, and thus will use the push attack whenever they attack in combat.
