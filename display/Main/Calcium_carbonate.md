### Obtaining calcium carbonate stone

These stones can be mined out just like any other, but only form under certain conditions: Chalk and limestone form in [sedimentary layers](Sedimentary_layer.md) near the surface, while marble is [metamorphic](Metamorphic_layer.md), making it a common find in the deeper Z-levels. Calcite only forms in clusters within layers of limestone and marble. Layers of calcium carbonate can often be spotted at [embark](Embark.md) by looking for "Flux Layer(s)" in the site description - so long as the flux stone in question is not [dolomite](Dolomite.md), you'll have access to calcium carbonate very close to the surface. [Dwarven](Dwarf.md) [caravans](Caravan.md) can bring any of the calcium carbonate stones as trade goods, but will not bring them in very large quantities.

### Uses

The one unique trait of calcium carbonate stone is that it can be converted to [quicklime](Quicklime.md) powder at a [kiln](Kiln.md) or [magma kiln](Magma_kiln~2.md). This reaction requires an empty bag, as well as whatever [fuel](Fuel.md) is necessary to operate the kiln. However, all calcium carbonate stone also qualifies as [flux](Flux~2.md), a crucial element in [steel](Steel.md) production. Steel is unarguably the more valuable and practical product to pursue, as quicklime can only be used to produce [milk of lime](Milk_of_lime.md) and [parchment](Parchment.md), but generally speaking, access to multiple layers of calcium carbonate stone will provide your fort with enough material to operate both industries. Additionally, all calcium carbonate stone has a [value](Value.md) twice that of most stone, making it a good candidate for use in stone [furniture](Furniture.md) or [crafts](Crafts.md).
