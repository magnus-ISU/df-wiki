**Pig iron** bars can be created at a [smelter](Smelter.md) by a dwarf with the [furnace operating](Furnace_operator~2.md) labor activated.

The recipe for pig iron is: one [iron](Iron.md) [bar](Bar.md), one [flux](Flux~2.md) material, and one unit of [fuel](Fuel.md) will create one pig iron bar. Note that the fuel is part of the reaction as a source of carbon - even when using a [magma smelter](Magma_smelter~2.md) you will need one unit of fuel, and for a normal smelter you will need two, with the second unit being used to *fuel* the smelter. The flux material must be a [stone](Stone.md), not a block, and the fuel must be coke or charcoal, not wood.

Pig iron is mainly used as a component in the manufacture of [steel](Steel.md) but can also be used for buildings and constructions that allow bars and have no restrictions on what type of [bars](Bar.md) to use; however, it cannot be used to make any other products. It is possible to [stud](Decoration.md) other objects with pig iron, although the option isn't available at the forge, and instead needs to be ordered by the [manager](Manager.md).

Dwarves can have a [preference](Preference.md) for pig iron and will be happier in a room whose floors and walls are made of it. Keeping a few spare pig iron bars may be marginally useful for [strange moods](Strange_mood.md).

### In real life

Pig iron is a high-carbon iron that is an intermediate product in the production of wrought iron as well as steel. It is very brittle and thus, as in *Dwarf Fortress*, only marginally useful except as a step towards better materials. Its name comes from the traditional shape of pig iron castings, consisting of a central runner with ingots on each side at right angles, resembling a sow suckling a litter of piglets.
