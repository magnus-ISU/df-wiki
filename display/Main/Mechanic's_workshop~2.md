The **mechanic's workshop** is a [workshop](Workshop.md) which produces [mechanisms](Mechanism.md) out of [stone](Stone.md)\*. It is also used to construct [traction benches](Health_care~2.md#traction-benches).

> *(\* For mechanisms out of [metal](Metal.md), a mechanic must use a [metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md) or [magma forge](Magma_forge~2.md).)*

### Menu

**Mechanic's Workshop**

- Make [rock](Rock.md) [mechanisms](Mechanism.md)
- Make [traction bench](Health_care~2.md#traction-benches)

### Common Uses

- Make [Mechanisms](Mechanism.md) for simple [traps](Trap.md), [levers](Lever.md), [pressure plates](Pressure_plate~2.md), and to link such triggers to [doors](Door.md), [bridges](Bridge.md), [floodgates](Floodgate.md), [cages](Cage.md), more complex [traps](Trap_design.md), etc., etc., etc.
- Make [Traction benches](Health_care~2.md#traction-benches) to equip [hospital](Activity_zone.md#hospital) [zones](Zone.md).

### Notes

- [Traction benches](Health_care~2.md#traction-benches) require:
  - a [Rope](Rope.md) (produced in [Clothier's shop](Clothier's_shop.md)), or a [Chain](Chain.md) (produced in [Metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md))
  - a [Table](Table.md)
  - a [Mechanism](Mechanism.md)
- For non-dwarf fortresses, the name of this workshop will change to match if your fortress's primary race provides a custom value for the `[PROFESSION_NAME:MECHANIC]` token.
