The **chief medical dwarf** is an appointed [noble](Noble.md) position in charge of the [healthcare](Healthcare.md) of the [fortress](Fortress.md) and particularly in charge of the [hospital](Hospital.md), which cannot function without this position assigned. This [Noble](Noble.md) position has no requirements and will not make any mandates.

The only relevant skill for the position is [diagnostician](Diagnostician.md), though it is not actually necessary for a dwarf to have any medical skills to be appointed to this position, or even for them to have the diagnosis [labor](Labor.md) enabled, though one may be inclined to turn their chief medical dwarf into a doctor for practicality's sake. Although the name specifically states *dwarf*, any citizen can be appointed, regardless of if they're a dwarf or not.
