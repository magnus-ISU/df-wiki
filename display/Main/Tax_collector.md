The **Tax collector** does not exist in this version of Dwarf Fortress.

[Before it was removed from the game](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/40d:Tax_collector), the tax collector was a [noble](Noble.md) who collected taxes from your dwarves on behalf of the [baron](Baron.md), as part of the [dwarven economy](Dwarven_economy.md).

The tax collector was removed, along with the rest of the dwarven economy, in DF v0.31.01. [Toady One](Toady_One.md) has stated that the dwarven economy may return after sweeping changes are made, although such changes are ["a long way off"](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=169696.msg8451817#msg8451817).
