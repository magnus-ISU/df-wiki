# Ore

**Ores** are left behind after [mining](Mining.md) certain varieties of [stone](Stone.md). Ore can be [smelted](Smelter.md) to create [bars](Bar.md) of pure [metal](Metal.md) or combined to create [alloys](Alloy.md). Many ores are quite valuable as mined without being refined, and a [mason](Mason.md) or [stone crafter](Stone_crafter.md) can work them the same as any stone. However, ores fall into the [economic stone](Economic_stone.md) category, and so must be designated for non-restricted use in the Stone use tab of the Labor menu [![Ui y.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/c/cb/Ui_y.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_y.png)y. Others can be combined when initially smelted into alloys, while others must first be made into bars of pure metal and then *those* combined to create the desired alloy - see the [alloy](Alloy.md) article for details.

Most ores are [fire-safe](Fire-safe.md) material; some are also [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md).

Each normal metal ore "stone" produces four bars of refined metal when [smelted](Smelter.md). Metal ores with two possible metals listed below always produce 4 bars of the first metal, and have a chance of creating 0-4 bars of the second metal as well. [Adamantine](Adamantine.md) ore cannot be smelted directly; it must be processed at a [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop~2.md) first. Different rules apply to creating alloys.

Note that, in some cases, ore [value](Value.md#material-value) (given below) and [metal](Metal.md) value are not the same (bismuth, iron ores). Alloys, too, often have a higher value than the ores/metals they are made of. In most cases, furniture made from the ore is as valuable as furniture made from a pure (non-alloy) metal, while requiring significantly less processing. However, furniture produced directly from ore cannot be [recycled](Melt.md), and refining the ore will generally produce an additional metal bar.

In ASCII mode, nearly all unmined ores are represented by the £ symbol. Exceptions are native aluminum, bismuthinite, and magnetite (which appear as ^, %, and ~, respectively), as well as [cinnabar](Cinnabar.md) and [cobaltite](Cobaltite.md) (which appear as £ but cannot be refined into mercury or cobalt as they would be in real life).
