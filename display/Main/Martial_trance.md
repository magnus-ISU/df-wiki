[![Announce martial icon.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/a/a0/Announce_martial_icon.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Announce_martial_icon.png) [Dwarves](Dwarves.md) enter a **martial trance** when [fighting](Combat.md) multiple enemies at once. The trance offers a boost to the dwarf's abilities – a dwarf in a martial trance has a significantly increased chance of blocking or parrying an attack, and is more likely to hit their opponent. However, it does not give a direct damage bonus like **enraged**. The [status icon](Status_icon.md) for a dwarf in a trance is `!` which will flash over their [icon](Status_icon.md). A dwarf also appears to occasionally enter a trance even when not fighting, though this behavior is rare and may be a bug.

Dwarves are the only creatures which enter martial trances without [modding](Modding.md). The RAW tag which determines this is `[TRANCES]`. A dwarven adventurer may also enter a martial trance in [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md). Fresh recruits can, when pressed to fight, go into a martial trance as well.

This is also a great way to dispose of [bogeymen](Bogeyman.md) in adventurer mode, as you can dodge most of their attacks, and most of your attacks will land successfully, regardless of the difficulty of the shot.
