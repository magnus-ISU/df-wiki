**Magnetite** is an [ore](Ore.md) of [iron](Iron.md) a mid-value metal useful for weapons and armor. , It appears as large, oval-shaped [clusters](Vein.md) inside [sedimentary layers](Sedimentary_layer.md) and small clusters in [metamorphic layers](Metamorphic_layer.md) and [igneous layers](Igneous_layer.md). A magnetite cluster may sometimes contain [veins](Vein.md) of [native platinum](Native_platinum.md), which can continue outside of the cluster itself.

In terms of [metalsmithing](Metalsmith.md), magnetite is identical to [hematite](Hematite.md) and [limonite](Limonite.md), producing four [iron](Iron.md) [bars](Bars.md) when smelted at a [smelter](Smelter.md). However, magnetite is distinguished by the extreme size of each deposit compared to the other iron ores, for sedimentary clusters anyway. While a vein might hold 100 tiles of ore, a cluster can contain around 750 -- this is offset somewhat by the 25% lower chance for a [mined](Mine.md) tile to drop an [ore](Ore.md) boulder, compared to vein stone, but the typical yield of the entire large cluster is still much higher.

### In the real world

Magnetite is the most magnetic of all the naturally occurring minerals. It is often found in the form of [black sand](Black_sand.md).
