<br> **Treasure rooms** are procedurally generated reward chambers inside specific [sites](Site.md) (for example, [labyrinths](Labyrinth.md), [mysterious dungeons](Mysterious_dungeon.md), or [vaults](Vault.md)) and are always guarded by [megabeasts](Megabeast.md) or other overprotective [creatures](Creature.md). They may contain [artifacts](Artifact.md) (such as a [primordial remnant](Primordial_remnant.md)) or [masterfully](Item_quality.md#masterpiece) crafted [armor](Armor.md) or [weapons](Weapon.md). You may freely take what you want in these rooms after you survive the encounter.
