> *A tiny underground creature made of a mass of appendages resembling human fingers. It creeps across the ground like a starfish and eats with a mouth on the bottom of its body.*

**Creepy crawlers** are a type of [evil](Evil.md) subterranean [vermin](Vermin.md). They are found in deep [caverns](Caverns.md), and may spawn near [rotting](Miasma.md) [food](Food.md). They can be captured by [trappers](Trapper.md) and turned into low-value [pets](Pet.md). When the cavern layer they inhabit is breached, they'll begin appearing on the surface, typically wandering on top of [food](Food.md) or [refuse](Refuse.md) stockpiles.

Unlike all other vermin, creepy crawlers can be butchered for a small amount of [meat](Meat.md) and organs (due to *not* having `[NOT_BUTCHERABLE]`, `[MILKABLE]`, or `[CAN_LEARN]`). Live creepy crawlers, captured by [trappers](Trapper.md) in [animal traps](Animal_trap~2.md), are automatically lined up for slaughter at a butchery, but only if they have not been tamed yet. If killed, e.g. by a [cat](Cat.md), only unusable [remains](Remains.md) will be left over.

Some [dwarves](Dwarves.md) [like](Preferences.md) creepy crawlers for their *freakish wriggling*.
