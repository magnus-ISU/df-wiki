**Slurry** is a material used in [paper making](Paper_industry.md). Slurry is created at a [mill](Mill.md) or [quern](Quern.md) from cloth [plants](Plant_fiber.md) and pressed into [paper](Paper.md) at a [screw press](Screw_press.md). This also creates [seeds](Seed.md).

The labor associated with making slurries is [papermaking](Papermaking.md), under crafts - *not* milling, as one might expect from the building.

Slurry is considered to be a [glob](Glob.md) in the [Stocks](Stocks.md) menu.

Oddly enough, despite not being food, slurries are stored in the food stockpile as a paste.

Here is a workflow for making plant slurry:

- Build stoneworker's workshop ([![Ui b.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b1/Ui_b.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_b.png)b[![Ui bo.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/a/aa/Ui_bo.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bo.png)o[![Ui bot.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/bf/Ui_bot.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bot.png)t)

- Produce stone quern using stoneworkers workshop (input: stone)

- Build quern ([![Ui b.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b1/Ui_b.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_b.png)b[![Ui bo.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/a/aa/Ui_bo.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bo.png)o[![Ui bof.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/0/0a/Ui_bof.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bof.png)f[![Ui bofq.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/6/62/Ui_bofq.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bofq.png)q)

- Add plant stockpile near your quern.

- Have your dwarves gather plants.

- Set a work order for your quern to produce 'slurry' if fewer than 10 'globs' exist. Specify "Adj" to be 'paper-slurry items. (input: plants) (Note: You need a dwarf with the '[Manager](Manager.md)' role and an [office](Office.md) assigned to use work orders. You can do that from [![Ui n.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/7/7b/Ui_n.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_n.png)n Nobles and Administrators)

### Types of Slurry

The following plants can be turned into slurry:

- [Cotton](Cotton.md)
- [Flax](Flax.md)
- [Hemp](Hemp.md)
- [Jute](Jute.md)
- [Kenaf](Kenaf.md)
- [Pig tail](Pig_tail~2.md)
- [Ramie](Ramie.md)
- [Rope reed](Rope_reed.md)

### Technical info

Slurry is the `[SOLID_PASTE]` state of [plant fiber](Plant_fiber.md) and its `[SOLID_PRESSED]` state is the [paper](Paper.md) itself. The structural material has `[MATERIAL_REACTION_PRODUCT:PRESS_PAPER_MAT:LOCAL_PLANT_MAT:THREAD]` to tell the milling [reaction](Reaction.md) to have slurry as the [product](Reaction.md#material-reaction-product), and the slurry has [class](Reaction.md#reaction-classes-and-products) `[REACTION_CLASS:PAPER_SLURRY]` to turn the [glob](Glob.md) into paper of the same material.

### Bugs

- Slurry stored on the floor in food stockpile (not in a barrel/pot) will trigger a [Clean job](Cleaning.md), resulting in the glob being destroyed.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[9884](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=9884)</sup> As a work-around, one can use the custom stockpile feature to disable paste from being stored in food stockpiles, and the slurry will remain safely in the quern/millstone until need at a screw press.
