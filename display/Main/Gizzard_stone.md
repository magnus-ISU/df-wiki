A **gizzard stone** is a rock obtainable by [butchering](Butcher.md) or slaughtering specific [animals](Animal.md), usually large birds or reptiles. The task to use them for encrusting furniture, ammo, or finished goods is "[Encrust](Encrust.md) (x) with polished stones". The [material](Material.md) of the gem is a randomly selected type of [stone](Stone.md), and may include materials not otherwise available on the map. Gizzard stones are stored in stockpiles as Gems / Cut stone. Not all [creatures](Creature.md) with a [gizzard](Gizzard.md) [body part](Body_parts.md) drop gizzard stones when butchered, however.

Creatures that drop gizzard stones on butchering:

- [Alligators](Alligator.md) and [giant alligators](Giant_alligator.md)
- [Beak dogs](Beak_dog.md)
- [Cave crocodiles](Cave_crocodile.md)
- [Elk birds](Elk_bird.md)
- [Jabberers](Jabberer.md)
- [Ostriches](Ostrich.md) and [giant ostriches](Giant_ostrich.md)
- [Saltwater crocodiles](Saltwater_crocodile.md) and [giant saltwater crocodiles](Giant_saltwater_crocodile.md)
- [Rocs](Roc.md)

Note: many dinosaurs introduced in the Dino Update, such as [therizinosaurus](Therizinosaurus.md) drop gizzard stones too. More info is necessary

All of these animals can be [tamed](Animal_Training.md) and bred as part of your [meat industry](Meat_industry.md), and the gizzard stones can then be used in your [gem industry](Gem_industry~2.md).

### In real life

Gizzard stones, also known as [gastroliths](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gastrolith), are rocks or sand in the digestive tract to help grind up food, serving in that role instead of teeth. Animals that use gastroliths for digestion often have a special muscular stomach, called the gizzard. Gastroliths are particularly common in birds and reptiles, which lack appropriate teeth for grinding food, but they're also found in seals, sea lions, fish, and invertebrates. In aquatic animals, the stones can also serve as ballast.
