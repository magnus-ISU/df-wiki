**Size** is a measure of how big a [creature](Creature.md) or [item](Item.md) is, as volume in cubic centimeters<sup>[\[1\]](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=30026.msg831163#msg831163)</sup>, and called `[BODY_SIZE]` or `[SIZE]` in the [raw files](Raw_file.md).

Size has many important effects on the game, many through its direct effect on item [weight](Weight.md), one of the many [material properties](Material_science.md) in Dwarf Fortress. Size directly affects such things as [which weapons](Weapon.md#size) your dwarves can equip, [butchering](Butcher.md) returns, [storage](Storage.md) limits, and [combat](Combat.md) effectiveness for both creatures and weapons.

As far as in-game location and movement, size does *not* matter; one item or creature always is located in just one tile, regardless of size. If a [path](Path.md) is open to a dwarf, it's open to every other creature in the game, no matter how large. A thousand full grown [dragons](Dragon.md) can occupy a single square (so long as [999 of them are lying down](http://www.bay12games.com/media/b12_call_1_transcript.html)), and a common [rabbit](Rabbit.md) blocks a [door](Door.md) or fills a [cage](Cage.md) as completely as a gigantic [bronze colossus](Bronze_colossus.md).

### Game mechanics

Size is used to calculate an item's weight, along with the [density](Density.md) of the underlying material(s):

> Weight (in Γ) = Density (in kg/m<sup>3</sup>) \* Size (in cm<sup>3</sup>) / 1,000,000 (cm<sup>3</sup> in a m<sup>3</sup>). Through weight, the size of an item has further ramifications in the game, such as [hauling](Hauling.md) speed, [pressure plate](Pressure_plate~2.md) activation, impact momentum, weight restrictions, and so forth.

Internally, all custom size numbers are rounded down to the nearest multiple of 10 - thus, if you define an item with `[SIZE:15]`, it will actually behave as if you had specified `[SIZE:10]`.<sup>[\[2\]](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=169696.msg8300987#msg8300987)</sup>

The weight of [creatures](Creature.md) is calculated from the densities and sizes of the layers of their body parts, which currently results in corpse weights that are about 1/3 heavier than expected.

### Buildings

The `[DIM]` [building token](Building_token.md) defines the size of a [workshop](Workshop.md) or [building](Building.md) in [tiles](Tile.md). This differs from creatures and items in that a building doesn't have a weight, and it's not in cm³, of course.

### Creatures

When it comes to creatures, size is a rough stand-in for [weight](Weight.md) since standard flesh weighs about one gram per cubic centimeter. However, in the typical complexity of *Dwarf Fortress*, there are a number of *other* [materials](Material.md) animals can include ([ivory](Ivory.md), [hair](Hair.md), [horn](Horn.md), [shell](Shell.md), etc.) which have their own densities, shifting a creature's actual weight relative to its size, sometimes significantly (elephant tusks weigh a *lot*). Creature size is determined by `[BODY_SIZE]` tokens, often with multiple tokens to set their base size at certain ages.

Bodysize determines several things:

- Average [butchering](Butcher.md) yields.
- How much [damage](Damage.md) they can absorb (along with morphology).
- How much damage they can inflict in melee (along with morphology and attack definition [tokens](Creature_token~2.md)).
- For creatures that can wear equipment (`[EQUIPS]`).

> - What size of equipment a creature can wear; clothing and armor are sized for a specific species and only creatures with a species base size near that size can wear them, an individual creature's actual size does not matter for this.
> - Weapons have a minimum size that a creature must be to wield them (`[MINIMUM_SIZE]` and `[TWO_HANDED]`), tissue layer thickness not included, see below.

The actual size of an individual creature is the result of different effects:

- The base BODY_SIZE for the species of creature.
- The [age](Age.md) of the creature; most creatures are born at minimum size and grow to a maximum.

> - Some, like dragons and most species of snake, grow throughout their entire lifetime and may not live long enough to reach the maximum.

- Inheritance; many creatures have `[BODY_APPEARANCE_MODIFIER]`) tokens that allow them to vary in height, width, or length, which they can maybe [pass on](Genetics.md) to children.

The following add to size, but are not included when determining how weapons are wielded.

- Muscle mass, determined by its [strength](Strength.md) attribute (a thin dwarf with ~44210 size will be ~64210 once they become unbelievably strong), due to muscle having `[THICKENS_ON_STRENGTH]`.
- Fat mass, due to `[THICKENS_ON_ENERGY_STORAGE]`.<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Size&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>

Creature sizes range from 1 (small insect [vermin](Vermin.md)) to 200,000,000 ([giant sperm whales](Giant_sperm_whale.md), the largest creature in the game). See [List of creatures by adult size](List_of_creatures_by_adult_size.md) for details.
