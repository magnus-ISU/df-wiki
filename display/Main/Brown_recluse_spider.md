> *A tiny brown bug known for its powerful poison.*

**Brown recluse spiders** are [hateable](Hateable.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) that, on top of giving [unhappy thoughts](Thought.md#unhappy-thoughts) to [dwarves](Dwarf.md) who detest them, are very [venomous](Syndrome.md). Brown recluse spider bites cause immediate nausea, fever, and pain, and a short time later result in severe localized necrosis. They are also much faster than your average [rat](Rat.md). Thankfully, the spiders don't seek the vicinity of dwarven settlements and even when a dwarf encounters a spider, this will almost never result in a bite.

Brown recluse spiders spin webs which can be collected by [weavers](Weaver.md) for the production of [silk](Silk.md). In their native biomes, brown recluse spider silk can be an important resource of the [textile industry](Textile_industry.md).

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) brown recluse spiders for their *venomous bite*.
