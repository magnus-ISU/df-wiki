> *For the month, see [Calendar](Calendar.md).*

**Granite** is an [igneous intrusive](Igneous_intrusive_layer.md) [stone](Stone.md) that can contain a large variety of [gems](Gem.md) and [ores](Ore.md), as well as many kinds of [stone](Stone.md) [clusters](Vein.md). It can form [layers](Stone_layers~2.md), but, unfortunately, isn't [magma safe](Magma_safe.md), and is also the name of the first month of the dwarven [calendar](Calendar.md), covering early Spring.

### In real life

Granite is one of the most common stones on Earth, as the base of all the continental crust is made of it. Granite can sometimes be found in outcrops where the rocks above the granite have eroded away.
