# Intro movie

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Intro_movie) &middot; revision 315814 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-12 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Main/Intro_movie.wiki)*

---
The **intro movie** to *Dwarf Fortress* played with old versions when the game was opened, unless it was [disabled](../DF2014/Technical_tricks.md#intro-movie).

### Opening credits

First, the [Bay 12 Games](Bay_12_Games.md) logo is displayed. Second, the Bay 12 Games motto (*Beyond Quality*) appears. In the next scene *A [Toady One](Toady_One.md) The Great Production* is pulled across the screen on a wheeled platform by a dwarf.

[![Intro movie 1.gif](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/9/9d/Intro_movie_1.gif)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Intro_movie_1.gif)

<br>

### Title

The camera pans down from a blue sky to the entrance to a dwarven mountainhome. The title *Slaves to Armok: God of Blood Chapter II: Dwarf Fortress* appears above the entrance. Dwarves can be seen on the ground, and one enters the mountainhome.

[![Intro movie 2.gif](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/9/91/Intro_movie_2.gif)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Intro_movie_2.gif)

<br>

### Blacksmith

The camera enters the mountainhome and finds its way to a blacksmith using an anvil and hammer to form a glowing-hot piece of metal.

[![Intro movie 3.gif](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/0/02/Intro_movie_3.gif)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Intro_movie_3.gif)

<br>

### Cave River

The camera then pans to the left and enters a door leading to a bridge that crosses the [cave river](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Cave_river).

[![Intro movie 4.gif](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/9/94/Intro_movie_4.gif)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Intro_movie_4.gif)

<br>

### Sparring dwarves

The camera enters a door at the end of that bridge and focuses on two dwarves [sparring](Sparring.md) with adamantine weapons.

[![Intro movie 5.gif](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/1/1d/Intro_movie_5.gif)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Intro_movie_5.gif)

<br>

### Chasm

The camera then goes into the door on the left and goes down several tunnels, including a bridge over the [chasm](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Chasm) until it comes to a dwarf mining. The miner strikes the wall of the cavern, makes a hole, and loses his pickaxe down said hole.

[![Intro movie 6.gif](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/6/62/Intro_movie_6.gif)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Intro_movie_6.gif)

<br>

### Fun

The miner looks into the hole and [bursts into flame](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Magma_flow#Initial_Flood).

[![Intro movie 7.gif](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/3/33/Intro_movie_7.gif)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Intro_movie_7.gif)
