### Fortress mode

Art is a generic term for a way of recording [history](Legends.md). You can order your dwarves to create art by ordering them to [smooth](Smoothing.md) and [engrave](Engraving.md) stone walls, or to [decorate](Decoration.md) objects. Art images can also appear on [coins](Coin.md) and [statues](Statue.md). Art in the form of [written works](Book.md), which can be created by [scholars](Scholar.md) in your [fortress](Fortress.md), will often reference historical events, or describe [civilizations](Civilization.md), smaller [groups](Army.md), or [historical figures](Historical_figure.md). Your [citizens](Citizenship.md), as well as [visiting](Visitor.md) scholars, can even create written works on the [subject of art](Book.md#art) itself.

Description of an engraved image, depicting a [civilization](Civilization.md)'s [group symbol](Names_and_symbols.md#symbols).

Art records your fortress's history, such as when your dwarves were [besieged](Siege.md) by [bloodthirsty](Boatmurdered.md) [elephants](Elephant.md).

Ordinarily, simply inspecting a piece of art only shows what the art is of. If you are in adventurer mode, or if the option is enabled in the [init.txt](Init.txt.md) file, inspecting a piece of art gives a history of what it depicts, including the names of those depicted.

### Adventure mode

Art at previous fortresses or other sites can be seen and appreciated. For objects in the world, right-click it, choose to view it. You can also use l to look around, then Enter over an object you want to look at, then the letter for the view option. For items in your inventory, you can see their description by clicking the [![Mag glass icon.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/4/47/Mag_glass_icon.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Mag_glass_icon.png), or by pressing the letter of the item, then a.

### Legends mode

Many works of art are created during [world generation](World_generation.md), as well as a consequence of [world activities](World_activities.md), often referencing [historical](Legends.md) events. They can even become [artifacts](Legendary_artifact.md) and coveted [heirlooms](Legendary_artifact.md#non-player-artifacts).
