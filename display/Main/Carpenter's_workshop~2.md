A **carpenter's workshop** is a [Tier 1](Workshop.md#tier-1-workshops) [workshop](Workshop.md) used by a [carpenter](Carpenter.md) to make most types of [furniture](Furniture.md), with the exception of [statues](Statue.md). The workshop can also make wooden [blocks](Block.md) or items like [buckets](Bucket.md), [crutches](Crutch.md), [shields](Shield.md), and [training weapons](Training_weapon.md). [Beds](Bed.md) can only be made at a carpenter's workshop.

The carpenter's workshop can be built from any building material, and must be built by a [dwarf](Dwarf.md) with the Carpentry [labor](Labor.md) enabled.

### Common Uses

- Make wooden [barrels](Barrel.md) for storage of [alcohol](Alcohol.md), [plants](Plant.md), and [seeds](Seed.md).
- Make wooden [beds](Bed.md), [chairs](Chair.md), [tables](Table.md), and [doors](Door.md) for [rooms](Zone.md). Beds in particular can only be made from wood at a carpenter's workshop.
- Make wooden [bins](Bin.md) for storage.
