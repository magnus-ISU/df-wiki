A **great pick** is an alternative to [picks](Pick.md) sized for large creatures. [Troll](Troll.md), [ogre](Ogre.md), and [blind cave ogre](Blind_cave_ogre.md) [miners](Miner.md) from dark [goblin](Goblin.md) [pits](Dark_pits.md) can arrive in [sieges](Siege.md) wielding this [weapon](Weapon.md).

A great pick is eight times the [size](Weapon.md#size) of a standard pick - that is, it has the same internal [attack properties](Attack_types.md), but hits with more force due to its size. A creature must be at least 225,000 cm³ to wield it, with [some large species](List_of_creatures_by_adult_size.md) of [animal people](Animal_people.md) being the smallest creatures that can equip it in [fortress mode](Fortress_mode~2.md).

When [melted](Melt_item.md) down, great picks produce roughly four bars of metal.
