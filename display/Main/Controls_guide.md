This is a beginner's guide to [Fortress Mode](Fortress_mode~2.md) interface [controls](Controls.md). This page assumes the default [key bindings](Key_bindings.md).

As a beginner it is recommended that you familiarize yourself with the **(View Unit - Preferences - Labor)** submenu, the **Query buildings** menu, the **Designate** menu, and the **Build** menu.

The creatures tab.

The Creatures command gives detailed information on creatures: a [dwarf](Dwarf.md), an [animal](Creature.md), a [goblin](Goblin.md), etc. This command has several different tabs:

- Citizens
- Pets/Livestock
- Others
- Dead/Missing

#### Tasks, t

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/7/7b/Tasks-tab.png/300px-Tasks-tab.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Tasks-tab.png)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Tasks-tab.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Tasks-tab.png)

The tasks tab.

A list of the tasks that your dwarves are doing, as well as pending tasks. You can jump to the specific dwarves, or go to their information pages from here.

#### Places, P

The Places command allows you to view various places, with tabs for:

- [Zones](Zone.md), such as refuse, pastures, clay gathering, rooms, and meeting halls.
  - You can also assign a room to belong to a specific dwarf here.
- Named [locations](Location.md) designated in your fortress (libraries, temples, guildhalls, taverns)
  - In this tab you can control the supplies desired for your locations, as well as designate a worker/workers for the location as appropriate.
- [Stockpiles](Stockpile.md) where you can change what types of objects are stored there, how many [barrels](Barrel.md) are used, etc.
- [Workshops](Workshop.md) where you can change what tasks are being worked on, set a particular task to repeat or be suspended, or change who is allowed to use the workshop.
- [Farm plot](Farm_plot.md) you can change what crops are being planted.

#### Build, b

Use this command to build rooms, place [furniture](Furniture.md), start [farm](Farm_plot.md) plots, and such. In addition to a long list of items which can be scrolled through with the +/- keys, there are also a few sub-menus:

- f **[Furniture](Furniture.md)**
- p **[Doors](Door.md)/[hatches](Hatch.md)**
- n **[Constructions](Construction.md)**
- m **[Machines](Machine.md)/fluids**
- r **[Cages](Cage.md)/[restraints](Restraint.md)**
- t **[Traps](Trap.md)**
- y **Military**
- T **Trade depot**

#### Zones, z

z Mark an [activity zone](Activity_zone.md) for drinking [water](Water.md), [fishing](Fishing.md), [dump](Dump.md), [pen/pasture](Pasture.md), [pit/pond](Activity_zone.md#pit2fpond), [sand](Sand.md) collection, [clay](Clay.md) collection, [meeting hall](Meeting_hall.md), [hospital](Hospital.md), [animal training](Animal_training~2.md). This interface is the reverse of designate and stockpile commands: you mark out the rectangular area first, and define what it does afterward.

#### Squads, q

This menu is used to control your military squads. You have to select what squads you want to apply what orders to using the checkboxes. The orders are:

- Attack - Selected squad will attack the selected enemies.
- Station - Selected squad will station to a specific location you choose.
- Patrol - Order the entire squad(s) to patrol a specified set of waypoints
- Defend [burrow](Burrow.md) - Defend a specific burrow
- Train - Order your squad(s) to train immediately
- Cancel orders - Cancels any of the above orders.
- Equip - Opens the equipment screen for the selected squad(s).
- Schedule - Brings up the military scheduling screen.

### Fortress Information

These commands take you away from the map, to an informational screen. Some of them also let you give orders.

#### World map, Y

##### Civilizations

```
A list of other civilizations that you have personally encountered (and thus within distance for trade or war). Civilizations are marked with a P if you are at peace with them, or with a W if you are at war. Pressing Tab lets you check total exports and imports, and standing trade agreements.
```

#### Military

**No longer existent in v50**

Access the [military](Military.md) screen

- p **Positions** Create and remove squads and assign dwarves to them.
- a **Alerts** Create configure alerts. Used in conjunction with burrows to define a **CIV Alert**, a safe place that dwarves will be forced to go when turned on. Can also be used to change the active alert schedule for your squads.
- e **Equip** Assign armor and weapons to military squads.
- n **Uniforms** Create or modify uniform templates which can be assigned using the Equip screen.
- u **Supplies** Controls if a squad carries food and drink.
- f **Ammunition** Assigns ammunition to squads.
- s **Schedule** Used to modify the [orders](Schedule.md) for an alert.

#### Fortress Overview

**No longer existent in v50**

This screen is filled with useful information, like your fortress's net worth, and how much food and drink you have left.

- **Animals** A list of all tame [animals](Creature.md) in your fort.
- **Kitchen** From here you can specify what [foods](Food.md) can be [cooked](Cook.md) or [brewed](Brew.md).
- **Stone** Controls the use of [ores](Ore.md) and [economic stones](Economic_stone.md)
- **Stocks** This is an in-depth listing of every item, down to every rotting rat corpse. The accuracy of the your item count depends on your [bookkeeper](Bookkeeper~2.md).
- **Justice** Shows the details of the dwarven [justice](Justice.md) system, once you have a [Sheriff](Sheriff.md).
- **Health** Shows an overview of the health status of the fortress, once you have a [Chief medical dwarf](Chief_medical_dwarf.md).

See [Status](Status.md) for more details.
