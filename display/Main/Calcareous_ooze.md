**Calcareous ooze** is one of the several types of [soil](Soil.md) that can be found on the floors of deep [oceans](Ocean.md). Not surprisingly, such layers occasionally contain an [aquifer](Aquifer.md).

Calcareous ooze is a type of [pelagic sediment](Soil.md). It is formed by particles of [calcium carbonate](Flux~2.md), as opposed to [siliceous ooze](Siliceous_ooze.md), which is formed by the silica in diatoms and other microorganisms.
