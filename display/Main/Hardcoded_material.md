**Hardcoded materials** are written into the game code itself, rather than being defined in [raw files](Raw_file.md), and, as such, they cannot be [altered](Modding.md) manually, usually playing special roles within the game. There are currently 20 hardcoded materials, excluding subtypes, which are as follows:

- AMBER - [amber](Amber.md)
- ASH - [ash](Ash.md)
- COAL - [charcoal, coke, and generic refined coal](Refined_coal.md)
- CORAL - [coral](Coral.md)
- FILTH_B - brown [filth](Filth.md)
- FILTH_Y - yellow [filth](Filth.md)
- GLASS_CLEAR - [clear glass](Clear_glass.md)
- GLASS_CRYSTAL - [crystal glass](Crystal_glass.md)
- GLASS_GREEN - [green glass](Green_glass~2.md)
- GRIME - [grime](Grime.md)
- INORGANIC - [magma](Magma.md)
- LYE - [lye](Lye.md)
- MUD - [mud](Mud.md)
- NONE - [unknown material](Unknown_material.md)
- PEARLASH - [pearlash](Pearlash.md)
- POTASH - [potash](Potash.md)
- SALT - [salt](Salt.md)
- UNKNOWN_SUBSTANCE - [unknown substance](Unknown_substance.md)
- VOMIT - [vomit](Vomit.md)
- WATER - [water](Water.md), [ice](Ice.md), [snow](Snow.md) and [steam](Steam.md)
