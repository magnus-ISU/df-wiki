**Shleggings** are [leggings](Leggings.md) made from [shell](Shell.md). The name is a portmanteau of "shell" and "leggings". The name seems to originate from a live video stream of the game from 2022-11-10, a recording of which was published on the Internet here: [Let's Learn Dwarf Fortress Steam Edition - EP1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?t=3462&v=bLN8cOcTjdo). It is linked at a timestamp before [Alexandra](Alexandra.md) says, "shleggings".
