**Unknown materials** can appear as a [placeholder](Hardcoded_material.md) as the result of [bugs](Bug.md) - specifically, missing [material tokens](Material_token.md). They have minimal material properties, typically appearing as a light gray solid unaffected by [temperature](Temperature.md).

An "Unknown material", as it is listed in [building](Building.md) descriptions, normally has no name. It has an internal [ID](Material_token.md) of -1 or `[NONE]`, the same as used in tasks that require items of any or an unspecified material.

[Doors](Door.md) and [hatch covers](Hatch_cover~2.md) in [dark pits](Dark_pits.md)<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[6776](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=6776)</sup> and [dead civilizations](Civilization.md#dead-and-struggling-civilizations)<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[3478](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=3478)</sup> have been known to generate as unknown material.

Since the material ID of -1 is used as a placeholder value for any material, items made of the unknown material cannot be specifically requested as an ingredient, even in [modded](Modding.md) [reactions](Reaction.md).

### Organic substances

`[CREATURE_MAT]` and `[PLANT_MAT]` fall back to "unknown creature/plant substance" if they do not have a proper subtype. If solid, they will additionally be listed as "frozen".

### Other forms

Other material categories have fallbacks in addition to their subtypes. Unlike unknown materials, they are functional when obtained.

Inorganic materials without a subtype become [magma](Magma.md) (`[INORGANIC:NONE]`), or generic "rock" if solid.

The generic form of charcoal and coke (`[COAL:NO_MATGLOSS]`) is known as [refined coal](Refined_coal.md), which cannot be legitimately obtained.
