**Primordial remnants**, alternatively known as **remnants of creation** and **mythical remnants**, are a type of procedurally-generated material that can be found as [artifacts](Artifact.md).

The name and appearance of a remnant is generated based on its associated [sphere](Sphere.md). This linkage is consistent between all remnants and [mysterious dungeons](Mysterious_dungeon.md) - "cosmic wood" might be guarded by "adepts of the forest," while a "primal crystal" might generate "gem worshippers". Currently, only one type of remnant can exist in a world.

Remnants have identical mechanical properties to [divine metal](Divine_metal.md) - their yield is 1,000,000 KPa in all dimensions, and fractures at 2,000,000 KPa - superior to [steel](Steel.md). They are perfectly inelastic and can be extremely sharp, with a strain-at-yield of 0 and a max edge of 12,000. Their density is 1,000 kg/m³, about as much as [wood](Wood.md). Remnants are completely fireproof.

(A remnant associated with the [sphere](Sphere.md) of [metals](Sphere.md#metals) gains the `[ITEMS_METAL]` token, and is thus recognized as being [metal](Metal.md).)

Because remnants are hidden in [mysterious dungeons](Mysterious_dungeon.md) without any historical interactions, they can't be viewed in [legends mode](Legends_mode.md) until they're encountered in play.

### Raw material

Primordial remnants originate from [mysterious lairs](Mysterious_dungeon.md), though [historical figures](Historical_figure.md) can steal them like other [artifacts](Artifact.md), and bring them into the wider world.

They have, as of yet, no known powers or uses (but equipment made from them has; see below). [Religions](Religion.md) seek to gather them, and therefore offer a [quest](Quest.md) to retrieve one. They are categorized as a "small rock," like those used for [knapping](Knapper.md).

### Equipment

[Jewelry](Jewelry.md) and [weapons](Weapon.md) made from primordial remnants are found in [mysterious dungeons](Mysterious_dungeon.md) and palaces, respectively. These items are also [magical](Magic.md), granting the wielder use of an [![Adv ui X.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/e/e5/Adv_ui_X.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Adv_ui_X.png)X [interaction](Interaction.md). Weapons made from these materials are as strong as [divine metal](Divine_metal.md), and use the same set of special graphics. The powers are based on the sphere of the primordial remnant, see the above table. For more information on magic powers and their effects, see [intelligent undead](Intelligent_undead.md).
