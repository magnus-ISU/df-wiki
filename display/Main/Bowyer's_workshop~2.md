The **bowyer's workshop** is a [Tier 1](Workshop.md#tier-1-workshops) [workshop](Workshop.md) used solely to make [crossbows](Crossbow.md) (and any other modded ranged weapons) out of [wood](Wood.md) or [bone](Bone.md); [metal](Metal.md) crossbows are made at a [metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md) (or [magma forge](Magma_forge~2.md)), and [ammo](Ammo.md) is made elsewhere. It requires any [building material](Building_material.md) to construct, and must be built by a [dwarf](Dwarf.md) with the [crossbow-making](Bowyer.md) [labor](Labor.md) enabled.

Note that many fortresses will never build a bowyer's shop, or will do so only upon arrival and then tear it down again. While wooden or bone crossbows are identical to metal ones for purposes of ranged combat (same accuracy, same range, etc), if (read "when") a marksdwarf's target(s) rush up and force melee combat, a lightweight wooden/bone crossbow is *significantly* inferior to any metal crossbow. So, the only advantages of wood/bone crossbows are 1) that they are slightly lighter in weight, and 2) that a fortress doesn't need a [metal industry](Metal_industry~2.md) up and running to produce them, just an [axe](Axe.md) and a tree to [cut down](Wood_cutter.md).

### Tasks

- Make [bone](Bone.md) [crossbow](Crossbow.md)
- Make [wooden](Wood.md) [crossbow](Crossbow.md)

### Common Uses

- Outfit an [archer](Archer.md), [military](Military.md) [squad](Squad.md), or [hunter](Hunter.md).

### Notes

- The name of this workshop will change if your fortress's primary race provides a custom value for the `[PROFESSION_NAME:BOWYER]` token.
- Use of a [crossbow](Crossbow.md) also requires [bolts](Bolt.md), which are made in a [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop~2.md) or [forge](Forge.md), and a [quiver](Quiver.md), made in a [leather works](Leather_works.md).
- If you're using a mod which allows dwarves to make and use [bows](Bow.md) and/or [blowguns](Blowgun.md) (or any other ranged weapons), they will automatically show up here.
