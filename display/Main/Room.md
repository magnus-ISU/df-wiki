A **room** was an area defined from an object that specified the purpose of the room (for example, a [bedroom](Bedroom.md) would be defined from a [bed](Bed.md)).

As of v50.01, you instead specify [activity zones](Activity_zone.md) that may or may not have objects inside them. For the behavior in previous versions, see [DF2014:Room](../DF2014/Room.md).
