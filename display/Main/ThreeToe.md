**ThreeToe** is the nickname used by **Zach Adams**, co-designer of *[Dwarf Fortress](Dwarf_Fortress.md)* and author of the [short stories](http://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/dev_story.html) which highlight *Dwarf Fortress*'s upcoming features. He can be reached at [About Bay 12 Games](http://www.bay12games.com/about.html) or the [Bay 12 Games Forum](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/).

The other designer, and programmer, is Zach's younger brother, Tarn ([Toady One](Toady_One.md)).

ThreeToe married on July 15, 2017. All we know about his spouse Annie Forsman-Adams is that she has "royal blood". According to him, their wedding was planned to be "as dwarfy as possible." [\[1\]](http://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/dev_2017.html#2017-07-13)

Since March 2019[\[2\]](http://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/dev_2019.html#2019-03-09), ThreeToe has been keeping four smaller [threetoes](Cavy.md): [Harry, Ramone, Sid and Bowie](Harry,_Ramone,_Sid_and_Bowie.md).
