A piece of **display [furniture](Furniture.md)** can be used to display other items. There are two available types of display furniture, pedestals and display cases.

Pedestals are created from [wood](Wood.md), [stone](Stone.md), [glass](Glass.md), or [metal](Metal.md) at a [carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop~2.md), [stoneworker's workshop](Stoneworker's_workshop.md), [glass furnace](Glass_furnace.md), or [forge](Forge.md), respectively; while wood, stone, and glass only require 1 material to craft, metal requires 2 bars. Being [tools](Tools.md), they are listed at a forge under "Other objects" rather than "Furniture", but still use the [blacksmithing](Blacksmithing.md) skill.

Display cases are created from a [log](Log.md) and a [window](Window.md) at a [carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop~2.md). The window used to create the display case does not affect it in any way; a ☼display case☼ made with a ☼crystal glass window☼ is worth as much as a ☼display case☼ made with a -green glass window-, and the window used is not mentioned in any context.

Other than the above-mentioned creation processes, their visual appearances and the fact that display cases are twice as large as pedestals (and thus, twice as heavy as a pedestal made out of the same material), pedestals and display furniture are completely identical, their behavior determined entirely by the [DISPLAY_OBJECT](Tool_token.md#tool-use) tool use.

In the building menu, they are listed in furniture [![Ui b.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b1/Ui_b.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_b.png)b[![Ui bf.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b0/Ui_bf.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bf.png)f[![Ui bfy.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/e/e1/Ui_bfy.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bfy.png)y. Once built, display furniture can be examined by clicking on it. Items to display can be selected with the "**Assign new display items**" option. This opens a menu similar to the [Stocks](Stocks.md) menu, listing only items that can be displayed. Clicking the checkmark on the left marks an item to be placed on the display by [item haulers](Hauling.md#item-hauling). Once placed, the item appears in the furniture menu with the label "On display". Its description can be viewed in the building materials list below by clicking [![Mag glass icon.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/4/47/Mag_glass_icon.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Mag_glass_icon.png) icon. Displayed items also have [![Icon semipermanent part.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/5/53/Icon_semipermanent_part.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Icon_semipermanent_part.png) icon, indicating they are not a permanent part of the building. [Meeting area](Meeting_area.md) designated over a display furniture will be called a "[museum](Museum.md)".

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/5/5a/Df_tomb_treasure.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Df_tomb_treasure.png)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Df_tomb_treasure.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Df_tomb_treasure.png)

A line of pedestals with [sacks](Bag.md) of *something* on them.

Dwarves will admire both the display furniture and the item on display, giving them happy thoughts. A dwarf must stand in or pass through the actual tile containing the display furniture in order to admire it; they cannot do so from a distance. This unfortunately leaves your artifacts on display vulnerable to sticky-fingered [villains](Villain.md). If the only objective is to increase the value of a location, however (for example, to instantly turn a meeting place into a grand guildhall), the object can be locked away, such as by vertical grates or bars.

Display furniture is [stockpiled](Stockpile.md) as furniture, under "Other Large Tools".

In [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md), pedestals can be found in many generated sites, sometimes alongside display cases. It is possible to place an item on a pedestal by standing next to (or on) it and [![Adv ui p.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/f/f9/Adv_ui_p.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Adv_ui_p.png)p putting the item onto the pedestal. Likewise, it is possible to remove an item from a pedestal by standing on the same tile as it and [![Adv ui g.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/f/f7/Adv_ui_g.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Adv_ui_g.png)g getting it. These actions will be seen by NPCs as, respectively placing or taking the item from the site, though they only care if the item is an [artifact](Artifact.md).
