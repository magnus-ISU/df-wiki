**Armor stands** are a type of [furniture](Furniture.md). Much like the [weapon rack](Weapon_rack~2.md), the armor stand can be used inside a [barracks](Barracks.md). Once built an armor stand can be placed with [![Ui b.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b1/Ui_b.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_b.png)b[![Ui by.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/8/8a/Ui_by.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_by.png)y[![Ui byn.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/d/db/Ui_byn.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_byn.png)n. It can also be placed in a [noble](Noble.md)'s [room](Zone.md) to fulfill their [housing requirements](Noble.md#needs).

When used in a barracks designated for individual and squad equipment, dwarves will prefer to store their armor on stands instead of stockpiles. Doing so in concert with [weapon racks](Weapon_rack~2.md) will allow you to create centralized armories for your military.

In [tombs](Tombs.md) designated for [nobles](Nobles.md) or [military dwarves](Military.md), armor stands will be used to store the deceased dwarf's armor.

Armor stands do not block movement.
