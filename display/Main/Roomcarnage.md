**Roomcarnage** is a v0.34.11 fortress presented in the style of a serialized epic. Over the course of more than seventy updates since June 2014, it chronicles the rise of the dwarves of the Momentous Dye in a particularly interesting embark location: a [terrifying](Terrifying.md) [glacier](Glacier.md) [volcano](Volcano.md) that rains [elf](Elf.md) blood. The most notable feature of the fortress is the ongoing weaponization of the volcano itself, finally realized in the twenty-second installment.

In addition to the constant reanimation of dead animals, dwarves, and parts thereof; foul fog zombie liaisons and caravans; and a mysterious vampire cult, the dwarves of Roomcarnage have dealt with the horrors of nakedness, encroaching FPS death, and — worst of all! — alcohol deprivation.

The story may be read [here](http://roomcarnage.com/index.html). Its thread on the Bay 12 Forums is [here](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=139393.0). The final chapter of the story was posted on [January 22nd, 2026](https://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=139393.msg8608755#msg8608755).
