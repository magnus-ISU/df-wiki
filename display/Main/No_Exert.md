**No Exert** creatures (those with the `[NOEXERT]` [token](Creature_token~2.md)) need no [sleep](Sleep.md), and never become tired or exhausted. In the case of a particularly dangerous creature like a [bronze colossus](Bronze_colossus.md), the NOEXERT tag guarantees that the foe will continue the battle as long as there are any [dwarves](Dwarves.md) left alive.

<br>
