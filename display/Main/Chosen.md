The **chosen** is an [adventurer](Adventurer.md) that receives aid and guidance from a patron [deity](Deity.md). It is a successor to the Demigod (now known as Easy) difficulty mode.

### Gameplay

The Chosen [destiny](Adventurer_mode.md#destiny) can be selected when creating an adventurer, as long as [temples](Temple.md) have been founded by playable civilizations. It functions as an in-world [tutorial](Tutorials.md), providing guidance specific to each [world](World.md).

At the beginning of a Chosen's adventure, their patron will instruct them to seek out a [priest](Priest.md) of the same [religion](Religion.md). Talking to the priest allows the player to [![Adv ui k.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/7/70/Adv_ui_k.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Adv_ui_k.png)k Offer service and accept a [quest](Quest.md) to obtain a [primordial remnant](Primordial_remnant.md) from a [mysterious lair](Mysterious_dungeon.md). To complete the quest, give the [artifact](Artifact.md) to the priest.

### Abilities

Chosen adventurers start off as regular creatures without extraordinary abilities. Rarely, their patron will deflect an incoming attack via "divine intervention" as if [shielded](Armor.md#shield), while also [stunning](Status_icon.md) the creature, though the circumstances for this event are not well understood.
