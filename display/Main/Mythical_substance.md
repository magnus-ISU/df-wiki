**Mythical substances** are procedurally-generated [liquids](Liquid.md) and [globs](Glob.md) with [magical](Magic.md) properties. Each world has a number of differently-named substances, such as "odd jelly" or "peculiar broth," though currently, all of them carry the same [syndrome](Syndrome.md).

### Healing potion

A [mysterious dungeon](Mysterious_dungeon.md)'s [guardian](Dungeon_guardian.md) carries a [waterskin](Waterskin.md) filled with a restorative substance that they can consume in combat. Ingesting it grants a powerful [syndrome](Syndrome.md) that cures nearly all [wounds](Wound.md), even [regrowing limbs](Syndrome.md#ce-regrow-parts) and [healing nerve damage](Syndrome.md#ce-heal-nerves), but not blood loss; however, it can allow you to live through Titan fire long enough to stop you from melting. Even [undead](Undead.md) creatures benefit from it, to the point of removing any rot their bodies have, restoring them to perfect condition.

Creatures that are [immune to poison](Construct_creature.md) (not `GENERAL_POISON`) do not benefit from this substance.
