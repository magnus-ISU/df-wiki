A **screw press** is a special workshop used to press liquids out of various substances and to press [slurry](Slurry.md) into [sheets](Sheets.md) of [paper](Paper.md).

Food-related jobs at the screw press consist of pressing [seed](Seed.md) pastes or fruit ([olives](Olive.md)) to yield [oil](Oil.md) (which can then be made into [soap](Soap.md) or cooked), [pressing cake](Press_cake.md) (which can be cooked), and pressing honeycombs to yield [honey](Honey.md) (which can then be brewed into [mead](Mead.md)) and [wax cake](Wax.md) (which can be made into wax crafts). These jobs require the [pressing](Pressing.md) skill.

Pressing seed paste or fruit into oil and honeycombs into honey require an empty [jug](Jug.md) to hold the resulting liquid.

Slurry may be pressed into sheets of paper at the screw press by a dwarf with the [papermaking](Papermaking.md) skill. Sheets of paper have quality levels, while other products at the screw press do not.

Constructing a screw press requires two [mechanisms](Mechanisms.md) and is done through the Build menu [![Ui b.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b1/Ui_b.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_b.png)b[![Ui bo.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/a/aa/Ui_bo.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bo.png)o[![Ui boR.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/e/e0/Ui_boR.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_boR.png)R.

### Bugs

- Screw presses sometimes do not allow the "press [honeycomb](Honeycomb.md)" job to be added directly; adding the job via the [manager](Manager.md) interface should get the work started.
