A **throne** (called a **chair** when made out of [wood](Wood.md)) is a piece of [furniture](Furniture.md) that dwarves use in their daily routines once built as a **seat** (the generic term). A throne can be made from [stone](Stone.md) at a [stoneworker's workshop](Stoneworker's_workshop.md) with [stone carver](Stone_carver.md), 3 [metal bars](Metal.md) at a [forge](Forge.md) with [blacksmithing](Blacksmithing.md), [glass](Glass.md) at a [glass furnace](Glass_furnace.md) with [glassmaking](Glassmaking.md), or from [wood](Wood.md) at a [carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop~2.md) by a dwarf with the [carpentry](Carpentry.md) [labor](Labor.md) (where it's listed as a "chair"). The material has no effect on its functionality; all thrones work the same. Note that for an equal skill level, wood chairs are produced faster than rock thrones<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Throne&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>, though different [materials](Material.md) have different final base [value](Value.md#material-multipliers) multipliers.

A built throne can be placed in the furniture menu ([![Ui b.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b1/Ui_b.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_b.png)b-[![Ui bf.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b0/Ui_bf.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bf.png)f-[![Ui bfr.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/6/6a/Ui_bfr.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bfr.png)r). A throne can only be placed [indoors](Inside.md).

Seats, like [tables](Table.md), are an obvious part of [dining rooms](Dining_room~2.md) -- Dining at a seat in a high-quality dining hall will give an (additional) happy [thought](Thought.md). If a dwarf wants to eat, they will look for a free seat. If they don't find one, or one without a free table next to it, they will have an unhappy thought. Dwarves don't like to share tables - they will get an unhappy [thought](Thought.md) if the table in front of them is being used by someone else. Having enough seats and tables and placing them in an unambiguous 1:1 pattern avoids these bad thoughts. Seats are also used to create [offices](Office.md) (and throne rooms, essentially the same thing) for [nobles](Nobles.md).
