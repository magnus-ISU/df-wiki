**Conglomerate** is one of many [sedimentary layer](Sedimentary_layer.md) [stones](Stone.md). It is notable for not only supporting [aquifers](Aquifer.md), but also frequently containing large [clusters](Cluster.md) of [puddingstone](Puddingstone.md) which will **also** support the aquifer.

### In Real Life

Conglomerate is a type of clastic sedimentary stone composed of large, well rounded grains, at least 2 millimeters in diameter; if the grains are angular, it is called a breccia (which is not present in *Dwarf Fortress*). If the grains are rounded pebbles, the conglomerate is called [puddingstone](Puddingstone.md).
