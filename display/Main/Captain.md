> *Not to be confused with either [Militia captains](Militia_captain.md) or the [Captain of the guard](Captain_of_the_guard.md)*

**Captain** is a position in the mostly-unrealized, civilization-wide dwarven army, acting as leader of a squad of ten soldiers. A captain is appointed by the [General](General.md) and commanded by a [Lieutenant](Lieutenant.md). They are not appointed in civilization creation, because their Position_token NUMBER is not fixed, it is AS_NEEDED. This means that they are only then created when they are needed, and those circumstances are very rare.
