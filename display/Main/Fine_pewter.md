**Fine pewter** is an [alloy](Alloy.md) of [tin](Tin.md) and [copper](Copper.md).

Fine pewter can be made using one of the following recipes:

- 3 [Tin](Tin.md) bars + 1 [Copper](Copper.md) bar = 4 Fine pewter bars
- 3 [Cassiterite](Cassiterite.md) + 1 [Native copper](Native_copper.md), [Malachite](Malachite.md), or [Tetrahedrite](Tetrahedrite.md) = 16 Fine pewter bars

Fine pewter has a material value of 5. This represents a significant increase over its component parts (which all have a value of 2), making fine pewter suitable for creating mid-value furniture and items. If you have an abundance of tin, this is the best form of [pewter](Pewter.md) to make.

Note that [bronze](Bronze.md) uses the same ingredients (though in different proportions), has the same material value, and is also useful as a mid-grade [weapons](Weapon.md) material, while [bismuth bronze](Bismuth_bronze~2.md) is more valuable. However, you can produce 16 bars of fine pewter with a single unit of [fuel](Fuel.md) (when smelting from [ores](Ore.md)), giving fine pewter a slight advantage over bronze if fuel is constrained.
