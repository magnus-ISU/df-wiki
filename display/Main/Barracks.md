A **barracks** is a [zone](Zone.md) designated for the purpose of housing, training, supplying and otherwise servicing your [military](Military.md). The barracks' options define how the zone will be used by the [squads](Squad.md) in your military. A single barracks may serve multiple squads.

### Designating

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/5/59/Granite_Dojo.jpg/300px-Granite_Dojo.jpg)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Granite_Dojo.jpg)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Granite_Dojo.jpg](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Granite_Dojo.jpg)

An over-engineered dwarven barracks

Barracks are defined from a [zone](Zone.md) [![Ui z.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/1/13/Ui_z.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_z.png)z. Select Barracks, then using the zone paint feature, designate the area for the barracks. Once you've designated a barracks, you'll notice a list of the squads in your fort in the information pane. This is where you set which squad uses the barracks, and how. To do so, press [![Ui z.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/1/13/Ui_z.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_z.png)z and select the barracks zone, then select the barracks add squad button ([![Ui assign squad.jpg](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/a/af/Ui_assign_squad.jpg/28px-Ui_assign_squad.jpg)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_assign_squad.jpg)), then select the relevant activity for each squad assigned to the barracks:

- **Sleep** - assigned squads will be housed here, requiring that enough beds be placed in the barracks (one for each squad member).
- **Training** - assigned squads will [train](Training.md) here. Note that [marksdwarves](Marksdwarves.md) assigned to the barracks will train in melee, including [hammering](Combat_skill.md#equipment-skills), to train their ranged skill, they require an [archery range](Archery_target.md) instead.
- **Individual Eq** and **Squad Eq** - Each dwarf in a squad will use a separate container (such as a [chest](Chest.md), [cabinet](Cabinet.md) or [armor stand](Armor_stand~2.md)) placed in the barracks to store their equipment. As you place them, they will be automatically assigned to each dwarf.

### Location

The location of your barracks is a matter of great importance - they may often be the location where your military dwarves will be training, sleeping, or otherwise performing activities. It is important to consider where you expect your enemies ([goblins](Goblin.md), [forgotten beasts](Forgotten_beast.md), [titans](Titan.md), etc.) to attack, such as entrances or focal points of your fortress. You may want to build your barracks relatively close to or just outside of high-traffic areas, main entrances to your fortress, or chambers with [traps](Trap.md) in them. Planning ahead will allow you to react quicker to an invasion by hostile creatures and save more civilians in the process.

### Notes

- Note that if a squad is [scheduled](Schedule.md) to train, but lacking an assigned barracks, a dwarf's job will be marked as Soldier (no activity), leaving the squad with nothing to do.
- Civilians will not sleep in open beds within a defined barracks.
