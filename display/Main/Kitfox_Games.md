**Kitfox Games** is a game studio focused on simulation games and roguelikes. Kitfox Games has developed several first-party titles, and serves as publisher for several indie games, including *[Dwarf Fortress](Dwarf_Fortress.md)*.

### Involvement with Dwarf Fortress

[Bay 12 Games](Bay_12_Games.md) posted on April 2019 that they partnered with Kitfox, after a medical scare that made them worry about their long-term finances. Kitfox hired artists to work on the [graphics set](Graphics_set.md) and [soundtrack](Soundtrack.md) for the [Premium](Premium.md) release.<sup>\[[source](https://www.patreon.com/posts/25343688)\]</sup>

In December 2022, Kitfox published *Dwarf Fortress* commercially to [Itch.io](https://kitfoxgames.itch.io/dwarf-fortress) and [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/975370/Dwarf_Fortress/).
