# Training weapon

[![Training axe sprite preview.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/d/de/Training_axe_sprite_preview.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Training_axe_sprite_preview.png)

**Training weapons** are [edgeless](Edge.md) [wooden](Wood.md) [weapons](Weapon.md) created at a [carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop~2.md). There are three kinds available; axes, spears, and swords.

[Dwarves](Dwarves.md) won't harm each other (or themselves) during [combat drills](Combat_drills.md), or [sparring](Training.md#sparring-and-demonstration) while using [metal weapons](Metal.md), so training weapons are only useful for actual "training" when you have no metal weapon of the kind you'd like your soldiers to train with, and in a few specific circumstances.

Training weapons are virtually useless in [combat](Combat.md), so make sure that your soldiers have exchanged them for real weapons before sending them into battle, even if it means using a weapon they are totally unfamiliar with. Their utter uselessness can prove useful for [live training](Live_training.md), however.

Note that soldiers equipped with training weapons for extended periods of time can grow [attached](Attachment.md) to their worthless wooden weapons. Congratulations - you now have a legendary stick.

Training weapons may also be used as non-lethal equipment for your [fortress guard](Fortress_guard.md), to avoid them [seriously wounding or killing](Fun.md) [criminals](Justice.md#crimes), including [mandate violators](Mandate.md). Though it is unclear how often this happens in *Dwarf Fortress* v50.

Training weapons can be useful in [weapon traps](Weapon_trap.md) if used creatively. Although they will only rarely even bruise a target, each weapon (max 10/trap) will trigger a "dodge" attempt, often inviting the target to step into a tile adjacent to the trap. If that tile, for example, has no floor for 10 [z-levels](Z-level.md), or is over [magma](Magma.md), then the weapon has done its job.

Additionally, training spears can be used in [upright spike traps](Trap.md#upright-spear2fspike), which is a useful design for a [danger room](Danger_room.md). Use the least-[dense](Density.md) wood available for extra safety (unless you want to train [medical skills](Health_care~2.md#skills-and-injuries)).

Training axes cannot be used for [cutting down trees](Wood_cutter.md).
