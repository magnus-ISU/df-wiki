Randomly generated personality of a [miner](Miner.md).

Some creatures dream of accomplishing certain **goals** in their life, and these goals can, presumably, affect their behavior. If a creature has such goals, they will be listed in the "Values" sub-tab of the "Personality" tab of their character sheet. Dwarves will receive a happy thought upon completion of their goals, receiving the notation of "and this dream was realized" in the personality description following their original goals. Goals would apparently *not* currently be limited to one per creature, but for some sort of oversight: [\[1\]](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=169696.msg8083871#msg8083871). A creature needs the ability to [learn](Learning.md) in order to have a goal in life.

According to Toady, syndromes can cause creatures to re-evaluate their goals in worldgen; the boost to anxiety and distrust given to necromancers makes it more likely for them to develop the goal of ruling the world. [\[2\]](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=169696.msg8275328#msg8275328)
