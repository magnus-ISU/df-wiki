A **morningstar** is an [edged](Attack_types.md) [weapon](Weapon.md) that consists of a spiked ball mounted on the end of a handle. Despite similarities to a [mace](Mace.md) in appearance, a morningstar's size and contact area are closer to those of a [war hammer](War_hammer.md). Specifically, a morningstar is 25% larger than a war hammer with the same contact area, and uses [piercing](Attack_types.md) damage to inflict internal injuries.

Morningstars use and train the [macedwarf](Macedwarf.md) skill. As foreign weapons, dwarves cannot [forge](Forge.md) morningstars, limiting supply to whatever low-[quality](Quality.md) specimens can be traded from human [caravans](Caravan.md) and scavenged from invaders. All dwarves can equip morningstars, though [a tiny fraction must use two hands](Weapon.md#size).
