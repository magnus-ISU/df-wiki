> *For a discussion of how DF displays different items, and decides which colors get altered and when, see [color](Color.md).*

*Dwarf Fortress* uses **color schemes** to determine how the game will be presented during play, with the default color scheme data located in the [colors.txt](Colors.txt.md) file, found in the `Dwarf Fortress/data/init` folder, but `prefs/colors.txt`<sup>v52.02</sup> will override that, if it exists.

[DFColorgen](https://manmademagic.github.io/DFColorGen/) is a web tool to create and visualize color schemes.

### What colors show up where

A color scheme is broken down into 16 color labels:

> BLACK, DGRAY
> BLUE, LBLUE
> GREEN, LGREEN
> CYAN, LCYAN
> RED, LRED
> MAGENTA, LMAGENTA
> BROWN, YELLOW
> LGRAY, WHITE

The easiest place to see all the colors next to each other is in the units list, where the different [professional categories](Skill.md#professions2c-colors2c-26-skill-categories) all have their identifying colors. In that order (and with the default color values), they are...

Some other color uses:

- [Magma](Magma.md) is LRED. (Magma 1 level lower than current view is RED.)
- [Water](Water.md) is LBLUE. (Water 1 level lower than current view is BLUE.)
- [Brooks](Brook.md) are LCYAN on their surface (with water below).
- For aesthetic purposes, almost all colours can be represented in stones (with the exception of LMAGENTA, CYAN, and BLACK).
  - BLACK: The background color, which should not be used by anything.
  - DGRAY: A wide variety of stones are DGRAY, one example being [gabbro](Gabbro.md).
  - BLUE: [Kimberlite](Kimberlite.md) is the only stone that is BLUE. [Nether-caps](Nether-cap.md) also produce BLUE wood.
  - LBLUE: [Cobaltite](Cobaltite.md) is the only stone that is LBLUE.
  - GREEN: [Olivine](Olivine.md) is GREEN, as are malachite and [serpentine](Serpentine.md).
  - LGREEN: [Garnierite](Garnierite.md) is the only LGREEN stone.
  - CYAN: While there are no stones, [spore trees](Spore_tree.md) are CYAN.
  - LCYAN: [Microcline](Microcline.md) is an LCYAN rock. [Adamantine](Adamantine.md) and its ore, [raw adamantine](Raw_adamantine.md), also display in LCYAN.
  - RED: [Bauxite](Bauxite.md) and [kaolinite](Kaolinite.md) are RED, as is the [blood thorn](Blood_thorn.md) tree.
  - LRED: [Realgar](Realgar.md), [petrified wood](Petrified_wood.md), and [cinnabar](Cinnabar.md) are LRED. [Goblin-caps](Goblin-cap.md) are also LRED.
  - MAGENTA: [Rutile](Rutile.md) and [pitchblende](Pitchblende.md) are MAGENTA, and [glumprongs](Glumprong.md) are MAGENTA trees.
  - LMAGENTA: No stones are LMAGENTA, but [tunnel tubes](Tunnel_tube.md) are.
  - BROWN: A lot of stones are BROWN; [chert](Chert.md) is one example.
  - YELLOW: [Brimstone](Brimstone.md), [gypsum](Gypsum.md), [orthoclase](Orthoclase.md) and many other stones are YELLOW, as is the [fungiwood](Fungiwood.md) tree.
  - LGRAY: Lots of stones are LGRAY, such as [granite](Granite.md).
  - WHITE: Many stones are WHITE, such as [limestone](Limestone.md), or [rock salt](Rock_salt.md). The [tower-cap](Tower-cap.md) and [feather tree](Feather_tree.md) both produce WHITE wood.

See [color](Color.md#by-color) for a more complete list.

## Default Scheme

The default *Dwarf Fortress* color scheme is based off the 16 standard HTML colors, as discussed [here](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_colors#HTML_color_names). (Some have been renamed in DF, but the tones themselves are the same.)

These are broken up into 8 pairs, one darker, one lighter, as ordered below. When a color is flashing or a displayed tile is "dark/bright" according to the game display, these colors are the two that work "together" to create that contrast. Keep this in mind if creating a [custom color scheme](Color_scheme.md#custom-color-schemes), as described below. (See [Color](Color.md) for a more complete discussion.)

If you lose the default scheme and neglected to make a backup *(didn't we warn you to use "care and caution" when modding?)*, you can find the standard scheme below, without having to download DF again.

Open Dwarf Fortress/data/init/[colors.txt](Colors.txt.md), and copy the text as seen below over the values found at the very bottom of the file. Save that file, restart *Dwarf Fortress*, and you're set.

## Custom color schemes

<tbody><tr><td><div><a href="https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Mod_icon.png"><img alt="Mod" height="48" src="https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/1/1a/Mod_icon.png/48px-Mod_icon.png" width="48"/></a></div></td><td>This page includes <b><a href="../Category/Mods.md">mods</a></b>. Players who are not satisfied with the default color scheme can alter things to suit their aesthetic tastes. In order for any scheme changes to take effect, the "colors.txt" file must be altered and saved. This file can be found in the data/init/ directory of your *Dwarf Fortress* installation. The changes will not take effect until after you close and restart *Dwarf Fortress*.

The 16 colors are not fixed, except by their definitions in colors.txt. If you wanted to change YELLOW to something slightly brighter or darker, or more orange, or into deep purple, you can by changing the values listed under the label "YELLOW". Note that the color names are *case sensitive* - the color is "YELLOW", not "Yellow" or "yellow".

When designing a custom color, 3 "lights" of color are used: **R**ed, **G**reen, and **B**lue (RGB), on a scale of 0-255. The more light (the higher the value), the brighter the color; the less light (the lower the value), the darker. Using "light" is not the same as using "pigment" *(the standard "paint mixing" formulae we learned in school, where Magenta, Yellow, and Cyan are the 3 basic ingredients)* - M+C does not give "blue" in this format.

Since we're using Red, Green and Blue light, some form of those three colors is relatively easy to achieve - but look at examples of the other standard colors below to understand how they mix together and how to "shade" or "tint", or brighten or darken a color - or you can simply find a color below that you like and go from that, or use one of these [standard RGB colors](Color.md#color-tokens). Always use caution to avoid ending up with colors that are "too similar".

To change to a new color scheme, copy/paste a color list (or part of one or more) found below (or make your own up) over the existing scheme - don't worry, if you don't like it, the original, default scheme is listed above.

*(Note also that when posting screenshots to this wiki (and the forum boards), custom color schemes (or [tilesets](Tilesets.md)) can cause confusion with players who do not use those specific mixes. This wiki requests you use one of the default set-ups that come with the game to post screenshots here.)*
