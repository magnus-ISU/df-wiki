**Acolytes** are a minor [noble](Noble.md) position used by [elven](Elf.md) civilizations, appointed by the [druid](Druid.md). Each civilization will only have one of these, and they have no assigned responsibilities. The only thing notable about them is that they rise to the position of [druid](Druid.md) in the event the current one suffers from [the usual unfortunate mishaps.](War.md)

They are assigned by druids and thus functions as their designated succesor.

While they can be encountered in [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md), they generally will not show up in [fortress mode](Fortress_mode~2.md).
