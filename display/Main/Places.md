Example zones tab, displayed after selecting [![Ui P.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/c/c5/Ui_P.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_P.png)P

The **place information menu** is accessed from the main view by clicking the [![Ui P.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/c/c5/Ui_P.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_P.png)P menu icon or pressing Shift - P. This allows you to view related information on [zones](Zone.md), [locations](Location.md), [stockpiles](Stockpile.md), [workshops](Workshop.md), [farm plots](Farm_plot.md) and [siege engines](Siege_engine.md) by selecting the corresponding tabs.

This menu has replaced the **building list screen** from v0.47 of the game.
