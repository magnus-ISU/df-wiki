**Iron** is an important resource, needed for [steel](Steel.md), the best mundane [metal](Metal.md) for [weapons](Weapon.md) and [armor](Armor.md), and if steel availability is limited, iron is next best, though only slightly better than [bronze](Bronze.md). Iron is also a [fire](Fire-safe.md) and [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md) [material](Material.md) that can be used to make most anything.

Three [ores](Ore.md) contain iron: [hematite](Hematite.md), [magnetite](Magnetite.md), and [limonite](Limonite.md).

Iron does not rust in game, so there's no harm in leaving iron objects out in the rain or submerging them in [water](Water.md).

### Uses

- Melee weapons. Mostly useful for edged weapons; good for bolts as well. You can also make crossbows out of iron, but there are better metals.
- Armor. It's pretty good, though not the best.
- Construction, if you are so inclined.
- Good-quality [furniture](Furniture.md). It is quite heavy, however.
- Magma-safe mechanisms, though there are plenty of magma-safe [stones](Stones.md) that would work just as well, if you can get your hands on them.
- [Anvils](Anvil.md), which can only be made from iron or [steel](Steel.md).

### Production

Iron comes from three ores: [hematite](Hematite.md) (veins in sedimentary, small clusters in all other stone layers), [magnetite](Magnetite.md) (large clusters in sedimentary, small clusters in all other stone layers), and [limonite](Limonite.md) (veins in sedimentary only). Any of these iron ores can be smelted at a [smelter](Smelter.md) to produce four iron bars. If you have a standard smelter, a bar of [fuel](Fuel.md) will be necessary; magma smelters require no fuel to operate (though steel still needs it as a carbon source).

Iron will also be available through [goblinite](Goblinite.md), as well as the dwarven and human caravans.

### Alloys

Iron may be combined with other materials at a [smelter](Smelter.md), mostly for the production of [steel](Steel.md)

- Iron + [Flux](Flux~2.md) + [Fuel](Fuel.md) = [Pig iron](Pig_iron~2.md)
- Iron + [Pig iron](Pig_iron~2.md) + [Flux](Flux~2.md) + [Fuel](Fuel.md) = 2 × [Steel](Steel.md)

Note that [fuel](Fuel.md) is used as a reactant: even [magma smelters](Magma_smelter~2.md) need fuel for these reactions. A standard smelter will require two bars of fuel, one as a reactant and one to operate the furnace. This conforms with the process of carbonization of iron and [pig iron](Pig_iron~2.md) in ferrous metallurgy in real life.
