**Cassiterite** is the only [ore](Ore.md) of [tin](Tin.md). This ore can also be smelted into the [alloys](Alloy.md) [bronze](Bronze.md), [trifle pewter](Trifle_pewter~2.md), and [fine pewter](Fine_pewter.md).

Cassiterite is also the main component of [tin glaze](Tin_glaze.md).

It is found in [alluvial](Alluvial.md) layers as small [clusters](Cluster.md) and in [granite](Granite.md) as [veins](Vein.md).

### Smelting

Cassiterite is a required ingredient to make several types of metal alloy bars from ore at a [smelter](Smelter.md):

- [Tin](Tin.md) bars
- [Bronze](Bronze.md) bars - with [native copper](Native_copper.md), [malachite](Malachite.md), or [tetrahedrite](Tetrahedrite.md)
- [Trifle pewter](Trifle_pewter~2.md) bars - with [native copper](Native_copper.md), [malachite](Malachite.md) or [tetrahedrite](Tetrahedrite.md) *(takes 2 cassiterite)*
- [Fine pewter](Fine_pewter.md) bars - with [native copper](Native_copper.md), [malachite](Malachite.md) or [tetrahedrite](Tetrahedrite.md) *(takes 3 cassiterite)*

The above can also be made from [bars](Bar.md) of [tin](Tin.md) combined with bars of the appropriate metals. Note that [bismuth bronze](Bismuth_bronze~2.md) and [lay pewter](Lay_pewter~2.md), both of which require tin, cannot be made directly from cassiterite, but must be made using tin bars.

See [metal](Metal.md) for a full discussion of formulae and options.
