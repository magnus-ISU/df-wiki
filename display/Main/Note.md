**Notes** were comments that you could leave around your fortress. Instead, now many elements of the game can be renamed:

- Names and titles of all creatures
- [Squads](Squad.md)
- All buildings, not including constructions
- [Stockpiles](Stockpile.md)
- [Zones](Zone.md), [locations](Location.md), and [burrows](Burrows.md)

Notes were removed in 0.50 and later versions of Dwarf Fortress, however this feature has been reinstated with a [DFHack](DFHack.md) plugin.
