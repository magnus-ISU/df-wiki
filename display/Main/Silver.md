**Silver** is a mid-value [metal](Metal.md) which is useful for [weapons](Weapon.md), [ammunition](Ammunition.md), [furniture](Furniture.md), [decorations](Decoration.md), and other objects. Silver may be combined with other [metals](Metal.md) in a [magma smelter](Magma_smelter~2.md) or [smelter](Smelter.md) to produce the following [alloys](Metal.md):

- [Billon](Billon.md) = Silver + [Copper](Copper.md)
- [Electrum](Electrum.md) = Silver + [Gold](Gold.md)
- [Black bronze](Black_bronze.md) = Silver + [Gold](Gold.md) + 2× [Copper](Copper.md) <sup>†</sup>
- [Sterling silver](Sterling_silver~2.md) = 3× Silver + [Copper](Copper.md) <sup>†</sup>

<sup>†</sup> these reactions may only be performed with bars (not ores)

Despite its name, [nickel silver](Nickel_silver.md) does not contain any silver.

### Properties

Silver has the highest [density](Density.md) of all the weapon-grade metals; its additional [weight](Weight.md) enhances the performance of [bludgeoning](Attack_types.md#blunt-weapons) attacks such as with [maces](Mace.md), [mauls](Weapon.md#foreign-weapons), and [war hammers](War_hammer.md), though all of the normal metals perform about the same and the extra weight of silver may exhaust the wielder slightly faster.[\[1\]](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=181479) Meanwhile, a low shear strength means silver [edged](Edge.md) attacks such as with [axes](Axe.md), [spears](Spear.md), or [swords](Sword.md) are quite terrible compared to other metals. Silver also has somewhat poor yield values important for armor, and is more prone to [wear](Wear.md).

While silver swords were said to be the only blades capable of slaying [werewolves](Werebeast.md) in legends, werebeast types in *Dwarf Fortress* will be vulnerable to a random metal, and [steel](Steel.md) will tend to be more reliable.
