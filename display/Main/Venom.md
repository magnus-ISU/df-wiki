# Venom

**Venom** is a naturally-occurring poison that will cause a [syndrome](Syndrome.md) in certain creatures when certain conditions are met, typically when entering the [blood](Blood.md) via a [serpent man](Serpent_man.md) or [giant cave spider bite](Giant_cave_spider.md). It is produced by certain creatures according to their [raw file](Raw_file.md), and may be [extracted](Extracts.md) as a [liquid](Liquid.md) and stored in [vials](Vial.md).

Each specific animal's venom delivers a [specific syndrome](Syndrome.md#list-of-syndromes), which is a known combination of [symptoms](Symptom.md), some short-term, some long-term, and some (in rare cases) permanent (aka "chronic"). Some few syndromes (such as [gila monsters](Gila_monster.md)') are seen as mild annoyances, some (such as a [giant desert scorpions](Giant_desert_scorpion.md)') are a death sentence, but most are a mortal threat, at least. Of the wide variety of possible symptoms, one of the mildest is [nausea](Symptom.md#nausea) and vomiting, but even that can dehydrate a dwarf and take them out of combat while they seek to quench their thirst. The most immediately dangerous venoms cause [paralysis](Symptom.md#paralysis), which can stop the lungs, leading to suffocation. Most any bite that causes strong [pain](Symptom.md#pain) can in turn cause unconsciousness, leaving the victim helpless in combat, which is rarely survived. Longer term, [blisters](Symptom.md#blisters) can lead to deadly infections, and [necrosis](Symptom.md#necrosis) (which causes tissue to die) of any body part can also lead to eventual death, but the usual treatment, amputation, is not much better. Even [mild dizziness](Symptom.md#dizziness) can be a death sentence to any dwarf who works or travels near magma, water, or long falls (and do any *not*?). There are few venoms that *can't* kill an unlucky dwarf, one way or the other; if your fortress has many venomous creatures nearby, have some [coffins](Coffin.md) ready.

Currently, it is disputed whether or not venom can be administered, but it may still be purchased or sold.

Research has shown that in at least some circumstances, venom and other syndrome-inducing materials can be spattered or covered upon weapons and bolts, which may result in attacks inflicting the related syndromes. <sup>[\[1\]](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=106397)</sup>

Some [forgotten beasts](Forgotten_beast.md) and [titans](Titan.md) are generated with venom, which they can inject through bites or stingers, alternatively covering their bodies entirely, or making their [blood](Blood.md) toxic.
