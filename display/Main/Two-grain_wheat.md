**Two-grain wheat** is an [aboveground](Aboveground.md) [crop](Crop.md). It can either be [milled](Mill.md) into [two-grain wheat flour](Flour.md) or [brewed](Still.md) into [two-grain wheat beer](Alcohol.md).

Some [dwarves](Dwarves.md) [like](Preferences.md) two-grain wheat for its *beer*.
