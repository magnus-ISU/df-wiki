<br> Dwarf cancels task: Job item lost or destroyed.

A dwarf whose tasked item is no longer in the same tile as when their job was assigned will cancel the job with this message. Often, the "missing" item will be a scant few tiles away, and the job can be reissued referencing its new location. Occasionally, the item will have **actually** been destroyed (possibly as the result of a [cave-in](Cave-in.md) or [atom smashing](Dwarven_atom_smasher~2.md)).

While dwarves [will not directly move tasked items](Dwarf_cancels_Construct_Building%253A_Item_blocking_site.md), they may *indirectly* move them (such as by [channeling](Channel.md) out the floor underneath the item, disassembling a [building](Building.md) containing the item, or removing the [construction](Construction.md) beneath the item). Items may also be moved by natural forces such as [cave-ins](Cave-in.md) and [flowing](Flow.md) liquids, or by one of the many thieving creatures in *Dwarf Fortress*.

This error may also occur if the tasked item changes state--such as a [raw hide](Leather.md) becoming [rotten](Rot.md), or an [ice](Ice.md) boulder melting.
