**Wood burner** is the [skill](Skill.md) associated with the wood burning [labor](Labor.md), where [dwarves](Dwarf.md) turn [wood](Wood.md) into either [ash](Ash.md) or [charcoal](Charcoal.md) at a [wood furnace](Wood_furnace~2.md). Dwarves with high skill level accomplish these tasks more quickly. Typically, a wood stockpile and a bar/block stockpile are placed near the furnaces.

### How do I get dwarves to burn wood?

Wood burner is, somewhat tenuously, considered a farming-related job (because [ash](Ash.md) can be made into [potash](Potash.md), which fertilizes fields) and thus the labor is filed under Farming/*Related* labor menu, and not [furnace operating](Furnace_operator~2.md).
