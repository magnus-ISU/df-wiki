> *A tiny amphibian that lives in the trees.*

**Green tree frogs** are unremarkable [amphibious](Amphibious.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) found in [temperate](Temperate.md) freshwater [biomes](Biome.md). They can be captured by [trappers](Trapper.md) and turned into low-value [pets](Pet.md). All green tree frogs possess Legendary [skill](Skill.md) in [climbing](Climber.md).

Despite the description, they do not live in trees. They will not appear in maps during the winter.

Some [dwarves](Dwarves.md) [like](Preferences.md) green tree frogs for their *distinct mating call*.
