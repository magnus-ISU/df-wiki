> *A small reptile.*

**Lizards** are a species of [hateable](Hateable.md) [above ground](Above_ground.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) found in any area that is not [freezing](Biome.md), but will not be found in winter. They are known to eat [stockpiled](Stockpile.md) [food](Food.md), and some [dwarves](Dwarf.md) will get an unhappy [thought](Thought.md) from encountering one. They can be captured by [trappers](Trapper.md) and turned into [pets](Pet.md). All lizards possess Legendary [skill](Skill.md) in [climbing](Climber.md).

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) lizards for their *beauty*.
