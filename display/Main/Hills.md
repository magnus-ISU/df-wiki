**Hills** are a landform characterized by moderate rainfall and high drainage. Unlike [mountains](Mountain.md), hills can have one or more [soil](Soil.md) layers along with moderate amounts of [vegetation](Crops.md) and [trees](Tree.md). They are not a [biome](Biome.md) as such. Depending on other factors, they can be a subtype of [shrubland](Shrubland.md), [savanna](Savanna.md), and/or [grassland](Grassland.md), with the appropriate flora and fauna.
