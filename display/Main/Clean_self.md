# Clean self

The **clean self** [job](Job.md) is a special job in that it is enabled automatically and cannot be disabled. [Dwarves](Dwarf.md) that have been [contaminated](Contaminant.md), be it by dirt, grime, or [goblin](Goblin.md) [blood](Blood.md), will take a bit of time off at some point to [clean](Clean.md) themselves if possible; dwarves that haven't had a bath in a while will do so as well on occasion. They can do this with access to a water source, be it a [murky pool](Murky_pool.md), a [brook](Brook.md), or a [well](Well.md) (but not [rivers](River.md) as they will be swept away). In the absence of such an area, or if the water source(s) are [forbidden](Forbid.md), they will not perform this job.

Cleaning oneself gets rid of anything on the dwarf, and doing so with a bar of [soap](Soap.md) in addition to some water has a good chance of providing a happy [thought](Thought.md). It is most useful as preventative medicine: dwarves that clean themselves lower their internal dirtiness levels (even more when they use soap) and thus dramatically lower chances of an [infection](Health_care~2.md#infection) in case they are wounded. Many fortresses, especially ones in [evil](Evil.md) biomes, include a [bathhouse](Stupid_dwarf_trick.md#bathtub) in their design. This is important, because a non-military dwarf caught in a rain of repulsive goo will immediately run to clean themselves, usually by dashing in to the various rotting limbs of an undead creature.
