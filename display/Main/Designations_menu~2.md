[![Designation icons preview.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/1/16/Designation_icons_preview.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Designation_icons_preview.png)

The **Designations menu** can be accessed along the bottom center of the screen, and provides the means to mark tiles with various designations including [mining](Mining.md), [wood cutting](Wood_cutting.md), [plant gathering](Plant_gathering.md), [stone detailing](Smooth.md) as well as [removing](Removing.md) constructions, controlling [traffic](Traffic.md) and other miscellaneous designations.

To designate an area ([![Ui mb.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/2/25/Ui_mb.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_mb.png)b), select the desired designation from the menu, place your cursor over the first tile, click once, then drag your cursor over to another tile - which will create a rectangle, then click again. You can also choose to "paint" an area ([![Ui mB.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/3/34/Ui_mB.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_mB.png)B), which lets you specifically designate tiles one by one. Depending on the designation, this also works across [z-levels](Z-level.md), allowing 3-dimensional designations in the shape of a cuboid.

To cancel a designation, use Remove Designations ([![Ui x.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/bd/Ui_x.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_x.png)x).

All designations can be prioritized, with 1 being the highest priority and 7 being the lowest. Note that if two different designations have the same priority, one type may still be favored over the other (allthough such obeserved behavior could just be a result of different distances - in the maximum norm - to the tiles in question). They also can be assigned as "Blueprint only", which is similar to the job being suspended, as it marks tiles in a blue-colored square. This is useful for planning out designations and rooms without dwarves suddenly moving in to do anything yet.

Also note that for any and all designations (wood cutting, plant gathering, smoothing etc.) a dwarf currently doing such a job will when choosing the next job of that type always pick an accessible, designated tile (which has not been flagged as the current/next working tile of another dwarf) with the minimum distance (in the maximum norm) from the current position, regardless of how long the path to actually get there is. And when starting to do such a job the first tile is choosen by the same metric (minimum distance in the maximum norm). If more than one tile have the same distance then it is random which tile is picked first.

### Priority

[![Priority anim.gif](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/5/5b/Priority_anim.gif)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Priority_anim.gif)

Designations can be assigned a priority by clicking [![Designation priority 1.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/1/16/Designation_priority_1.png/25px-Designation_priority_1.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Designation_priority_1.png)-[![Designation priority 7.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/1/10/Designation_priority_7.png/25px-Designation_priority_7.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Designation_priority_7.png). By default, all designations are made with a priority of [![Designation priority 4.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/c/ca/Designation_priority_4.png/25px-Designation_priority_4.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Designation_priority_4.png), but higher priority jobs can be marked by using priorities [![Designation priority 1.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/1/16/Designation_priority_1.png/25px-Designation_priority_1.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Designation_priority_1.png)-[![Designation priority 3.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/b/b6/Designation_priority_3.png/25px-Designation_priority_3.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Designation_priority_3.png), and lower priority ones with [![Designation priority 5.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/3/35/Designation_priority_5.png/25px-Designation_priority_5.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Designation_priority_5.png)-[![Designation priority 7.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/1/10/Designation_priority_7.png/25px-Designation_priority_7.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Designation_priority_7.png). Note that [![Designation priority 1.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/1/16/Designation_priority_1.png/25px-Designation_priority_1.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Designation_priority_1.png) is the *highest* priority, and [![Designation priority 7.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/1/10/Designation_priority_7.png/25px-Designation_priority_7.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Designation_priority_7.png) the *lowest*.

When a dwarf has several jobs of different priority to do, the ones with higher priority are executed first, regardless of how convenient they are for the dwarf in question; a job marked [![Designation priority 1.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/1/16/Designation_priority_1.png/25px-Designation_priority_1.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Designation_priority_1.png) on the other side of the map will take precedence over a job marked [![Designation priority 2.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/a/a9/Designation_priority_2.png/25px-Designation_priority_2.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Designation_priority_2.png) right next to them.

Care should be taken not to set important tasks on too low of a priority, as many other incoming and existing tasks will take precedent over those lowest priority tasks, meaning those tasks will never get done unless their priority is changed manually.

### Blueprint mode

Designations can be placed in the default standard mode—the jobs are eligible to begin as soon as the game is unpaused—or in blueprint mode. Switch the mode of new designations with [![Ui M.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/a/a0/Ui_M.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_M.png)M. Blueprint mode designations appear cyan instead of beige.

Dwarves will not actually undertake blueprint mode designations. This can be used to plan future projects, and it offers a few advantages:

- There is no need to designate the entire project at once. Doubtful portions can be deferred until nearby terrain is revealed, for example.
- Blueprint designations will not disrupt jobs already underway.
- The full range of priorities is available, as opposed to delaying the project by forcing low priorities. Thus the new project can take priority over existing designations when it's ready, without the need to remove the old designations.

When it is time for your dwarves to begin a blueprint-mode project, designate a [![Ui Mk.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/f/f5/Ui_Mk.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_Mk.png)k standard toggle and choose an area that contains all the blueprint designations you wish, in all three dimensions. All blueprint-mode designations within will change to standard mode, and vice versa. The opposite ([![Ui ML.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/f/f2/Ui_ML.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_ML.png)L) can be used to temporarily deactivate existing standard designations as well.
