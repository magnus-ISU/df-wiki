**Night trolls** `Ñ` are a type of procedurally generated [night creature](Night_creature.md). They are supernatural monsters which kidnap [dwarves](Dwarf.md), [humans](Human.md), or [elves](Elf.md), transform them into spouses, and then mate with them to produce young. The children are all the same sex as the original night troll, never that of the spouse, except if they breed somehow in fortress mode. In [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md), night trolls and their spouses can be given as the targets of [quests](Quest.md). They are not related to normal [trolls](Troll.md).

Graphically, the default sprites that night trolls use will be of malformed, humanoid creatures that resemble the randomly-generated appearance they've been given. This same setup is used for other procedurally-generated creatures, such as [bogeymen](Bogeyman.md) and smaller [experiments](Experiment.md). The sprites night trolls use are smaller than those of other procedurally generated creatures, such as [demons](Demon.md), [angels](Angel.md) and [megabeasts](Megabeast.md).

### Characteristics

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/c/cb/Night_troll.jpg/310px-Night_troll.jpg)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Night_troll.jpg)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Night_troll.jpg](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Night_troll.jpg)

*Art by anonimus*

A number of different types of night trolls are procedurally generated at every world creation, similar to [bogeymen](Bogeyman.md) and [titans](Titan.md). Their numbers are normally dependent on world size, but can be directly controlled with [advanced world generation](Advanced_world_generation.md) – a world seed with "`Number of Night Troll Types`" set to `0` in advanced world generation will not have any night trolls in it. Not all night troll species are named as such – the possible names, apart from words indicating night, evil, or darkness, include bridegroom, crone, freak, groom, hag, horror, man, monster, ogre, ogress, troll, and woman. Night trolls belong to the [spheres](Sphere.md) of death and night, and will be named appropriately.

While procedurally generated, night trolls all possess certain characteristics in common: they are all [fanciful](Fanciful.md), evil-aligned humanoids with either extra or missing features; for example, large feathered women, or large skinless one-eyed men with horns and trunks. Some night trolls possess wings which give them flight, though they will only use them to fight adventurers who are also flying. Their description always gives their movement method (e.g. *moves with uneven, jerking motions*) and detailed descriptions of their skin (smooth, rough, warty, leathery, etc.). Their [lairs](Lair.md) are mounds or holes in the ground with [doors](Door.md) or [hatch covers](Hatch_cover~2.md). Depending on the night troll, they can contain various tools (knives, forks, meat cleavers), small amounts of discarded [coins](Coin.md) and [clothing](Clothing.md) from their victims, [corpses](Corpse.md), organs or skeletons of sentient creatures prepared as [food](Food.md), cauldrons of [blood](Blood.md) or [water](Water.md) with various food (meat, bones, full skeletons, vermin remains, rarely parts of armor), [bone meal](Bone_meal.md), and ground [vermin](Vermin.md), victim skulls as trophies, and even [querns](Quern.md). Most night troll lairs are inhabited by a single creature, but sometimes you'll encounter entire families of five or even ten. The night trolls' lairs, [according to Toady One](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=60554.msg1729406#msg1729406), have a unique tag that causes items placed there to not be scattered around or rot.

All night trolls possess Talented [skill](Skill.md) in [wrestling](Wrestling.md), [biting, fighting, striking, kicking, dodging](Combat_skill.md) and [observing](Observer.md), on top of possessing bonuses to all body [attributes](Attribute.md). Night trolls can [breathe underwater](Amphibious.md), [feel no exertion](No_Exert.md), [cannot be stunned](No_Stun.md) and are [immune to pain](No_Pain.md), nausea, dizziness and fevers. Night trolls need no food, water or sleep to survive, nor do they need to breathe, meaning they cannot be drowned or strangled. Interestingly, their raws also reveal an [immunity to traps](Trapavoid.md), the ability of [destroying buildings](Building_destroyer.md) and a [pet](Pet.md) value of 2,000; due to existing in [fortress mode](Fortress_mode~2.md) only if the player embarks on their lair, night trolls almost never get to destroy buildings or interact with [traps](Trap.md), and they don't possess the necessary tokens to be [trained](Animal_trainer.md). All night trolls can sense unseen living creatures as ☼ in a similar manner to [vampires](Vampire.md), though this cannot normally be observed, as night trolls are not a playable race.

The potential size range of night trolls is unknown, but it is known to vary greatly, from slightly bigger than a [dwarf](Dwarf.md) to as large as an adult [deer](Deer.md). Spouses are sometimes smaller than their captors, but this depends on the night troll species. Adventurers are able to [smell](Smell.md) night trolls with o, with them being described as smelling variously like "death", "cooked flesh" or "bug innards".

Dwarves may [like](Preferences.md) a species of night troll for their *macabre ways*.

### Reproduction

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/7/7a/Adv_night_troll.png/200px-Adv_night_troll.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Adv_night_troll.png)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Adv_night_troll.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Adv_night_troll.png)

An adventurer discovering a night troll in its cave.

As stated above, night trolls must kidnap a dwarf, human, or elf and transform their victim into another night troll of the opposite sex in order to reproduce. The kidnapping troll can be either male or female, each species of night troll has one sex as the kidnapper and the other sex as the spouse. When the kidnapped victim is transformed into a night troll, they will share the same name as their captor, but with the inclusion of something indicating their spousal status. For example, a "Dusk Brute" will turn its victim into a "Dusk Brute consort" or "Spouse of the Dusk Brute", with the exact same appearance as the captor.

If night trolls give birth in fortress mode, the children will be friendly. Night trolls will not haul their babies, except if dfhacked into being a citizen of your fort.

### Fighting night trolls

Night trolls are somewhat challenging opponents in battle, but not especially dangerous if encountered alone, though one is very capable of dispatching the overconfident. Since they are fairly large, wrestling them is quite difficult, though still possible, and they are a bit harder to harm with blunt attacks than humans. In addition, they are immune to pain and do not need to breathe, so they cannot be made to pass out and will not suffocate.

Night trolls can, rarely, wield one of the butchery [tools](Tool.md) generated in their [lairs](Lair.md), like boning knives or meat cleavers. Usually they will attack unarmed and can, due to their large size, cause serious harm with their punches, kicks, and especially, bites. In fact, teeth are usually their most dangerous weapon, so if the night troll in question is holding a cutting tool, you'd best *not* disarm it, since they always prioritize armed attacks. Avoid the spine in particular: this is never a mortal [wound](Wound.md) for a night troll, since they don't need lung function to live; all it will do is make them unable to use their legs or, if they have any, wings – both of which can already be disabled with aimed blows – and then leave them forced to always use their *strongest* attack.

Night trolls can die from blood loss, brain damage, beheading and bisection (cutting off the lower body). They never wear clothing or [armor](Armor.md) and so are quite susceptible to having limbs cut off – hacking and slashing attacks work remarkably well against them. Be especially wary of trolls and other night creatures that possess horns.

Indeed, the loss of their upper or middle spinal nervous tissue is no emblem of handicap, but rather the catalyst for an epiphany of clarity in combat, as well as a show of just how dedicated the night troll is to utterly stamping their metaphorical boot on the hopes of any dwarf, elf, human, or goblin that is stupid enough to get in their way (and then proceeding to smash and rend any life out of them before promptly making them into steaks for their psychotic tea parties). The titans could only wish to be able to emulate such unmistakable malice and ill will.
