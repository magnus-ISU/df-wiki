**Sheets** are used in the production of [scrolls](Scroll.md) and [books](Books.md). They can be made from either paper or parchment, and are stored under Sheet (top [stockpile](Stockpile.md) category).

Sheets have [quality](Quality.md) levels, which don't seem to affect the [value](Value.md) of resulting books, however.<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Sheet&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>

For more information, see: [Paper industry](Paper_industry.md).

### Paper

Paper is made from [plant fibers](Plant_fiber.md) mashed into [slurry](Slurry.md) (at a [quern](Quern.md)/[millstone](Millstone.md)) and then pressed on a [screw press](Screw_press.md). Both of these actions use the [papermaking](Papermaking.md) labor.

### Papyrus

Papyrus sheets are made directly from the [papyrus sedge](Papyrus_sedge.md) plant (which grows in any [tropical](Tropical.md) [wetland](Wetland.md)) at a [farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop~2.md). Note that the simple term "papyrus" can refer to *either* the plant *or* the paper.

### Parchment

Parchment is made from [skin](Skin.md) and [milk of lime](Milk_of_lime.md) at a [tanner's shop](Tanner's_shop~2.md), and has a value of 50☼. Parchment made from the [skin](Skin.md) of a [cow](Cow.md) is called vellum. <br><br>
