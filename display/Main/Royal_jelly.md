# Royal jelly

**Royal jelly** is a product of the [beekeeping industry](Beekeeping_industry~2.md) collected from an artificial bee [hive](Hive.md). Royal jelly is stored in [jugs](Jug.md), and can only be used as a [liquid](Liquid.md) ingredient in [prepared meals](Prepared_meal.md). Due to a bug, dwarves may store royal jelly jugs as finished goods; combined with a [cook](Cook.md)'s aversion to liquid ingredients, it is very rare to see royal jelly cooked at all. That isn't necessarily a bad thing, though, since royal jelly is only available in single-unit stacks and has the same base value as most other foods.

The contents of a [hive](Hive.md) with a colony of [honey bees](Honey_bee.md) that is toggled to "gather any products" will be harvested as part of the [beekeeping industry](Beekeeping_industry~2.md). In order to collect the royal jelly, you must have a dwarf with the [beekeeping](Beekeeper.md) labor active and empty [jugs](Jug.md). A [honeycomb](Honeycomb.md) will also be produced.

Royal jelly and [honey](Honey.md) are the edible products of bees. In real life, it is used as a nutritional substance for larvae.

### Bugs

- Jugs of royal jelly may be stored in bins as finished goods, preventing its use in the food industry.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[4229](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=4229)</sup>
- Collecting royal jelly may require more than 1 jug in storage to begin.
- Cooks will only cook jelly and other fluids as a last resort, instead preferring to cook solid foods with solid foods.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[2393](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=2393)</sup> For more information, see the [kitchen](Kitchen.md#bugs) page.
