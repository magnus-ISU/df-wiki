> *A tiny arthropod that walks tilted upright on two large legs. Six spined projections radiate outward from its head shield.*

**Mimetaster** is an [extinct](Extinct.md) [aquatic](Aquatic.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) from the [Devonian](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devonian) era, found in [temperate](Temperate.md) [oceans](Ocean.md) and saltwater [pools](Pool.md). They might be<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Mimetaster&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup> usable for the [fishing industry](Fishing_industry.md), but only by [capturing a live fish](Fishery.md), rather than normal fishing, and also [cookable](Kitchen.md) without cleaning.

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) mimetaster for their *tilted walk*.
