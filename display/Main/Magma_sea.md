# Magma sea

The **magma sea** is a large, multi-level "sea" of [magma](Magma.md) that spans the entire world. These can typically be found by around Z level -120 (though this seems to vary widely (minimum -5, maximum -450 z level)<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Magma_sea&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>. The magma sea is always found below the last [cavern](Cavern.md) level.

It is not uncommon to find multiple z-levels of pillars of [raw adamantine](Raw_adamantine.md) in the magma sea (in fact, a 3×3 embark square guarantees at least one).

In addition, around and under the magma sea is [semi-molten rock](Semi-molten_rock.md), which is impassable. There are [a few](Hidden_Fun_Stuff.md) [exceptions](Underworld_spire.md), however.

Any items or natural rock walls dropped into the magma sea (specifically, onto "magma flow" tiles above semi-molten rock) will vanish without a trace.

Stockpiles may not be placed on a magma flow tile. However, the magma flow tile can be "destroyed" by creating a construction on top of it and removing the construction.
