**Unconscious** [![Status unconscious icon.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/1/1f/Status_unconscious_icon.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Status_unconscious_icon.png) is the state wherein a [creature](Creature.md) is unable to move, act, or perceive sight or sound. It is noted as **Unconscious** in the Overview screen of a selected creature, but does *not* appear as a [health problem](Wound.md) in the Health -\> Status screen.

Creatures that are [sleeping](Sleep.md) are unconscious, though being unconscious is different from performing the [rest](Rest.md) action as dwarves that are unconscious will not heal from their injuries. [Syndromes](Syndrome.md) can also cause periodic unconsciousness as one of their symptoms, and creatures caught in the dust of a [cave-in](Cave-in.md) are momentarily knocked unconscious.

Creatures may fall unconscious during [combat](Combat.md), most often due to the extreme [pain](Symptom.md#pain) of sustained injuries whereupon the creature **gives in to pain.** Attacks that damage a creature's bones are highly likely to cause it to fall unconscious in this way due to the high amount of pain that [bone fractures](Wound.md) cause. The amount of pain required to knock out a creature is based on its [toughness](Attribute.md#toughness) [attribute](Attribute.md).

Any unconscious creature can be caught in a [trap](Trap.md), including creatures that are normally [immune](Trapavoid.md) to them. This includes the player's own dwarves in [fortress mode](Fortress_mode~2.md), who might decide to take a nap they won't wake up from on a tile with 10 [giant axe blades](Trap_component.md).

If a dwarf is stuck in a state of endless unconsciousness, they can still be awoken by [expelling](Emigration.md) them.<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Unconscious&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>
