**Tan sand**, simply known as **sand** in the game, is one of the several types of [sand](Sand.md) that can be found in [soil layers](Soil_layer.md). Tan sand layers occasionally contain an [aquifer](Aquifer.md).

It can be gathered into [bags](Bag.md) and used for the purpose of [glassmaking](Glassmaking.md), and can also be used for [farming](Farming.md) just like any other [soil](Soil.md) layer.

Sand always appears as `≈` and `~` and cannot become furrowed like other soil.
