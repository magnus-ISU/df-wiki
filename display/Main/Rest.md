**Rest** is a [job](Job.md) in [fortress mode](Fortress_mode~2.md) that is given to [dwarves](Dwarves.md) too badly injured to do anything else. Generally, they lie where they fall until they are rescued and put in a [bed](Bed.md)--though they may be able to walk to a bed in a [hospital](Hospital.md) for certain [injuries](Wound.md), such as badly mangled arms. The injured dwarf will usually remain on the ground or in bed until they are either fully healed, or dead. A dwarf's reaction to a Rest period varies according to [personality](Personality.md) -- some will feel contented, others restless.

### Healing

The Rest job is the way that dwarves heal injury. If you see a dwarf is resting, it means they are healing naturally. This is different from the [Unconscious](Unconscious.md) status, where the dwarf is simply asleep, or has fainted from pain or injury - Rest is simply recuperating in bed. A dwarf must be resting to heal. If the dwarf is too badly injured to rest and keeps falling unconscious instead, they are likely doomed to a [fun](Fun.md) ending.

### Interference with jobs

Resting happens randomly; when an injured dwarf that is capable of moving is doing a job, you will see the message "**Urist Mcinjured cancels X: Resting injury**" and the dwarf will go to their bed and Rest to heal. This is automatic and cannot be controlled.
