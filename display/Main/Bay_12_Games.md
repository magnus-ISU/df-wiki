[Bay 12 Games](http://www.bay12games.com/) is a game company owned by [Tarn Adams](Toady_One.md) and his brother [Zach Adams](ThreeToe.md). It currently develops and publishes *[Dwarf Fortress](Dwarf_Fortress.md)*, and has also created several ASCII games.

Its motto is *"Beyond Quality."*

[Kitfox Games](Kitfox_Games.md) is responsible for contracting the new additions to the team for art, audio, and programming since the [Premium](Premium.md) update. ([Summer 2024 FAQ](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/975370/view/4237410439220288178?l=english)) They are listed, among others, in /data/credits.txt.
