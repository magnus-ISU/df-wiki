A **figurine** ([![Figurine sprite.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/f/f9/Figurine_sprite.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Figurine_sprite.png) / `Å`) is a [trade good](Trade_good.md) made at a [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop~2.md) or at a [metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md) under any particular category. Once queued, a figurine may be customised by pressing [![Mag glass icon.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/4/47/Mag_glass_icon.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Mag_glass_icon.png) on the task in the workshop's job list. Here, a specific player-defined image or civilisation-related artwork may be commissioned. They are like a small handheld [statue](Statue.md) in some senses.

Dwarf civilians may claim ownership of figurines, and will carry them around much like how they wear [jewelry](Jewelry.md). While they use one hand to hold them, it doesn't appear to interfere with any [jobs](Job.md) requiring hands. A dwarf holding a figurine may use it for self-defense in [combat](Combat.md), making use of the [Misc. Object User](Combat_skill.md) [skill](Skill.md).

On rare occasions, a gem figurine, instead of a large gem, can be made at a [jeweler's workshop](Jeweler's_workshop~2.md). Similar to [engravings](Engraving.md), they can show historic events, depict creatures, venerate religious figures, or list something about a person the [craftsdwarf](Craftsdwarf.md) knows.
