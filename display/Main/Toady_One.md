**Toady One** (or just **Toady**) is the nickname used by **Tarn Adams**, co-designer and programmer of *[Dwarf Fortress](Dwarf_Fortress.md)*. He can be reached at [About Bay 12 Games](http://www.bay12games.com/about.html) or the [Bay 12 Games Forum](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/).

Tarn Adams has a Ph.D in Mathematics from Stanford University. His dissertation was entitled *[Flat Chains in Banach Spaces](http://searchworks.stanford.edu/view/6058597)*.

In April 2009, Toady was adopted by [Scamps](Scamps.md).

The other designer is Zach Adams ([ThreeToe](ThreeToe.md)), his older brother.
