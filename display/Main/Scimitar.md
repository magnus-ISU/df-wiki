A **scimitar** is an [edged](Attack_types.md) [weapon](Weapon.md) with a curved blade that is very similar to a [short sword](Short_sword.md). In fact, the only differentiating factor appears to be the contact area of the pommel, which is half the size of those on a short sword.

Scimitars use and train the [swordsdwarf](Swordsdwarf.md) skill. As foreign weapons, dwarves cannot [forge](Forge.md) scimitars, limiting supply to whatever low-[quality](Quality.md) specimens can be traded from human [caravans](Caravan.md) or scavenged from invaders. All dwarves can equip scimitars, though, like short swords, [a tiny fraction must use two hands](Weapon.md#size). Given their marked similarity to short swords, your dwarves are almost certainly better served by equipping high-quality short swords.
