**Glob** is a broad classification in the [stocks](Stocks.md) menu, used for a variety of amorphous solids. [Rock nut paste](Quarry_bush~2.md), [fat](Fat.md), [tallow](Tallow.md) (processed fat), [slurry](Slurry.md), usable [wax](Wax.md), and [magma](Magma.md) are all types of glob.

### Fatty

Most fleshy [creatures](Creature.md) have [layers](Tissue_definition_token.md) of [fat](Fat.md) to store energy in. When [butchered](Butcher.md), the creature will yield a number of globs of fat based on its body size and proportions.

A [cook](Cook.md) can render fat into useable [tallow](Tallow.md) at a [kitchen](Kitchen.md). Tallow can then be used to make [soap](Soap.md) or as another ingredient in [prepared meals](Prepared_meal.md).

Tallow and fat, along with [wax](Wax.md), are stored together in food [stockpiles](Stockpile.md) under the "Fat" category.

### Paste

[Plant](Plant.md)-based paste and [slurry](Slurry.md) is milled at the requisite workstation, either at a [quern](Quern.md) or a [powered](Power.md) [millstone](Millstone.md). These materials are intermediaries for later tasks at the [screw press](Screw_press.md).

A [miller](Miller.md) can grind [rock nuts](Quarry_bush~2.md) and a small variety of other [oil-bearing](../Category/Pressable.md) [seeds](Seed.md) using the "mill seeds/nuts to paste" job, with seed paste needing to have [oil](Oil.md) pressed from it to serve as a [soap](Soap.md) component.

Most [thread](Thread.md)-bearing plants (not counting [papyrus sedge](Papyrus_sedge.md)) must be processed into [slurry](Slurry.md) at a mill before they can be [pressed](Screw_press.md) into [paper](Paper.md) . The "mash plants into slurry" job is performed by a [papermaker](Papermaker.md).

Slurry and paste are stored in food [stockpiles](Stockpile.md) under the "Paste" category.

### Pressed

A [presser](Presser.md) working at a [screw press](Screw_press.md) generates [press cakes](Press_cake.md) as a byproduct of pressing plants and seed paste into [oil](Oil.md). Press cakes and pomaces are unremarkable ingredients, only used as filler for a [prepared meal](Prepared_meal.md).

A presser can also press a [honeycomb](Honeycomb.md) to receive [wax](Wax.md) and [honey](Honey.md), producing a raw material for [wax workers](Wax_worker.md) to make into [finished goods](Finished_goods~2.md).

Press cakes and pomaces are stored in food [stockpiles](Stockpile.md) under the "Pressed Material" category.

### Other Globs

Liquid items that [freeze](Temperature.md) or re-solidify will become a glob.

Creature [interactions](Interaction.md), such as that of the [magma crab](Magma_crab.md)'s molten [basalt](Basalt.md) spit, can launch globs of material.

If a glob is `[EDIBLE_RAW]`, then it can be [eaten](Food.md) in [Adventure mode](Adventure_mode~2.md). This property is used by certain [mythical substances](Mythical_substance.md), but can also apply to paste and [press cakes](Press_cake.md).
