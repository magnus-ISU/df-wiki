A range of old buckets.

**Buckets** are handheld vessels made of [wood](Wood.md) at a [carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop~2.md) or [metal](Metal.md) at a [metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md) or [magma forge](Magma_forge~2.md).

They are used for carrying [water](Water.md), filling designated [ponds](Pond.md), making [lye](Lye.md), milking [animals](Animals.md), cleaning wounded dwarves, and giving water to thirsty dwarves who are wounded or busy. Milk and lye can also be *stored* in buckets, but dwarves will prefer to empty the buckets into suitable [barrels](Barrel.md). A bucket is also needed for the construction of a [well](Well.md), [soap maker's workshop](Soap_maker's_workshop.md), [ashery](Ashery.md), or [dyer's shop](Dyer's_shop.md).

Buckets of all types are stored in furniture [stockpiles](Stockpile.md).

[Metal](Metal.md) buckets are one of the few objects made with [blacksmithing](Blacksmith.md) that uses 1 [bar](Bar.md), with [blocks](Block.md) being the other. This makes them useful for training blacksmiths, as the buckets can be melted down for a single bar, and training can occur even when metals are scarce.

In [temperatures](Temperature.md) below freezing, such as within [glacier](Glacier.md) layers, water will freeze inside buckets, rendering them unusable (and disabling wells, which will show a "bucket full" message). To avoid that, build wells and [hospitals](Hospital.md) (the main destination of water) down below the glacial [ice](Ice.md), in its earthen layers. To recover a frozen bucket, [dump](Dump.md) the ice within it.

In order to empty a filled bucket at a specific location, one can construct and place a [display furniture](Display_furniture.md) at the desired location. Then, assign the desired liquid(s) to this display and deconstruct the furniture. The liquid spills on the ground upon deconstruction.

### Trivia

- Sometimes, rather than acquiring menacing spikes, hanging rings, bands, encrustation or an image, a bucket will instead gain a material for its handle when decorated.
