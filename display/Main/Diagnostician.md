**Diagnostician** is the skill associated with the [diagnosis](Diagnosis.md) [labor](Labor.md), as well as the hospital [occupation](Occupation.md) associated with it. Diagnosers are responsible for deciding how a dwarf who is hurt should be treated at a [hospital](Hospital.md).

Diagnostician [skill level](Skill.md) is related to the speed at which the labor is completed, and it also affects the probability of identifying any active [syndromes](Syndrome.md). It is the only relevant skill of the [chief medical dwarf](Chief_medical_dwarf.md) noble position. Graphically, diagnosticians wear all black clothes.

Contrary to popular belief, an unskilled diagnoser will **not** misdiagnose injuries and prescribe inappropriate treatments.

Diagnosers determine the freshest hell unleashed on your fortress' injured as part of the [health care industry](Health_care~2.md). The diagnostician skill is also used by [scholars](Scholars.md) when thinking about medical science-related [topics](Topics.md):
