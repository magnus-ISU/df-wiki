# Orthogonal

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Orthogonal) &middot; revision 303513 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-12 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Main/Orthogonal.wiki)*

---
**Orthogonal**, or **orthogonally**, is a technical word used to mean "not diagonally". It's shorthand for "East-West or North-South" or *(for 2 dimensions)* "up or down or left or right".

```
   Diagram 1:
    xox
    oCo
    xox
```

> - The center tile, C, is orthogonal to the four o tiles.<br>
> - Each o tile is orthogonal to the center C tile, plus two x tiles.<br>
> - The x tiles are all orthogonal to two o tiles.<br>
> - No o tiles are orthogonal to each other, nor are any x tiles orthogonal to each other or the C tile.

<br> In the domain of Dwarf Fortress, this concept is crucial in many aspects of [construction](Construction.md), fluid [pressure](Pressure.md), and other areas. Especially in [above-ground construction](Tower.md), where a tile not connected orthogonally to something either cannot be built, or will [collapse](Collapse.md).

These █'s are all connected orthogonally to at least 1 other █. If this was constructed walls or flooring, it would hold up:

```
  ██████   ███
    ██     █
     ███████
```

<br> These █'s are *not* all connected orthogonally to another █ - some connections are only diagonal. If this was constructed walls or flooring, some parts would [collapse](Collapse.md), or not be legal to construct:

```
  ███ ██    ██
    ██     █
      ██████
```
