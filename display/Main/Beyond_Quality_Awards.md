<br> The **Beyond Quality Awards** are a set of awards that were given out by [Bay 12 Games](Bay_12_Games.md) to various community members and games other than [Dwarf Fortress](Dwarf_Fortress.md), as part of the Dwarf Fortress 20th Anniversary Show held on August 5 2026 ([Twitch VOD](https://www.twitch.tv/videos/2838060900); [YouTube VOD](https://youtu.be/-X8KEqHdekE)).

| Category | Description | Winner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Origins Award | The best game that influenced Dwarf Fortress. | [Angband](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angband_(video_game)) |
| Storyteller Award | For the most memorable Dwarf Fortress stories. | [Kruggsmash](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCaifrB5IrvGNPJmPeVOcqBA) |
| Best Roguelike Award | The best roguelike according to the Adams brothers. | [Caves of Qud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caves_of_Qud) |
| Best Descendant Award | The best game inspired by Dwarf Fortress. | [RimWorld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RimWorld) |
| Inside Scoop Award | For contribution to the popularization of Dwarf Fortress. | [BlindiRL](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCcqk8pdIsCFU32ofbjJkzOg) |
| Moment in Time Award | For the best documentary work about Dwarf Fortress. | [Noclip](https://youtube.com/channel/UC0fDG3byEcMtbOqPMymDNbw) |
| Outpost Liaison Award | For contributions to community moderation outside of the [Bay 12 Forums](Bay_12_Forums.md). | [clinodev](https://www.reddit.com/user/clinodev/) |
| Wizardry Award | For contributions to improving the Dwarf Fortress gaming experience. | [DFHack](DFHack.md) |
| Hidden Gem Award | The best recent little-known game. | [Shadows of the Forbidden Gods](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1741640/Shadows_of_Forbidden_Gods/) |
