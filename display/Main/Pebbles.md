**Pebbles** are a naturally-occurring [map tile](Map_tile.md) found commonly across the surface of the world near [rivers](River.md) and [brooks](Brook.md), and occasionally on [beaches](Beach.md). They are the same stone type as the substrate; for example, if the first stone layer beneath [soil](Soil.md) is [basalt](Basalt.md), then pebbles will be composed of basalt. Pebbles may be found near [boulders](Boulder.md) of the same substrate.

Unlike [boulders](Boulder.md), pebbles cannot be designated for [smoothing](Smoothing.md). (This may be a bug)

In [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md), a pebble tile is an unlimited source of small rocks, which can be useful for [throwing](Thrower.md) and [knapping](Knapper.md).
