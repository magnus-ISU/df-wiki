A **table** is a piece of [furniture](Furniture.md) necessary for [dwarves](Dwarf.md) to eat [food](Food.md) in comfort. A table is also needed to define a [dining room](Dining_room~2.md) and serve as an operating surface in a [hospital](Hospital.md). Tables are also a component to build [traction benches](Traction_bench~2.md).

Tables can be created from one [wood](Wood.md) log at a [Carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop~2.md), one [stone](Stone.md) at a [Mason's workshop](Mason's_workshop~2.md), three metal [bars](Bar.md) at a [Metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md), or one bag of [sand](Sand.md) at a [Glass furnace](Glass_furnace.md).

#### Dining

A table cannot be used without a chair, and a chair without a table is insufficient for avoiding a negative thought for "ate with no table".

If more than one chair is placed around a single table, other dwarves *will* sit down to eat but will receive [thoughts](Thought.md) for "eating at a crowded table". Their reaction, positive or negative, depends on their [gregariousness](Personality_facet.md#gregariousness), [bashfulness](Personality_facet.md#bashful), [cheerfulness](Personality_facet.md#cheer-propensity), [orderliness](Personality_facet.md#orderliness), and [decorum](Personality_value.md#decorum). There is no corresponding thought for eating alone, so building tables with only one chair prevents potential [stress](Stress.md).

Dwarves primarily use tables to eat [food](Food.md), avoiding the negative [thought](Thought.md) for eating without a proper table. Since dwarves first try to sit down and only then check whether there's a table to put their food on, the proper placement of chairs is more important for proper dining. Tables without an adjacent chair will simply not be used for eating; if two tables are adjacent to a single chair, one will go unused. Chairs without adjacent tables, however, will cause unhappy thoughts whenever dwarves sit on them to eat, and a single table with multiple adjacent chairs can also lead to unhappy thoughts if two dwarves sit on the chairs: only one can put their food on the table, the other will have to eat from their lap and will complain about having no table.

An unassigned [dining room](Dining_room~2.md) will be used by any dwarves without an assigned dining room, resulting in positive [thoughts](Thought.md) scaled to the [quality](Zone.md#quality-and-value) of the dining room. Creating a high-value dining room is one of the easiest ways to give your dwarves a happy thought. Note, however, that dwarves with a table (or chair) in their [quarters](Quarters.md) may opt to eat there instead, so it is generally best to only to do so when [required](Noble.md#needs) by [nobles](Noble.md).

#### Other uses

In a [hospital](Hospital.md) zone, tables are used during [surgery](Surgery.md) as an operating surface. Multiple dwarves can be operated on at the same time on one table *(this may be a bug).*

Tables are also used as an input, along with [mechanism](Mechanism.md) and [ropes](Rope.md), at the [Mechanic's workshop](Mechanic's_workshop~2.md) to make a [traction bench](Traction_bench~2.md). The quality of each component is not reflected in the quality of the traction bench, and only the material of the table is used as the material for the bench.
