The **ashery** is a [workshop](Workshop.md) used to make [lye](Lye.md), [potash](Potash.md), and [milk of lime](Milk_of_lime.md). To build an ashery, you need a [block](Block.md)\*, a [barrel](Barrel.md), a [bucket](Bucket.md), and someone with the [potash making](Potash_maker.md) or [lye making](Lye_maker.md) [labor](Labor.md) enabled.

> (\* Notably, this is distinct from most other workshops, in that you cannot use a rough [stone](Stone.md) or [log](Log.md) to build an ashery, it *must* be a block of some sort. The block does not need to be [fire-safe](Fire-safe.md).)

Lye is one of the main ingredients for [soap](Soap.md), which is used for [cleaning](Cleaning.md) [wounds](Wound.md) and the fortress, while milk of lime is used to make [parchment](Parchment.md) [sheets](Sheet.md) out of raw animal [skins](Skin.md) for the [paper industry](Paper_industry.md). [Potash](Potash.md) is used for fertilizing [farms](Farm_plot.md), allowing an increased output of [plants](Plants.md), as well as for the creation of [pearlash](Pearlash.md), necessary for [clear glass](Clear_glass.md) and [crystal glass](Crystal_glass.md).

### Tasks

- Make lye
- Make potash from lye
- Make potash from ash
- Make milk of lime
