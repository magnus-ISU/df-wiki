# ASCII art reward

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/ASCII_art_reward) &middot; revision 258264 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-11 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Main/ASCII_art_reward~2.wiki)*

---
Previously, when you sent a [donation](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Dwarf_Fortress_Wiki:Site_support&action=edit&redlink=1) to [Bay 12 Games](Bay_12_Games.md), you could choose between two rewards: a Crayon Art Reward or an ASCII Art Reward. The ASCII art was a small scene made of a few [ASCII](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASCII) characters, along with a unique story written by [ThreeToe](ThreeToe.md) happening in the *Dwarf Fortress* world. Each scene had a chance to appear later in some form in one of [ThreeToe's Stories](http://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/dev_story.html). If the same donor gave more than one time, it was also possible that the bits of ASCII Art followed each other and formed a story.

Each piece of ASCII Art Reward belongs to the donor who received it, but sharing them for posterity might be a good thing.

On February 1st, 2020 they announced that they would not be doing rewards after that month.
