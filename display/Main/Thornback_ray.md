> *A small winged fish found in shallow seas.*

**Thornback rays** are a type of aquatic [vermin](Vermin.md) found in [temperate](Temperate.md) and [tropical](Tropical.md) [oceans](Ocean.md) year-round, and are a ready source of [food](Food.md) when [cleaned](Fish_cleaning.md) at a [fishery](Fishery.md).

Some [dwarves](Dwarves.md) [like](Preferences.md) thornback rays for their *back spines*.
