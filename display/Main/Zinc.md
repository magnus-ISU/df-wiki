**Zinc** is an uncommon, low-value [metal](Metal.md) primarily useful in the creation of [alloys](Alloy.md). It is [smelted](Smelter.md) from [sphalerite](Sphalerite.md). Note that, unlike [sphalerite](Sphalerite.md) itself, refined zinc is **not** [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md), or even fire-safe, and will melt in a grass fire. Zinc is the [lightest](Density.md) of the low value metals, slightly lighter than [tin](Tin.md), but still much heavier than rare [aluminum](Aluminum.md).

Zinc may be combined with other metals at a smelter to form the following alloys:

- [Brass](Brass.md) = Zinc + [Copper](Copper.md)
- [Nickel silver](Nickel_silver.md) = Zinc + [Copper](Copper.md) + 2x [Nickel](Nickel.md)
