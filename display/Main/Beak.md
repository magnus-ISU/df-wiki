A **beak** is a [body part](Body_parts.md) that can be generated from killing an animal, but not from [butchering](Butcher.md). Also, beaks, unlike tails or heads, can not be butchered or used in any way, but may be stored in [refuse stockpiles](Refuse_stockpile.md).

There has been a [report](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Dwarf_Fortress_Wiki_talk:Improvement_Drive#New_Article.3F) of a beak being claimed for use during a [strange mood](Strange_mood.md); the beak will be treated like regular [bone](Bone.md) and be listed as such in the finished [artifact](Artifact.md).
