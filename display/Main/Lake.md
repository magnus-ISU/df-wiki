A **lake** is a [biome](Biome.md); a large area covered with fairly deep [water](Water.md). Lakes can be filled with fresh, salt or brackish water. They often have slopes leading into the water, making them ideal for learning how to swim in [adventure mode](Adventure_mode~2.md).

The settings you choose during [world generation](World_generation.md) have a large impact on how many lakes will be formed. For example, a Large Island world will typically have many fewer lakes than a Large Region world.

There are six types of lakes, divided by climate and the salinity of the water.

They are listed in the following table:

|  | **Freshwater** | **Brackish water** | **Saltwater** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Temperate** | Temperate freshwater lake | Temperate brackish lake | Temperate saltwater lake |
| **Tropical** | Tropical freshwater lake | Tropical brackish lake | Tropical saltwater lake |
