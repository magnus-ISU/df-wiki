<br> **Monk** is a profession for some inhabitants of generated [sites](Site.md), like [towns](Town.md). Some monks show up as [visitors](Visitor.md) in Fortress mode, and [agents](Agent.md) sometimes try to pose as monks. Monks are recruited into [monastic orders](Religion.md), and when there are enough monks in an order; they will build a [monastery](Monastery.md). Monks are [shown in white](Unit_type_token.md#monk).

In adventure mode, you will occasionally see monks passing by when walking through a [human](Human.md) [hamlet](Hamlet.md). Following these monks will eventually lead to finding them standing stock-still in the middle of a field, unmoving, but still willing to talk to you.

### Fortress mode

Monks will not [migrate](Immigration.md) to your fortress in this version.
