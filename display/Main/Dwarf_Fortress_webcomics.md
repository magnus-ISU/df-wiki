# Dwarf Fortress webcomics

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Dwarf_Fortress_webcomics) &middot; revision 315759 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-12 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Main/Dwarf_Fortress_webcomics.wiki)*

---
*Dwarf Fortress*, due to a combination of its insane level of complexity and minimal artistic depiction, attracted the attention of various webcomic writers and artists - detail-minded folk, often attracted to games, and with very active imaginations. These webcomic 'strippers have immortalized *Dwarf Fortress*, sometimes as individual episodes in their ongoing webstrips, sometimes as brand-new webcomics devoted entirely to *Dwarf Fortress*.

### Graphic sagas

#### The Littlest Cheesemaker

[The Littlest Cheesemaker](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=136384.0) is an illustrated interactive story chronicling the adventures of Mistêm "Wheeldream" Kolnåzom (Misty) on her quest to become a legendary [cheesemaker](Cheesemaker.md).

#### Losing Is Fun

[Losing Is Fun](https://losingisfun.org/) ([old archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20191102233928/http://losingisfun.com/)) is an interactive webcomic about a [kobold](Kobold.md) on a quest to steal an [artifact](Artifact.md) dagger from a dwarf fortress. [First forum thread](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=164165.0) [Second forum thread](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=178723.0) (Currently Ongoing)

#### Bronzemurder

[Bronzemurder](Bronzemurder.md) is an epic illustrated saga by [Tim Denee](http://timdenee.com/bronzemurder/), concerning a [forgotten beast](Forgotten_beast.md).

#### Oilfurnace

[Oilfurnace](http://www.timdenee.com/oilfurnace/) is another *Dwarf Fortress* illustrated saga by Tim Denee, and printed in the July 2010 issue of PCPowerplay magazine, an Australian gamer mag, making it the first *Dwarf Fortress* comic in print. (Warning: contains minor spoilers)

#### Adventures of Likot Udendeb

[Adventures of Likot Udendeb](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=109582.0) is an illustrated, interactive saga originating from Russian *Dwarf Fortress*-centered imageboard [DFach](http://chuck.dfwk.ru/). The story is written and illustrated by several anonymous contributors based on suggestions of the imageboard's users. Starting as a story of one completely ordinary dwarf and his cruel fate of being the hero, it eventually evolved into an epic saga with multiple arcs, various characters and global setting that's loosely based on *Dwarf Fortress* game mechanics rich with DF-related memes and influence of general Russian imageboard culture. Permanently in indefinite state of being written/frozen and is being translated (badly) from Russian with a certain lag. Admired for its flexible storyline and several [animated episodes](http://www.youtube.com/channel/UC6Crmf54js5ZEyGlKtEbggg/feed).

#### Matul Remrit (Bravemule)

**THE LINK IS BROKEN.** [Matul Remrit](http://www.bravemule.com/matulremrit) ([archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20210823135449/https://bravemule.squarespace.com/matulremrit/)) is a tale of a band of dregs who struggle to build their new home amidst strife, internal politics, and a scourge of elf attacks.

#### Thabostobok

**THE LINK IS BROKEN.** [Thabostobok](https://www.dream-gears.com/sites/comicwolf.com/index.php?app=comics&app=comics&action=listview&catid=1) A fully illustrated comic based on a DF game session.

### Ongoing *Dwarf Fortress* webcomics

Ongoing webcomics primarily about *Dwarf Fortress*.

#### Bridgedflickered

[Bridgedflickered](https://tapas.io/series/Bridgedflickered/info/) is a yonkoma-style strip by Lee Keegan, chronicling a playthrough of *Dwarf Fortress*.

#### Revenge of the FUN

[Revenge of the FUN](http://rotf.thecomicseries.com/) is a comic series about dwarves striking the earth, and the [FUN](Fun.md) striking back, in all outrageous fashions.

#### The Chronicles of Boatmurdered

**THE LINK IS BROKEN.** [The Chronicle of Boatmurdered](http://boatmurdered.smackjeeves.com/comics/735733/chronicles-of-boatmurdered-cover/) is an illustrated version of the infamous [succession game](Succession_game.md) [Boatmurdered](Boatmurdered.md), with art by Deon. The webcomic began in November 2009 and stopped unfinished in May 2010.

#### *Dwarf Fortress*: Histories of...\*

[*Dwarf Fortress*: Histories of...\*](http://dfstory.thecomicseries.com/comics/1/) is a DF chronicle with art and story by Elthar. The webcomic began in July 2010 and died after six pages.

#### KittyProblem

[https://archive.org/details/smackjeeves-85447](https://archive.org/details/smackjeeves-85447) An archived short comic created by two brothers about the dangers of [catsplosion](Catsplosion.md). Story began on 2nd September 2010.

#### Ask Amxu

*Oi, My name is [Amxu Osmösprosusm](http://askamxu.tumblr.com/). I'm a [goblin](Goblin.md) and I work as a [compulsory freelance social worker](Snatcher.md) for the dwarves in my region. I'm always happy to answer questions for the curious.*

#### Lord of the Dwarfs

[Lord of the Dwarfs](https://web.archive.org/web/20180106125719/http://www.dawnsomewhere.com/lotd/) is a webcomic produced by Dawn Somewhere about a group of dwarves attempting to find their way back to their fortress after an unusual encounter with HFS.

### Defeated Webcomics

These are links to comics that once were, but now are not. They are lost in the great wasteland of The Barren 404 Desert.

#### PixelPainted

[PixelPainted A *Dwarf Fortress* Adventure](http://www.dfa.webatu.com/?page=111001) is a flash comic by Glyphgryph, and based on a Choose Your Own Adventure-style suggestion thread. The webcomic began in September 2009 and is currently on hiatus.

**Dead:** December 2014

#### I'm Not Mad

A 3D model comic with three *Dwarf Fortress* strips, *Dwarf Fortress*: Overachiever,*Dwarf Fortress*: Work Music, and *Dwarf Fortress*: Suspicion. The first comic deals with [Hidden Fun Stuff](Hidden_Fun_Stuff.md), while the second deals with [tantrums](Tantrum.md).

**Dead:** July/August 2015

#### Dwarven Trail

[Dwarven Trail: A *Dwarf Fortress* Comic](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=36953.0) was a sprite comic by Matthew (Opirian) Tracy. It used RPG Maker VX for the graphics, which mimicked some of the popular [*Dwarf Fortress* graphic sets](Graphics_set_repository.md). It followed the journey to and establishment of a new fortress. [Urist](Urist.md) was a recurring character. The comic was hosted on a couple different sites at times, but they seem to no longer be available or safe; the forum post appears to provide all the pages, albeit with hosting watermarks. The Wayback Machine on the Internet Archive seems to only include [page 9](https://web.archive.org/web/20110506035108/http://www.webcomicsnation.com/dwarventrail/dwarventrail/series.php?view=single&ID=154017).

#### Effigies

**THE LINK IS BROKEN.** [Effigies](http://effigies.smackjeeves.com/) is a character-driven world-building webcomic by Fault, based on *Dwarf Fortress* [Adventure mode](Adventure_mode~2.md). The comic began in November 2009 and was ongoing, updating on a rough weekly schedule. It currently lays dormant.
