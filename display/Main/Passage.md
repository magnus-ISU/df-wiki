# Passage

**Passages** are [cavern](Cavern.md) features that connect one cavern layer to the next, being natural tunnels connecting two layers by [ramps](Ramp.md) and short, twisted tunnel sections. Passages can be easily sealed with a constructed [wall](Wall.md), and discovering what lies so many layers below may be worth the temporary risk of attack from [forgotten beasts](Forgotten_beast.md) or the like. The [announcement](Announcement.md) window will let you know you've found a downward passage even if you happen to discover it from the bottom.

Note: Because of <sup>[Bug](#bugs):[6390](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=6390)</sup>, you may not see a ramp when the discovery of this is first announced. You should [channel](Channel.md) that tile *and the surrounding tiles* (as it may not be *exactly* where the announcement placed it) to find it.
