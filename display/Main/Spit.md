# Spit

**Spit** is a kind of [contaminant](Contaminant.md), like [blood](Blood.md) or [vomit](Vomit.md), that results from the action of spitting, treated as a "natural ability" by most creatures. Spitting mostly occurs in [Adventure mode](Adventure_mode~2.md), when NPCs encounter people that they hate, typically due to a bad [reputation](Reputation.md) (as e.g. a murderer) or belonging to different [factions](Faction.md) without being outright hostile (e.g. criminal organizations in [towns](Town.md)).

As an adventurer you can spit on anyone or anything you want, aiming it just like you would with a projectile. This is done in the abilities menu ([![Adv ui X.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/e/e5/Adv_ui_X.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Adv_ui_X.png)X). If you get spit on, you can also drink it in an attempt to slake your [thirst](Thirst.md), but it will have little (if any) effect, and won't add to fullness.

Note that some creatures like [magma crabs](Magma_crab.md) have very dangerous [spit](Magma.md) indeed; appropriate [modding](Modding.md) can turn this mostly harmless expression of spite into a deadly weapon.
