**Chromite** is a dark purple stone found within [olivine](Olivine.md) as veins, and notable in that it is the sole source of valuable [demantoid](Demantoid.md) gemstones, as well as being a potential source of [native platinum](Native_platinum.md). Like olivine, chromite is also [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md).

### In real life

In real life, chromite is the sole ore of chromium.
