# Meat

[![Meat sprite large.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/c/c7/Meat_sprite_large.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Meat_sprite_large.png)

**Meat** is a collective term for all non-aquatic-[vermin](Vermin.md) edible, animal [butchery](Butcher.md) products, and a [food](Food.md) staple which can make up a significant part of a fortress's diet. Although the [status](Status.md) bar and [stocks](Stocks.md) window categorize all edible butchery products as meat, the game names them variously: true "{creature type} meat", which is muscle, and [prepared organs](Prepared_organs.md), both produced when the [creature](Creature.md) is [butchered](Butcher's_shop~2.md). The amount of meat products produced depends on the individual creature's absolute size, which is a combination of the species' average size and any creature-specific modifiers (height, broadness, etc.).

Dwarves (*and humans, of course*) are omnivores; having meat to eat is not compulsory, but does increase variety (munching on the same gruel day after day will eventually cause negative [thoughts](Thought.md)). If there is no meat in your stocks, carnivorous creatures (such as some species of [animal people](Animal_people.md)) will go hungry, and [animal trainers](Animal_trainer.md) will be unable to perform the initial training on any wild carnivores. [Starving](Starving.md) carnivorous citizens will auto-assign the job "hunt for small animal". [Prepared meals](Prepared_meal.md) containing meat apparently also qualify as meat, for the purpose of feeding carnivores.

Like most types of [food](Food.md), meat will [rot](Rot.md) if not [stockpiled](Stockpile.md) appropriately, becoming unfit for consumption and emitting [miasma](Miasma.md) over time if left [underground](Underground.md).
