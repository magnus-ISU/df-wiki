# Alliance

In [world generation](World_generation.md), [civilizations](Civilization.md) may form **alliances** to stave off [necromancer](Necromancer.md) and [evil](Evil.md) invasions. When civilizations are feeling set upon by the more evil threats (any group that requires the killing of neutrals, like [goblins](Goblin.md) and the [undead](Undead.md)), they can join up, for as long as feels necessary, and beyond, if they get along. This has the effect of keeping the necromancers in line. A typical scenario is that the necromancer will bide their time, raise many zombies, and attack a small market in an attempt to get a 'snowball' going. This causes an alliance to be formed, and the necromancer is subsequently defeated.

Alliances only seem to play a role in the wider world. In [fortress mode](Fortress_mode~2.md), allied and non-allied peaceful civilizations are identical in behavior when interacting with the player. <br>
