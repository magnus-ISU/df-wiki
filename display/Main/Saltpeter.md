**Saltpeter** is a non-economic and low-value yellow [stone](Stone.md) found in [small clusters](Vein.md) within [sedimentary layers](Sedimentary_layer.md). With a melting point of 10601 °U and a boiling point of only 10720 °U, it is one of the few stones which will *boil* in [magma](Magma.md). Saltpeter is also the second least [dense](Density.md) non-economic stone\*, making it better than most stone for objects that must be hauled frequently.

> *(\* Saltpeter is about 60% heavier than [jet](Jet.md), more than 21% lighter than typical stone, but about 350% (3 1/2 times) the weight of typical [wood](Wood.md) items.)*

In real life, its best known use is as a primary ingredient of gunpowder, along with [charcoal](Charcoal.md) and [sulfur](Brimstone.md). This process does not exist in *Dwarf Fortress* (yet; it seems [Toady One](Toady_One.md) has plans to include gunpowder later, but only for simple uses[\[1\]](https://www.bay12games.com/media/df_talk_8_transcript.html)), and therefore gunpowder is also non-existent. Saltpeter, as a source of both potassium (like [potash](Potash.md)) and nitrogen, is also a fertilizer, though it is not possible to use it as such in *Dwarf Fortress*.

Despite persistent myths to the contrary, adding saltpeter to your male dwarves' military rations will *not* help to control your fortress's population *or* reduce incidences of romantic infidelity.
