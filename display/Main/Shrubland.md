**Shrublands** are [biomes](Biome.md) covered in [grass](Grass.md), additionally many other [plants](Plants.md) occur, very similarly to [grasslands](Grassland.md) or [savannas](Savanna.md). These biomes contain fewer [trees](Tree.md) than forests, although they usually have enough to get by unless you need large amounts of wood for [fuel](Fuel.md). They usually have plenty of [murky pools](Murky_pool.md), and often have [brooks](Brook.md) or even [rivers](River.md). They tend to have less [ore](Ore.md) than [mountains](Mountain.md), but as with any biome, you can find some if you dig down a few [z-levels](Z-level.md) and look hard enough.

Shrublands come in [temperate](Temperate.md) and [tropical](Tropical.md) varieties.
