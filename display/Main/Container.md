> *This article is about buildable containers, for all types of storage items that "contain" other items, see: [storage](Storage.md)*.

**Containers** is a general term used for various types of similar [items](Item.md) which are used for [storage](Storage.md). Containers are named differently depending on what they are made of (see table, below), but they all fill the same function. They are a common requirement for [nobles](Noble.md), and they are also good for increasing the [value](Room_value.md) of a [room](Zone.md). Each different type of container holds a varying number of items. Dwarves may walk through tiles with containers.

If a dwarf does not own a container in their [bedroom](Bedroom.md), they will leave any possessions on the floor. Containers are also used in [barracks](Barracks.md) to store [squad](Squad.md) equipment, [taverns](Tavern.md) and [temples](Temple.md) to store musical instruments (and mugs for [tavern keepers](Tavern_keeper.md)), and [libraries](Library.md) to store blank writing materials such as [quires](Quire.md). Note that a [bookcase](Bookcase.md) is needed for *written* [materials](Book.md).

Containers are listed under "tools" in some menus.

**Container names by material type**

| Name | Material | Required materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Box** | [Glass](Glass.md) | 1 bag of sand (+ pearlash if crafting clear glass), or 1 uncut rock crystal gem + 1 pearlash |
| **Coffer** | [Stone](Stone.md) | 1 rock |
| **Chest** | [Wood](Wood.md), [Metal](Metal.md) | 1 log or 3 bars of metal |
