> *This article is about the Dwarven name. For the weapon, see [Dagger](Dagger.md).*

The first [name](Name.md) **Urist** is generally accepted in the DF community as *the* generic dwarven name, and often used as a reference name if someone talks about a dwarf and doesn't have (or want to bother with) an actual name, similar to a generic Anglo/American called "Bob", a Russian being called "Ivan", or a Frenchman called "François", etc. etc.

This first name is almost always paired with a last name with the [Gaelic surname prefix](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celtic_onomastics) "Mc", e.g. "McMiner" or "McUselessNoble". The suffix of the surname is always a description of the dwarf or the dwarf's job, even though this is not a valid form for in-game dwarven names. The "Mc" probably stems from both the Scottish accents commonly given to dwarves in fantasy films and video games, and the stereotypes of Scots being drunk and violent, stereotypes also associated quite accurately with dwarves in *Dwarf Fortress*.

##### Examples

> - Urist Mc[Miner](Miner.md) can't find his [pick](Pick.md)!
> - It's time for Urist McUseless[Noble](Nobles.md) to have an [unfortunate accident](Unfortunate_accident.md).
> - Urist McIdiot [dodged](Dodging.md) into the [magma](Magma.md).

The correct pronunciation of "Urist" depends on dialect. Dwarves from your civilization will always pronounce it the right way. Everyone else is wrong.

### Origins

The origin of the name's popularity seems to be the tale "[One Dwarf Against the World](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=15572.0)," a well-received narrative style account of a hermit fortress played in the 2D version. That dwarf's full name was Urist Borushdumat (Raked-Roughness). Despite that dwarf having been female, the name is used for either gender of generic dwarf.

"Urist" is the [dwarven](Dwarven_language.md) word for "[dagger](Dagger.md)", somewhat of a misnomer *per se*, as dwarves really aren't that sharp.

### Other uses

> *Main article: [Temperature](Temperature.md)*

**Urist** is also the term for the [temperature](Temperature.md) units used by [Toady One](Toady_One.md) and has since been adopted by players.

It has also unofficially expanded to be the tongue-in-cheek unit of measurement for *any* dwarven measuring system. For example, an artifact [valued](Value.md) at 100,000☼ (normally "dwarfbucks") could be valued *"at 100,000 urists!"*, an item with [weight](Weight.md) of 50Γ could be described as "weighing 50 urists" (especially in very old versions, back before the weight unit was redefined to match the real-world kilogram), or a 10 x 10 tile room could be referred to as *"100 square urists"* in area. As long as it's understandable in context, it works just fine.

### In popular culture

- Typing "Urist" into the games *[Super Scribblenauts](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Scribblenauts)*, *[Scribblenauts Remix](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scribblenauts_Remix)* and *[Scribblenauts Unlimited](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scribblenauts_Unlimited)* creates a dwarf.
- Immigrants in *[Papers, Please](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papers,_Please)* are occasionally named Urist.
- Urist McPrudent is a semi-random name in *[Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cataclysm:_Dark_Days_Ahead)*.
- There is a chance that the Demolitionist NPC dwarf in *[Terraria](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terraria)* can be named Urist.
- Members of the ship crew in the game *Star Traders: Frontiers* can spawn with the surname McUrist.
- The example email address used in [Mailchimp](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mailchimp)'s developer documentation is `Urist.McVankab@freddiesjokes.com`
- In *[Borderlands 3](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borderlands_3)*, a randomly-spawning mission will task you to kill Urist McEnforcer, a shorter dwarfish version of the game's usual Enforcer-type enemy.
- In [Runescape](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runescape) the quest [From Tiny Acorns](https://runescape.wiki/w/From_Tiny_Acorns) contains a dwarf named [Urist Loric](https://runescape.wiki/w/Urist_Loric). The quest also features other references to *Dwarf Fortress*.
- In [Space Station 13](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Station_13) the default character's name is Urist McHands, and in [Space Station 14](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Station_14) Urist is the default first name of debug NPCs spawned
- In the fantasy city-builder [Songs of Syx](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Songs_of_Syx), [Dondorians](https://songsofsyx.wiki.gg/wiki/Dondorian) (the game's version of dwarves) can be named Urist.
- In the [Forkheads](https://www.forkheads.net/) Adobe/ShockWave-Flash-game ["Sticker Tycoon" on Newgrounds](https://www.newgrounds.com/portal/view/733502) There is a Medal/Trophy which can be earned on newgrounds by playing the game Called "Honorary Dorf" the description text reading "Your have earned the noble title of Urist."

### Fun facts

- Animals can be named Urist as well.
- Dwarves CANNOT obtain the name Urist McUristurister - whichever [elf](Elf.md) wrote that deserves a [magma](Magma.md) bath.
- Yurist (Юрист) is "a lawyer" in Russian. The Finnish equivalent is "juristi", which sounds similar.
- The first Steam card award for the *Dwarf Fortress* Steam release is named Urist.
