# Lair

A **Lair** ([![Icon site lair burrow.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/d/d7/Icon_site_lair_burrow.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Icon_site_lair_burrow.png)) is the hideout of either a [megabeast](Megabeast.md), a [semi-megabeast](Semi-megabeast.md) or a [named](Name.md#animal-and-megabeast-names) large predator (usually giant animals, if the said creature manages to kill at least one [historical figure](Historical_figure.md) during [world generation](World_generation.md)). There are three types of lairs: burrow, mound, and wilderness location. In ASCII mode, burrows and mounds are marked as • on world map, while wilderness locations are marked as •.

### Burrow

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/f/fd/Burrow-Lair.png/150px-Burrow-Lair.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Burrow-Lair.png)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Burrow-Lair.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Burrow-Lair.png)

A typical burrow-type lair.

*(not to be confused with fortress mode [burrows](Burrow.md))*

A burrow is a default type of lair. Most beasts will settle in them, including predatory [animals](Animal.md), [werebeasts](Werebeast.md), rare wild [vampires](Vampire.md), certain [megabeasts](Megabeast.md), and some [night trolls](Night_troll.md). A burrow typically consists of two small underground chambers connected through narrow tunnels. Lairs of [megabeasts](Megabeast.md) contain all the [items](Item.md) stolen by them. Burrows of [night trolls](Night_troll.md) have the entrance covered by a [hatch cover](Hatch_cover~2.md) and contain some [furniture](Furniture.md) and [vermin](Vermin.md) remains.

The entrance of a burrow may be difficult to spot in [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md); If you are a Hero, the directions in the upper left corner of the screen will navigate you to it. Otherwise, look carefully for downward pointing ramps!

<br>

### Mound

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/d/de/Mound.png/150px-Mound.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Mound.png)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Mound.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Mound.png)

A typical mound-type lair.

A mound is home to a [night troll](Night_troll.md), or a whole family of them. The inside of a mound consists of a few rooms which may contain [furniture](Furniture.md), cutlery, [food](Food.md), [cages](Cage.md), and [vermin](Vermin.md) remains.

<br>

### Wilderness location

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/a/a2/Roc_nest.png/150px-Roc_nest.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Roc_nest.png)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Roc_nest.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Roc_nest.png)

A roc's nest at a wilderness location with a lot of stolen stuff.

A wilderness location is a place inhabited by a [roc](Roc.md). Its appearance doesn't differ from the surrounding terrain, except that it may contain [items](Item.md) stolen by the beast. Beasts which inhabit wilderness locations often settle in [caves](Cave.md), instead. Roc lairs have a [nest](Nest.md) for them to use and it often contains roc eggs.

<br>
