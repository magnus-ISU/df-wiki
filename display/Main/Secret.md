A **secret** is a piece of [knowledge](Knowledge.md) that originates [from the gods](Deity.md) and can then be passed onto others. Currently, the only secret implemented is [necromancy](Necromancy.md) - further secrets can be defined with [Interaction tokens](Interaction_token.md).
