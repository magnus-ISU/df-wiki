**Spinosaurus** is a large, semi-aquatic extinct carnivorous dinosaur from the Cretaceous period. There are two species of Spinosaurus in *Dwarf Fortress*:

- [Spinosaurus aegyptiacus](Spinosaurus_aegyptiacus.md)
- [Spinosaurus mirabilis](Spinosaurus_mirabilis.md)
