> *For the great beasts sometimes conflated with "bosses" in other games, see the articles on [Megabeasts](Megabeast.md), [Semi-megabeasts](Semi-megabeast.md), [Forgotten beasts](Forgotten_beast.md), and [Titans](Titan.md), though be warned that they all contain minor spoilers.*

**Bosses** are the leaders of groups of [criminals](Criminal.md), specifically leading criminal organizations or gangs of bandits. They may also be called a ringleader, master, warlord, chieftain, etc.

Criminal bosses can often be located in a [dungeon](Dungeon.md), while bandit leaders can be found in [camps](Camp.md). When you encounter them, they will be flashing like other leaders in the game. They are effectively treated as [lords](Quest.md#commander) of their respective place of residence, meaning talking to a boss (if they haven't been provoked yet) will allow you to join them as a [lieutenant](Hearthperson.md), lay claim to their territory, etc. Bosses are often part of the [intrigue](Intrigue.md) system in some capacity.

Criminal leaders and their subordinates can occasionally be [weapon masters](Combat_skill.md), and are often the only ones that are generated during [world generation](World_generation.md). Certain criminal bosses in capital cities will also come equipped with a random assortment of masterwork-quality items.
