A **champion** is an appointed [noble](Noble.md) position, which becomes available after a fortress becomes a [barony](Baron.md). They have no standard [room](Zone.md) requirements, however they may make one custom [demand](Demand.md) for furniture. The Champion is akin to a cultural champion, that promotes the values of their [civilization](Civilization.md), and seeks to encourage the denizens of the fort.

The Champion does not perform menial labors, instead merely standing in meeting areas (with a job description of "Noble") and talking to anyone nearby. Dwarves who speak to the Champion will get a special [thought](Thought.md) about "talking to a pillar of society", with the happiness bonus or penalty depending on the dwarf's [opinions on tradition](Personality_value.md#tradition), and they will also satisfy their [need](Need.md) to "uphold tradition".
