# Dwarf Fortress Talk

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Dwarf_Fortress_Talk) &middot; revision 279333 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-12 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Main/Dwarf_Fortress_Talk.wiki)*

**Dwarf Fortress Talk** is the official *[Dwarf Fortress](Dwarf_Fortress.md)* podcast, where [Rainseeker](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Rainseeker&action=edit&redlink=1), [Capntastic](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Capntastic&action=edit&redlink=1) and [Toady One](Toady_One.md) talk about *Dwarf Fortress* and answer questions from the forum.

### List of Dwarf Fortress Talks

For credits, teasers and more info see the *[Dwarf Fortress Talk page on the official bay12 website](http://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/df_talk.html)*.
