**Vermin** includes small land [creatures](Creature.md) such as [rats](Rat.md), [bats](Bat.md), and [lizards](Lizard.md), and also the type of [fish](Fish.md) which are caught by [fisherdwarves](Fisherdwarf.md). They are below 2 kg (4 lb) in size, much smaller than [cats](Cat.md). Vermin are hard to see, but can be noted if you are particularly observant, as they will occasionally blink into and out of view on the screen. Unlike other non-vermin creatures, their [profile](Profile.md) contains only their species description, unless they are caught or fully [tamed](Animal_trainer.md).

The main distinctions between vermin and creatures are that vermin:

1. Cannot be engaged in [combat](Combat.md) or trigger [traps](Trap.md), save those specifically made to trap them, though some (such as the [cave spider](Cave_spider.md)) can bite and inflict syndromes;
2. Do not usually provide [meat](Meat.md) or [bones](Bone.md) - exceptions are the [creepy crawler](Creepy_crawler.md), which can be butchered, and the [purring maggot](Purring_maggot.md), which can be milked;
3. Do not breed, but "spawn", spontaneously appearing in their natural environment or [biome](Biome.md). Some types of vermin are inexhaustible and will always be able to spawn, regardless of how many are killed or captured;
4. Are sometimes "[hateable](Hateable.md)", meaning dwarves can have an anti-[preference](Preference.md), which gives them a negative [thought](Thought.md) when they see the hated vermin.

Vermin can be problematic, as many types feed on [stockpiles](Stockpile.md), thus making it more difficult to keep enough [food](Food.md) and [drink](Alcohol.md) to survive. Vermin can be hunted by [cats](Cat.md) and [peregrine falcons](Peregrine_falcon.md) to reduce this problem, though the [remains](Remains.md) will still need to be [removed](Activity_zone.md#garbage-dump). Cats and falcons can be [pastured](Pasture.md) at the relevant stockpiles to further reduce the problem.

Some vermin can be captured in [animal traps](Animal_trap~2.md), but those with the `[VERMIN_NOTRAP]` token can only be caught with a "Capture a Live Land Animal" job, while those with the `[VERMIN_SOIL]` token will be ignored for that job. Once caught, some can be trained as [pets](Pet.md). A few particular captured vermin can be used to produce [extracts](Extract.md) using a glass [vial](Vial.md) and the [animal dissector](Animal_dissector~2.md) labor. Captured [purring maggots](Purring_maggot.md) can also be [milked](Milk.md) at a farmer's workshop. Vermin don't die of old age at all - if you capture one, it should last forever.

Captured vermin can be assigned to built cages, but not to pastures.

Graphically, the sprites of some flying vermin, such as the [acorn fly](Acorn_fly.md), [fly](Fly.md), [blood gnat](Blood_gnat.md), [honey bee](Honey_bee.md), [mosquito](Mosquito.md) and [pixies](Pixie.md), will resemble multiple bugs of that type in a single tile; they can be seen in amounts of one, three, five and nine; depending on how large the swarm is. Dwarves will eat vermin if no other [food](Food.md) source is available, resulting in an unhappy [thought](Thought.md). Most vermin cannot be spawned in the [object testing arena](Object_testing_arena.md), as those are not able to engage in combat anyway.

In [adventure mode](Adventure_mode~2.md), you can reveal the location of previously-hidden, nearby vermin by pressing [![Adv ui L.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/0/09/Adv_ui_L.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Adv_ui_L.png)L. Your character can also pick up vermin if they are on the same tile. Attempting to *eat* any collected vermin, however, will make a unique message appear: No. That's disgusting., unless the character is actually starving. In [fortress mode](Dwarf_fortress_mode~2.md), caged vermin can be obtained, this also includes meat products from them, such as "prepared mosquito brain" or "prepared louse heart".

Vermin can be used as thrown weapons or ammunition, with rather devastating results. A thrown [Fluffy wambler](Fluffy_wambler.md) was used to decapitate a [bronze colossus](Bronze_colossus.md), and a [hamster](Hamster.md) launched from a [ballista](Ballista.md) matches, if not exceeds, the power of the ballista's steel bolts.

### Reading the Table

| Graphic | Tile | Name | Playable | Hostile | Food Source | Adult Body Size | Pet Value (×) | Biome | Features |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

The above columns indicate, in order:

- **Graphic:** The sprite assigned to the creature; seen only in the [premium versions](Premium_version.md#premium-version-282020e28093present29).
- **Tile:** The symbol assigned to the vermin, how you will see it without a graphic set.
- **Name:** The name of the vermin as it shows up in-game.
- **Playable:** Whether the vermin is playable in any of the game modes. As of now, no vermin are playable.
- **Hostile:** Whether the vermin is hostile to the player. As of now, no vermin are hostile to dwarves.
- **Food Source:** If "Yes" then the vermin can be turned into food when processed in a [fishery](Fishery.md).
- **Adult Body Size:** The average size of the vermin when an adult. This can be anywhere from 1 for a [fly](Fly.md), to 2,000 for a [fox squirrel](Fox_squirrel.md). More or less equals the creature's weight in grams.
- **Pet Value:** This is the value the vermin can be bought and sold for as a [pet](Pet.md), during [trading](Trading.md).
- **Biome:** Where the vermin can be found.
- **Features:** Any special features the vermin possesses, these can include alignment and special properties. The value after 'Eater' indicates the vermin's [container penetration value](Creature_token~2.md#penetratepower).
