[Creatures](Creatures.md) with the **`[TRAPAVOID]`** [token](Creature_token~2.md) cannot be affected by normal [traps](Trap.md), and do not set off [pressure plates](Pressure_plate~2.md). By ignoring a large part of some players' [defenses](Defense_guide.md), such creatures can be difficult to deal with.

### Creatures who avoid traps

These creatures possess the `[TRAPAVOID]` token.

- [Angels](Angel.md)
- [Bogeymen](Bogeyman.md)
- [Demons](Demon.md)
- [Experiments](Experiment.md)
- [Forgotten beasts](Forgotten_beast.md)
- [Gremlins](Gremlin.md)
- [Kobolds](Kobold.md)
- [Night trolls](Night_troll.md)
- [Titans](Titan.md)
- [Werebeasts](Werebeast.md) (in werebeast form only)

Note that all [tamed](Animal_trainer.md) and formerly tame animals, along with [dwarves](Dwarves.md), will never trigger traps, so it's safe to put traps right in front of your doorways.

### Trapping trapavoiders

Despite the difficulty in trapping trapavoiders, it's not necessary to rely on your [military](Military.md) to defeat them. Several special trapping techniques can be used instead.

#### Spike trap

[Retracting spike](Trap.md#upright-spear2fspike) traps can be used against trapavoiders as easily as against any other creature. Of course, such traps need to be triggered somehow, either by [lever](Lever.md), or by a [repeater](Repeater.md) of some sort. (Any other manually triggerable [trap design](Trap_design.md) can be used instead.)

#### Web or loss of consciousness

Trapavoiders become vulnerable to traditional traps when they are [webbed](Web.md) or [unconscious](Unconscious.md). One can take advantage of this, usually to [cage](Cage.md) the uncageable, by building traps in a [silk farm](Silk_farm.md) or by building a [constructed floor](Construction.md) on top of a [support](Support.md) and surrounding the area under it with traditional traps-- what is known as a [cave-in](Cave-in.md) trap.

#### Trap by rule-out

The very fact that trapavoiders are unable to set off pressure plates allows one to build special traps to catch trapavoiders. By building a pressure plate set to be triggered by all weights, including civilians, you can be relatively certain that anything that doesn't set off the pressure plate is a trapavoider. Designs based around this concept can route civilians and trap-vulnerable creatures one way, and divert everything that doesn't trigger the pressure plate through another corridor-- perhaps one filled with retracting spikes connected to a repeater.
