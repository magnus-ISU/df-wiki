The **siege workshop** is used to make [ballista](Ballista.md) and [catapult](Catapult.md) parts, as well as to assemble siege ammunition (namely, [ballista arrows](Ballista_arrow.md)).

A dwarf must have the [siege engineer](Siege_engineer.md) [labor](Labor.md) enabled in order to use a siege [workshop](Workshop.md). [Siege engineers](Siege_engineer.md) can also assemble [ballista](Ballista.md) and [catapult](Catapult.md) parts into stationary [siege engines](Siege_engine.md) to be loaded and fired by [siege operators](Siege_operator.md).
