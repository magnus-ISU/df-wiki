**Borax** is an unremarkable white stone which can be found in large quantities within [rock salt](Rock_salt.md) and in small quantities within [gypsum](Gypsum.md).

Unlike in real life, Borax **CANNOT** be used as [flux](Flux~2.md) in game.

### Real life

In real-life, borax is used as powder to either protect wood or is used as a [flux](Flux~2.md) while forging iron among many other uses (borax is a great aid for hot-forging two metals, for example it is needed to forge "damascus steel").
