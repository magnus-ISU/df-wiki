**Giant animals** are variants of normal [animals](Creature.md), characterized by simply being much larger. Giant animals appear in [caverns](Caverns.md), or on the surface-level of [savage](Savage.md) biomes. Giant animals are based on all kinds of animals, ranging from [the smallest of vermin](Giant_thrips.md) to [the largest of beasts](Giant_sperm_whale.md), from [peaceful grazers](Giant_hare.md) to [ferocious predators](Giant_lion.md). The main exception to this are the [domestic animals](Domestic_animal.md) – there is no giant [turkey](Turkey.md), for example. Creatures with no basis in reality are also exempt from becoming giant animals. [Extinct](Extinct.md) [creatures](Creature.md#extinct) do not have giant variations, most likely due to most of them being massive dinosaurs anyway.

Both the largest creature in the game, the [giant sperm whale](Giant_sperm_whale.md), and the largest land creature, the [giant elephant](Giant_elephant.md), are giant animals. Other giant animals, such as the [giant orca](Giant_orca.md), [giant rhinoceros](Giant_rhinoceros.md), [giant elephant seal](Giant_elephant_seal.md) and [giant walrus](Giant_walrus.md), are still larger than the fort-threatening monsters that are [megabeasts](Megabeast.md), [titans](Titan.md) and [forgotten beasts](Forgotten_beast.md).

The rule in *Dwarf Fortress* is that a giant creature should be at least as large as a [grizzly bear](Grizzly_bear.md), even if it's a giant version of a small insect. As the real-world creatures approach grizzly bear size, and then beyond, a curve is applied, so that the largest ones are only roughly twice as large in every dimension as their counterpart. Giant whales are very, very large, but they are just eight times as massive as a normal whale, rather than the kind of scaling you see when you go from a spider to a giant spider.

Many variants are only found on the surface of savage biomes. However, some giant animals are practically universal across caverns, regardless of savagery level. [Giant rats](Giant_rat.md), [Giant olms](Giant_olm.md), [Giant moles](Giant_mole.md), [Giant cave spiders](Giant_cave_spider.md), [Giant cave swallows](Giant_cave_swallow.md), [Giant earthworms](Giant_earthworm.md) and [Giant cave toads](Giant_cave_toad.md) are readily found even in non-savage biomes, as long as you dig down to at least the first cavern level.

Three giant animals are called "gigantic" instead of "giant" to avoid confusion with real-life creatures - they are the [gigantic panda](Gigantic_panda.md), [gigantic squid](Gigantic_squid.md) and [gigantic tortoise](Gigantic_tortoise.md). The [giant tortoise](Giant_tortoise.md) and [giant grouper](Giant_grouper.md) are not technically giant animals, despite having "giant" in their names. Unlike most other giant creatures, the [Giant](Giant.md), [Giant bat](Giant_bat~2.md), [Giant cave swallow](Giant_cave_swallow.md), Giant Grouper Fish, Giant Tortoise, Giant earthworm, Giant mole, Giant olm, Giant rat, Giant cave spider, and Giant cave toad do not `[COPY_TAGS_FROM]` their base creatures. ### History

Giant animals have existed since the [2D days of Dwarf Fortress](Release_information.md#2d-version). Initially, the only giant animals were [giant bats](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Giant_bat), [giant cave spiders](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Giant_cave_spider), [giant cheetahs](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Giant_cheetah), [giant leopards](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Giant_leopard), [giant lions](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Giant_lion), [giant moles](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Giant_mole), [giant rats](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Giant_rat), [giant tigers](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Giant_tiger), and [giant toads](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Giant_toad). The [giant desert scorpion](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Giant_desert_scorpion) was also one of these.

More giant animals were added up until sometime during [0.31](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/v0.31:Release_information), when creature variations were added. After this, any new giant animal was added as a creature variant. Several new giant animals came with [0.34.01](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/v0.34:Release_information), due to the animals of the [animal sponsorship drive](Animal_sponsorship_drive.md) receiving giant variants.

Sometime during [DF2014](Release_information.md), the original giant animals were refactored to be creature variants, instead of being defined separately from their common versions. The [giant cave spider](Giant_cave_spider.md) was an exception from this.

[0.42.04](Release_information/0.42.04.md) added many giant animals as variants of existing creatures, including the [giant elephant](Giant_elephant.md). The [giant desert scorpion](Giant_desert_scorpion.md) was removed, as there existed no normal ”desert scorpion”.
