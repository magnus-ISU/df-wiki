**Smoothing** rough [stone](Stone.md) is a simple way to increase the value of a [zone](Zone.md). Walls and floors become Smooth *Material* Wall/Floor when smoothed. It is impossible to [climb](Climb.md) a smooth wall.

### How to Smooth

A dwarf must have the [Stonecutting](Stonecutter.md) labor active. Designate the area to be smoothed ([![Ui v.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/e/ea/Ui_v.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_v.png)v[![Ui vm.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/f/fa/Ui_vm.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_vm.png)m). Note that this can only be done with natural stone – soil cannot be smoothed, and [constructions](Construction.md) are already considered to be smooth for most purposes.

Smoothed "natural" [ice](Ice.md) has different names from regular smoothed items. Smoothed ice walls are "straight" and smoothed ice floors are "level".

### Follow up

After a natural stone surface is smoothed, it may be further improved by [engraving](Engraving.md). A wall can only be engraved once (for floors, there is a [workaround](Engraving.md#removal)). This increases the room's value, but, unlike smoothing, engraving has quality levels. Engraving is a separate skill from stone cutter. Using only skilled engravers will result in higher room and fortress values. Note that smoothing (as well as engraving) floors will clean any blood, vomit, or any other liquid on the tile, making this method a good way to clean unwanted messes.

Smoothed walls are also a prerequisite for carving [fortifications](Fortification.md). Engraved walls can also be carved into fortifications, but the engravings will be destroyed in the process.
