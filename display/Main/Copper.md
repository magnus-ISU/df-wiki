**Copper** is a [metal](Metal.md) used in a large number of alloys, among them [bronze](Bronze.md), which is almost as good as [iron](Iron.md) for smithing [weapons](Weapon.md) and [armor](Armor.md), and [brass](Brass.md), which has the largest value increase of all non-[steel](Steel.md) alloys. Copper itself is a common, low-value metal and a sub-par arms material – more specifically, it is the second-worst metal available for slashing weapons, but is pretty good for blunt weapons.

Copper is [fire-safe](Fire-safe.md) but *not* [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md).

### Major uses

- Early-game melee weapon and armor production, if you have no [tin](Tin.md) to make [bronze](Bronze.md) or no [iron](Iron.md). You will probably do best to find better metals though.
- Making [picks](Pick.md) (As of update 53.07, pickaxe material affects mining speed, and copper pickaxe is the slowest.)
- Making [bronze](Bronze.md), which is better overall than copper for military applications.
- Training metalworking dwarves: copper is generally plentiful. This is possibly the best use for copper if you have better metals.
- Copper [crossbows](Crossbow.md) and copper [bolts](Bolts.md). Copper crossbows are the best crossbows if your [marksdwarves](Marksdwarves.md) are forced into melee – [silver](Silver.md) crossbows are only brought by humans and have no quality modifiers. Copper bolts are heavy enough and sharp enough to work as a good projectile, and iron/steel is generally too rare to be used in bolts. Both uses also train your [weaponsmiths](Weaponsmith.md), which is always a good thing.
- Bins, cages, and barrels: although wooden ones are generally cheaper and lighter, copper containers are fire-safe and resistant to [vermin](Vermin.md). On maps where wood is scarce, copper can serve as a suitable substitute.
- Copper giant spiked balls for weapon [traps](Trap.md).

### Alloys

Copper is smelted (at a [smelter](Smelter.md)) from [native copper](Native_copper.md), [malachite](Malachite.md), or [tetrahedrite](Tetrahedrite.md).

Copper may be combined with other metals at a [smelter](Smelter.md). The following is a list of recipes involving copper:

- [Brass](Brass.md) = Copper + [Zinc](Zinc.md)
- [Bronze](Bronze.md) = Copper + [Tin](Tin.md)
- [Billon](Billon.md) = Copper + [Silver](Silver.md)
- [Fine pewter](Fine_pewter.md) = Copper + 3× [Tin](Tin.md)
- [Trifle pewter](Trifle_pewter~2.md) = Copper + 2× [Tin](Tin.md)
- [Lay pewter](Lay_pewter~2.md) = Copper + 2× [Tin](Tin.md) + [Lead](Lead.md)
- [Nickel silver](Nickel_silver.md) = Copper + 2× [Nickel](Nickel.md) + [Zinc](Zinc.md)
- [Black bronze](Black_bronze.md) = 2× Copper + [Silver](Silver.md) + [Gold](Gold.md)
- [Sterling silver](Sterling_silver~2.md) = Copper + 3× [Silver](Silver.md)
- [Rose gold](Rose_gold.md) = Copper + 3× [Gold](Gold.md)
- [Bismuth bronze](Bismuth_bronze~2.md) = 2× Copper + [Tin](Tin.md) + [Bismuth](Bismuth.md)
