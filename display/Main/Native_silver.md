**Native silver** is an ore of [silver](Silver.md), a mid-value metal. Native silver may also be [smelted](Smelter.md) with other ores to create [electrum](Electrum.md) and [billon](Billon.md). Native silver can be found as veins in [gneiss](Gneiss.md) and [granite](Granite.md). When mined, hunks of native silver are called **silver nuggets**.

[Horn silver](Horn_silver~2.md) is mostly identical to native silver, other than its name and physical properties. [Tetrahedrite](Tetrahedrite.md) and [galena](Galena.md) are related ores which, once smelted, **may** produce a bar of silver.

### In Real Life

Native silver is not technically an ore; it's just chunks of mostly pure metallic silver, naturally formed. As silver is not very reactive, as metals go, this is not an extremely uncommon form to find silver in.
