A temperate savanna with a bunch of rhesus macaques. Classic mode, with a [graphics set](Graphics_set_repository.md).

**Savannas** are a mixed woodland-grassland [biome](Biome.md), characterized by the sparseness of its trees - the unclosed canopy gives the ground enough light for grasses and shrubs to thrive. They occur in the narrow rainfall band that's wetter than [grassland](Grassland.md), but not enough to make [shrubland](Shrubland.md) or a [marsh](Marsh.md). The first few layers of savannas are soil (possibly including an [aquifer](Aquifer.md)), which benefits [farming](Farming.md).

These biomes contain fewer [trees](Tree.md) than [forests](Forest.md), although they usually have enough to get by unless you need large amounts of wood for [fuel](Fuel.md). They usually have plenty of [murky pools](Murky_pool.md), and often have [brooks](Brook.md) or even [rivers](River.md). They tend to have less [ore](Ore.md) than [mountains](Mountain.md), but as with any biome, you can find some if you dig down a few [z-levels](Z-level.md) and look hard enough.

Savannas come in [temperate](Temperate.md) and [tropical](Tropical.md) varieties.
