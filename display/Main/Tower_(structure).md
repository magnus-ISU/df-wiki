A **tower** is a structure inside human [towns](Town.md). They are built by rich [historical figures](Historical_figure.md) like town officials, [merchants](Counting_house.md), or those who had a windfall at gambling in the local [tavern](Tavern.md). Towers can be inherited, sold, or bought, and are an excellent place to conduct [schemes](Intrigue.md), as well as hide [artifacts](Artifact.md). However, they should not be confused with [necromancer's towers](Tower_(necromancy).md).

### World generation

During world generation, rich [historical figures](Historical_figure.md) will buy properties in towns and, if they have enough money, build a tower. Then, over the following years, the tower can be remodelled to include some improvements, of which these currently are the following:

**Dungeons**
> This is currently something that happens in legends only. The [dungeons](Dungeon.md) in adventurer mode are delayed for now.
**Courtyard**
> A walled courtyard with flowering plants will be added, filling out the plot.
**Feasthall**
> A feasthall with tables and chairs is added.
**Fortifications**
> Fortifications will be added at the top level.

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Df_townhouse_tower.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Df_townhouse_tower.png)

A five-level tower inside a human city in adventurer mode. According to legends mode, it has been improved with a courtyard, a dungeon and fortifications. Only the courtyard and fortifications are visible.

### Adventurer Mode

You can visit towers in adventurer mode. Sometimes, their owners will be present in them.
