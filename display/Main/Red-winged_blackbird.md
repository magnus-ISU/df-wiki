> *A small black bird with bright red markings under the wings. They live in the marshland.*

**Red-winged blackbirds** are a type of [above ground](Above_ground.md) [vermin](Vermin.md) found flying around any [temperate](Temperate.md) [marsh](Marsh.md) year-round. They may be captured in [animal traps](Animal_trap~2.md) and turned into [pets](Pet.md). All red-winged blackbirds possess Legendary [skill](Skill.md) in [climbing](Climber.md).

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) red-winged blackbirds for their *coloration*.
