**Ruins** ([![Icon site ruin1.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/d/d7/Icon_site_ruin1.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Icon_site_ruin1.png) [![Icon site ruin2.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b9/Icon_site_ruin2.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Icon_site_ruin2.png) [![Icon site ruin dwarf.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/0/0e/Icon_site_ruin_dwarf.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Icon_site_ruin_dwarf.png) [![Icon site ruin castle.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/c/c6/Icon_site_ruin_castle.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Icon_site_ruin_castle.png) [![Icon site ruin castle2.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/2/20/Icon_site_ruin_castle2.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Icon_site_ruin_castle2.png) [![Icon site ruin castle3.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/8/89/Icon_site_ruin_castle3.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Icon_site_ruin_castle3.png) [![Icon site ruin small1.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/a/ac/Icon_site_ruin_small1.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Icon_site_ruin_small1.png) [![Icon site ruin small2.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/d/d5/Icon_site_ruin_small2.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Icon_site_ruin_small2.png)) are [sites](Site.md) which will appear on the map when a player-made [fortress](Fortress_mode~2.md) is [abandoned](Abandon.md) to ruin rather than retired, or when sites belonging to any civilized race are abandoned or destroyed due to invading [armies](Army.md) or [megabeasts](Megabeast.md). These abandoned fortresses and other [sites](Site.md) will appear as μ on the world and [fast travel](Adventurer_mode.md#fast-travel) maps in both fortress and [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md). Any sites that once belonged to the player race (or the player him/herself) (normally dwarves) may be reclaimed using [reclaim fortress mode](Reclaim_fortress_mode~2.md).

In ASCII mode, mountain hall ruins will appear as μ, hillocks ruins will appear as μ, both of those will appear as μ if taken over by night creatures. Fortress ruins will appear as μ, or μ if taken over by night creatures. Ruins of [forest retreats](Forest_retreat.md) will appear as μ or ₧, depending on what kind of terrain/biome they are on, and will appear as μ or ₧ respectively if taken over by night creatures. Ruins of a [dark fortress](Dark_fortress.md) will appear as μ or μ, or μ if taken over by a night creature. Dark put ruins appear as ª, or ª if taken over. Human town ruins appear as μ, or μ if taken over. Hamlet ruins appear as = ≡ æ or Æ depending on the land; or = ≡ æ Æ if taken over. Castle ruins appear as ○ if taken over.

Barring abandonment, ruins typically appear when a [civilization](Civilization.md) which is at [war](War.md) with another civilization doesn't `[LIKES_SITE:x]` the type of site that they have just taken over, leaving only a tiny few straggler-forced converts that can be slain by beasts. Megabeasts such as [forgotten beasts](Forgotten_beast.md) and [titans](Titan.md) also appear to frequently kill and/or drive out the residents of, and take up residence in, dwarven fortresses. This has the potential of making those reclaims exceptionally [fun](Fun.md) depending on the monster. Abandoned settlements and such may or may not be reclaimed by their parent civilization during either [world generation](World_generation.md) or gameplay.

See the [map legend](Map_legend~2.md) for more information on map symbols.
