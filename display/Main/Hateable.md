**Hateable** [vermin](Vermin.md) (creatures with the `[VERMIN_HATEABLE]` [token](Creature_token~2.md)) can appear as negative dwarven [preferences](Preference.md). Dwarves who encounter detested vermin may experience a negative [thought](Thought.md). Being hated by some dwarves does not prevent the vermin from appearing as a positive preference for other dwarves. Be careful when setting up your [zoos](Zoo.md) - poor selections of creatures can lead to [civil unrest](Tantrum_spiral.md).
