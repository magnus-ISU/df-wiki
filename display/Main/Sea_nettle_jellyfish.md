> *An invertebrate found off the coast. It has a sting which can be severe.*

**Sea nettle jellyfish** are a type of aquatic [vermin](Vermin.md) found in [temperate](Temperate.md) [oceans](Ocean.md) year-round, unless the water freezes, in which case they will be wiped out and come back next year. They are a ready source of [food](Food.md) when [cleaned](Fish_cleaning.md) at a [fishery](Fishery.md). Sea nettle jellyfish are distinct for being the only species of vermin capable of [beaching](Beaching.md), though being vermin, this already-rare behaviour is even more difficult to notice.

Some [dwarves](Dwarves.md) [like](Preferences.md) sea nettle jellyfish for their *beauty*.
