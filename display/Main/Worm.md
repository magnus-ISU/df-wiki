> *A tiny burrowing creature, found in moist soil. It is legless, long and thin.*

**Worms** are a species of soil-dwelling [vermin](Vermin.md) found in the soil of [taiga](Taiga.md), [temperate](Temperate.md), and [tropical](Tropical.md) areas, that can be tamed as [pets](Pet.md). While they are very common, the combination of the `[VERMIN_SOIL]` and `[VERMIN_NOTRAP]` tokens prevents them from being trappable<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Worm&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>.

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) worms for their *wriggling*, but others can "[absolutely detest](Hateable.md) worms" getting [unhappy thoughts](Thoughts.md) when they encounter them.

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Vermin-Worm.JPG](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Vermin-Worm.JPG)

Admired for its *wriggling*.
