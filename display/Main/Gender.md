**Gender** (along with [orientation](Orientation.md)), determines if two creatures can [breed](Breed.md). Gender may also influence the size, attacks, or other characteristics of very dimorphic creatures (e.g. [elephant seal](Elephant_seal.md), [moose](Moose.md)) - for instance, female [giant mosquitos](Giant_mosquito.md) alone can suck blood from other creatures. On the other hand, intelligent species are generally not very dimorphic. For instance, the only difference between male and female dwarves - apart from breeding ability - is the presence of a [beard](Beard.md). This means that, apart from the occasional (and infamous) soldier giving birth mid-fight, the player can pretty much treat male and female citizens interchangeably. (A notable exception is [animal people](Animal_people.md), whose dimorphism (and other attributes) reflect that of the animal they're sourced from.)

If a creature is genderless, or if the gender is unknown, the game will simply refer to that creature as "it".

### Determining gender

When viewing a unit, a symbol appears below its name:

| Gender | Symbol | Common names | Other uses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Male** | ♂ | Boar | [Bags](Bag.md) |
| **Female** | ♀ | Sow | [Amulets](Finished_goods~2.md#crafts) |

Clicking on the creature will bring up an informative character sheet, where the creature's gender (or lack thereof) can be seen.

A [gelded](Gelding.md) male's gender will appear as x♂x.

If mods give a female a body part marked [GELDABLE](Body_token.md#geldable) then, when "[gelded](Gelding.md)" that creature will display the gender x♀x.

### Raws

In the [raws](Raws.md), genders are defined using generalized [caste](Caste.md) tokens (not to be confused with [casts](Cast.md)). Castes are often just defined as "male" or "female", but there are a few [notable exceptions](Antman.md).

### Trivia

- Transgender dwarves were confirmed to be a planned future feature [on the Bay 12 Games twitter in March 2019](https://twitter.com/Bay12Games/status/1107118932077346817). Intersex dwarves were confirmed in the same tweet.
- There is no symbol in the game for creatures without gender, nor unknown genders. In real life, the symbol for being genderless is "⚲" or "∅", while the symbol for unknown genders is "△".
- A common way to implement a kind of sterility for a creature is to make it one-gendered. In vanilla, this is the case for [mules](Mule.md), which are male-only in-game. As such, they effectively can't reproduce.
