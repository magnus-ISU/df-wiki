**Morul Cattenmat, the Most Interesting Dwarf in the World**, is the result of forum-user Martin's quest to create the most interesting [dwarf](Dwarf.md) in history. Morul was created with the singular intent to get him to [Legendary](Legendary.md) in every [skill](Skill.md) possible in [Fortress Mode](Fortress_Mode.md). Doing almost all of the work himself, Morul progressed from a simple dwarf with the near-useless [Fish Dissector](Fish_dissector.md) and [Animal Dissector](Animal_Dissector.md) skills into a demigod of epic proportions, capable of singlehandedly wiping out entire [sieges](Sieges.md) of size 25/damblock 50 orcs all by himself.
