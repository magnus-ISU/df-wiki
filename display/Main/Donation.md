# Donation

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Donation) &middot; revision 256387 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-12 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Main/Donation.wiki)*

---
If you feel like supporting Bay 12 Games for making this awesome game, there is a [Patreon](http://www.patreon.com/bay12games) and a [PayPal link](http://www.bay12games.com/support.html) on the [official website](http://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/).

Up until 29 February 2020, donators had the opportunity to receive an [ASCII Art Reward](ASCII_Art_Reward.md) or a unique drawing, most likely in crayon. As the amount of donations grew [Toady](Toady.md) and [ThreeToe](ThreeToe.md) tried to meet the demand for rewards, but eventually lagged behind and realized they had to stop. At that time, well over 3000 stories and 2000 crayon drawings had been sent. [\[1\]](https://www.patreon.com/posts/33653513)
