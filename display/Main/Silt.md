**Silt** is one of the many types of [soil](Soil.md) that can be found in [soil layers](Soil_layer.md). Silt layers occasionally contain an [aquifer](Aquifer.md).

[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/ce/Stones_eroding_into_flash_flood_silt_bed_IMG_8398.JPG/330px-Stones_eroding_into_flash_flood_silt_bed_IMG_8398.JPG?utm_source=commons.wikimedia.org&utm_campaign=imageinfo&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Stones_eroding_into_flash_flood_silt_bed_IMG_8398.JPG)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Stones_eroding_into_flash_flood_silt_bed_IMG_8398.JPG](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Stones_eroding_into_flash_flood_silt_bed_IMG_8398.JPG)

Not a good place to build a [meeting hall](Meeting_hall.md) under.
