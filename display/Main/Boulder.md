A **boulder** is a [map tile](Map_tile.md) that occurs randomly across the surface of the world, which represent fragments of the substrate [stone](Stone.md) layer. `[BOULDER]` is also the [item token](Item_token.md) for an ordinary [large rock](Stone.md) (as opposed to a small rock), usually obtained from [mining](Mining.md).

### Removal

Aside from serving no utility whatsoever in [fortress mode](Fortress_mode~2.md), they can impede the movement of [wagons](Wagon.md) (but can be walked over by [dwarves](Dwarves.md) and other [creatures](Creature.md)<sup>[\[1\]](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/v0.34_Talk:Boulder)</sup>). To remove a boulder, press [![Ui v.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/e/ea/Ui_v.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_v.png)v and choose smooth stone, designate the boulder, and an [engraver](Engraver.md) will turn the boulder into a smooth [floor](Floor.md). The resulting floor is the material of the boulder, rather than the surrounding material of the ground that the boulder was on. Alternatively, you can place and then remove a [construction](Construction.md) on the boulder's tile, which will typically change the tile into a [soil](Soil.md) floor.

### Notes

- In ASCII mode, boulders covered in "a pile of [snow](Snow.md)" are **not** visible unless looked at. When viewed from one z-level up, however, they will appear as . (a period) instead of the · representing open space.

### Adventure mode

In [adventure mode](Adventure_mode~2.md), a boulder cannot be interacted with, and acts like any other open ground.
