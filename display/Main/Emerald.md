**Emeralds** (a green form of the mineral beryl), are precious [gems](Gem.md), found in small [clusters](Cluster.md) within [schist](Schist.md), [granite](Granite.md), and [marble](Marble.md). They have a value of 40, which makes them excellent trade goods or [decorations](Decoration.md) - if they do appear on the map, emeralds tend to be much more plentiful than [all](Faint_yellow_diamond.md) [other](Ruby.md) [gems](Sapphire.md) of equal value; however, unlike those, emerald clusters never contain more valuable gem inclusions.

Due to the layers where they are found, they are often in small clusters adjacent to the [magma sea](Magma_sea.md), leaving only 1 or 2 crystals far enough away from the magma to be mined.

<br> Some adventurers have been known to try to use emeralds as a form of currency, though most shopkeepers insist on [coins](Coin.md) (or simple bartering). When mining, emeralds are not solely found deep beneath biomes of extreme hills.<br>

Despite many rumors and depictions from [humans](Human.md), [hedgehog men](Hedgehog_man.md) are not known to collect seven different-colored emeralds in order to gain supernatural invulnerablity. These emeralds are also not connected to much larger "super emeralds" that are used to protect floating islands from within hidden palaces.
