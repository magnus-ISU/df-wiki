> *For the mineral, see [Rock salt](Rock_salt.md).*

**Salt** is a [contaminant](Contaminant.md) that makes [oceanic](Ocean.md) [water](Water.md) unsuitable for drinking.

Randomly generated creatures, such as [forgotten beasts](Forgotten_beast.md), may sometimes be composed either of salt (in powder form), or of solid salt.

Also found in a dwarf's work-induced sweat.
