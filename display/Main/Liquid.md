**Liquid** (internally `[LIQUID_MISC]`) is a classification under the [stocks](Stocks.md) menu, containing fluids (alcoholic [drinks](Drink.md) are separate) such as [water](Water.md), [milk](Milk.md), [lye](Lye.md), [dwarven syrup](Dwarven_syrup.md), [honey](Honey.md) and [royal jelly](Royal_jelly.md).

Royal jelly which is still part of one of your [hives](Hive.md) is also shown here, while honey will only be shown after it has been [pressed](Screw_press.md) from a [honeycomb](Honeycomb.md). It also lists [ichor](Ichor.md), [blood](Blood.md) and [venom](Venom.md), which [caravans](Caravan.md) have been known to bring filled barrels of.

All liquids can be [![Adv ui e.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/f/fd/Adv_ui_e.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Adv_ui_e.png)e [ingested](Thirst.md) in [Adventure mode](Adventure_mode~2.md), regardless of [ethics](Ethic.md).

### Water

Though dwarves prefer [alcohol](Alcohol.md), drinking [water](Water.md) from a [river](River.md) or a [well](Well.md) will satisfy their basic needs. [Militia-dwarves](Soldier.md) equipped with any type of [flask](Flask.md) are able to [store](Storage.md) water on their person.

Water can be transported to and from [pond zones](Zone.md#pit2fpond) in [buckets](Bucket.md) and through [minecart](Minecart.md) routes.

An adventurer starts with a [waterskin](Waterskin.md) filled with 3 units of water by default, and can interact with it near a source of water to fill it up again.

### Extracts

*Main article: [Extract](Extract.md)*

[Extracts](Extract.md) are made from organic sources by many professions, including some specialized in the creation of extracts. [Milk](Milk.md) is stored in [buckets](Bucket.md) and [stockpiled](Stockpile.md) [barrels](Barrel.md), [plant](Plant.md) extracts use either [vials](Vial.md) or [barrels](Barrel.md) depending on type, and liquid products from [beekeeping](Beekeeping.md) and a [screw press](Screw_press.md) use [jugs](Jug.md) (or any tool with the `LIQUID_STORAGE` usage).

Some extracts are [poisonous](Syndrome.md), such as the [venom](Venom.md) of a spider or [gnomeblight](Gnomeblight.md), an [oddly](Dark_gnome.md)-[specific](Mountain_gnome.md) toxin extracted from [kobold bulbs](Kobold_bulbs.md). Fortress citizens cannot apply poisons to [weapons](Weapon.md) on their own, as it requires contaminating the item with a [spatter](Contaminant.md) of poison.

### Chemicals

A [lye maker](Lye_maker.md) working at an [ashery](Ashery.md) can refine [lye](Lye.md) and [milk of lime](Milk_of_lime.md), intermediary products for [soap](Soap.md) and [parchment](Parchment.md) respectively. While these materials are toxic in real life, spilling them on a creature lacks any [adverse effects](Syndrome.md).

[Magma](Magma.md) may also be listed as a liquid. This happens whenever the magma fluid melts a non [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md) [stone](Stone.md), or when a [minecart](Minecart.md) is set to pick up magma. As the raws for any stone list their liquid state as magma, when the magma fluid melts the rocks, they become listed as a magma [glob](Glob.md) on the [stocks](Stocks.md) page.
