A **long sword** is an [edged](Attack_types.md) [weapon](Weapon.md) that is more than twice the size of a [short sword](Short_sword.md), but still 30% smaller than a [two-handed sword](Two-handed_sword.md). While not as powerful as a [two-handed sword](Two-handed_sword.md), it has the potential to be used in one hand, making it a better defensive option, as the other hand retains the ability to use a [shield](Shield.md). Long swords have the same basic attacks as other swords, but benefit from increased damage potential over short swords.

Long swords use and train the [swordsdwarf](Swordsdwarf.md) skill. As foreign weapons, dwarves cannot [forge](Forge.md) long swords, limiting supply to whatever low-[quality](Quality.md) specimens can be traded from human [caravans](Caravan.md) or scavenged from invaders.

Although longer than standard short swords, dwarves are still [able to equip](Weapon.md#size) long swords.
