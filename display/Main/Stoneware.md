**Stoneware** is a type of [ceramic](Ceramic.md) made from [fire clay](Fire_clay.md) at a [kiln](Kiln.md). Much like [green glass](Green_glass~2.md) (and [clear glass](Clear_glass.md), to a lesser extent), stoneware items can be produced in unlimited quantities as long as you have a source of [fire clay](Fire_clay.md) in your fortress. They are green in color and have a material value of 4, greater than that of [earthenware](Earthenware.md) despite not actually being more difficult to produce.

Unlike earthenware, stoneware containers can be used to store liquids without being [glazed](Glaze.md).
