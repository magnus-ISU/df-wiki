**Red sand** is one of the several types of [sand](Sand.md) that can be found in [soil layers](Soil_layer.md). Red sand layers occasionally contain an [aquifer](Aquifer.md).

It can be gathered into [bags](Bag.md) and used for the purpose of [glassmaking](Glassmaking.md), and also for [farming](Farming.md), just like any other [soil](Soil.md) layer.
