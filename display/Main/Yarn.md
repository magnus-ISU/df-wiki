# Yarn

**Yarn** is a type of thread that is [spun](Spinner.md) from [animal](Animal.md) [wool](Wool.md). Only a [select group of animals](../Category/Shearable.md) have hair of a quality to be considered wool; these animals may be [butchered](Butcher.md) or [sheared](Shear.md) to produce wool. Yarn can be [woven](Weaver.md) into [cloth](Cloth.md) at a [loom](Loom.md). Yarn cloth can then be used to create a variety of textile products such as [bags](Bag.md), [clothing](Clothing.md), [rope](Rope.md), or [crafts](Crafts.md).

A [dwarf](Dwarf.md) with the [spinner](Spinner.md) [labor](Labor.md) enabled can process wool into yarn at a [farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop~2.md) with the "Spin Thread" job order. With a [loom](Loom.md), any dwarf with the [weaver](Weaver.md) labor enabled can [automatically](Standing_orders.md) turn any yarn, or only dyed yarn, into cloth.
