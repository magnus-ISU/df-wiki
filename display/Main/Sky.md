> *You may be looking for [open space](Open_space.md). Sky is open space (and appears as open space when looked at), but cannot exist underground.*

▓

**Sky** is represented by a mass of solid blue (cyan) [tiles](../Category/Map_tiles.md) across the map. Sky only appears if there are two levels of successive open space below the current view, *and* there are no floors or constructions above the current view. A tile that has direct, vertical access to the sky (above the tile) is classified as [outside](Outside.md) (as opposed to [inside](Inside.md)). "Outside" tiles can cause sickness and [vomiting](Vomit.md) in [cave-adapted](Cave_adaptation.md) [dwarves](Dwarves.md).
