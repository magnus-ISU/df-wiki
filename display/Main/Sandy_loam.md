**Sandy loam** is one of the many types of [soil](Soil.md) that can be found in [soil layers](Soil_layer.md). Sandy loam layers occasionally contain an [aquifer](Aquifer.md). It is not to be confused with [loamy sand](Loamy_sand.md).

Despite its name, it can *not* be used as a source of [sand](Sand.md) for [glassmaking](Glassmaking.md).
