> *For the skill and labor, see [Bookbinder](Bookbinder.md).*

A **book binding** is made from stone, wood, metal or glass. They are made at a craftsdwarf's workshop using [stone](Stone.md) by a [stonecrafter](Stonecrafter.md) or [wood](Wood.md) by a [woodcrafter](Woodcrafter.md) , using [metal](Metal.md) [bars](Bar.md) at the [metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md) by a [metalsmith](Metalsmith.md), or [glass](Glass.md) at a [glass furnace](Glass_furnace.md) by a [glassmaker](Glassmaker.md).

Book bindings are stockpiled under Finished Goods \> Tools.

A written-on [quire](Quire.md) can be bound into a [book](Book.md) by combining it with [thread](Thread.md) and a book binding. Bookbinding is done in the [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop~2.md) by a dwarf with the [bookbinding](Bookbinding.md) labor.
