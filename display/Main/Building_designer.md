The **Building Designer** [skill](Skill.md), originally enabled via the *Architecture* [labor](Labor.md) and associated with the *Architect* [profession](Profession.md), no longer exists by that name in this version of *Dwarf Fortress*, and its role has been changed into what is now called "[Mason](Mason.md)".

In prior versions, it was used when making certain types of buildings, where higher skill levels would further increase those buildings' [value](Value.md) by boosting their "design quality", a value which was distinct from the "construction quality" that remains.
