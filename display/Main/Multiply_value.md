A [creature](Creature.md)'s `[MULTIPLY_VALUE]` affects the [value](Item_value.md) of animal products such as [meat](Meat.md), raw hides, [silk](Silk.md) [thread](Thread.md), and anything made from these raw [materials](Material.md), including [leather](Leather.md) or silk [clothing](Clothing.md). While it's possible for `[MULTIPLY_VALUE]` to apply to only specific parts of a creature, all non-modded creatures use `SELECT_MATERIAL:ALL`, so all [parts](Body_parts.md) of the animal are multiplied.
