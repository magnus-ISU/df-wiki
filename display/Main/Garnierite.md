**Garnierite** is the only ore of [nickel](Nickel.md), a low-value [metal](Metal.md), that only occurs in [gabbro](Gabbro.md), appearing in the form of [veins](Vein.md). With [classic graphics](Classic.md), it is also the only stone/ore that is bright green, making it uniquely useful for [color-coded](Color.md) [levers](Lever.md) as well as general decorative purposes. Despite being found in magma-safe stone and producing a magma-safe metal, garnierite itself is not magma-safe.
