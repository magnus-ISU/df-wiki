A smelter smelting smeltable metal.

**Smelting** is the act of refining raw stone [ore](Ore.md)(s) into useful [bars](Bar.md) of pure metal(s) or alloys. Smelting is similar but distinct from [melting](Melt_item.md), which reclaims existing metal objects back into (fractional) bars. Metal bars produced at a [smelter](Smelter.md) are usable in a wide variety of goods, from [weapons](Weapons.md), to [goblets](Goblet.md), to [furniture](Furniture.md), to [buildings](Building.md).

Smelting also refines [bituminous coal](Bituminous_coal.md) or [lignite](Lignite.md) into [coke](Coke.md), a [fuel](Fuel.md) which is equivalent to [charcoal](Charcoal.md), but first needs fuel to do this. Fuel is required to operate a smelter, metalsmith's forge, kiln, or glass furnace, but not their magma-powered equivalents.

Smelting requires the [ore](Ore.md) (or coal) to be processed, a [smelter](Smelter.md), either [fuel](Fuel.md) or (for [magma smelters](Magma_smelter~2.md)) [magma](Magma.md) at least 4/7 deep, and a dwarf with the [furnace operator](Furnace_operator~2.md) [labor](Labor.md) enabled. Smelting an ore gives 4 bars of metal, and refining fuel gives 9 bars of coke from [bituminous coal](Bituminous_coal.md) or 5 bars from [lignite](Lignite.md). Training in the skill is not necessarily required - since bars have no [quality](Quality.md) the only difference is the time the job takes.

Since smelting coke requires [fuel](Fuel.md) in a conventional (non-magma) smelter, usually one [wood](Wood.md) is burned in a [wood furnace](Wood_furnace~2.md), to create one charcoal to start the process. Alternatives are to bring one bar of charcoal or coke with the expedition at embark, [trade](Trade.md) for one from [caravans](Caravan.md), or go straight to magma smelters.
