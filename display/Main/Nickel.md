**Nickel** is a low-value but uncommon [metal](Metal.md) which can only be smelted from [garnierite](Garnierite.md), which is only found in [gabbro](Gabbro.md). Nickel can be used to create most metalcrafting and construction objects (but not weapons/armor).

It has the advantage of being one of the few metals that is [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md). Only one [alloy](Alloy.md) requires nickel, [nickel silver](Nickel_silver.md) (which is *not* magma-safe). However, the [zinc](Zinc.md) also required for nickel silver is often better saved for producing [brass](Brass.md), which is more than twice as valuable as nickel silver ([material value](Material_value.md) 7 vs. 3).

As a magma-safe metal, nickel can also be used to construct minecarts that are intended to be [submerged to collect magma](Magma.md#minecarts). As the several other such metals are rare or [weapons-grade](Metal.md#weapon-and-armor-quality) (or both) and thus generally far more valuable, nickel is often the go-to for such purposes. It is certainly the cheapest minecart metal to buy from [Caravans](Caravan.md) if need be.
