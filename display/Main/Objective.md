**The basic point of [fortress mode](Fortress_mode~2.md) is:**

- Build/[dig](Dig.md) a place for your dwarves to work and live
- Aid their survival by providing [food](Food.md) and [water](Water.md)/[drink](Alcohol.md) for every dwarf
- Provide basic comfort by building [beds](Bed.md)/[quarters](Bedroom.md), a [dining room](Dining_room~2.md)
- Defend your fortress ([traps](Trap.md)/[military](Military.md))
- Accumulate [wealth](Wealth.md) and [treasures](Objects.md)
- Build and sustain a bustling fortress impressive enough to attract your civilization's [monarch](Monarch.md)
- Please that monarch so well that they [declare](Monarch.md#becoming-a-mountainhome) your fortress to be the [mountainhome](Mountainhome.md) of your civilization
- From the way [Toady One](Toady_One.md) implements some features, have a lot of (twisted) [fun](Fun.md)

**Some other things that can be done:**

- Expand the fortress to have specific areas for every purpose ([Bedrooms](Bedroom.md), [workshops](Workshop.md), [farms](Farm.md) etc.)
- Create various items for export and become the region's greatest [trade](Trade.md)) hub
- Build the world's most popular [tavern](Tavern.md)
- Create and collect the world greatest [library](Library.md)
- Make a [temple](Temple.md) complex for your fortress's [deities](Deity.md) that other gods envy
- Create a working [military](Military.md) that [goblins](Goblin.md) and [elves](Elves.md) alike fear to even speak of
- Send [squads](Squad.md) out on [missions](Mission.md) to bring that fear home to them
- ... or to bring spoils or [stolen valuables](Mission.md#artifact2fcitizen-recovery) home to you
- Dig out the riches of the earth like [platinum](Platinum.md), [gold](Gold.md), [gems](Gem.md), and [mythical materials](Adamantine.md) previously only dreamed of
- Experiment with and experience the full depth of all game features
- Complete [megalomaniac construction projects](Megaprojects.md)
- Experiment with and/or discover [stupid dwarf tricks](Stupid_dwarf_trick.md)
- Do it all over again with one skill-less dwarf in a [terrifying](Terrifying.md) biome
- Profit!

If you feel the need to play a more goal-oriented game, check out the list of [game goals](Challenge.md) for suggestions.

Ultimately, it's really up to your own imagination what you wish to do, this game is an [open-ended game](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_gameplay#Open_worlds_and_Sandbox_modes).

**And that's just fortress mode, there's also [adventure mode](Adventure_mode~2.md), an [open world](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandbox_game) [roguelike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roguelike) in which you control an individual intelligent creature – or party of any number of them – known to dwarven, elven, or human civilizations, possibly including:**

- Dwarves, elves, and humans, of course
- Often goblins, and rarely [kobolds](Kobold.md)
- Various [animal people](Animal_people.md) from [savage](Savage.md) lands
- Occasionally [creatures](Experiment.md) created by dark powers

**On adventures, to explore the world and:**

- Complete quests as your deity's [chosen](Chosen.md) champion
- Complete quests as a [member](Hearthperson.md) of your home's military
- Choose your own self directed, and self rewarded, quests from rumors you hear
- Find stolen [treasures](Artifact.md) and return them to their owners ... or claim them as your own
- Rescue [kidnapped children](Snatcher.md) or war [prisoners](Prisoner.md) and return them to their family
- Travel the world as a [performance troupe](Performance_troupe.md), entertaining in taverns with song, story, poetry, or dance
- Travel the world as a [famous](Reputation.md) drunk, drinking in those taverns
- Find rare [secrets](Vault.md) hidden in the wilderness
- Be "[cursed](Cursed.md)" to roam the world as a monster
- Die a quick death
- Die a glorious death
- Acquire the [power](Necromancer.md) to stop being dead
- Acquire other [powers](Intelligent_undead.md)
- Find a way to the underworld and test your powers there
- ... die again
