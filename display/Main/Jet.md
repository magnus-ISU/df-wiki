**Jet** is a non-economic dark purple [stone](Stone.md) found in large [clusters](Cluster.md) in [sedimentary layers](Sedimentary_layer.md). It is *not* [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md), and so should only be used where any other stone would suffice, aside from one notable property.

Jet is the most [lightweight](Density.md) stone besides [raw adamantine](Raw_adamantine.md) and [lignite](Lignite.md), ~40% lighter than the next lightest non-economic stone ([Saltpeter](Saltpeter.md)). This property makes it preferable for making [large pots](Large_pot.md) and other commonly-[hauled](Hauling.md) items, and absolutely *inappropriate* for [stone-fall traps](Trap.md) and [catapults](Catapult.md). Likewise, jet [crafts](Craft.md) are even more lightweight than normal crafts; this can be very helpful for maximizing [caravan](Caravan.md) output.

### In Real Life

In real life, jet is actually a product of decaying wood under extreme pressure, and is an extremely low grade of [lignite](Lignite.md). In DF, however, jet is not a source of [fuel](Fuel.md). Since it is very soft and easily carved, historically it has been a popular source of jewelry and [crafts](Finished_goods~2.md).
