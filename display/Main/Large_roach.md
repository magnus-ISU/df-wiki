> *A small insect that seeks out unwatched food and garbage. They fear light.*

**Large roaches** are a type of [hateable](Hateable.md) above-ground [vermin](Vermin.md) found in any area that is not [freezing](Biome.md#freezing), can eat stockpiled [food](Food.md), and will cause [dwarves](Dwarves.md) that see them to experience an unhappy [thought](Thought.md), so make sure to bring some [cats](Cat.md) to deal with them. All large roaches possess Legendary [skill](Skill.md) in [climbing](Climber.md).

Large roaches possess a [pet](Pet.md) value of 5, but can't be captured in [animal traps](Animal_trap~2.md). Like certain types of real-life roaches, they can fly, and so you may sometimes see them flying through open space.

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) large roaches for their *ability to disgust*.
