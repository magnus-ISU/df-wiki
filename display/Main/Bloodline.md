# Bloodline

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Bloodline) &middot; revision 245899 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-11 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Main/Bloodline.wiki)*

---
A **bloodline game**, otherwise known as a "[Succession game](Succession_Games.md)", is a game of Dwarf Fortress, normally played in Fortress mode, which is passed between multiple players in succession. Generally each player has their own unique management strategy, so with each turn one could expect significant changes to be made to the fortress. A classic example, from an earlier version of Dwarf Fortress, is [Boatmurdered](Boatmurdered.md).

Bloodline games are often described turn by turn as a story, preferably with screenshots, illustrating everything that happens under the rule of each player.

Visit [here](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Special:PrefixIndex&namespace=100) for a list of bloodline games available on the wiki and [here](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?board=14.0), for a larger list of community stories and bloodline games, found on the Bay12 forum.
