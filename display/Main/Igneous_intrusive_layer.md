Created from rocks that have crystallized from the heat of magma.

**Igneous intrusive** [stone layers](Stone_layers~2.md) can consist of [granite](Granite.md), [diorite](Diorite.md), or [gabbro](Gabbro.md), all of which typically contain [gold](Gold.md) and [tetrahedrite](Tetrahedrite.md).

Gabbro can also contain [garnierite](Garnierite.md) (the ore of [nickel](Nickel.md)), [kimberlite](Kimberlite.md) (which may contain [diamonds](Diamond.md)), and [olivine](Olivine.md) (which may contain [native platinum](Native_platinum.md)). Granite can contain [cassiterite](Cassiterite.md) (the ore of [tin](Tin.md)), [bismuthinite](Bismuthinite.md) (the ore of [bismuth](Bismuth.md)), and [galena](Galena.md) (the ore of [lead](Lead.md), plus a 50% chance of silver).

### In the real world

Igneous intrusive [stone](Stone.md) is formed by magma solidification occurring below the surface, as opposed to [igneous extrusive](Igneous_extrusive_layer.md) stone, which forms above the surface of the earth.
