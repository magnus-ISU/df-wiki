**Clay** is one of the many types of [soil](Soil.md) that can be found in [soil layers](Soil_layer.md). [Aquifers](Aquifer.md) are never present in clay layers.

### Ceramics

> *Main article: [Ceramic industry](Ceramic_industry.md)*

Five different soil types are classified as clay: clay, [clay loam](Clay_loam.md), [sandy clay](Sandy_clay.md), [silty clay](Silty_clay.md), and [fire clay](Fire_clay.md), each of which can be gathered for use in [ceramics](Ceramic_industry.md). If your [embark](Embark.md) site has at least one type of clay, it will be shown as *Clay* in the embark screen's biome viewer. Notably, soil of the types [sandy clay loam](Sandy_clay_loam.md) and [silty clay loam](Silty_clay_loam.md) *can not* be used for ceramics despite their names.

The gathering and use of clay is done from a [kiln](Kiln.md) or [magma kiln](Magma_kiln~2.md) and behaves similarly to most material production, but includes elements similar to [glass](Glass.md) production. In order to gather clay, a [gathering zone](Activity_zone.md#clay-collection) must first be designated over a clay-bearing tile (note that clay can be collected from adjacent clay walls, even diagonally), much like with [sand](Sand.md); however, gathering clay creates a clay "boulder" (the same as raw stone) and does not require a bag. As with sand, clay can be gathered indefinitely from a single tile – any dwarf with the item hauling [labor](Labor.md) enabled can collect clay.

Clay can be made into pots, [bricks](Block.md), [statues](Statue.md), [hives](Hive.md) and [crafts](Finished_goods~2.md). Small pots, called [jugs](Jug.md), function similarly to bags and waterskins, while large pots are treated like barrels. Creating any object from clay requires a unit of fuel, or a magma kiln.

Before an [earthenware](Earthenware.md) pot (large or small) can hold liquids, it must be **glazed**. Glazing is performed at a [kiln](Kiln.md) and requires either a unit of [ash](Ash.md) or a unit of [cassiterite](Cassiterite.md) ([tin](Tin.md) ore), plus a unit of fuel. [Stoneware](Stoneware.md) and [porcelain](Porcelain.md) pots do not need to be glazed.

When an underground plant ([trees](Tree.md), [shrubs](Shrub.md), [grass](Grass.md) or moss) grows on a muddy stone floor tile (after discovering a [cavern](Caverns.md)) and is either trampled, gathered, cut down or removed via building a dirt road on top of it, the floor tile turns into a soil type appropriate to the [biome](Biome.md) – for biomes which lack soil layers altogether (such as mountains and glaciers), a random soil type will be selected, which might sometimes be clay.

[Grass](Grass.md), [saplings](Tree.md) and [shrubs](Shrub.md) can grow on clay soil, which causes the underlying clay to be temporarily unavailable for collection, giving the message "Urist McClaydigger cancels Collect Clay: Clay vanished" if the job is in progress, or "Urist McClaydigger cancels Collect Clay: Need valid, active clay collection zone" if the job is new. You can easily remove the grass *and* prevent it from growing back by either building a dirt [road](Road.md) ([![Ui b.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b1/Ui_b.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_b.png)b[![Ui bn.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/c/c7/Ui_bn.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bn.png)n[![Ui bnO.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/9/9f/Ui_bnO.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bnO.png)O) over the grass, or put a floor [grate](Grate.md) over the clay floor to prevent growth while allowing clay to be collected, but you must first remove the grass/sapling/shrub.

Occasionally, it is possible to "cultivate" clay by channeling down into the lowest normal layer of the caverns, revealing a "[magma flow](Semi-molten_rock.md)" above a floor of [semi-molten rock](Semi-molten_rock.md) (and a [ramp](Ramp.md) on the level below, if the square had not previously been revealed). If a [floor](Floor.md) is constructed over the flow, then deconstructed, a natural floor of clay or [sand](Sand.md) may be left behind. In some cases, this may be the sole domestic source of clay for a fortress.

Gathered clay is treated as a boulder, though its uses are limited compared to stone. In addition to being usable for pottery, it can be used as a building material. However, it cannot be carved directly into finished goods, [polished](Jeweler's_workshop~2.md) for decoration, fired from [catapults](Catapult.md), or loaded into [stone-fall traps](Stone-fall_trap.md).
