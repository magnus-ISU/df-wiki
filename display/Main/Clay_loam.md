**Clay loam** is one of the many types of [soil](Soil.md) that can be found in [soil layers](Soil_layer.md). [Aquifers](Aquifer.md) are never present in clay loam.

Clay loam can be used as a source of [clay](Clay.md) for production of [earthenware](Earthenware.md).
