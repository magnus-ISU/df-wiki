A **flail** is a [blunt](Attack_types.md) [weapon](Weapon.md) that consists of a rounded weight attached to a handle by a length of chain. Flails are the same size as a [morningstar](Morningstar.md), but have a contact area twice as large as a much larger [maul](Maul.md). Against soft targets in the [civilized creature](Creature.md#civilized) size ranges, their large surface area, deep penetration and high velocity modifiers allow them to pulp body parts more easily than many other blunt weapons.

Flails use and train the [macedwarf](Macedwarf.md) skill. As foreign weapons, dwarves cannot [forge](Forge.md) flails, limiting supply to whatever low-[quality](Quality.md) specimens can be traded from human [caravans](Caravan.md) and scavenged from invaders. Almost all dwarves can equip flails, though [some must use two hands](Weapon.md#size).
