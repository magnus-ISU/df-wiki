> *An insidious form of vermin. It lives only to steal food from others.*

**Demon rats** are a species of [above ground](Above_ground.md) [vermin](Vermin.md), found in any [evil](Evil.md) areas that are not [freezing](Biome.md). They can steal and eat [food](Food.md) in [stockpiles](Stockpiles~2.md), even biting through [containers](Barrel.md) to do so. They can be captured by [trappers](Trapper.md) and turned into low-value [pets](Pet.md). Despite their names, they have no relation to [you-know-what](Hidden_Fun_Stuff.md).

A curious oddity concerning demon rats is their blood is a gray-colored goo.

Some [dwarves](Dwarves.md) [like](Preferences.md) demon rats for their *insidious nature* and their *diabolical hunger for the food of others*.
