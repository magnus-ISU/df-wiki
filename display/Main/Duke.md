The **Duke** (or Duchess) is the next step up from a [Count](Count.md). Your county becomes a duchy after satisfying certain requirements and meeting with the [liaison](Liaison.md); once the liaison leaves the map, your [Count](Count.md) will be promoted. As with the upgrade to a County, this is automatic and cannot be rejected.
