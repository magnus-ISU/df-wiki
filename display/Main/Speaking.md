**Speaking** creatures (those with the `[CAN_SPEAK]` token) are able to hold conversations with other such creatures. Adventurers from species *without* that token (such as e.g. a [plump helmet man](Plump_helmet_man.md)) will be unable to talk. [Civilizations](Civilization.md) without this token will wage endless [wars](War.md) because their inability to speak means they can't negotiate treaties. Strangely, this token is not necessary for creatures to gain [social skills](Social_skill.md).

In fortress mode, civilized creatures can also speak in the midst of combat - you can read what they say in the combat report screen. Their dialog lines are pretty similar to those of NPCs in adventure mode ("Stop! This isn't happening!").
