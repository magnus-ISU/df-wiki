# Bow

[![Bow sprite preview.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/5/54/Bow_sprite_preview.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Bow_sprite_preview.png)

A **bow** is a ranged [weapon](Weapon.md) that uses [arrows](Arrow.md) as [ammunition](Ammunition.md). Compared to [crossbows](Crossbow.md), bows are less accurate but can be loaded faster. [Stronger](Attributes.md#strength) and more skilled bowmen fire arrows at higher velocities.

As ranged weapons, bows use and train the [bowman](Bowman.md) (bowdwarf) skill; as melee weapons, bows use and train the [swordsman](Swordsman.md) (swordsdwarf) skill, instead (although a blunt, wooden "sword" is rarely much of a threat). Dwarves cannot [forge](Forge.md) bows nor make [arrows](Arrow.md), limiting supply to rare artifacts and whatever low-[quality](Quality.md) specimens can be traded from elven or human [caravans](Caravan.md) and scavenged from invaders. Trading for ammunition with humans is preferred because they can have metal arrows. Bows are not always made out of [wood](Wood.md); [like most other weapons](Weapon.md#material), they can also be found made of metal.

All dwarves can equip bows. However, in all but rare cases, your dwarves would be better served with crossbows and bolts instead, as they are native weapons. Bows can be used in [weapon traps](Weapon_trap.md), using arrows as ammunition.
