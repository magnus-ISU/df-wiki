The symbol selection menu

In the [Nobles and administrators](Noble.md) [![Ui n.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/7/7b/Ui_n.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_n.png)n, by clicking on the crown+ icon next to a relevant position, available objects in your fortress can be made into a symbol of a [noble or administrative](Noble.md) position. If multiple objects are assigned to a position or a [citizen](Citizenship.md) is assigned to more than one position with an assigned symbol, they will try and equip them all.

When a new object is assigned as a symbol, unless it is already an [artifact](Artifact.md) or [named object](Named_objects.md), you will have the opportunity to choose a [custom name](Names_and_symbols.md#custom-names) for the object or accept the default name generated by the game. As this also makes them a [named object](Named_objects.md), items that have been designated as a symbol of office will count as artifacts for [stockpile](Stockpile.md) storage quality settings and they cease to gain wear levels or dull but it remains uncertain whether or not they gain the other bonuses that [artifact](Artifact.md) [weapons](Weapon.md) and [armor](Armor.md) have.
