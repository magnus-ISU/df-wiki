**Leather works** is a [workshop](Workshop.md) which produces [leather](Leather.md) items out of tanned [hides](Skin.md) made at the [tanner's shop](Tanner's_shop~2.md). These items include [armor](Armor.md), [backpacks](Backpack.md), [waterskins](Flask.md), [quivers](Quiver.md) for [military](Military.md) dwarves, and [clothes](Clothes.md) and [bags](Bag.md) for civilian use. [Leather armor](Armor.md#types-of-armor) is significantly less protective than metal armor, but is [lighter and thus better](Armor.md#encumbrance) for initial [armor wearing](Armor_user.md) training and for [squads](Squad.md) that value [movement speed](Gait.md) over defensive ability.

[Decorating](Decoration.md) [finished goods](Finished_goods~2.md) with leather is also done at this workshop.

[Crafts](Crafts.md) made from leather are made at the [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop~2.md).

To create differently sized clothing or armor, request it to be made from the workshop as usual. Afterwards, go back to the main workshop menu and, by [![Mag glass icon.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/4/47/Mag_glass_icon.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Mag_glass_icon.png) button, set the details of the issued job. Click specify size, search for a species ([e.g. humans](Humans.md)), and click their name in the list.
