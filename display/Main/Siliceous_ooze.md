**Siliceous ooze** is one of the several types of [soil](Soil.md) that can be found on the floors of deep [oceans](Ocean.md). Not surprisingly, such layers occasionally contain an [aquifer](Aquifer.md).

Siliceous ooze is a type of pelagic sediment. It is formed by the silica in diatoms and other microorganisms, as opposed to [calcareous ooze](Calcareous_ooze.md), which is formed by particles of [calcium carbonate](Flux~2.md).
