# Glaze

<br> **Glaze** is a special coating that can be applied to [jugs](Jug.md), [statues](Statue.md), [large pots](Large_pot.md), and [crafts](Crafts.md) made from either [stone](Stone.md) or [ceramic](Ceramic.md). Jugs made from [earthenware](Earthenware.md) cannot be used to store liquids until they have been glazed, while jugs of other materials (and other containers in general) have no such restrictions.

Two types of glaze can be applied: [ash glaze](Ash_glaze.md) and [tin glaze](Tin_glaze.md). Glaze is applied at a [kiln](Kiln.md) or [magma kiln](Magma_kiln~2.md), and the type of glaze to be used cannot be chosen - the nearest glaze material to the relevant worker will be used and the corresponding glaze will be applied. (Note that glazes are not products in themselves, you do not make a glaze and then apply it to the ware.)

The application of glaze to stone and ceramic goods is performed by dwarves with the [glazing](Glazing.md) labor enabled and grants experience in the [glazer](Glazer.md) skill.

Glazes otherwise work like [decorations](Decoration.md). Ash glaze increases the glazed item's [value](Item_value.md) by 50☼, tin glaze by 100☼, multiplied by the [quality](Quality.md).

### Glazing whatever you want

With the help of [DFHack](DFHack.md) it's possible to queue a job for glazing arbitrary items instead of the usual selection. The following tutorial will explain how to do so.

First, queue a glazing [job](Job.md) at a [kiln](Kiln.md). Here it's glazing [jugs](Jug.md), which we'll edit later.

[![Glazing with dfhack 1.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/9/91/Glazing_with_dfhack_1.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Glazing_with_dfhack_1.png)

<br> Then, type `gui/gm-editor` in the DFHack window to open the editor:

[![Glazing with dfhack 2.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/e/ec/Glazing_with_dfhack_2.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Glazing_with_dfhack_2.png)

This is how the glazing job looks like in the editor. As you can see it has many parts, but the relevant one is what items it requires (highlighted).

<br> After opening `job_items` you will see a list of three items used for the glazing job:

[![Glazing with dfhack 3.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/e/e7/Glazing_with_dfhack_3.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Glazing_with_dfhack_3.png)

The first item is the glaze. You don't need to change anything about it. The second is the item to be glazed, which we'll define in the next step. The third item used is [fuel](Fuel.md). It doesn't affect the final product, so you may delete it with Alt+D if you feel so.

<br> After opening the item definition, you will be greeted by this bountiful description:

[![Glazing with dfhack 4.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/3/34/Glazing_with_dfhack_4.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Glazing_with_dfhack_4.png)

Here's how the game knows what should be glazed. First it describes the type of item used (`TOOL`) and its subtype (`11`th tool is the jug). Here, choose what `item_type` you want to glaze instead, and for the `item_subtype` write `-1` to skip the subtype check, as most items do not have subtypes at all.

Next, take a look at `vector_id`, it defines which list of items will be searched for suitable ingredients. In our case it's looking for `TOOL`s again. Change this to `ANY` so nothing gets filtered out.

Then, check all the `flags`, which are additional criteria the item has to meet. Most glazing jobs require the item to be `empty` and `unimproved`, but for example glazing crafts also requires `any_craft`s specifically, a requirement you would need to turn off.

Finally, the `reaction_class` is `CAN_GLAZE`, which means the item has to be made of glazable [ceramic](Ceramic.md). Blank the CAN_GLAZE so you may glaze much more than just ceramic.

<br> After all that, the glazing job is all set up, and your [glazer](Glazer.md) will happily start working on whatever you told him to.

<br>

#### What hacked items need to be glazed

Certain uncraftable containers, which may be obtained by [modding](Modding.md) or [cheating](Cheating.md), require glazing just like earthernware. [Pots](Pot.md), [jugs](Jug.md) and [goblets](Goblet.md) made of the following solid materials have nonzero `[ABSORPTION]`, and as such cannot store liquids until glazed:

- All types of raw [clay](Clay.md), [soil](Soil.md), and [sand](Sand.md)
- [Bone](Bone.md)
- [Shell](Shell.md)
- [Skin](Skin.md), [leather](Leather.md), and [parchment](Parchment.md)
- [Scales](Scale.md) and [chitin](Chitin.md)
- [Meat](Meat.md) and organs
- [Fat](Fat.md) and [tallow](Tallow.md)
- [Cartilage](Cartilage.md)
- [Silk](Silk.md), [yarn](Yarn.md), and plant [thread](Thread.md)
- [Hair](Hair.md)
- [Feathers](Feather.md)
- [Nail](Nail.md), [tooth](Tooth.md), [horn](Horn.md), and [hoof](Hoof.md)
- [Pearl](Pearl.md)
- [Soap](Soap.md)
- [Cheese](Cheese.md)
- [Sponge](Sponge.md)
- [Eggshell](Egg.md)
- Miscellanous [plant](Plant.md) materials such as [fruit](Fruit.md) and [seed](Seed.md)
