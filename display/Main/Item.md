An **item** is any object that is tangible, easily movable, and not a [creature](Creature.md). Items can include [boulders](Stone.md), [buckets](Bucket.md), [furniture](Furniture.md), [weapons](Weapon.md), [crafts](Craft.md), and other similar or even completely different objects. Most items can be stored in [stockpiles](Stockpile.md) via [hauling](Hauling.md), and a subset can additionally be stored in various containers, such as [bags](Bag.md), [bins](Bin.md), or [barrels](Barrel.md). Containers are, themselves, also items.

All items have a [value](Item_value.md), item type, [material](Material.md), and volume. From these, the [weight](Weight.md) of the item can be determined. Some items additionally have a [quality](Item_quality.md). Many items can participate in [reactions](Reaction.md), and all but the raw materials and some animal extracts are the product of a reaction.

An item can be stored on the ground, in a creature's inventory, in a container, in a workshop, [cabinet](Cabinet.md), or [chest](Chest.md), or even as a building material for [buildings](Building.md) and even [constructions](Construction.md). Because of the wide variety of items and applications, there are varied ways to destroy items: waiting for [rot](Rot.md), consuming them (either through ingestion or through a reaction), [wearing](Wear.md) them out, [burning](Fire.md) them, moving them off-screen, dropping them into a [bottomless pit](Hell.md) or [magma flow](Magma_sea.md), crushing them in a [cave-in](Cave-in.md), or using a [dwarven atom smasher](Dwarven_atom_smasher~2.md).

### Named items

> *See also: [Legendary artifact](Legendary_artifact.md)*

An item that is named in either [fortress mode](Fortress_mode~2.md) or [adventure mode](Adventure_mode~2.md) can arise from four circumstances: being created as a [legendary artifact](Legendary_artifact.md), being named for a notable [kill list](Kill_list.md), being chosen as a [symbol of office](Symbol_of_office.md) (which makes them a [named *object*](Named_objects.md)), or when created as [written content](Book.md). In fortress mode, named items will be listed their respective tabs in the Objects menu [![Ui O.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/7/71/Ui_O.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_O.png)O.

### Fortress mode

Items can be viewed in fortress mode from the look screen. They will, most of the time, be displayed in [brown](Color.md) (yellow when highlighted as the currently active item). When selected, Enter can be used to view a brief description of the item, one that includes the [weight](Weight.md), contents (if any), and name of an item. From there, v can be used to obtain a detailed description of the item written in prose; this information includes the item type, any [decorations](Decoration.md), and other information.

[Caravans](Caravan.md) will haul their purchases away, and [thieves](Thief.md) will attempt to escape with stolen items. All creatures will leave the screen with all items in their possession, including [weapons](Weapon.md) and [ammo](Ammo.md) lodged in their wounds. Using fluids or dumping into the pits [deep down](Hell.md) are the only other methods of moving items off-screen.

### Object testing arena

In the [object testing arena](Object_testing_arena.md), items are appointed from the creature creation screen. This is done by clicking the "Equipment" tab after a creature has been selected. There, an item is added by clicking on it at the bottom, this will add an item to the creature's inventory. The amount of items a creature is holding can be increased or decreased by clicking [![Gui ota plus.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/6/6b/Gui_ota_plus.png/17px-Gui_ota_plus.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Gui_ota_plus.png) and [![Gui ota minus.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/7/7f/Gui_ota_minus.png/17px-Gui_ota_minus.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Gui_ota_minus.png) respectively.

Only certain items can be appointed in the arena. Aside from objects obtained from creatures or butchering, and their derivatives, only [weapons](Weapon.md) and training weapons, [tools](Tool.md), [ammunition](Ammunition.md), and [armor](Armor.md) can be created.
