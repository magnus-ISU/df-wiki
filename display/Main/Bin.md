A **bin** is a specially made basket for certain [stockpiles](Stockpile.md). A bin can be made from one [wooden log](Wood.md) at a [carpenter's workshop](Carpenter's_workshop~2.md) or forged from 3 [metal](Metal.md) [bars](Bar.md) at a [forge](Forge.md). Bins make it possible to store lots of objects into one tile of a stockpile. A stockpile without a bin will likely fill up fast, leaving any remaining items where they are to get in the way. Graphically, when a bin is empty, the sprite will be that of an empty bin with no lid, whereas a bin with multiple types of items will have a lid, albeit slightly tilted. Bins are a component in building a [bolt thrower](Bolt_thrower.md). Bins that are filled with a specific type of item will show those items sticking out the top of the bin:

[![Bins variants preview.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/0/0f/Bins_variants_preview.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Bins_variants_preview.png)

Bins are therefore incredibly useful to a maturing fortress. The best material for bins is generally [wood](Wood.md), because wooden bins are the lightest bins and will therefore save your haulers' time. Of course, if you intend to use bins next to a [volcano](Volcano.md) or something equally [hot](Fire.md), you may want to use metal bins.

If your fort suffers from a lack of wood but [charcoal](Fuel.md) is also your only [fuel](Fuel.md) source, producing metal instead of wooden bins will actually **increase** your wood consumption. Forging each bin will cost one unit of fuel and smelting the metal bars will also require fuel. Charcoal is produced at a rate of one bar per log. You can only reduce wood consumption by making metal bins if you use coke or [magma](Magma.md) to heat your smelters and forges.

Stockpiles that use bins are [ammo](Ammunition.md), [armor](Armor.md), [bars](Bar.md)/[blocks](Block.md), [cloth](Cloth.md), [coins](Currency.md), [finished goods](Finished_goods~2.md), [gems](Gems.md), [leather](Leather.md) and [weapons](Weapon.md). [Food](Food.md) stockpiles use [barrels](Barrel.md) and [large pots](Large_pot.md) instead of bins. Other stockpiles do not use anything at all.

Bins are a fast way to transport trade goods to a [trade depot](Trade_depot~2.md). Instead of carrying the individual item, the dwarf will haul the whole container for trade. Though if the contents inside are heavy, it will still take a long time.

For information on managing bins in stockpiles, see [using bins and barrels](Using_bins_and_barrels.md).

[Melting](Melt_item.md) metal bins return a single bar.

### Bugs

- Hauling jobs block access to all the items in the destination containers until the hauling is complete. This often results in cancellation spam and work delays.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[9004](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=9004)</sup> One workaround is creating a "feeder stockpile" with containers disabled.
- Items in bins are sometimes not found for tasks.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[8755](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=8755)</sup>
