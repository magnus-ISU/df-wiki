**Sandy clay loam** is one of the many types of [soil](Soil.md) that can be found in [soil layers](Soil_layer.md). Sandy clay loam layers occasionally contain an [aquifer](Aquifer.md).

Despite its name, it can neither be used as a source of [sand](Sand.md) for [glassmaking](Glassmaking.md) nor [clay](Clay.md) for [pottery](Pottery.md).
