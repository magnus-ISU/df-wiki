The **sheriff** is the [noble](Noble.md) responsible for crude [law enforcement](Justice.md) until your fortress grows large enough to require a [captain of the guard](Captain_of_the_guard.md), designated through the [![Ui n.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/7/7b/Ui_n.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_n.png)n [nobles screen](Nobles_screen~2.md). They will punish lawbreaking dwarves through beatings or imprisonment. Without a [jail](Jail.md), jail sentences will be "commuted" to beatings as well.

When appointing a sheriff, note that having a weak dwarf take the position is sometimes a good thing. A sheriff with good strength or training could heavily injure or kill a criminal who might have done nothing worse than push over a [handmill](Quern.md) in anger.

When your fortress first elects a [mayor](Mayor.md) (after reaching 50 dwarves), the sheriff's position will disappear and the sheriff will revert to their normal profession title. A [captain of the guard](Captain_of_the_guard.md) must be nominated to enforce the law in larger settlements.
