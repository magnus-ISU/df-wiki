**Cheese** is a [food](Food.md) item made from [milk](Milk.md) at the [farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop~2.md) with the [cheese making](Cheese_making~2.md) labor. It can be eaten as is or cooked into [prepared meals](Prepared_meal.md).

A single "Make cheese" job will take a barrel of milk to the workshop and produce several stacks of cheese of size 5 or less - for example, a barrel containing 17 units of milk will produce 3 stacks of 5 cheese and 1 stack of 2 cheese. A container of frozen milk will, obviously, not be made into cheese.

The milk of any milk-producing animal can be converted to cheese. With the exception of dwarven cheese, which is made from [dwarven milk](Dwarven_milk.md), all cheeses are priced at the value of 10☼.

<br> You are likely acquainted with the dwarven fondness for cheese, but did you know that a dwarf actually invented cheese? She was trying to ferment milk and turn it into a new kind of alcohol, and was quite surprised when she ended up with something else entirely. That's why the dwarven word for cheese is 'shokmug.' She was **sho**c**k**ed that it couldn't be drunk from a **mug**. Meanwhile, the goblin word for cheese is 'ursus', which is a [whole](Grizzly_bear.md) [new](Black_bear.md) [can](Panda.md) [of](Sloth_bear.md) [worms](Polar_bear.md).

Some humans have divined a positive correlation between possessing an enormous amount of cheese and enormous amount of friends.

Rarely, a migrant may appear with a [dream](Dream.md) of creating a [masterwork](Masterwork.md) wheel of cheese. This is presumably a bug, as cheese currently lacks quality levels. Since an eternally unfulfilled goal may make the dwarf [moody](Stress.md) and prone to [tantrums](Tantrum.md), the best solution to the problem is to arrange them an [audience](Unfortunate_accident.md) with [royalty](Vampire.md).
