**Climate** in *Dwarf Fortress* is a [temperature](Temperature.md) rating used as a tool to define the [weather](Weather.md) patterns of a region. Most importantly, this will determine how the weather patterns of an area will affect above ground water sources. Overview of the temperature of an area is important for the lives of your dwarves and animals, as too-cold or too-hot temperatures will discourage or kill organic beings. Climate is a temperature rating only; actual ground cover is called a [biome](Biome.md).

*Note: If temperature is turned off, none of the mentioned effects will occur.*

</p>

</td></tr>

</th>
<td>Any water source can be used during the whole year. This makes it the easiest climate to embark on. Very rarely, especially when very close to the edge of a temperate biome, one can have an extremely brief period of water freezing, usually only lasting a few days, thus providing no danger of death by thirst.
</td></tr>

</th>
<td>Keep in mind that murky pools can still dry up from repeated usage, especially with large populations. Try to find an alternate water source if the region is dry.

<td rowspan="4">Temperate
</td></tr>
<tr>
<th>Peak Summer Temperature
</th>
<td>47-82°F (8-27°C) (20-66 in worldgen?)
</td></tr>
<tr>
<th>Availability of water
</th>
<td>Surface <a href="Pool.md">pools</a> and rivers may freeze for part of the year.  The length of the freeze varies: pools and rivers may thaw sometime in <a href="Spring.md">spring</a>, remaining liquid until <a href="Winter.md">winter</a>; or they may already be liquid at the beginning of spring, and freeze for only a month or so in winter.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<th>When embarking in this region
</th>
<td>If you lack a <a href="River.md">river</a>, you can dig out a <a href="Reservoir.md">cistern</a> in the non-freezing seasons. You can also use a source of heat (such as <a href="Magma.md">magma</a>) to obtain liquid <a href="Water.md">water</a>. If the ground is covered in snow when you arrive, be sure to scout out the surface pools so that none of your dwarves find themselves <a href="Swimming.md">suddenly submerged</a> when the pools thaw later in the spring.
</td></tr>

<td>Surface pools and rivers are usually frozen, but can thaw for a few months each year.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<th>When embarking in this region
</th>

</td></tr>

<td rowspan="4">Freezing

<tr>
<th>Peak Summer Temperature:
</th>
<td>32°F (0°C) or less (0 or below in worldgen)
</td></tr>
<tr>
<th>Availability of water:
</th>
<td>Freshwater surface pools and rivers remain frozen throughout the year.
</td></tr>
<tr>
<th>When embarking in this region:
</th>
<td>You must use a source of heat (<a href="Magma.md">magma</a>) to obtain liquid <a href="Water.md">water</a>. Glacial ice can also be melted. Another option is an underground source of water. <i>See <a href="Freezing.md">Freezing</a> for more details.</i>
<p>In extremely cold regions (below 9964 °U<sup> </sup>, -48 in the worldgen scale), <a href="Dwarves.md">dwarves</a> and livestock may succumb to frostbite if they remain outdoors for extended periods of time. <a href="Trade_goods.md">Goods</a> left outdoors may experience <a href="Wear.md">wear</a> at an accelerated rate. <a href="Embark.md">Embark</a> goods should be stored below ground as soon as possible, and <a href="Trade_depot~2.md">trade depots</a> should be built below ground.
</p>
