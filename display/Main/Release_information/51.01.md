> Alexandra here with a post-Adventure Mode beta launch update. We hope you’re enjoying all the mischief and mayhem that the beta has to offer. Some of the stories you’ve been sharing have been hilarious and thought-provoking to say the least. [This story](https://www.reddit.com/r/dwarffortress/comments/1c6eya2/i_accidentally_killed_a_traveler/) about stealing a shield gone wrong made me laugh a lot.

— Kitfox and Bay 12 Games, April 18, 2024

- [Patch notes on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/975370/view/4186734162824193648)

The v51 [Adventure mode](../Adventure_mode~2.md) beta ran from April 17, 2024, to January 23, 2025. During this period, Steam users could access the beta branch to play [Premium](../Premium.md) Adventure mode. Many non-adventure-specific features were added to later v50 updates during the testing period, as development ran in parallel with the main branch.

### v51.01, Beta 11

April 17, 2024 [patch notes on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/975370/view/4186734162819864096):

- [Adventurer mode](../Adventurer_mode.md) is now playable, though missing several features at this state
- New [soundtrack](../Soundtrack.md) and [audio](../Audio.md)
- Fixed region map scrolling crash
- Crash fix related to multithreading
- Portrait [graphics](../Graphics.md) (included in [50.13](50.13.md))

### v51.01, Beta 12

April 18, 2024 [patch notes on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/975370/view/4186734162824193648):

- Fixed region map scrolling crash
- Crash fix related to multithreading

### v51.01, Beta 13

April 22, 2024 [patch notes on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/975370/view/4186734162838584127):

###### New functionality

- right click menu has option to move more than one tile
- npc sheets no longer give away their names unless you know them

###### Major fixes

- cleaned up an intermittent crash from playing multiple adventure worlds
- fixed crash from some old adventurers

###### Minor fixes

- stopped lockpicking menu from popping up until that's ready
- fixed cave adaptation
- optimization for loading speed with regards to flow events
- restricted some actions like bookkeeping from happening outside fort mode
- centered display of some wide-bodied creatures (some bears, anaconda, python, some animal people

### v51.01, Beta 14

June 5, 2024 [patch notes on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/975370/view/4193492098983971176):

- Added the trade/barter/demand [conversation](../Talking.md) menu
- [Sites](../Site.md) without [tables](../Table.md) in their stockpile get emergency tables for their shops for now
- Printed tabletop items on tables, and stopped many other building items from printing on top of creatures
- Sound off init option should work in adventure mode now
- Adventurers are no longer unaware of their own name
- Adventurers should no longer be sprinting/walking/etc forever when retired into a fort
- Fixes that linux crash on the trainer menu again
- Makes liquids/powders not spill out of containers on unretire

### v51.01, Beta 15

June 17, 2024 [patch notes on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/975370/view/6340585850824991924):

- Abilities menu! Raise the dead if you learn the secrets, pet animals, spit, breathe fire and use other special powers in mods or as an arena-controlled creature/undead
- Left click to move to distant tiles
- Interaction-given [syndrome](../Syndrome.md) tracking/timing improved
- NPCs should [run](../Gait.md) and [sleep](../Sleep.md) again

July 17, 2024 [patch notes on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/975370/view/4356753293368674937):

#### Advancing the beta

- Wrestle button now handles wrestling
- Encumbrance is visible in inventory screen and there are a few alert levels for being over-encumbered
- [Speed](../Gait.md) is visible in the movement options
- [Indicators](../Status_icon.md) for ongoing movement, attacks, and dodges during player's turn in combat

#### General bug fixing

- Throwing/shooting crash fixed

- Handled several issues with gifting pets

- [Chosen](../Chosen.md)/hero get bonus reputation for the purpose of gaining followers
- Environmental display in top right ([weather](../Weather.md), light, temp, etc.)
- Quest journal outline on right side of main screen
- [Vampire](../Vampire.md) blood sense markers
- Memory map
- Stealth vision cones
- [Goblin](../Goblin.md)/[kobold](../Kobold.md) glowing eyes in the dark
- Added liquid/ramp arrow toggles to adv mode
- Announced when somebody pushes past you and cancels your action
- Made people stop moving when you talk to them (if they aren't fighting)
- Added shared items to wrestling interface so you can struggle for possession without using inventory screen
- Fixed audio for snow/mud footsteps
- Added buttons so ramps can be used with hatches
- Separated adv/fort volume settings
- Fixed jump tooltip
- Stopped adventurers from starting in pits if other sites are available
- Stopped `rbut` from quitting out of character creation
- Removed adventure mode feature discovery announcements
- Updated more classic tiles and brought back cursors

#### Other changes

These changes are also included in [50.15](50.15.md)

- Dye vs profession coloration toggleable in both modes
- Stopped [migrant](../Migrant.md) families with negative age younger siblings in young worlds
- Removed case sensitivity from unit filters
- Fixed a crash from subtabbing to certain buildings
- Made help close button act consistently when help is minimized
- Fixed bug where syndrome would target too few tissues
- Fixed issue with occasional infinite loop on population placement
- Fixed bug where many priests would move to different homes after world generation
