51.13 was a hotfix released on June 27, 2025, one day after [51.12](51.12.md), on the [Bay12 Forums](https://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=183326.0) and [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/975370?emclan=103582791464215078&emgid=547864982714319413).

### General updates

- Fixed a timing error introduced by ammo loading that caused many actions to be performed in slow motion, leading to all of the dwarves dying over a long period as drinking could not catch up with thirst.
- Adventurer can wait when under attack while nocking or loading.
