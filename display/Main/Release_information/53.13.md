### Patch notes

#### Diplomacy

- Your appointed [messengers](../Messenger.md) can now do a variety of diplomatic [missions](../Mission.md) from the [world screen](../Civilization_and_World_Info.md). Make peace, declare war, make contact, and improve [trade](../Trade.md). You can also seek alliances if you have the [monarch](../Monarch.md) in your fortress.
- Increased the priority of messenger actions.
- It's possible to open trade with [humans](../Human.md) and [elves](../Elf.md) before their usual time if you send a messenger. You can also open trade with multiple [civilizations](../Civilization.md) of the same type.
- If traders don't make decent profits and you do not engage in [negotiations](../Trade_agreement.md), trade will taper off.
- Added confirmation to mission interface.
- Changed some colors on mission interface.
- Added a dialog line for dwarven traders from other civilizations.
- Fixed issue where [turning enemies off](../Difficulty.md#individual-enemy-toggles) lead to early caravans (requires resetting difficulty to apply).

#### General updates

- Optimization: [zombies](../Undead.md) don't consider their surrounding until disturbed.
- Optimization: throttled creatures trying to repeatedly failing [path](../Path.md).
- Added announcements for residents and creatures that have begun throttling pathing.
- Can view activity details again from the [residents list](../Unit_list.md).
- Stopped scroll bar from disappearing when closing bins near the bottom of the trade list.
- Increased the maximum number of [announcements](../Announcement.md) that can be saved.
- Prioritized the removal of [sparring](../Sparring.md) announcements from the announcement list.
- Tries to repair corrupted musical [instruments](../Instrument.md) upon load.
- Did some exception handing for map saving and loading.
- Added the ability to display all logged errors on screen (useful for debugging mods etc).

### 20th Anniversary of Dwarf Fortress

[![DF20thAnniversary.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/7/76/DF20thAnniversary.png/400px-DF20thAnniversary.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:DF20thAnniversary.png)

This year we are celebrating the 20th anniversary of Slaves to Armok: God of Blood Chapter II: Dwarf Fortress. To start, we have a new special logo for the occasion featuring Armok himself, who has never been depicted until now! Credit goes to artist Jacob for the new portrait. You can expect to see more 20th anniversary events throughout the rest of the year. If you’re interested in hosting your own Dwarf Fortress gathering, we’ve opened up a [form where you can apply for merch support here](https://forms.gle/TsimLyvt5zLyTikU7).
