### Alexandra's notes

Hey everyone,

It's Alexandra here as we continue along with the routine patches after [Adventure Mode](../Adventure_Mode.md) release. We have quality of life fixes for [Fortress Mode](../Fortress_Mode.md) including the new ability to use the previous [material](../Material.md) when replacing [constructions](../Constructions.md). No need to go back through the menus over and over again! Over in Adventure Mode your [speed](../Speed.md) is now displayed properly. Lots of other adjustment and fixes here as well.

So far the lua tests are going smoothly and we are receiving really great feedback. If you're a mod-minded urist and want to play round with the [lua](../Lua.md) update, you can check it out on the Experimental Branch.

See you in the next patch,

[Alexandra](../Alexandra.md)

Release notes for 51.07 (March 12, 2025):

Continuing along with the routine patches after the adventure mode release.

### Fortress mode

- Allowed use of last material when placing simple one-stage buildings and constructions
- Stopped [land holders](../Land_holder.md) without a [residence](../Residence.md) from automatically moving to and gumming up [capitals](../Capital.md)
- [Animals](../Animal.md) marked for [slaughter](../Slaughter.md) and then assigned as [war animal](../War_animal.md) pets are now unmarked for slaughter
- Stopped hidden [zones](../Zone.md) in NPC [fortresses](../Fortress.md) on [reclaims](../Reclaim_fortress_mode~2.md) from clouding information in [tomb](../Tomb.md) and [dungeon](../Dungeon.md) sheets
- [Work orders](../Work_order.md): changed "stone stones" to "stone boulders" and "stones" to "boulders", because they can be made from ice

### Adventure mode

- Allowed ordinary [destiny](../Destiny.md) adventurers to start anywhere in their civilization
- Handled infinite loop having to do with [sites](../Site.md) near [oceans](../Ocean.md)
- Unstackable items no longer stacked in character creation
- Movement blocking announcements are displayed during [travel](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Travel&action=edit&redlink=1) properly
- FPS display no longer blocks the speed readout
- Potential [containers](../Container.md) are no longer shown with nonsense directions when placing items
- Classic combat indicators are displayed in the correct position
- Changes to fort mode sound level sliders no longer affect adv mode sound levels

### General updates

- Stopped projectiles and falling creatures from falling through thin floors if they hit a sideways obstacle first
- Creatures with [multigrasp](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Multigrasp&action=edit&redlink=1) weapons should no longer be generated with shields
- Fixed potential troublesome loop involving stone inclusion loops
- Creatures without exertion have their exertion set to zero
- Handled situation where FPS caps could underflow
- Fixed potential overflow in corpse sizes
