**Stonecutter** is the skill associated with the **stonecutting** [labor](Labor.md). Stonecutters are responsible for [smoothing](Smoothing.md) walls, carving walls into [fortifications](Fortifications.md), carving [minecart](Minecart.md) [tracks](Tracks.md) into floors and ramps, and making rock [blocks](Blocks.md) at a [stoneworker's workshop](Stoneworker's_workshop.md).

Graphically, stonecutters wear all white clothes.

Note that Stonecutters are distinct from both [Stone Carvers](Stone_carver.md) and [Stonecrafters](Stonecrafter.md). The former, 'Carvers, use a [stoneworker's workshop](Stoneworker's_workshop.md) to make [quality](Quality.md) stone [furniture](Furniture.md), while the latter, 'Crafters, work at a [craftdwarf's workshop](Craftdwarf's_workshop.md) and generally create household items, [trade](Trade.md) goods or [decorations](Decoration.md) out of stone.

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/a/a8/Smoothed_wall.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Smoothed_wall.png)

Unsmoothed room (left) and a smoothed one (right).

### Blocks

Because blocks have no [quality](Quality.md) and neither do smoothed walls, having a skilled stonecutter is mostly a matter of speed; however, since your entire fortress is likely to start smoothing as soon as you designate an area for smoothing, it may be desirable to use the [work detail](Work_detail.md) for it regardless. Once you have a large population in your fortress it may be tempting to have everyone do stone smoothing to speed things up, but in the event of one of your dwarves getting a [strange mood](Strange_mood.md) they may end up becoming a useless legendary stonecutter vs a more desirable skill since stonecutting is a moodable skill (even if they are just a novice stonecutter).

A single [boulder](Boulder.md) will produce 4 [blocks](Block.md); blocks are lighter/easier to carry than boulders; blocks have a higher [value](Value.md) than boulders, increasing the architectural [created wealth](Wealth.md) of the resulting constructions; and block walls are more difficult to climb than rough stone walls, nor do they need to be smoothed before being [engraved](Engraving.md). Blocks can't be used to make furniture, crafts or other stone objects the way boulders can, though, nor can they be used in reactions such as steelmaking.

### Fortifications

> *Main article: [Fortification](Fortification.md)*

Fortifications are semi-permeable walls that allow some objects, such as projectiles and liquids, to pass through them. They can be created in natural and constructed stone walls as well as constructed from the build menu.

### Tracks

> *Main article: [Tracks](Minecart.md#tracks)*

Tracks can be carved from natural stone floors and ramps, as well as constructed from the build menu. They are used to transport minecarts which are [tools](Tools.md) used mostly for [hauling](Hauling.md).

### Training

> *Main article: [Cross-training](Cross-training.md#renovation)*

Having stonecutters smooth large areas can be a good way to train them since it doesn’t consume resources, doesn’t create garbage, and will not produce low-quality results. It also builds experience very quickly at higher skill levels.

For repeatable training, minecart tracks can be carved into smooth stone and then smoothed over. This cycle can be repeated indefinitely, and builds experience during both halves of the cycle. Due to minecart tracks only being designated one row/column at a time, this is rather designation-intensive.

Players concerned with using as few non-renewable resources as possible can instead resort to building constructed walls, designating them for fortification via v-f, and de-constructing them afterwards. Since fortifications don’t have quality, this will never cause a negative [thought](Thought.md) for the stonecutter.
