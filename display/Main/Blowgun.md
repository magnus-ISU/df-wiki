# Blowgun

A **blowgun** is a ranged [weapon](Weapon.md) that fires projectiles called [blowdarts](Blowdart.md). Blowguns can be fired incredibly quickly, though they are not very accurate and darts are lightweight. Blowgunners with higher skill and [endurance](Attributes.md#endurance) can shoot at higher velocities. Blowguns are relatively rare, being used primarily by subterranean [animal people](Animal_people.md).

As ranged weapons, blowguns use and train the [blowgunner](Blowgunner.md) skill; as melee weapons, blowguns use and train the [swordsdwarf](Swordsdwarf.md) skill, instead. Dwarves cannot normally [forge](Forge.md) blowguns nor darts, limiting supply to whatever low-[quality](Quality.md) specimens can be wrested from indigenous populations. During a [strange mood](Strange_mood.md), however, an aspiring yet misguided [bowyer](Bowyer.md) may create an [artifact](Artifact.md) blowgun at a [bowyer's workshop](Bowyer's_workshop~2.md). Even if you do manage to get your hands on a blowgun, the severe lack of ammunition will make it nearly impossible to field an effective blowgun-dwarf. All dwarves can equip blowguns.

Units from other [civilizations](Civilization.md) spawned as blowgunners are hardcoded to special behaviour. Unlike other ranged weapon users, they lack [quivers](Quiver.md), but their ammunition will be covered in any animal [extract](Extract.md) that is available to the civilization and causes an injected or contact [syndrome](Syndrome.md). Currently, it is not possible for your dwarves to add a poison or toxin to a weapon without significant workarounds (and a bit of luck), making blowguns even more underwhelming.
