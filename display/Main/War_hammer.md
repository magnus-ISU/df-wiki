A **war hammer** is a [blunt](Attack_types.md) [weapon](Weapon.md) that is essentially a hammer with a long handle. A war hammer is half the size of a [mace](Mace.md) with half the contact area as well, and less than 1/3 the size of a [maul](Maul.md). Because blunt damage focused in a small contact area is mostly unaffected by [armor](Armor.md), war hammers are considered one of the best weapons for fighting humanoid opponents.

War hammers use and train the [hammerdwarf](Hammerdwarf.md) skill. Dwarves can [forge](Forge.md) effective war hammers out of any weapons-grade [metal](Metal.md), though those with higher [densities](Density.md) (like [silver](Silver.md)) or higher impact fracture/yields (like [steel](Steel.md)) tend to cause more damage. All dwarves can equip war hammers, though [a tiny fraction must use two hands](Weapon.md#size).

### Forging and melting

- Metal war hammers cost **one** [metal](Metal.md) bar to [forge](Forge.md), or **three** [adamantine](Adamantine.md) wafers.
- When a non-adamantine metal war hammer is [melted down](Melt.md), it will return **0.9** metal bars, for an **efficiency of 90%**.
- When an adamantine war hammer is melted down, it will produce **0.9** wafers, for an **efficiency of 30%**
