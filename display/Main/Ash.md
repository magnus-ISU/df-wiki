> *For the tree, see [Ash (tree)](Ash_(tree).md).*

**Ash** is an intermediate good used to make [potash](Potash.md), [lye](Lye.md), or to [glaze](Glazer.md) [ceramics](Ceramic_industry.md). Ash is produced by burning [wood](Wood.md) at a [wood furnace](Wood_furnace~2.md), and comes in [bars](Bar.md), stored in a [bar/block stockpile](Stockpile.md#bar2fblock), and viewable in the [stocks](Stocks.md) screen under "bars". Unlike other bars, an ash bar is not a valid construction material.

Procedurally-generated creatures (such as [titans](Titan.md) and [forgotten beasts](Forgotten_beast.md)) may occasionally be composed of ash, though killing them will just result in a useless pile of ash for your dwarves to clean up. A killed [fire man](Fire_man.md) will leave behind a usable bar of ash, though these creatures are rare.

Ash is one of the [hardcoded materials](Hardcoded_material.md).
