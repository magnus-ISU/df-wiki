**Unknown frozen plant substance** is a placeholder [plant](Plant.md) [material](Material.md). It can occasionally appear in-game as a result of [bugs](Bug.md) (*either that, or some [very unwise mod-adjacent meddling](Duplicated_raws.md)*). It is a light gray solid that is not affected by [temperature](Temperature.md), similarly to other undefined materials.
