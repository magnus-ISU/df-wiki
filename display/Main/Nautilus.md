> *A tiny, mollusk with a large shell and many tentacles.*

**Nautiluses** are a source of food for fortresses located near the [ocean](Ocean.md). They are also one of the few types of creatures, along with [oysters](Oyster.md), [cave lobsters](Cave_lobster.md), [mussels](Mussel.md), and [turtles](Turtle.md), that are common sources of [shells](Shell.md). After being caught by a [fisherdwarf](Fisherdwarf.md), they require [cleaning](Fish_cleaning.md) at a [fishery](Fishery.md), which produces a usable shell and an edible nautilus.

According to the raws, nautiluses have 90 tentacles and [copper](Copper.md)-based [blood](Blood.md) (though the tentacles are not yet implemented as part of their body structure).

Some dwarves [like](Preference.md) nautiluses for their *shells* and their *many tentacles*.
