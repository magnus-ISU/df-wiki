# Open space

An **Open space** is a [map tile](Map_tile.md) state that indicates there is nothing there - no [floor](Floor.md), [walls](Wall.md), or [trees](Tree.md); just air, [water](Water.md), or [magma](Magma.md) (and maybe [creatures](Creature.md)).

In both [Dwarf fortress mode](Dwarf_fortress_mode~2.md) and [Adventure mode](Adventure_mode~2.md) you can look at the world around you with the [mouse](Mouse_control.md); in adventure you can also look with the keyboard. A description of the tile will be displayed below the right hand end of the [status](Status.md) bar (same place in adventure mode even though there is no status bar), including if it is open space.
