An **account** is an abstract number that traces the amount of wealth of a given entity ([companies](Company.md), [guilds](Guild.md), etc.) or [historical figure](Historical_figure.md). Although the game doesn't (yet) have a proper [economy](Economy.md), the account system gives some targets for corruption mechanisms such as embezzlement (which shifts 'funds' from a site to a person) or sabotage (which abstractly reduces a site's funds). In addition, wealthy [historical figures](Historical_figure.md) with a large account may grab [houses](House.md) or towers in [towns](Town.md). [Mercenary](Mercenary.md) companies also draw funds from their account to upgrade their weapons, and may disband if they go broke.
