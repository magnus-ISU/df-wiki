**Sandstone** is a [sedimentary layer](Sedimentary_layer.md) of [stone](Stone.md). It can sometimes contain an [aquifer](Aquifer.md), can be found along the [ocean](Ocean.md) shore, and is also named for the eighth month of the dwarven [calendar](Calendar.md), covering mid-Autumn.

### In real life

Sandstone is a sedimentary stone composed of broken pieces of minerals, usually quartz or [potassium feldspar](Orthoclase.md), cemented together with either the minerals [hematite](Hematite.md) (Fe<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>), [calcite](Calcite.md) (CaCO<sub>3</sub>), or silica (SiO<sub>2</sub>), or through sheer pressure compacting the grains together. When the individual grains are larger than 2 millimeters, the stone is called a [conglomerate](Conglomerate.md) if the grains are rounded, or a breccia (not present in *Dwarf Fortress*) if the grains have sharp points or angles. If the individual grains are smaller than 1/16th of a millimeter, the stone is called a [siltstone](Siltstone.md). When sandstone is exposed to high heat, such as through [magma](Magma.md) coming into contact with the stone, the grains melt and are recrystallized, forming the stone [quartzite](Quartzite.md). It is found in numerous colors, the most common being yellow, red, gray, white, tan, and brown.
