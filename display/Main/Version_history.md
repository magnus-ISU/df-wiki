### Dwarf Fortress v0.34

In a [devlog](http://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/index.html) entry for 2011-01-30, Threetoe announced plans for a series of nine numbered releases ("Short-term Goals" on the [dev page](http://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/dev.html)) to finish off the long-planned "caravan" development arc. Release 1, improving towns and introducing markets, along with a variety of supernatural creature types, was predicted to have a version number of [0.33 or 0.34](http://www.bay12games.com/media/df_talk_14_transcript.html#14.16) and was released on Valentine's Day 2012 as version 0.34.01.

### 3D Version (before 2010)

A test branch including 0.28.181.40d2 thru 0.28.181.40d19: OpenGL optimizations, macros, zooming, and native Linux support. Not available on [Bay 12 Games](Bay_12_Games.md), but can be found on the [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/dwarf-fortress?tab=collection&and%5B%5D=year%3A%5B2009+TO+2010%5D)
