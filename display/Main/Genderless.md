> *Not to be confused with [non-breeding creatures](../Category/Non-Breeding.md).*

**Genderless** creatures have only one gender, due to lacking `[CASTE:MALE]` and `[CASTE:FEMALE]` in their raws.

Genderless creatures are presumably spawned during [worldgen](Worldgen.md).
