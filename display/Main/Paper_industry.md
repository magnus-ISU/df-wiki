The **paper industry** deals with the creation and processing of paper [sheets](Sheet.md), and further processing into [quires](Quire.md) and [scrolls](Scroll.md).

### Sheets

There are three major ways to create paper for books and scrolls - each depending on a different raw material.

The finished sheets can be stored in their own stockpile.

#### Raw materials

The raw materials that can be used:

- [Papyrus](Papyrus_sedge.md)
- Animal [hide](Skin.md)
- Cloth plants
  - [rope reed](Rope_reed.md)
  - [pig tail](Pig_tail~2.md)
  - [hemp](Hemp.md)
  - [cotton](Cotton.md)
  - [ramie](Ramie.md)
  - [flax](Flax.md)
  - [jute](Jute.md)
  - [kenaf](Kenaf.md)

#### Processing

The method, workshops, and labors required to process a material into sheets depends on the type of material.

##### Papyrus

[Papyrus](Papyrus_sedge.md) can be directly made into sheets at a [farmer's workshop](Farmer's_workshop~2.md), using the [Papermaking](Papermaking.md) labor.

Paper industry overview

##### Parchment

Processing [animal hides](Skin.md) is more complex; you need to produce [quicklime](Quicklime.md) from [calcium carbonate](Calcium_carbonate.md) stones at a [kiln](Kiln.md), then process the [quicklime](Quicklime.md) to [milk of lime](Milk_of_lime.md) at an [ashery](Ashery.md). Finally, the hide and [milk of lime](Milk_of_lime.md) are used in a [tanner's shop](Tanner's_shop~2.md), using the [tanning](Tanner.md) labor, to create a parchment sheet. For each sheet of parchment, 1 hide is needed, disregarding the size of the original animal.

If the hide is from a cow, it's called "vellum sheet", otherwise it will take on the name of the animal (i.e. pig parchment sheet, sheep parchment sheet).

##### Plant fibers

Cloth plants are mashed to [slurry](Slurry.md) with a constructed [millstone](Millstone.md) or [quern](Quern.md), then pressed to a sheet in a [screw press](Screw_press.md). Both processes use the [Papermaking](Papermaking.md) labor.

### Finished goods

The sheets can then be further processed into finished goods.

#### Quires

1 sheet of paper can be crafted into 1 [quire](Quire.md) at a [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop~2.md), using the [Bookbinding](Bookbinding.md) labor.

#### Codices

After a [quire](Quire.md) has been filled with text, it can be crafted into a [codex](Book.md) at a [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop~2.md) using the [Bookbinding](Bookbinding.md) labor - scrolls cannot be made into codices. This requires 1 [quire](Quire.md), 1 [book binding](Book_binding.md) and 1 unused [thread](Thread.md). Animal [hair](Hair.md) thread can be used for this purpose.

#### Scrolls

1 sheet of paper can be combined with 1 [scroll roller](Scroll_rollers.md) at a [craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop~2.md) to create a [scroll](Scroll.md) using the [Bookbinding](Bookbinding.md) labor. Paper quality does not have any effect on scroll quality.
