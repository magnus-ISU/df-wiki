*This page is a list of hidden or unexpected aspects of the game, once known as HFS, or Hidden Fun Stuff. Though technically 'HFS' is used in the [dev logs](Dev_logs.md) to refer to anything Toady doesn't want us to know about until we find it ourselves, players have appropriated the name to only refer to... well, [we won't spoil it for you](HFS.md).*

#### Fluctuating Subtitle

When [starting up the game](Your_first_fortress.md), on the title screen where the player first chooses what to do, the top reads "Histories of X and Y", where X and Y will be randomly selected each time the game is opened. Some combinations include "Histories of Avarice and Resourcefulness" and "Histories of Jealousy and Tenacity." Generally, the words will include a synonym each of "greed" and "stubbornness", which as we all know fit the hairy little blighters like a [cave spider silk](Cave_spider.md) [glove](Clothing.md#articles).

#### Dwarven Daydreams

Some dwarves [like](Preferences.md) Toads for their *beauty*, an obvious inside reference to [Toady One](Toady_One.md).

#### So What If I'm Dead?

Checking the [weather](Weather.md), temperature, odor, or [date](Calendar.md) in [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md) after dying, will instead return a snarky message reminding you of your current medical state.

#### Toads as the default creature

Toads are the first creature entry to be parsed in the [raw files](Raw_file.md), being the first creature listed in the first file ("creature_amphibians"). Hence, many incidences where the game attempts to pick a creature type and fails, will result in "toads" being picked as a default.

When a creature is named (not for killing a dwarf), you get a message like: "The dwarves named a resident Cave Swallowman Urist McSwallowman". If you are in adventure mode and all the dwarves/humans/elves/goblins are dead, you get the message: "The *toads* named a resident Cave Swallowman Urist McSwallowman".

#### Moss

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/0/07/Goblin_agreements.png/300px-Goblin_agreements.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Goblin_agreements.png)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Goblin_agreements.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Goblin_agreements.png)

Trading is a serious business.

A small egg, mostly because few have seen it. Reclaiming an old fort after around 50 years or so will cause [moss](Moss.md) to appear on its stone block constructions. Not much is known about moss, except that it can only occur in certain [biomes](Biome.md).<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Easter_eggs&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>

#### Trade agreements with enemies

If you view a civilization you are at war with in the civilizations menu (provided that there is one) and navigate to the Trade agreements tab, it will say that you have an agreement with them that they provide you with Terror and you give them Vengeance in return. Similarly, viewing kobolds will reveal an "agreement" in which they import Petty Annoyance and you export Death.

#### Cats as Legendary Climbers

All [cats](Cat.md) are legendary [climbers](Climber.md) by default, which is impressive, given that cats can't gain even Novice in any other skill, and especially given how difficult it is to reach Legendary Climber as an adventurer. It's worth noting that [giant cave spiders](Giant_cave_spider.md) and [many](Giant_lizard.md) [other](Large_roach.md) [creatures](Hungry_head.md) also have the same default climbing skill.
