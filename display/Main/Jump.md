# Jump

**Jumping** is a means of crossing otherwise uncrossable obstacles. Unlike with [climbing](Climber.md), there is no associated skill with jumping.

Most creatures can jump - those that cannot; have the `[CANNOT_JUMP]` tag.

### Fortress mode

In [dwarf fortress mode](Dwarf_fortress_mode~2.md), dwarves and other creatures will sometimes jump, usually when trying to get to an enemy on the far side of water. This has implications for [fortress defense](Defense_guide.md). One-tile moats, for example, are unlikely to be a significant obstacle to [invaders](Invader.md), and your own dwarves may opt to leap from your defenses to complete an already-active job. Jumping creature are shown with an "airborne" [status icon](Status_icon.md).

### Adventurer mode

In [adventurer mode](Adventurer_mode.md), your adventurer can jump by using the [![Adv ui j.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/5/5a/Adv_ui_j.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Adv_ui_j.png)j key. If your adventurer is walking, the jump is limited to two squares. By setting [![Adv ui m.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/8/84/Adv_ui_m.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Adv_ui_m.png)m the walking [speed](Gait.md) to "Sprint" or "Run" and picking up speed until the maximum is reached, the distance you can jump is drastically increased. Indeed, this even allows an adventurer untrained in [swimming](Swimmer.md) to cross [rivers](River.md).

Jumping can also be used in combat - provided it hits, it will send similarly-sized targets flying, do some damage to them (both from "skidding on the ground", and from collision with something - sometimes even another enemy) and leave them stunned for easy follow-up attacks until they recover. It can also be used to knock enemies that are wrestling your companions away. Since wrestling prevents dodging, this will always connect.
