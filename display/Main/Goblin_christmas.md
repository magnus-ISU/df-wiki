**Goblin Christmas** (sometimes also [Elf Christmas](Elf.md), but only if your dwarves have been *very* [good](War.md)) is the wonderful [time](Siege.md) of year when ["A vile force of darkness has arrived and is unloading its goods!"](Siege.md). All the good little dwarves stay [underground](Underground.md) while [Goblin Claus](Goblin.md) leaves his treats in the [trap](Trap.md)-lined entrance to the fortress.

In the morning, when the all-clear is sounded, everyone rushes to the entryway, and gathers up the [Goblinite](Goblinite.md) for [melting](Melt.md) and the [silk](Silk.md) [socks](Clothing.md) for [decorating](Decorate.md) the magma fireplace.

Don't be like the bad dwarves, who peek at their presents too early or leave the doors open for Goblin Claus to get in. [Bad things](Death.md) happen to those dwarves.

☼beak dog roast☼ is a traditional meal of this [wonderful](Fun.md) holiday. [Goblin Claus](Demon.md) will send [ingredients for it](Beak_dog.md) in the trap-lined hallway for the dwarves to use. If the dwarves have been especially [good](Wealth.md) this year, he may send [more exotic ingredients](Cave_dragon.md).

Recently, Goblin Claus has returned to the tradition of the days of [yore](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Main_Page), sending his [helpers](Troll.md) and making Goblin Christmas much more [Fun](Fun.md). In rare occasions, [Goblin Claus himself](Demon.md) has been reported to make a personal appearance.

[Human Christmas](Human.md) can also happen, but because of the more advanced nature of the Humanite it is often advised to take great care in receiving gifts from [Human Santa](Human.md).
