A **Magma smelter** is a special variation of the basic [smelter](Smelter.md) that uses [magma](Magma.md) as a source of heat, instead of [fuel](Fuel.md).

To function, magma smelters must be built with at least one of the eight edge tiles occupying an open space with at least 4/7 magma on the next floor down. This magma can be natural, or channeled from a larger source, or otherwise [transported from another level](Magma.md#bringing-magma-up) to a trench designed to hold the magma.

Magma smelters do not consume any magma from their magma source - you can use a single tile of 4/7 (or deeper) magma below the smelter as an infinite heat source. Note that both [pig iron](Pig_iron~2.md) and [steel](Steel.md) production still require either [coke](Coke.md) or [charcoal](Charcoal.md) as a part of the recipe, as a source of carbon for the reaction.

Creatures can climb up through the open tile that the building is built on, making them a potential entry point into your fortress. The smelter in older versions had an [impassable tile](Impassable_tile.md) that could be used to prevent this, but it has been removed as of v50.
