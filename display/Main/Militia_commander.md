The **militia commander** is an appointed [noble](Noble.md) who serves as the senior leader in the [military](Military.md) command hierarchy of your [fortress](Fortress.md). They can be assigned at any point (from either the [![Ui n.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/7/7b/Ui_n.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_n.png)n [nobles screen](Nobles_screen~2.md) or the [![Ui q.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/3/39/Ui_q.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_q.png)q [squad screen](Squad.md)), and have no room [requirements](Noble.md#needs). The militia commander is only surpassed by the [general](General.md) in terms of military rank among dwarves who may inhabit a playable fortress.

The militia commander administrates your armies, and the position is necessary to expand your militia with new [squads](Squads.md) and to assign [militia captains](Militia_captain.md) to serve as squad leaders. If desired, they can act as a [militia captain](Militia_captain.md) to lead a squad of their own, though this is not necessary. Their role is primarily a figurehead and grants no bonuses unless personally participating in military activities. As such, they can be completely unskilled in [combat skills](Combat_skill.md); in fact, many fortresses avoid assigning a useful military dwarf to the role. However, if their squad is created and activated, they participate in the training, venture with them on [missions](Missions.md) and serve the same as any other squad leader.

Some [skills](Skill.md) have been reported to aid the role of the militia commander, though none are mandatory for that position. [Organizer](Organizer.md) speeds up the time to organize [training](Training.md#organized-squad-training) sessions. [Military tactics](Military_tactics~2.md) is used when squads are sent into missions<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Militia_commander&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>, and shows up as a "relevant skill" when assigning the role in the nobles screen. [Ambusher](Ambusher.md) skill is also useful for raids where dwarves will attempt to avoid detection.<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Militia_commander&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>
