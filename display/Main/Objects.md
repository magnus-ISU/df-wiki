The **Objects status screen** contains four tabs ([Artifacts](Legendary_artifact.md), Symbols, Named objects, and [Written content](Book.md)), each of which is a list of special, named, or unique items.

Each list entry contains the following columns (from left to right):

- An image of the item.
- The [name](Name.md) of the item and its translation.
- Buttons to go to the item's location or open its item sheet, if applicable.
- The [entity](Entity.md) that [claims](Claim.md#artifact-claim) the object, if applicable.

### Artifacts tab

> *Main article: [Legendary artifact](Legendary_artifact.md)*

### Symbols tab

Symbols are items given to [nobles](Noble.md) in the [Nobles and administrators](#nobles-and-administrators-status-screen) screen. They may be [artifacts](Legendary_artifact.md), or they may be other items, but will always be named (giving a noble a symbol includes naming the item if not already named).

### Named objects tab

This tab lists all named, non-[artifact](Legendary_artifact.md) items in your fortress. This may include non-artifact items also in the Symbols Tab.

### Written content tab

> *Main article: [Book](Book.md)*

Written content never has a [claimant](Claim.md#artifact-claim), so the last column will be empty. Typically, the work's translated name will be the same as its original name, with a few exceptions. The list will only include original works, not copies. While the majority of written content will be [scrolls](Book.md#scroll) and [quires/codices](Book.md#quire-and-codex), other objects, such as [slabs](Slab.md) containing the [secrets](Secret.md) of [life and death](Necromancy.md) can be included.
