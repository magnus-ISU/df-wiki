**Tools** are finished goods, used for a variety of mundane purposes by civilians and [night creatures](Night_creature.md). Some tools can also be used as [weapons](Weapon.md), though it is usually much better to use, say, a [sword](Sword.md) over a carving fork. [Other tools](Tools.md#other-tools) can be created and used by your dwarves in [fortress mode](Fortress_mode~2.md), including as [furniture](Furniture.md). [Honeycombs](Honeycomb.md) also count as tools, and are stockpiled as such.

You can select handheld tools as starting equipment if your adventurer hails from a human civilization.
