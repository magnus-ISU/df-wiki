**Chains** and **Ropes** are items used to make [traction benches](Traction_bench~2.md), [wells](Well.md), [restraints](Restraint.md) and [minecart](Minecart.md) rollers. Ropes are not actually a distinct item type - they are merely what chains are called when they are made of cloth.

They are both listed under the "chains" heading in the k-stocks menu, are stored in finished goods [stockpiles](Stockpile.md), and are generally interchangeable.

Chains are created at the [metalsmith's forge](Metalsmith's_forge.md) with one [metal](Metal.md) [bar](Bar.md) (or four [adamantine](Adamantine.md) wafers), using the [metal craft](Metal_crafter.md) [skill](Skill.md) (but are found listed under "Furniture" and not "other objects" in that menu). Ropes are created at the [clothier's shop](Clothier's_shop.md) with one [cloth](Cloth.md), using the [clothier](Clothier.md) [skill](Skill.md).

Taking a rope with you at [embark](Embark.md) lets you construct a [well](Well.md) more quickly. While it's usually much cheaper to take raw materials and produce ready goods after embark, the [item value](Item_value.md) of a fiber rope is 20 vs. 12 of the [thread](Thread.md). The dwarves will not enjoy using a base quality item as much, but cost-wise it matters 3 times less than the mechanism. On the other hand, you can make 4 metal chains after smelting one copper ore (cost 6) into 4 bars once you have your forge up and running, but that won't happen as fast - lots to consider.

Chains and ropes are considered superior vs. [cages](Cage.md) for dwarven [justice](Justice.md), as you can construct a bedroom around the restraint and allow the prisoner some mobility to use a bed, eat at a table and chair, and otherwise enjoy any fixtures of their "cell" that you have installed there.

- **Bug**: When releasing a creature from a restraint, if you deconstruct the chain/rope first, the animal will then still have that around their neck - with no way to remove it, and no way to re-construct it elsewhere. If you value the chain or rope, take the time to first release the animal, *then* deconstruct the chain from its current location. On the other hand, if you want a nifty ≡gold chain≡ as a permanent collar around the neck of a favorite guard tiger, this is how you'd get that.
