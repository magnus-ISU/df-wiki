Olive oil is unusual in that it is pressed from the whole olive fruit, rather than from oil-bearing seeds.

**Oil** is an ingredient in [cooking](Cooking.md) and [soap](Soap.md) making, made by [pressing](Pressing.md) one of the several [seed](Seed.md) pastes or fruit at a [screw press](Screw_press.md). The press squeezes the material, releasing the oil, which is stored in a [liquid](Liquid.md)-capable [jug](Jug.md). The oil can then be used to supplement a fortress's [food](Food.md) supply, or can be combined with [lye](Lye.md) to make [soap](Soap.md).

### Oil-producing crops

The following crops produce seeds that can be milled and pressed into oil:

| Plant Name | Underground? | Oil Name | Oil Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Quarry bush](Quarry_bush~2.md) | Yes | Rock Nut oil | 5☼ |
| [Cotton](Cotton.md) | No | Cottonseed oil | 5☼ |
| [Flax](Flax.md) | No | Linseed oil | 5☼ |
| [Hemp](Hemp.md) | No | Hempseed oil | 5☼ |
| [Kenaf](Kenaf.md) | No | Kenaf seed oil | 5☼ |
| [Olive](Olive.md) (fruit) | No | Olive oil | 5☼ |

### Value

Oil will be used in the [kitchen](Kitchen.md) when 'prepare meal' tasks are queued if it is enabled in the [Kitchen tab](Labor.md#kitchen) in the Labor menu [![Ui y.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/c/cb/Ui_y.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_y.png)y.

Most of the seeds can be cooked directly, but have a base value of 1☼. However, [milling](Milling.md) the seeds to a paste and pressing them into oil generates a [press cake](Press_cake.md) with a value of 1☼ and a unit of oil with a value of 5☼, a 500% value increase for only two [labors](Labor.md).

### Bugs

Cooks will only cook oil and other fluids as a last resort, instead preferring to cook solid foods with solid foods. <sup>[Bug](#bugs):[2393](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=2393)</sup> For more information, see the [kitchen](Kitchen.md#bugs) page.
