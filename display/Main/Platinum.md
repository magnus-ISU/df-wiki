**Platinum** is a rare and extremely valuable [metal](Metal.md), behind only [adamantine](Adamantine.md) and [divine metal](Divine_metal.md) (and tied with [aluminum](Aluminum.md)). Platinum is [magma-safe](Magma-safe.md) and is significant for being reasonably strong and the heaviest metal, making it the best material for [artifact](Artifact.md) impact [weapons](Weapon.md).

Platinum is extremely heavy, making it a good choice for your [moody](Mood.md) dwarves looking to create [artifact](Artifact.md) [war hammers](War_hammer.md) or [maces](Mace.md). They're also a good choice for [minecarts](Minecart.md): platinum minecarts destroy creatures of virtually any size by crushing.
