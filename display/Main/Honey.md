# Honey

**Honey** is a product [pressed](Screw_press.md) from a [honeycomb](Honeycomb.md). Honey can be made into [mead](Mead.md) at a [still](Still.md) (as a separate task) or used in [cooking](Cook.md) [prepared meals](Prepared_meal.md).

Honeycombs are produced by [honey bees](Honey_bee.md) at a [hive](Hive.md), harvested as part of the [beekeeping industry](Beekeeping_industry~2.md). In order to press the honeycomb, you must have a dwarf with the [pressing](Presser.md) labor active, a [screw press](Screw_press.md), and empty [jugs](Jug.md). A [wax](Wax.md) cake is produced as a byproduct, and a [wax worker](Wax_worker.md) can use it to create wax crafts in the [Craftsdwarf's workshop](Craftsdwarf's_workshop~2.md). Honey is the only animal product that can be brewed, and it, along with [royal jelly](Royal_jelly.md), are the edible products of bees. Honey has the lowest value of 1, and is brewed into 5 mead, with a value of 2 each.

Honey jugs will be stored either in a [finished goods](Finished_goods~2.md) stockpile allowing the storage of "tools" or a food stockpile set to allow "animal extracts - honey bee honey". Turning it into mead works regardless, but if bins are allowed on a finished goods stockpile where honey is stored, [hauling](Hauling.md) of the bins can make the honey temporarily unavailable and force the cooking or brewing jobs to cancel.
