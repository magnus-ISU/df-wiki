# Dwarf Fortress Wiki

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Dwarf_Fortress_Wiki) &middot; revision 296909 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-12 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Main/Dwarf_Fortress_Wiki.wiki)*

---
The **Dwarf Fortress Wiki** is a [free-access](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/gratis), [free-content](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/free_content) *[Dwarf Fortress](Dwarf_Fortress.md)* [wiki](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/wiki), supported and hosted by ~~[some guy called Briess](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User:Briess)~~ [Emi](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User:Emi) and [Lethosor](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User:Lethosor). Those who have [created an account](http://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Special:UserLogin&type=signup) on the site can edit most of its articles. The Dwarf Fortress Wiki is ranked among the ten most popular *Dwarf Fortress*-related websites<sup>a</sup>, and constitutes the [Internet](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet)'s largest and most popular *Dwarf Fortress* reference<sup>b</sup>.

It launched on October 29, 2007, and has [10,345](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Special:Statistics) articles.

The hosting costs for the wiki have generously been provided by [Kitfox Games](https://www.kitfoxgames.com) since November 2022.

### History

#### 40d era

Briess launched the Dwarf Fortress Wiki on October 29, 2007.

#### v0.31 era

A new version of the wiki was cloned for the 2010 release. It appears that the 23a version of the wiki was retroactively created during this time.

#### v0.34 era

A new version of the wiki was cloned for the 2012 release.

#### DF2014 era

A new version of the wiki was cloned for the 2014 release.

#### v50 era

A new version of the wiki was cloned for the initial [Premium](Premium.md) release, in 2022. This is the most recent version.

### Community

The Dwarf Fortress Wiki has [38,856](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Special:ActiveUsers) registered editors, and [15](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Special:ActiveUsers) active editors. An editor is considered active if they make one or more edits in said month.

### Operation

#### Software operations and support

The operation of the Dwarf Fortress Wiki depends on [MediaWiki](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MediaWiki), a custom-made, [free](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/free_software) and [open source](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/open_source_software) [wiki software](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/wiki_software) platform written in [PHP](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PHP) and built upon the [MySQL](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MySQL) database system.
