**Forests** are [biomes](Biome.md) which are generally characterized by extremely dense [tree](Tree.md) growth and large numbers of [murky pools](Murky_pool.md) - the main difference between them is the particular kind of wildlife that inhabits the area. [Elves](Elf.md) build their homes (known as [forest retreats](Forest_retreat.md)) in any type of forest.

Particularly dense forests may have so many trees that, in order for [wagons](Wagon.md) to arrive, you will first need to cut a path to the edge of the map. Then, building a [road](Road.md) along this path will prevent trees regrowing and blocking the way. There are only three kinds of trees that are specific to the alignment of the forest. These are [feather trees](Feather_tree.md) (good), [glumprongs](Glumprong.md) (evil), and [highwoods](Highwood.md) (savage).

The six types of forests you will find are:

- [Taiga](Taiga.md)
- [Temperate](Temperate.md) broadleaf forest
- Temperate coniferous forest
- [Tropical](Tropical.md) moist broadleaf forest
- Tropical dry broadleaf forest (\* requires poles and a Medium or Large world size to generate due to a bug)
- Tropical coniferous forest

[Taigas](Taiga.md) and tropical forests are situated in sufficiently harsh climates that acquiring water can become a challenge.
