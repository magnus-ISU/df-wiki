The game interface is made up of several different parts. In the screenshot you will notice:

- **A**: This is the area where [announcements](Announcements.md) pop up.
- **B**: In the lower left corner are menu buttons for [creatures](Unit_list.md), tasks, [places](Location.md), [labor](Labor.md), [work orders](Work_order.md), [nobles](Noble.md), objects, and [justice](Justice.md).
- **C**: If enabled, the [FPS](FPS.md) counter is shown here, as game [ticks](Time.md) (and graphical FPS).
- **D**: The center [designation](Designation.md) tools shape the world:
  - [![Ui m.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/d/d7/Ui_m.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_m.png)m [Mining](Mining.md)
  - [![Ui l.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b2/Ui_l.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_l.png)l [Logging](Woodcutter.md)
  - [![Ui g.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/e/e2/Ui_g.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_g.png)g [Gathering](Plant_gathering.md)
  - [![Ui v.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/e/ea/Ui_v.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_v.png)v [Smoothing](Smoothing.md) and [engra\[v\]ing](Engraving.md)
  - [![Ui x.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/bd/Ui_x.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_x.png)x Removing [designation](Designation.md) orders
  - [![Ui b.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b1/Ui_b.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_b.png)b [Building](Building.md)
  - [![Ui p.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/4/4a/Ui_p.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_p.png)p [Stock\[p\]iles](Stockpile.md)
  - [![Ui z.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/1/13/Ui_z.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_z.png)z [Zones](Zone.md)
  - [![Ui U.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b3/Ui_U.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_U.png)U [B\[U\]rrows](Burrows.md)
  - [![Ui h.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/1/10/Ui_h.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_h.png)h [Minecart](Minecart.md) \[h\]aul routes (H could remain in saves created prior [53.09](Release_information/53.09.md#general-updates))
  - [![Ui T.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/3/3a/Ui_T.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_T.png)T [Traffic](Traffic.md)
  - [![Ui i.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/e/eb/Ui_i.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_i.png)i [Melt](Melt.md), [dump](Dump.md), [hide](Hide_Items_or_Buildings.md), or [forbid](Forbid.md) \[i\]tems
- **E**: The lower right corner are buttons for your [military](Military.md) menu and an [overview](Civilization_and_World_Info.md) of rest of the world.
- **F**: This bar indicates how deep the currently viewed [elevation](Z-level.md) is; blue is above ground, the rest is below.
- **G**: A mini-map of the current elevation your entire site with the area you can currently see outlined in yellow, and the specific elevation number below.
- **H**: These are some game controls:
  - Pause, play (can switch between these with Space), settings (also available in ESC menu), and help.
  - [Hotkey menu](Hotkeys.md), also H.
  - Move view to surface at this position.
  - Move view to lowest revealed level at this position.
  - Zoom view in, also \[ or Ctrl+Wheel, and display fluid level, also f.
  - Zoom view out, also \] or Ctrl+Wheel, and display ramp indicators, also r.
- **I**: This is the overall [Status](Status.md) of your fortress, including name, happiness levels, some food [Stocks](Stocks.md) counts, and [calendar](Calendar.md) information; this bar can vary in size and components depending on game resolution and UI scale settings.
