[![Bag of Dimple Cup dye powder.](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/4/48/Dye_bag_preview.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Dye_bag_preview.png)

**Dye** can be used at the [dyer's shop](Dyer's_shop.md) to change the [color](Color.md#color-tokens) of clothing materials.

### Production

Dyes can be produced by milling a number of [plants](Crop.md) at a [millstone](Millstone.md) or [quern](Quern.md) using [Miller](Miller.md) (which also produces seeds) as well as from other sources at a [dyer's shop](Dyer's_shop.md) using [Thresher](Thresher.md) (which does **not** produce seeds). Usually, all [caravans](Trading.md) will bring dye for [trade](Trade.md) too. Importing dye, however, is only useful if you have a somewhat-skilled [dyer](Dyer.md), otherwise, little value is added and the work wasted. It is better to buy or grow the 'source' plants if available, and mill them yourself. Of course, the nice colors are a value in themselves, if you don't stuff it all in bins.

Dyes can be applied either to woven [cloth](Cloth.md), raw [thread](Thread.md), or [leather](Leather.md)<sup>v52.03</sup>. Dyed items have their [value](Value.md) increased by the value of the dye (see table below), multiplied by the [quality](Quality.md) with which the dye was applied. There is not known to be any difference in the end product based on when the dye was applied during the production process. Dyes can't be applied to finished goods made from cloth, such as clothing, bags, or crafts.

Dyes can be mixed to create additional colors. The desired color can be chosen at the [dyer's shop](Dyer's_shop.md) with the "dye cloth ([color](Color.md))" job, blending dyes with each other, or the material's natural color, to produce the chosen color. If the "display worn clothing dyes" option is enabled (which have two variants for both fortress mode and adventurer mode), any clothing wearing [creatures](Creature.md) will be seen in clothing colors as they truly appear. If this option is disabled, the clothing color will resemble their closest occupation instead.

### Storage

On the [stocks](Stocks.md) screen, dyes are listed under [powder](Powder.md). Dyes are [stockpiled](Stockpile.md) as "Food/Milled Plants", along with [flours](Flour.md). [Barrels](Barrel.md) that contain only powders are called 'Mill barrel'. Each barrel can contain up to 60 dye [bags](Bag.md), each bag containing one stack of dye items.

### Work Order Conditions

Bag contents are not recognized in the [manager's work order conditions](Manager.md#work-orders). As such, item type (powder) and material (e.g. 'Dimple dye', 'Redroot dye', 'potato skin dye', etc) will not work (despite being the recommended condition on create dye jobs). As a workaround, reference all items/materials and use adjectives.

Examples:

- Count all stacks of dye: Item type 'items', material type 'no material', adjective 'dye item'
- Count all stacks of midnight blue (dimple cup) dye: Item type 'items', material type 'no material', adjective 'dye item', adjective 'Color midnight blue'

### Trivia

- A number of plants have comments in their [raw files](Raw_file.md) suggesting they should produce dye, dating from before more dyes were added in [52.03](Release_information/52.03.md). [Wild carrot](Wild_carrot.md) and [rhubarb](Rhubarb.md) produce dyes from different parts than their comment suggests: flowers and roots in the respective comments. [Pendant amaranth](Pendant_amaranth.md) flowers are noted to produce dye, but do not have any in-game dye materials.
- Sliver barbs are only found in [evil](Surroundings.md#evil) areas, therefore, [elves](Elf.md), [dwarves](Dwarf.md), and [humans](Human.md) won't bring sliver barbs to trade. To obtain it, you must embark in an evil area and pull it from the ground yourself.
- [Redroot dye](Hide_root.md) is the only dye priced at 10[☼](Currency.md).
