**Altars**, also known as **Offering Places**, are a [furniture](Furniture.md) item optionally used in [temples](Temple.md). They can be placed with [![Ui b.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b1/Ui_b.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_b.png)b[![Ui bf.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/b/b0/Ui_bf.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bf.png)f[![Ui bfp.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/e/ee/Ui_bfp.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Ui_bfp.png)p, though they currently serve no purpose other than increasing a [room's value](Zone.md#quality-and-value) and decoration, as [temples](Temple.md) don't require them to function.

In fortress mode, altars can be made from most materials like wood, stone, glass, and metal at their respective [workshops](Workshop.md). Altars can be stored in furniture [stockpiles](Stockpile.md), and fall in the "other large tools" item type category.

Altars are extremely lightweight compared to other "furniture" (1/20 to 1/60 the weight) with the same base [value](Value.md), and so make excellent trade items to produce. Dwarves have been known to place [skulls](Skull.md) on altars.

In [adventure mode](Adventure_mode~2.md), you can often find [dice](Dice.md) that can be rolled on altars.

##### Curses

A dwarf that knocks over an altar within a dedicated temple, during a tantrum or otherwise, will be [cursed](Cursed.md) by that temple's deity with a random were-curse (as long as they are a believer of that deity). There is no cure. The player will receive a pop-up notification stating that the god has cursed their dwarf, and that dwarf will transform into a hostile beast during [the next full moon](Werebeast.md#full-moons-2f-transformation-dates).
