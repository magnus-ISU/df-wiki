**Divine fabrics** are special, procedurally generated [fabrics](Cloth.md) that can be found in [vaults](Vault.md). Divine fabrics are used as material for items and [clothing](Clothing.md) used by [angels](Angel.md), who ruthlessly protect the vault's priceless treasures. Unlike [divine metals](Divine_metal.md), divine fabrics cannot be modified into other items, nor are they appreciably better than their mundane counterparts.

As a quirk, [human](Human.md) and [elven](Elven.md) caravans may bring [instruments](Instrument.md) and instrument parts made out of divine fabric for trade.<sup>[Bug](#bugs):[9275](https://dwarffortressbugtracker.com/view.php?id=9275)</sup>

### Traits

Divine fabrics appear to have identical [material properties](Material_definition_token.md) to standard [silk](Silk.md) [cloth](Cloth.md) except a lack of ignition point. Although they cannot catch [fire](Fire.md), they still suffer damage from high temperatures and are not [fire-safe](Fire-safe.md). The only differences between types of divine fabric are their name and their color<sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Talk:Divine_fabric&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>, both decided by a selected [sphere](Sphere.md) of the deity involved in their creation.

[![](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/thumb/5/5a/Divine_fabric_preview.jpg/300px-Divine_fabric_preview.jpg)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Divine_fabric_preview.jpg)

[https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Divine_fabric_preview.jpg](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Divine_fabric_preview.jpg)

Its texture can cure depression. Its color can remove pain. Its sheen can spawn happiness.
