### About this wiki

Dwarf Fortress Wiki used to be hosted by a guy named [Briess](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User:Briess). He is awesome. The server has many numbers and the numbers are very impressive. [Briess](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User:Briess) is a credit to his team.

His Bay 12 forum profile is [here.](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?action=profile;u=18951)

[Emi](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User:Emi) has been keeping things running for a while now. She is also awesome.

The hosting costs for the wiki have generously been provided by [Kitfox Games](https://www.kitfoxgames.com) since November 2022.
