If you think you would be a good admin, please fill out a request for adminship. All users are encouraged to vote on adminship requests.

### Voting Guidelines

This is not a vote where you only get to vote once. Instead, you are encouraged to look at each administration request, participate in discussion, and vote either **SUPPORT**, **NEUTRAL**, or **OPPOSE** once per candidate.

You are also allowed to change your vote up until the moment voting ends. For example, if you originally voted **NEUTRAL** and then saw an answer from the candidate that pushed you to support, go in and change your vote to **SUPPORT**.

Voting for a particular candidate will last for a minimum of 7 days, or a total of 10 votes (cumulative **SUPPORT** / **NEUTRAL** / **OPPOSE** count), whichever comes last. A RfA can only last a maximum of 30 days before the votes are tallied as is. If a RfA was not successful, the candidate is allowed to reapply for Adminship after 30 days have passed since the end of voting. There is no limit on the number of times someone can request Adminship.

These guidelines are largely arbitrary and are open for community editing.
