## User page requests

**User pages** are pages for personal use — Users often use these pages for personal projects, wiki syntax testing, etc.

#### Notes:

- **If you are requesting your own, top-level user page (i.e. not a subpage), use** `{{upr|~~~}}` to place a request easily (remember to add it at the top of the list, followed by a newline).
- For subpages (such as sandboxes), use `[[User:Your username/subpage name]]`. Remember to include the slash!

<br>

**Please place a `*` before requests** — this puts them in a list, keeping them organized.

Remember to sign your posts with `~~~~` (4 tildes).
