# Dwarf Fortress Wiki:Unblock

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Dwarf_Fortress_Wiki:Unblock) &middot; revision 196778 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-13 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Dwarf_Fortress_Wiki/Unblock.wiki)*

---
If you are mistakenly blocked as a spammer, please post on the [wiki board](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?board=22.0) of the Bay12 Forums and a wiki admin will reverse the block as soon as possible. Alternatively, you can send a PM on the forums to the admin who blocked you (note that "Briess" on this wiki is "Locriani" on the forums).

Do not try to create another account – it will almost certainly be autoblocked by the wiki software, and it can be hard to unblock your first account if your second one gets blocked too.
