#### General notes and recent updates

The wiki currently has quite a bit of ambiguity in terminology as a result of some recent updates, and terminology relating to new features. Most notably (though not exclusively) due to the new menus and tabs introduced with the [v50 update](../../Main/Release_information/50.01.md).
