# Dwarf Fortress Wiki:Centralized Discussion/Template for DFHack

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Dwarf_Fortress_Wiki:Centralized_Discussion/Template_for_DFHack) &middot; revision 239643 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-13 &middot; [wikitext](../../../mirror/wikitext/Dwarf_Fortress_Wiki/Centralized_Discussion/Template_for_DFHack.wiki)*

Given how central DFHack has become to the user experience of many users, and the frequency at which hacks/exploits/bugfixes involving it pop up in the wiki, I think there should be a special template for content involving DFHack, **in adddition** to the one used for mods. This way, new players can make the distinction between "I have to edit a few files to do this" and "I have to download a third party program and run scripts to do this". One is much less daunting than the others, and the players are better informed. [Severedicks](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User:Severedicks) ([talk](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User_talk:Severedicks)) 11:25, 25 January 2019 (UTC)

> With the prevalence of the [LNP](../../Main/LNP.md), I imagine most new players find simple DFHack tweaks \*easier\* than editing the raws--removing [aquifers](../../Main/Aquifer.md) is a prime example (though the LNP makes it easier still with a simple checkbox in the launcher). The {{[mod](../../Template/Mod.md)}} template exists primarily to differentiate non-standard content; since most references to DFHack are short and directly linked to the [DFHack](../../Main/DFHack.md) article, I'm not convinced that an additional template would be helpful. Can you cite a few pages that you feel would benefit from such a template?--[Loci](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User:Loci) ([talk](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User_talk:Loci)) 17:40, 27 January 2019 (UTC)

A simple banner for DFHack stuff will be enough, I believe. > > > [Your first example](../../Main/Faction.md#loyalty-cascade) is exactly the type of situation that I was afraid of--you've got a template box and a section heading dedicated to a single sentence which already states that DFHack is required and links to the DFHack article just in case someone doesn't understand what DFHack is. What additional information could your DFHack template provide that would justify bloating a single sentence to 5x the size? I will grant that a DFHack notice template wouldn't seem unreasonable on a few of your examples where there is already a large section of DFHack-specific information (e.g. [extinction](../../Main/Extinction.md) and [meat industry](../../Main/Meat_industry.md)), but I really don't want to see every sentence on the wiki that mentions DFHack separated into its own section heading and accented by a template notice box.--[Loci](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User:Loci) ([talk](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User_talk:Loci)) 00:07, 3 February 2019 (UTC)
