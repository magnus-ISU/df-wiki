I am a little confused about the use of some terms on the wiki.

(A) The [Skill articles](../../Category/DF2014%253ASkills.md) utilize an infobox with terms 'Labor', 'Job Title', and 'Profession'.

- With 'Job title' referring to the in-game 'profession' name. Regarded as profession title in [profession](../../Main/Profession.md)-\>[labor](../../Main/Labor.md) entry, which make the point of distinguishing it from Jobs.
- while 'Profession' refer to the in-game color coding. Regarded as professional category in [Skill#Professions](../../Main/Skill.md#professions).

> This is a bit confusing, maybe a (1) different terminology should be employed in the infobox, or (2) a tooptip or link to relevant article should be added to the infobox. (3) possibly try for a clearer explanation of the basic terminology in [labor](../../Main/Labor.md).

(B) The lead of Skill articles is all over the place. Some articles focus on:

- the skill - "Miner is the skill", "The bowyer skill is used to"..

> Others focus on:

- the 'Job title' - "A Cook is a dwarf whose highest skill is in cooking."
- the 'Job title' using the term 'profession' - "A gem cutter is the profession of a dwarf whose"
- the person doing the associated labor e.g. [Brewer](../../Main/Brewer.md): "Brewers brew .." "Butchers do the dirty job of killing.."

> Some use all the above but doesn't link to [mining](../../Main/Mining.md) labor nor explain what associated profession is.

- [Miner](../../Main/Miner.md) is the skill associated with both the mining labor and its associated profession.

> I think it might be best to (4) improve interlinking of all relevant article, making the treasure trove of information on the wiki more accessible, (5) figure out a more consistent lead based on input from experienced users here.

Any thougts? --[Jan](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User:Jan) ([talk](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/User_talk:Jan)) 21:42, 19 December 2021 (UTC)
