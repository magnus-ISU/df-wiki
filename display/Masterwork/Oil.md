Oil can be produced at the [screw press](Screw_press.md) from pig tails, shadowleaf bushes or quarry bushes.

Oil can be used to polish wood, harden leather, make soap or burning ammo.

[Pig tail](Pig_tail.md) oil can be cooked with. <sup>\[ [Verify in gameplay](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Verify_in_gameplay&action=edit&redlink=1) \]</sup>
