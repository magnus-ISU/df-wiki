Slag is a material, usually found as bars or massive bars after smelting metals. It is intended as a waste product, causing clutter in the smelter or other workshops. It has no stockpile entries, so it cannot be transported by [wheelbarrow](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Wheelbarrow&action=edit&redlink=1) or [minecart](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Minecart&action=edit&redlink=1).

Slag may be "disposed of" in a [Slag pit](Slag_pit~2.md), with reactions to produce nothing, or to produce [concrete](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Concrete&action=edit&redlink=1) blocks.
