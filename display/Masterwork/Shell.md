Created at a tanner, **shell** can be used to create armour equivalent to bronze. It can also be combined with a second piece of shell at a tanner to create **rigid shell**, which is as strong as iron. It is as heavy as silver, however, which will slow down dwarves wearing clothing and armor made of it if they are inexperienced [armor users](../Main/Armor_user.md).
