**Fine bricks** are similar in purpose to regular [bricks](Brick.md), but are five times more valuable. Most regular bricks have an item value of 5☼, while fine bricks have a value of 25☼. Because of their higher value, they are a good choice for [trade](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Trade&action=edit&redlink=1) goods or improving fortress [value](../Main/Value.md).

A batch of four fine bricks requires two stone boulders, as opposed to the single-stone requirement of normal [bricks](Brick.md).
