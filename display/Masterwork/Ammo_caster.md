An **Ammo caster** is where Smiths create improved [metal](../Main/Metal.md) ammo from metal [bars](../Main/Bar.md).

### Menu

**Ammo caster**

- c Cast copper broadhead bolts (50)
- C Cast copper piercing bolts (50)
- alt+c Cast copper hammerhead bolts (50)
- b Cast bronze broadhead bolts (50)
- B Cast bronze piercing bolts (50)
- alt+b Cast bronze hammerhead bolts (50)
- i Cast iron broadhead bolts (50)
- I Cast iron piercing bolts (50)
- alt+I Cast iron hammerhead bolts (50)
- s Cast steel broadhead bolts (50)
- S Cast steel piercing bolts (50)
- alt+s Cast steel hammerhead bolts (50)
- a Cast adamentine broadhead bolts (50)
- A Cast adamentine piercing bolts (50)
- alt+a Cast adamentine hammerhead bolts (50)
- c Cast copper broadhead arrows (50)
- C Cast copper piercing arrows (50)
- alt+c Cast copper hammerhead arrows (50)
- b Cast bronze broadhead arrows (50)
- B Cast bronze piercing arrows (50)
- alt+b Cast bronze hammerhead arrows (50)
- i Cast iron broadhead arrows (50)
- I Cast iron piercing arrows (50)
- alt+I Cast iron hammerhead arrows (50)
- s Cast steel broadhead arrows (50)
- S Cast steel piercing arrows (50)
- alt+s Cast steel hammerhead arrows (50)
- a Cast adamentine broadhead arrows (50)
- A Cast adamentine piercing arrows (50)
- alt+a Cast adamentine hammerhead arrows (50)
- c Cast copper broadhead javelins (25)
- C Cast copper piercing javelins (25)
- alt+c Cast copper hammerhead javelins (25)
- b Cast bronze broadhead javelins (25)
- B Cast bronze piercing javelins (25)
- alt+b Cast bronze hammerhead javelins (25)
- i Cast iron broadhead javelins (25)
- I Cast iron piercing javelins (25)
- alt+I Cast iron hammerhead javelins (25)
- s Cast steel broadhead javelins (25)
- S Cast steel piercing javelins (25)
- alt+s Cast steel hammerhead javelins (25)
- a Cast adamentine broadhead javelins (25)
- A Cast adamentine piercing javelins (25)
- alt+a Cast adamentine hammerhead javelins (25)

### Uses

- Broadhead ammo can sever body parts
- Hammerhead ammo deals bludgeoning damage making it effective against heavily armored targets
- piercing ammo is an improved version of the regular ammo.

### Notes

- The hotkeys c, C, alt+c, b, B, alt+b, i, I, alt+i, s, S, alt+s, a, A, alt+aare listed as being used multiply times. Using these keys creates the items first listed with the hotkey in the menu (for example, b creates armor stands, not magma-safe blocks).
