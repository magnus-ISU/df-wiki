### V3.e

Introduce Teduks Fine Goods. Allows to trade Rare and valuable materials and items for coins.

- Each merchant needs a "trade license" which you have to order from the liaison first. Its under the misc section in the tradescreen.
- Each merchant accepts coppers (copper), shillings (silver) or sovereigns (gold).
- Trading uses the "diplomacy/ambassador" skill, the better your dwarf can talk and haggle, the higher the quality of the bought goods.
