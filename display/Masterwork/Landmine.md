**Landmines** are devices that attack enemies by emitting a cloud, similar to [Dragonfire](../Main/Dragonfire.md) in vanilla DF. They can harm other creatures in a variety of ways, including excessive heat, cold, or by releasing dust or acid.

### Obtaining landmines

According to their descriptions, landmines can be obtained from the mountainhomes. It is unclear whether other races such as [kobolds](Kobold.md) and [orcs](Orc.md) can obtain them. <sup>\[[Verify](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork_Talk:Landmine&action=edit&redlink=1)\]</sup>

### Mechanics

Note that landmines can also harm each other, or even themselves (this is often accidental, resulting from an attempted attack on a creature within 1-2 tiles).

Upon "death", landmines leave no corpse, and are not butcherable. They also leave no usable body parts behind. However, they will leave behind a spattering of hydraulic fluid (which, being a contaminant, is essentially useless).

### Types of landmines

There are currently nine types of landmines in Masterwork DF:

- [Acid landmines](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Acid_landmine&action=edit&redlink=1)
- [Dryice landmines](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Dryice_landmine&action=edit&redlink=1)
- [Dust landmines](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Dust_landmine&action=edit&redlink=1)
- [Fire landmines](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Fire_landmine&action=edit&redlink=1)
- [Flash landmines](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Flash_landmine&action=edit&redlink=1)
- [Frag landmines](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Frag_landmine&action=edit&redlink=1)
- [Hellfire landmines](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Hellfire_landmine&action=edit&redlink=1)
- [Ice landmines](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Ice_landmine&action=edit&redlink=1)
- [Warpstone landmines](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Warpstone_landmine&action=edit&redlink=1) (see [Warpstone](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Warpstone&action=edit&redlink=1))
