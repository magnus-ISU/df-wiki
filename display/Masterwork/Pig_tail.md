**Pig tails** are an [underground](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Underground&action=edit&redlink=1) [crop](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Crop&action=edit&redlink=1). They can be [grown](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Farming&action=edit&redlink=1) only in summer and autumn. They may be [processed](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Plant_processing&action=edit&redlink=1) at a [farmer's workshop](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Farmer%27s_workshop&action=edit&redlink=1) to create [pig tail thread](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Thread&action=edit&redlink=1), which is then used to [weave](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Weaving&action=edit&redlink=1) [pig tail cloth](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Cloth&action=edit&redlink=1) at a [loom](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Loom&action=edit&redlink=1). Otherwise, they may be [brewed](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Brewing&action=edit&redlink=1) into [Dwarven ale](Dwarven_ale.md). Not to be confused with tails of [actual pigs](Pig.md).

With [Harder Farming](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Harder_Farming&action=edit&redlink=1) turned on pig tails will take one year to grow to maturity. Without it they will take about one season.
