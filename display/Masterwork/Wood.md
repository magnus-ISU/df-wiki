**Wood** in Masterwork Dwarf Fortress is greatly simplified from vanilla DF, a feature that helps prevent FPS death.

### Types of wood

There are five types of wood in Masterwork DF:

- [Rough wood](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Rough_wood&action=edit&redlink=1), produced from most above-ground [trees](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Tree&action=edit&redlink=1)
- [Fungiwood](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Fungiwood&action=edit&redlink=1), produced by most underground trees
- [Netherwood](Netherwood.md), produced by deep-underground trees
- [Ironbark](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Ironbark&action=edit&redlink=1) and [Steeloak](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Steeloak&action=edit&redlink=1), only produced by rare above-ground trees

### Processing

Wood and [planted trees](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Tree&action=edit&redlink=1) can be processed at the [timberyard](Timberyard.md).
