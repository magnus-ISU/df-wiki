**☼Masterwork☼ Dwarf Fortress** (MDF) is a [mod package](../Main/Mod.md) that makes *Dwarf Fortress* four times as big, but still keeps true to the original atmosphere of the game. It adds over 330 [buildings](Building.md), over 6000 [reactions](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Reaction&action=edit&redlink=1), over 1000 [creatures](Creature.md) and up to 35 [races](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Races&action=edit&redlink=1). It includes a selection of [utilities](../Main/Utilities.md) and two playable race plugins ([Orc Fortress](Orc_Fortress.md) and [Kobold Camp](Kobold_Camp.md)). As of v6.0, it's also possible to play as Gnomes, Warlocks, Succubi and Humans, and the 'race' of the Hermit, in which you control a single unit through fort mode.

The Masterwork mod uses a system of generic materials. For example, [leather](../Main/Leather.md) is only called leather (as opposed to "creature-name" leather). This also applies to [silk](../Main/Silk.md), [hair](../Main/Hair.md), [milk](../Main/Milk.md), [meat](../Main/Meat.md) and [blood](../Main/Blood.md), and [bones](../Main/Bone.md). You won't have to scroll through 50 building materials in your list, but only 10. This is one of the few features of MDF that can't be turned on and off. Generic or decreased number of types can also be enabled or disabled for trees, gems, soil, stone, wood, meat, and gem shapes. Together with some other performance improving tweaks, Masterwork DF could actually increase your [frame rate](../Main/Frame_rate.md) by about 25% from vanilla DF.

There are a wide range of tough enemies to up the challenge level, exotic materials, new [industries](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Industry&action=edit&redlink=1); even threats from within the fortress like [diseases](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Disease&action=edit&redlink=1) and hostile [cults](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Cult&action=edit&redlink=1). [Religion](Religion.md), [guns](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Gun&action=edit&redlink=1), [mages](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Mage&action=edit&redlink=1) and [slaves](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Slave&action=edit&redlink=1). [Runes](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Masterwork:Rune&action=edit&redlink=1), [mithril](Mithril.md), [automatons](Automaton.md) and [raptors](Raptor.md). There are [libraries](Library.md), [garrisons](Garrison.md) and [guildhalls](Guildhall.md) to skill up your dwarves. In version 3f, a [magic system](Magic.md) was added. Add only the parts that appeal to you; most settings can be customized by the player with the included Settings.exe user interface. You can enable and disable parts of the mod just by pressing a button and generating a new world.

There are lots of new [materials](Metal.md) — iron, silver and steel [weapons](Weapon.md) are just the beginning.
