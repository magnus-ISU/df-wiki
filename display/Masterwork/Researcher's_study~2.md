This building is the fundamental building of the researches available. Each reaction researches a different building in several steps, each with a chance to fail and a chance to produce a random breakthrough. These breakthroughs allow you to build the more advanced buildings. Once the [School of Wizardry](School_of_wizardry.md) is researched and built further research in [magic](Magic.md) can occur there.
