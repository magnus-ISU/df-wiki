**Skin** is a tissue layer common to most [creatures](../Main/Creature.md). Different types of creatures have different types of skin.

- Most mundane creatures have raw hide, which displays in-game as **skin**. This is the only type that can be tanned into [leather](Leather.md).
- Insectoids creatures have [Chitin](Chitin.md).
- Reptilian creatures and fish generally have [Scales](Scale.md).

All skin types are stored in a refuse stockpile (both fresh and rotten).

### Skin uses

Raw skin, Chitin and Scales can be processed at a [Tanner](Tanner.md) by a dwarf with the [Tanning](../Main/Tanning.md) labor enabled in order to produce Leather, Chitin and Scale respectivly. If skins are not tanned automatically, try using your [manager](../Main/Manager.md) to manually queue "Tan a Hide" jobs.
