A **stonecrafter's workshop** is where [stonecrafters](../Main/Stone_crafter.md) create [stone](../Main/Stone.md) furniture from stone [blocks](../Main/Block.md). This includes items that cannot be made by the [mason's workshop](../Main/Mason's_workshop~2.md) such as [beds](../Main/Bed.md), [bins](../Main/Bin.md), [barrels](../Main/Barrel.md) and [buckets](../Main/Bucket.md). A small selection of [magma-safe](../Main/Magma-safe.md) objects are also available.

### Common Uses

- Construct stone beds, not normally available to be made out of stone.
- Construct magma-safe blocks and furniture from those blocks, useful in magma traps and on [volcano](../Main/Volcano.md) embarks.
- Construct stone bins and barrels for item storage, useful when wood supplies are low.

### Notes

- This workshop can be extremely effective when paired with the mass-produced concrete from a [slag pit](Slag_pit~2.md) or [bricks](Brick.md) from a [brick oven](Brick_oven~2.md).
- The hotkeys b, d, h, r, and u are listed as being used twice. Using these keys creates the items first listed with the hotkey in the menu (for example, b creates armor stands, not magma-safe blocks).
