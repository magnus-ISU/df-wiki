> *A stocky humanoid fond of drink, industry and destruction. The Chaos Legion are bound by strange and terrible oaths to a dark aspect of the blood god Armok.*

Chaos dwarves are, for all intents and purposes, identical to normal dwarves. If you enable them, expect first some baby snatchers trying to sneak up your fort. They'll rarely attack in the first or second year. When they come, have a really well trained army or all entrances to the fortress filled with traps, preferably cage ones, since weapon traps are much weaker in Masterwork compared to vanilla. Most of the time, they will lay siege, but it is not uncommon for them to sneak in an ambush.
