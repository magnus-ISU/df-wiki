### The Tale of Girdertrampled: Introduction: Strike The Earth

*From the journals of 'Schmendreck' Avuzkekim*

#### 20 Obsidian 1050

I have been recently informed by our fortress' nobles that I am to be the leader of a new fortress.

#### 23 Obsidian 1050

Tomorrow I will meet the fellow dwarfs who will accompany my on this endeavor.

#### 24 Obsidian 1050

I met my fellow dwarfs.

We are doomed.

They are among the laziest and most slovenly of any dwarfs I have yet encountered. There is another miner, a mason, two planters who double as cook and brewer, a carpenter, and finally one dwarf who seemed to specialize in as many areas as he could (building designer, stonecrafter, mechanic, judge of intent.)

#### 25 Obsidian 1050

I have been given a map of the location which we are supposed to settle. [![Girdertrampled embark.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/7/72/Girdertrampled_embark.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Girdertrampled_embark.png)

At least its not in the desert.

#### 26 Obsidian 1050

We embark.

#### 1 Granite 1051

We arrive

Of course the first thing every one wants to do is stand on the edge of a cliff. [![Girdertrampled embark cliff.png](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/4/42/Girdertrampled_embark_cliff.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Girdertrampled_embark_cliff.png) Goddammit.
