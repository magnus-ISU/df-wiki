### About The Game

See the [Dwarf Fortress thread on the xkcd forums](http://forums.xkcd.com/viewtopic.php?f=21&t=19469&start=0&st=0&sk=t&sd=a) The idea of starting a succession game was thrown around for a while. One poster suggested a community fortress, where every player starts off on the same map at the same location, and then designs their own individual fortresses.<br>However, [Schmendreck](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=User:Schmendreck&action=edit&redlink=1) started this bloodline game and offered other players to join. So begins the tale of Girdertrampled.
