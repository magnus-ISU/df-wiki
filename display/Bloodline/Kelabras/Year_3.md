### **3rd Year in Kelabras** by Adil Itebasmel

#### 13th Felsite 225

Last winter was very hard for us and with spring came some relief. 15 new dwarfs arrived this morning as a great relief. It's time we recruit a few of them for a defense force and completely rework duties for our fellow dwarfs. We also have to dig new rooms for these dwarfs. I'm very optimistic about this year.

#### Autumn 225

A caravan came this autumn but was overrun by raiders. We also lost several important dwarfs including our leader. No immigrants came. It's not likely that we will survive this winter so we decided to return home with the remnants of the caravan.
