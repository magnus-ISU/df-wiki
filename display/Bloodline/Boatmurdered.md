**Boatmurdered** was a [succession game](../Main/Succession_game.md) of *Dwarf Fortress* on the Something Awful forums from 2007, beginning in version 0.22.110.22f and presumably finishing in version 0.23.130.23a. From this spawned an epic tale, incorporating hordes of belligerent dwarf-eating [elephants](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Elephant), floods of biblical proportions, flaming [puppies](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Dog), and more hilarious pranks. After many goblin raiders and elven traders were fried with floods of lava, there was a brief period of tranquility. Then one of the last few dwarves, unhappy from the recent defacement of a masterwork engraving, caught [fire](../Main/Fire.md) and started a fist fight with another dwarf (setting it on fire too), causing a [tantrum](../Main/Tantrum.md) and [insanity](../Main/Insanity.md) spiral that eventually destroyed the whole fortress. To be fair, she'd given plenty of warnings that she was becoming unhinged, such as attempting to drink from the [magma river](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/23a:Magma_flow) and making numerous engravings depicting dwarves on fire, elephants killing dwarves, and a carving of a carving of cheese. How many other fortresses manage to create artifact caskets?

The entire saga can be [read online](http://lparchive.org/LetsPlay/Boatmurdered/), or downloaded to read offline [in PDF format](https://dffd.bay12games.com/file.php?id=4120). As the editor says, the madness surrounding Boatmurdered speaks for itself. Be warned: the language contained within can cause necrosis of the ears, and should be avoided by [children](../Main/Children.md), the stunningly susceptible to disease, [kittens](../Main/Kitten.md), and other worthless [disease](../Main/Syndrome.md) [spreaders](../Main/Noble.md).

### Works

The saga can also be heard as a [narrated tale](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLJtPFXAjo-GdOkFwQsC_5zyIHLaMFsaOM), with screenshots and fan art.

There was also an animation of Boatmurdered being made by LavaLevel, but [the project was cancelled.](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=60750.msg4628801#msg4628801) The forum thread is still available if you want to see [the progress which was made.](http://www.bay12forums.com/smf/index.php?topic=60750.0)

[Koganusan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FKv-liYc4qU), by Dabu & Simon Swerwer, is the 15th track in the first Premium [soundtrack](../Main/Soundtrack.md) volume, playing after fortress has been abandoned or defeated.
