### Summary

Ringfissures is a succession game that has found its origin on the /tg/ board of 4chan and embarked on February 18th 2009. The general idea is to play for a set period of time, in this case a single year, from spring till the next spring, then pass on the save game to the person that comes next. The number one goal has been decided to be survival, but everyone is free to start any crazy projects they want. Players get about three days to finish their turn, once they are done they get to name one dwarf after themselves to represent them in the game.<br><br>Currently, the central point of communication, including logs, is located [here](http://undergrad.proboards77.com/index.cgi?board=dfal) <br><br> The order of players, as determined by random dicerolls is:<br> - Phrost<br> - Bobothebum<br> - Quor<br> - Slant/Rhyme<br> - Misterlich<br> - Crowbeard<br> - SaturdaySaint<br> - CKscientist<br> - Rimtlan<br> - Meinzer<br>
