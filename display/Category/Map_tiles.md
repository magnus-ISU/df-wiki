# Category:Map tiles

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Category:Map_tiles) &middot; revision 235834 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-13 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Category/Map_tiles.wiki)*

---
A **map tile** is a single unit of space on a map. The term [map tile](../Main/Map_tile.md) is used when referring to the individual square under the k view cursor. [Walls](../Main/Wall.md), [ramps](../Main/Ramp.md), [trees](../Main/Tree.md), [grass](../Main/Grass.md), [boulders](../Main/Boulder.md), [open space](../Main/Open_space.md), [brooks](../Main/Brook.md) and [rivers](../Main/River.md) are all examples of map tiles that make up mountains, plains, cliffs and valleys. [Stairs](../Main/Stair.md) that you dig up and down are also types of map tile, but not the ones that require material to construct. The difference is subtle, but map tiles are a part of the map, while [constructions](../Main/Construction.md) are built on-top of the map. (*This is a running theory, based on observation*.) Whereas constructions must be built like buildings, map tiles exist as part of the map during world generation.

- See [digging](../Main/Digging.md) for an alternative view on tile states
