Animals with the TRAINABLE or TRAINABLE_HUNTING [creature token](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/v0.34:Creature_token#T) can be trained for hunting by an [animal trainer](../Main/Animal_trainer.md). Trained hunting animals gain speed, agility, and possibly ambusher and observer bonuses. Once trained, they can be assigned to follow specific [hunters](../Main/Hunter.md).
