**[Breeding](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Category:DF2012:Breeding&action=edit&redlink=1)** creatures (those with the necessary `[CHILD]` tokens) can produce offspring in fortress mode. Conversely, **[non-breeding](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Category:DF2012:Non-Breeding&action=edit&redlink=1)** creatures cannot reproduce in fortress mode, even though they may do so during worldgen (e.g. [dragons](../Main/Dragon.md)). Breeding creatures are significantly more useful as stock for a [meat industry](../Main/Meat_industry.md) than their non-breeding counterparts.
