**Beaching** is a characteristically odd behavior carried out by [creatures](../Main/Creature.md) with a `[BEACH_FREQUENCY]` [creature token](../Main/Creature_token~2.md#b). These [aquatic](../Main/Aquatic.md) animals will occasionally strand themselves on land, where they are immobile and unable to breathe. This is intended to model the poorly-explained real-life behavior of similar animals; see [Beached whale](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beached_whale) on Wikipedia for more information.
