# Category:Professions

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Category:Professions) &middot; revision 37192 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-13 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Category/Professions.wiki)*

---
Professions are categories of [skills](../Main/Skills.md). A dwarf's best non-military skill determines what profession that dwarf will belong to (if not Activated for the [military](../Main/Military.md)).

Dwarf Fortress uses "profession" to indicate the dwarf's specific skill specialty, but for the purposes of the DF Wiki it is used to indicate the supercategory that specialized skills belong to.
