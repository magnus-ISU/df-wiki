Creatures with the [TRAPAVOID token](../Main/Creature_token~2.md#t) cannot be affected by normal [traps](../Main/Trap.md), and do not set off [pressure plates](../Main/Pressure_plate~2.md). By ignoring a large part of some players' [defenses](../Main/Defense_guide.md), such creatures can be difficult to deal with.
