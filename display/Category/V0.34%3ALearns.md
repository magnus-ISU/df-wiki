**Learning** [creatures](../Main/Creature.md) (those with the [CAN_LEARN creature tag](../Main/Creature_token~2.md#c)) may increase their [skills](../Main/Skill.md) through practice and use; conversely, creatures that cannot learn will never experience a skill increase. \[CAN_LEARN\] and \[CAN_SPEAK\] are implied by \[INTELLIGENT\]. Creatures with \[CAN_LEARN\] will improve their social skills when standing near other members of their own species, but not when standing near \[CAN_LEARN\] members of other species.
