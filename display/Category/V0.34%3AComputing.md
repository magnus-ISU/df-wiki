Computing is the practice of making (mostly) primitive computers using [fluids](../Main/Water.md), [levers](../Main/Lever.md), [pressure plates](../Main/Pressure_plate~2.md), [mechanisms](../Main/Mechanism.md), and even animals. Having little to no practical purpose within the game, computing is a type of meta-game for more technically minded players.
