**Shearable** tame creatures (those with the SHEARABLE_TISSUE_LAYER [creature token](../Main/Creature_token~2.md#s)) can be [sheared](../Main/Shearer.md) to produce [wool](../Main/Wool.md) (without harming the creature itself). Only mature creatures may be sheared, and previously sheared creatures may not be sheared again until their wool has regrown.
