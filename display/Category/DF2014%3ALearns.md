<br> Creatures with `[CAN_LEARN]` are able to learn [skills](../Main/Skill.md) and gain [experience](../Main/Experience.md). They are considered sapient, and this has a number of [ethics](../Main/Ethic.md) related considerations, e.g. your dwarves won't [butcher](../Main/Butcher.md) them, and killing them will cement your [reputation](../Main/Reputation.md) as a killer or a murderer in adventure mode. Learning creatures do not [breed](../Main/Breed.md) with just anyone and must be [marriage](../Main/Marriage.md) compatible to have children, even if they cannot actually marry. In fortress mode, sapient members of your fort need to eat, drink and sleep. [Training](../Main/Training.md) a creature that can learn will result in their applying for [citizenship](../Main/Citizenship.md).
