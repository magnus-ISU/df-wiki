# Category:Buildings

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Category:Buildings) &middot; revision 6058 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-13 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Category/Buildings.wiki)*

---
A [building](../Main/Building.md) is a structure made by a sentient life-form out of raw or processed materials, which is operated in order to produce objects or otherwise perform work. Buildings include [workshops](../Main/Workshop.md), [machines](../Main/Machine.md), and the like.

Buildings are distinct from [rooms](../Main/Room.md).
