This is the section of the wiki that contains information about the worlds created in *Dwarf Fortress*. To begin playing in DF, you must first create a world to play in. This requires you select **Create New World** or **Design New World with Advanced Parameters** to enter either the [world generation](../Main/World_generation.md) screen or its [alternative](../Main/Advanced_world_generation.md). Although each world is generated using a series of fractal algorithms, many things in each world remain constant.
