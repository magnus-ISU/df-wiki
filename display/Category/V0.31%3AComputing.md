<br> [Computing](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/v0.31:Computing) in Dwarf Fortress is the practice of making (mostly) primitive computers using fluids, levers, pressure plates, mechanism, and even animals. Having little to no practical purpose within the game, computing is a type of meta-game for more technically minded players.
