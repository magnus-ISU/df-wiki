Creatures with the CURIOUSBEAST_GUZZLER [creature token](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/v0.34:Creature_token#C) will happily devour your [alcohol](../Main/Alcohol.md) stocks if given the chance. This can prove particularly [fun](../Main/Fun.md) when your newly-founded fort's entire alcohol supply is still sitting in the [wagon](../Main/Wagon.md) topside.

This property can also be used to capture certain creatures (such as [grizzly bears](../Main/Grizzly_bear.md)) by surrounding a small drink stockpile with [cage](../Main/Cage.md) traps.
