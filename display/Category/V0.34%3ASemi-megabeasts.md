**Semi-megabeasts** are large and dangerous enemies that are similar to but smaller than regular [megabeasts](../Main/Megabeast.md). In fortress mode, semi-megabeasts are attracted by the [wealth](../Main/Wealth.md) of your fortress, and will not appear until a certain population size has been reached. When a semi-megabeast appears, the game will pause and display a notification of its arrival.

Semi-megabeasts are:

- [Minotaurs](../Main/Minotaur.md)
- [Giants](../Main/Giant.md)
- [Ettins](../Main/Ettin.md)
- [Cyclopes](../Main/Cyclops.md)
