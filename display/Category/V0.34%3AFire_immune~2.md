**Fire-Immune** creatures (those with a FIREIMMUNE or FIREIMMUNE_SUPER [token](../Main/Creature_token~2.md#f)) cannot be injured by [fire](../Main/Fire.md), including fireball and firejet attacks. Creatures with the FIREIMMUNE_SUPER tag are also immune to dragonfire attacks.
