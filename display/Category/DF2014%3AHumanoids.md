**Humanoids** are [creatures](../Main/Creature.md) that share the same body shape and physiology as [humans](../Main/Human.md). Additionally, humanoids commonly have advanced mental functions of humans and [dwarves](../Main/Dwarves.md), such as [speech](../Main/Speech.md) and high-level reasoning. Most of the humanoid creatures in *Dwarf Fortress* can develop [civilizations](../Main/Civilization.md). Most humanoids can be chosen as player characters in [Adventure mode](../Main/Adventure_mode~2.md).<br>

Many humanoid creatures share the same natural abilities of their non-humanoid counterparts. For example, a [cheetah man](../Main/Cheetah_man.md) shares the same ability of fast running as that of a standard [cheetah](../Main/Cheetah.md).
