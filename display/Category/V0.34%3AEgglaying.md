**Eggs** are laid by creatures with the [LAYS_EGGS](../Main/Creature_token~2.md#l) token. This includes ordinary fowl and reptiles, and also some more exotic creatures such as [giant eagles](../Main/Giant_eagle.md), [dragons](../Main/Dragon.md), [kobolds](../Main/Kobold.md), and many others. Eggs can be used by dwarves as [food](../Main/Food.md), or they can be hatched. Eggs can only be eaten raw if at least one of their component materials (yolk, white, or shell) is edible raw; otherwise, they have to be cooked into prepared meals first.

Egg laying animals will claim an empty [Nest box](../Main/Nest_box.md) if one is available.

Males can also be [modded](../Main/Mod.md) to lay eggs, although these may not hatch.<sup>Needs clarification</sup>
