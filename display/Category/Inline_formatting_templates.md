# Category:Inline formatting templates

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Category:Inline_formatting_templates) &middot; revision 190627 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-13 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Category/Inline_formatting_templates.wiki)*

---
[Formatting templates](Formatting_Templates.md) are templates that can be used to quickly style their contents in some sort of way. *Inline* formatting templates are a subcategory of that group, basically a number of small inline utility macros intended to simplify simple tasks and repetitive wiki-markup tasks. Achieving general familiarity with this class of templates is very useful for editing on this wiki.
