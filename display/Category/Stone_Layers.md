When picking a starting location, you will be shown what layers of stone are prominent in the area you have selected, and this will give you a clue on what kind of [ore](../Main/Ore.md) to expect, if [flux](../Main/Flux~2.md), [sand](../Main/Sand.md) or some other special feature is available.
