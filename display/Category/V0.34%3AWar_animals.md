*For information on the selection and training of war animals, see [animal trainer](../Main/Animal_trainer.md).*

Animals with the TRAINABLE or TRAINABLE_WAR [creature token](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/v0.34:Creature_token#T) can be trained for use in war by an [animal trainer](../Main/Animal_trainer.md). Trained war animals are capable of causing greater damage in combat and can be assigned to follow specific dwarves.
