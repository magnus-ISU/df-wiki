**No Exert** creatures (those with the NOEXERT [token](../Main/Creature_token~2.md#n)) need no [sleep](../Main/Sleep.md), and never become tired or exhausted. In the case of a particularly dangerous creature like a [bronze colossus](../Main/Bronze_colossus.md), the NOEXERT tag guarantees that the foe will continue the battle as long as there are any dwarves left alive.
