*Dwarf Fortress* features a wide variety of **animals**. Some, you can bring with you when you first set off on your journey, others [immigrants](../Main/Immigrant.md) will bring with them, and lots more will show up roaming the wilderness. Some can be [tamed](../Main/Tame.md), others may become [pets](../Main/Pet.md) or [mounts](../Main/Mount.md), some make good [hunting](../Main/Hunting.md) targets, some can be bred for [livestock](../Main/Livestock.md)...and yet, others, like the dread [elephant](../Main/Elephant.md), can be dangerous terrors.
