[Creatures](../Main/Creature.md) with the `[NOSTUN]` [creature token](../Main/Creature_token~2.md#n) cannot be [stunned](../Main/Status_icon.md). These creatures can [fall](../Main/Gravity.md) multiple [z-levels](../Main/Z-level.md), suffer great [pain](../Main/Pain.md), or be injected with stunning [venom](../Main/Venom.md) without the momentary vulnerability of becoming stunned. Creatures with this token will attack relentless in combat until they are killed or knocked unconscious from pain. Luckily creatures with this token are rare.

Interestingly, this protection does not extend to large [webs](../Main/Web.md); webbed creatures will still pause and trigger any [traps](../Main/Trap.md) in their current tile.
