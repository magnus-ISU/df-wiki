# Category:Modding

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Category:Modding) &middot; revision 279247 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-13 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Category/Modding.wiki)*

---
**Modding** refers to modifying Dwarf Fortress's files (or, indeed, the files of any other game) to change some element of gameplay, usually by a third-party developer or the end user. In *Dwarf Fortress*, this usually refers to changes to the so-called *raws*, the game files that describe a wide range of elements, ranging from what [plants](../Main/Plant.md) can be milled, to what kinds of [rock](../Main/Rock.md) exist, to how empires are formed.

Most editing involves adding or removing "tokens" from the raw files. The [tokens](../Main/Token.md) are the actual parameters for each item, and that article is a good place to start.
