Shrubs in south Texas.

A [shrub](../Main/Shrub.md) is a small [plant](../Main/Plant.md) that grows naturally, as opposed to a domesticated [farm](../Main/Farm.md) crop. They can be found all over the world in [grassy](../Main/Grass.md) areas, [savannas](../Main/Savanna.md), [forests](../Main/Forest.md), and [deserts](../Main/Desert.md). They will also sprout on soil and muddy stone underground if a [cavern](../Main/Cavern.md) has been breached. Shrubs are instrumental for [plant gathering](../Main/Plant_gathering.md), and in environments with a significant amount of [vegetation](../Main/Crop.md), they can be an excellent supply of food. Gathering shrubs also serves to provide a supply of [seeds](../Main/Seed.md) for use in planting surface [crops](../Main/Crop.md), such as [longland grass](../Main/Longland_grass~2.md) and [strawberries](../Main/Strawberry.md).

<br> While many [crops](../Main/Crop.md) can also grow as shrubs, plants in this category **only** appear as shrubs.
