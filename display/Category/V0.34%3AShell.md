Assorted shells

A **Shell** is a hard external covering used by certain creatures for defense. Shells can be obtained by capturing or killing [shell-bearing creatures](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=Category:DF2012:Shell&action=edit&redlink=1) and processing them at a [fishery](../Main/Fishery.md) (or in rare cases, a [butcher's shop](../Main/Butcher's_shop~2.md)). Examples of shelled [vermin](../Main/Vermin.md) critters are [turtles](../Main/Turtle.md), [oysters](../Main/Oyster.md) and [mussels](../Main/Mussel.md); larger shelled [creatures](../Main/Creature.md) include [giant snails](../Main/Giant_snail.md) and some [forgotten beasts](../Main/Forgotten_beast.md). Shells currently cannot be acquired via [trading](../Main/Trading.md) -- turtles, crustaceans and shellfish purchased from traders have already been processed and had their shells removed.

Shells can be stored in a [refuse stockpile](../Main/Stockpile.md#refuse), but stored shells may disappear due to [vermin](../Main/Vermin.md) such as [rats](../Main/Rat.md). Shells can be used for [crafts](../Main/Craft.md) and [decorations](../Main/Decoration.md) at a [craftsdwarf's workshop](../Main/Craftsdwarf's_workshop~2.md) using the [bone carver](../Main/Bone_carver.md) skill. Shells may occasionally be requested by a [moody](../Main/Strange_mood.md) dwarf, so it is best to keep a few on hand just in case.
