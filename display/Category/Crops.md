# Category:Crops

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Category:Crops) &middot; revision 298727 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-13 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Category/Crops.wiki)*

**Crops** are [plants](../Main/Plants.md) that may be grown at [farm](../Main/Farm.md) plots. There are two types of crops: above ground and subterranean. The [seeds](../Main/Seed.md) of subterranean crops may be brought from the starting screen or purchased from the [dwarf](../Main/Dwarf.md) [traders](../Main/Trade.md). Above ground crops may be gathered by dwarves with the [plant gathering](../Main/Plant_gathering.md) labor enabled.
