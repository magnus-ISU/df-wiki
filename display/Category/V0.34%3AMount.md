Enemies in [sieges](../Main/Siege.md) will often arrive riding on other [creatures](../Main/Creature.md), their **mounts**. These mounts can not only add to the attacks of those mounted on them, but may also give their riders more abilities: their mounts might be able to [fly](../Main/Flying.md) or [swim](../Main/Swimmer.md), might be [building destroyers](../Main/Building_destroyer.md), might prevent the operation of [bridges](../Main/Bridge.md), might be faster and fleeter of foot, or they might just be plain scary in combat.
