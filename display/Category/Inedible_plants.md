Not all plants the [dwarves](../Main/Dwarf.md) can either find, or grow, can be [eaten](../Main/Food.md) - either raw or [cooked](../Main/Kitchen.md). Other purposes include use in making [cloth](../Main/Cloth.md), [thread](../Main/Thread.md), [dye](../Main/Dye.md) or [alcohol](../Main/Alcohol.md).
