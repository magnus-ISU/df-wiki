Creatures with the `[NOPAIN]` [creature token](../Main/Creature_token~2.md#n) feel no [pain](../Main/Pain.md) in (or out of) [combat](../Main/Combat.md). These creatures will suffer grievous [wounds](../Main/Wound.md) and continue fighting without pause, making them significantly harder to kill - many foes are dispatched by a blow to the head after they fall [unconscious](../Main/Unconscious.md) from pain. Luckily creatures with this token are rare.
