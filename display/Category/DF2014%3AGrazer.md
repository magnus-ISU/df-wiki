Tame grazing animals (those with the GRAZER [token](../Main/Creature_token~2.md#g)) require a constant source of [grass](../Main/Grass.md) or [cave moss](../Main/Cave_moss.md) to survive. [Pastures](../Main/Pasture.md) are currently the easiest such source, however care must be taken to ensure that the pasture is large enough to provide food for all the assigned animals (and any auto-assigned babies).
