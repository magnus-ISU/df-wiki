**Aquatic** creatures have the `[AQUATIC]` [creature token](../Main/Creature_token~2.md), and so are restricted to life underwater. An aquatic creature that is located in water less than 4/7 [deep](../Main/Water_depth.md) or removed from water entirely will air-drown; aquatic animals caught in such situations even briefly will be momentarily [winded](../Main/Status_icon.md). Usually such situations are the result of dwarven interaction, except in the case of [beached](../Main/Beaching.md) [whales](../Main/Whale.md). Note that aquatic creatures in [cages](../Main/Cage.md) (or [aquariums](../Main/Aquarium.md) for flavor) do not currently drown, unless they were drowning when they were placed or caught in the cage.
