<br> **Genderless** creatures do not have [castes](../Main/Caste.md) for male and female members of their species. As such, they are incapable of [breeding](../Main/Breed.md). This means the population size of genderless creatures is capped at [worldgen](../Main/Worldgen.md) and won't go higher, and in fortress mode whatever groups wander in the maps are all you're going to get.

Genderless creatures are generally unusually bizarre in appearance or constitution, even by DF standards, and presumably they don't have gender because it wouldn't have made sense to have them reproduce in game. They're also relatively rare and generally found in the [caverns](../Main/Cavern.md), if found at all.
