### Signing your comments

To make conversations easier to follow, **please sign your comments** on talk pages with 4 tildes: `--~~~~`, `–~~~~`, or `~~~~` are suggested (slight variations are also acceptable; only the 4 tildes are required to produce the signature). This works for *any* users (anonymous/unregistered users and registered/logged-in users). If you are [logged in](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Special:UserLogin), you can change your signature in your [preferences](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Special:Preferences).

### Template usage

Please [subst](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Template_substitution) this template when signing others' comments.

To do so, type

```
{{subst:unsigned|username}}
```

This turns into:

– unsigned comment by [username](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php?title=User:Username&action=edit&redlink=1)
