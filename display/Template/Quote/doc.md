### Usage

{{quote}} adds a quotation **box** to an article page. The main difference between Quote and [Quote Box](../Quote_Box.md) is that Quote creates a centered box that encompasses the whole page, while Quote Box creates a smaller, left-aligned box.

This is easier to type and more wiki-like than the equivalent HTML \<blockquote\>, and has additional pre-formatted attribution and source parameters.

#### Synopsis

**Parameters**

`{{quote|text}}`

#### Example

**Wikitext**

`{{quote|Cry "Havoc" and let slip the dogs of war.}}`

**Result**

*Cry "Havoc" and let slip the dogs of war.*
