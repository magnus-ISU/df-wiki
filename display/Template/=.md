# Template:=

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Template:%3D) &middot; revision 194585 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-13 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Template/=.wiki)*

---
=

---

Most templates bork when an equals sign is included directly in an ordered parameter; with this template, now if you use {{=}} instead of = you'll be fine.

Compare the following:

<table align="center">
<tbody><tr>
<th>Code</th>
<th>Result
</th></tr>
<tr>
<td><pre>{{Colored Notice Box|#f00|2+2=5}}</pre></td>
<td><table>
<tbody><tr><td></td><td>Message  </td></tr></tbody></table>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td><pre>{{Colored Notice Box|#f00|1=2+2=5}}</pre></td>
<td><table>
<tbody><tr><td></td><td>2+2=5  </td></tr></tbody></table>
</td></tr>
<tr>
<td><pre>{{Colored Notice Box|#f00|2+2{{=}}5}}</pre></td>
<td><table>
<tbody><tr><td></td><td>2+2=5  </td></tr></tbody></table>
</td></tr></tbody></table>
