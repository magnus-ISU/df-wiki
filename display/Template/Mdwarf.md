# Template:Mdwarf

*[Source](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Template:Mdwarf) &middot; revision 133720 &middot; retrieved 2026-09-13 &middot; [wikitext](../../mirror/wikitext/Template/Mdwarf.wiki)*

---
[![{{{2}}}](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/images/c/cf/Dwarf2.png)](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/File:Dwarf2.png)

**Template:mdwarf** can be used to display dwarves close to what they appear as in Dwarf Fortress. It can be used together with the [raw tile template](Raw_Tile.md), however it doesn't scale with the user's font size. This might lead to problems for users with uncommon settings.

### Usage

```
{{mdwarf|<color>|<size>}}
```

#### Examples

```
{| style="color: #fff; background: #000"
|-
| {{mdwarf|7:0|10px}} || Wrestlers
|-
| {{mdwarf|6:1|10px}} || Spearmasters
|-
| {{mdwarf|7:1|10px}} || Elite Wrestlers
|-
| {{mdwarf|2:0|10px}} || Marksdwarves
|-
| {{mdwarf|0:1|10px}} || Recruits
|-
| {{mdwarf|2:1|10px}} || Elite Crossbowdwarves
|-
| {{mdwarf|1:1|10px}} || Axe Lords
|-
| {{mdwarf|5:0|10px}} || Hammerdwarves
|-
| {{mdwarf|3:0|10px}} || Swordsdwarves
|-
| {{mdwarf|4:0|10px}} || Macedwarves
|-
| {{mdwarf|1:0|10px}} || Axedwarves
|-
| {{mdwarf|6:0|10px}} || Speardwarves
|-
| {{mdwarf|4:1|10px}} || Mace Lords
|}
```
