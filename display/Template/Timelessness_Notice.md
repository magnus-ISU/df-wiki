### Usage

Use this notice at the top of pages that have broken [Rule T](../Dwarf_Fortress_Wiki/Community_Portal.md#t). The notice should be removed when the page is corrected, or discussion indicates that the page is free of violations. Timelessness is not a concern in articles in the main space, such as [Dwarf Fortress](../Main/Dwarf_Fortress.md) or [Caravan arc](../Main/Caravan_arc~2.md).

Add {{Timelessness Notice}} to the top of any offending page.
